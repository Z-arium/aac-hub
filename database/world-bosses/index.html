Major world bosses

Fixed spawn times so groups can prepare.

Kraken

Lv 50

West of the Arcadian Sea

Giant squid. Only takes damage from ships.

3 Gilda Stars from the daily kill quest.

See spawn times

Lv 50

Karkasse Ridgelands

5 Gilda Stars from the daily kill quest.

See spawn times

See spawn times

Thunderwing Titan

Lv 55

Reedwind

Anthalon

Sungold Fields

Delphinad Ghost Ship

Lv 50

Arcadian Sea (several spots)

Daily challenge for ships. Stays up for 12 hours.

3 Gilda Stars from the daily kill quest.
