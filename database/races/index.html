Races

Racial skills are instant cast. Classic swaps some racial skills between races for balance. From the AAClassic wiki.

Nuian Alliance (west)

Nuian

  • Explosive Expert · 30 min

    +3% ship and tank cannon damage for 5 min

  • Nui's Blessing · 30 min

    +8% Strength, Spirit, Intelligence, Stamina and Agility for 5 min, fading over time

Elf

  • Lithe Flow · 30 min

    +5% swim speed and 30 s longer breath for 5 min

  • Earth Lover · 30 min

    +10% max health for nearby allies' mounts for 5 min

Dwarf

  • Catlike Reflexes · 30 min

    No falling damage for 5 min

  • Dwarven Artifice · 30 min

    -30% Machining production time for 5 min

Haranyan Alliance (east)

Harani

  • Jungle Tamers · 30 s (as listed)

    -30% logging and herb gathering time, resets Recall cooldown, for 5 min

  • Explosive Expert · 30 s (as listed)

    +3% ship and tank cannon damage for 5 min

Firran

  • Strong Claws · 30 min

    Climb 50% faster and +10% move speed while dashing for 5 min

  • Catlike Reflexes · 30 min

    No falling damage for 5 min

Warborn

  • Lithe Flow · 30 min

    +5% swim speed and 30 s longer breath for 5 min

  • Brute Strength · 30 min

    +10% move speed while carrying trade packs for 5 min
