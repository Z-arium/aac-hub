## Kinds of fishing

- **Bait fishing:** a rod and bait worms in any water. Gives various fish.
- **Sport fishing:** freshwater (lakes and ponds) or saltwater (at sea).
- **Ice fishing:** in the Mirror Tundra.
- **Advanced fishing:** use **Ground Fish Chum** to bait **Big Belly Fish** in the Sunspeck Sea. Needs a capable rod and max Fishing proficiency.

## Rods

Classic brings back later rods, so you choose between **cast time** and **damage**. The **Ultimate** rod has both.

## Sport fishing

- **Chum:** any chopped fish bucket except squid works on a saltwater pool. Chopped squid is for freshwater.
- When a fish bites, a minigame starts. Use the skill that matches the fish's current debuff. For example, press *Stand Firm Left* when it shows.
- **Big Reel In** and **Reel In** do most of the damage. Don't miss them.

## What to do with fish

- Sell them as they are.
- Chop them into chum for fishing spots.
- Turn them into **Dawn Lake Essence**. 3 Solzreed Mackerel make 1 Essence.
- Big Belly Fish open like crates and can hold treasure maps.

## Classic changes

- **Old-style pools** expire on a timer instead of a fish count, and each has a set fish type. A Marlin pool can be worth a lot.
- Pool timers are always visible.
- Every fish type takes every chum type.
- Labor use is doubled and gold per labor is adjusted. Some of the labor now goes to the turn-in, so you lose less if pirates take your catch.
- **Sunfish Schools** sometimes spawn after a feeding frenzy. Chumming one alerts the whole server.

On Saturdays, the Fish Market quest pays 20 [Activity Tokens](/guides/weekly-activity-tokens). Fishing boats are on the [ships](/database/ships) page.
