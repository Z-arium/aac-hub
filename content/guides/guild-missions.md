Prestige is the guild currency. You earn it from guild missions and spend it in the Prestige Shop (Community, Guild, Manage, Prestige Shop). Each mission also gives your guild XP.

## Daily missions

| Mission | Prestige | Guild XP |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Colonizing Auroria | 10 | 20 |
| Defending Auroria | 10 | 40 |
| Gather Experia patches | 10 | 0 |
| Kill a world boss | 10 | 40 |

**Colonizing Auroria:** do one of the 5 quests in your faction's base in Diamond Shores. Craft an Archeum Bundle at the Auroria Archeum Bench and use it to repair the bridge or walls, or hand it to an NPC (A Grand Construction 1 to 3).

**Defending Auroria:** destroy an Alemine bunker, fortress, mana tower or idol. The easy way is to pick up a Harpoon Cannon from a cache near the Alemine Stragglers Bunkers and shoot one down.

**Gather Experia patches:** harvest 4 Sunflower, 3 Pumpkin, 2 Duck or 1 Pine Experia patch. Sunflower patches grow in 4 hours and need 25 Sunflower Seeds, 4 Memory Ink and 3 Scented Leaves.

**World boss:** kill any world boss.

## Repeatable

**Guild: Materials** gives 1 Prestige each time. Hand in 5 basic materials (fabric, leather, stone brick, iron ingot or lumber) plus 4 Blue Salt Hammers, and you get a new one.

## Ocleera Rift

The Ocleera Rift in west Diamond Shores also gives Prestige: 2 for each of the 3 mark quests and 4 for killing the Hateful Ocleera.
