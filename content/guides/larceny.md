Larceny is farming monsters for profit. Mobs drop **coin purses**, **crates** and random gear you can salvage for archeum. It needs no upfront investment, so it's a good first profession.

## Where to farm

There are four tiers. Monsters get harder in this order:

| Tier | Where |
| --- | --- |
| Noble | Mid-level main continent zones, like Sanddeep |
| Jester | Higher-level main continent zones, like Karkasse |
| Prince | Every Auroria zone |
| Queen | Around purified lodestones in Auroria |

Zones in **conflict or war** drop more loot.

## What crates give

Each crate rolls several groups. Each group has its own chance to trigger, then picks one item.

**Prince's Crate**

| Group (chance) | Item | Amount | Pick rate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 (100%) | Sunlight Archeum Dust | 5 to 6 | 40% |
| | Moonlight Archeum Dust | 5 to 6 | 40% |
| | Starlight Archeum Dust | 5 to 6 | 20% |
| 2 (80%) | Glowing Prism | 1 | 25% |
| | Mysterious Garden Powder | 1 | 25% |
| | Lunarite | 1 | 50% |

**Queen's Crate**

| Group (chance) | Item | Amount | Pick rate |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 (100%) | Regrade Brazier | 1 to 3 | 50% |
| | Radiant Archeum Tree Sapling | 1 to 3 | 50% |
| 2 (90%) | Glowing Prism | 1 | 25% |
| | Mysterious Garden Powder | 1 | 25% |
| | Lunarite | 1 | 50% |
| 3 (5%) | Clear Synthium Shard | 1 | 100% |

Spending labor on Larceny also counts toward the daily *Active Adventurers Appreciated* quest (5 Activity Tokens).
