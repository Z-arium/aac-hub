The box shows up in your inventory as soon as you hit **level 50**.

| Item | Amount | What it's for |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Divine Hellkissed Weapon Crate | 3 | Main hand, offhand, bow or a two-hander |
| Celestial Darukissed Flute | 1 | About 70 Stamina |
| Celestial Soulforged Armor Box | 1 each | Cloth, leather and plate |
| Honorable Victory Rank 5 | 6 | 6,000 Honor Points |
| 1,000 Vocation Badge Boost | 5 | 5,000 Vocation points |
| Warrior's Medal | 100 | Enough for a rank 7 Halcyona Necklace |
| Professional Tonic | 10 | More labor XP |
| 20% XP Increase Tonic | 30 | More XP from everything |
| Scroll: 30 Day Fish-Find Longliner | 1 | Fishing boat |
| Scroll: 30 Day Merchant Schooner | 1 | Sea trade ship, about 20 packs |
| Scroll: 30 Day Farm Freighter | 1 | Land trade vehicle, 9 packs |
| 30 Day Mirage Snowlion | 1 | Fast mount |
| T3 Toughness Gem [Mastery] | 10 | 100% slot rate, for pants |
| T3 Resilience Gem [Flexibility] | 10 | 100% slot rate, for your chest |

## Tips

- **Healers:** convert the mage cloth to healer cloth at a Soulforged Anvil. Look for "Conversion: Soulforged..." in your Folio.
- Put the Toughness gems in your pants and the Resilience gems in your chest piece. They make you much harder to kill in PvP.
