Gilda Stars buy ships, mounts and gear upgrades. If you do everything below you can earn around 70 a day. Use the [checklist](/tools/checklist) to keep track.

**Boss quests need no NPC.** Every "Defeat ...!" boss quest is a *Path of Glory* quest. Help kill the boss and it completes. Each one pays once a day.

**The raids pay the most** (about 30 Gilda a day). Their steps are in the [daily world events guide](/guides/daily-world-events):

## Delphinad Ghost Ship

A giant ghost ship in the **Arcadian Sea**. It spawns once a day and stays up for about 10 hours. Check [timers](/timers). You fight it at sea, so you need a ship with cannons or a seat on someone else's. Raids call it **DGS**.

1. **Get the quests first.** At the docks of a port town, find an **Archeologist** (Toro, Roycia, Burnar, Bruce, Lozinda, Karna, Carson, Hermas, Waji, Jeisson, Apitus, Lao Xiam, Bashudi and others).
2. Take **Ghosts from the Depths**. That unlocks **Ghost Ships of Delphinad** from the same Archeologist. Take it as well.
3. On **Saturdays**, also take the weekly [Have You Ever Seen A Ghost?](/guides/weekly-activity-tokens#have-you-ever-seen-a-ghost) from the Shadow Daru. The same kill pays 33 Activity Tokens.
4. **Find a ship raid.** Watch faction chat for `x dgs` and join. Most groups sail warships, and the ghost ship hits hard, so going alone doesn't work.
5. **Sail out** to the Delphinad Ghost Ship in the Arcadian Sea.
6. **Fight:** man a cannon or help repair and crew your ship. Keep firing until it sinks. If your ship goes down, swim to another raid ship.
7. When it sinks, **Ghost Ships of Delphinad** completes. Hand it in to an Archeologist.

**Rewards:** **3 Gilda Stars**. Its loot also drops Growling Yawl Design Fragments (55 make a small warship).

## Ghosts from the Depths

1. Take **Ghosts from the Depths** from an **Archeologist** at the docks of a port town.
2. Sail out on a combat ship. Join a ship group if you can, because the quest is hard alone.
3. Sink the **ghost ships roaming the Arcadian Sea** that the quest asks for.
4. Hand it in to an Archeologist.

**Rewards:** **1 Gilda Star**. Finishing it also opens Ghost Ships of Delphinad (above).

## Enemies of Sea Trade

1. Take **Enemies of Sea Trade** from **Martus, Suiharan, Bormas, Elle or Sorgo**, the naval quest NPCs at the docks.
2. Sail out and kill the **Seabugs** the quest marks on your map.
3. Find the targets it lists in **Ezna, Austera, Freedich Island, Growlgate Isle and Diamond Shores**.
4. Hand it in to any of the same NPCs.

**Rewards:** **1 Gilda Star**.

## Jola, Meina and Glenn

These three world bosses are called **JMG** in chat. Each pays 3 Gilda Stars a day.

1. Watch faction chat for `x jmg`, or for `x jola` / `x meina` / `x glenn`.
2. **Jola the Cursed:** go to **Exeloch**, where he is performing a ritual, and help kill him.
3. **Meina** and **Glenn:** go to **Sungold Fields** and help kill each one.
4. **Defeat Jola the Cursed!**, **Defeat Meina!** and **Defeat Glenn!** complete on their own.

**Rewards:** **3 Gilda Stars**, 2 Clear Synthium Shards and 7 Growthstones each. Jola also drops Jola's Scaly Frill and Meina drops Meina's Ring.

## Large world bosses

Each of these pays **3 Gilda Stars** a day through its own Path of Glory quest. The Red Dragon pays 5.

1. Pick a boss that is up soon:
   - **Captain Rangora**, Freedich Island, every 8 hours
   - **Dahuta's Champion Nazar**, Sunspeck Sea, every 4 hours
   - **Morpheus the Forsaken**, Growlgate Isle, every 8 hours
   - **Hanure the Hunter**, Hasla, every 14 hours
   - **Zaldrane the Enchantress**, Sanddeep
   - Scheduled ones are on [timers](/timers): **Red Dragon** (Karkasse Ridgelands), **Kraken** and **Hanure the Berserker** (Hasla)
2. Join the raid in faction chat (`x` plus the boss name).
3. Help with the kill. The quest completes on its own.

## Minor world bosses

These pay **1 Gilda Star** each, and most can be soloed at level 55. Each has its own Path of Glory quest, so do as many as you like.

1. Pick a route. Fast respawns first:
   - **Solzreed Peninsula:** Akanun the Tidelord (every hour), Gilneas, Castigant Reaper
   - **Arcum Iris:** Karon the Herdlord (every 30 minutes), Rainbow Ostrich
   - **Villanelle:** Reedpipe Leader Palaxis (every hour), Singing Greenman Shoon
   - **Elsewhere (every 4 hours):** Hellhook the Impaler (Lilyut Hills), Verith's Masterpiece (White Arden), Annulta the Yatamaton (Tigerspine Mountains), Werespider Arak and Jamerath the Shadowmage (Silent Forest), The General's Spirit (Perinoor Ruins)
   - **Level 55 Shadow Rift bosses (8 to 14 hours):** Daruda the Watcher (Cinderstone), The Croakfiend (Hellswamp), Tanis the Despoiler (Karkasse), Blightshade (Rookborne Basin), Goretusk the Leveler (Windscour Savannah), Yaksa Leader (Perinoor Ruins)
2. Fly or ride to the boss and kill it.
3. Each quest completes on its own.

## Workstation meals

You need a **Farmer's Workstation** on your land.

1. Gather the base ingredients for each meal: **15 Cultivated Ginseng, 50 Rice and 25 Garlic**.
2. Add the meat for each meal:
   - **Hearty Meal:** 5 Raw Chicken Meat
   - **Premium Hearty Meal:** 5 Raw Duck Meat
   - **Rare Hearty Meal:** 5 Raw Goose Meat
3. Go to your Workstation and take its daily cooking quests.
4. Cook each meal at the Workstation and hand it in.

**Rewards:** 1 Gilda Star per meal quest. The wiki counts **4 Gilda** a day from the Workstation.

## Farmhouse quests

Farmhouses (Thatched, Harvester's, Rancher's and Miner's Farmhouse) offer daily quests worth **2 or more Gilda Stars**.

1. Go to your Farmhouse.
2. Open its daily quests from the building.
3. Do what it asks, usually farming or raising tasks on your land, and turn it in at the Farmhouse.

_These steps haven't been confirmed in game yet. If something is different, let us know._

## Tradesman, Apothecary and Armorer quests

The **Tradesman's Manor**, **Apothecary's Manor** and **Armorer's Manor** each offer daily quests worth **2 or more Gilda Stars**.

1. Go to your Manor.
2. Open its daily quests from the building.
3. Craft or deliver what it asks for, then turn it in at the Manor.

_These steps haven't been confirmed in game yet. If something is different, let us know._

## Proficiency quests

Once you reach **50,000 proficiency** in **Mining, Gathering, Farming or Husbandry**, a daily quest for that skill opens. Each one is worth **1 Gilda Star**.

1. Raise the skill to 50,000 proficiency by spending labor in it.
2. Take the skill's daily proficiency quest.
3. Spend labor in that skill, for example mining veins or harvesting crops, until the quest is done.
4. Turn it in. With all four skills at 50,000, that's 4 Gilda Stars a day.

_These steps haven't been confirmed in game yet. If something is different, let us know._

## Blue Salt dailies

Five quests, **1 Gilda Star each**. Take them from the **Blue Salt Wanderer** and the **Animal Trainer** on your side of the world.

**East.** The NPCs are in Caernord (Ynystere), Lutesong Harbor (Villanelle) and Austera (Solis Headlands).

1. **Signs of the Salphira Cult:** collect 12 Hellhound leashes outside the **Palace Cellar** in Mahadevi.
2. **Raising a Wild Snowlion:** raise the Snowlion you are given by caring for it until it grows.
3. **Raising a Malfunctioning Leomorph:** raise it the same way.
4. **A Rare Perfume:** hand in 30 Chopped Produce, Dried Flowers, Ground Grain or Ground Spices.
5. **Guerilla Marketing:** unlocks after A Rare Perfume. Place vases around the Tigerspine Mountains.

**West.** The NPCs are in Seachild Wharf (Cinderstone), Ezna (Two Crowns) and Marianople.

1. **Roadsend in Danger:** collect 8 Faulty Gunpowder around the **Roadsend Guardpost**.
2. **Raising a Wild Horse:** raise the foal you are given until it grows.
3. **Raising a Wild Elk:** raise it the same way.
4. **A Rare Perfume:** the same as in the east.
5. **Guerilla Marketing:** the same as in the east.

Hand each one in where you took it.
