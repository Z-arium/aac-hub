Gilda Stars buy ships, mounts and gear upgrades. You can earn around 70 a day if you do everything below. Use the [checklist](/tools/checklist) to keep track.

## Raids (the big ones)

| Task | Gilda |
| --- | --- |
| [Grimgast Rift](/guides/daily-world-events) daily quest | 8 |
| Grimgast Nightmares (Blade and Bowstring) | 3 each |
| Mistmerrow: Kadum | 4 |
| [Crimson Rift](/guides/daily-world-events) daily quest | 3 |
| Crimson Rift Hound of Kyrios | 3 |
| Aegis Island: Final Sealbreaker | 4 |
| Whalesong: Jakar | 3 |
| Anthalon (after the Sungold Crimson Rift) | 3 |

## Boss kill quests

- **Large world bosses** (Rangora, Nazar, Morpheus, Hanure the Berserker, Red Dragon and more): 3 Gilda. Red Dragon gives 5.
- **Jola, Meina and Glenn** (The Prophecy): 3 each.
- **Delphinad Ghost Ship**: 3, plus 1 more for killing ghost ships 3 times.
- **Minor and medium world bosses**: 1 each. Most respawn every 4 to 8 hours.

## Ocean quests

- Kill Seabugs (Enemies of Sea Trade): 1
- Kill 5 Lusca: 1

## Building quests

- Cook 4 meals at a workstation: 4
- Farmhouse quests: 2 or more
- Tradesman, Apothecary or Armorer quests: 2 or more

## Other dailies

- **Blue Salt dailies: 5.** One Gilda each. East side: Signs of the Salphira Cult, Raising a Wild Snowlion, Raising a Malfunctioning Leomorph, A Rare Perfume, Guerilla Marketing. West side: Roadsend in Danger, Raising a Wild Horse, Raising a Wild Elk, A Rare Perfume, Guerilla Marketing.
- **Diamond Shores bridge and wall:** 2
- **Ahnimar, watering the Darus:** 1
- **Proficiency tasks** (mining, gathering, farming, husbandry): 1 each
