The [checklist](/tools/checklist) follows this order. Daily quests reset at **midnight UTC**.

## Log in and claim your login reward

1. Log in.
2. Open the **Daily Schedule** (bottom right of the screen).
3. Go to the **Login Tracker** tab.
4. Claim today's reward.

While the Daily Schedule is open, the **Daily Quests** and **Events** tabs show what's on today.

## Start of day

Get everything that runs in the background going first:

1. **Take the merit quest.** Start [Active Adventurers Appreciated](/guides/weekly-activity-tokens#active-adventurers-appreciated) so the labor you spend today counts toward it.
2. **Start your guild missions.** See [guild missions](/guides/guild-missions#start-your-guild-missions).
3. **Harvest and replant** your [Experia patches](/guides/guild-missions#gather-experia-patches).
4. **Do the quick building quests** at home: [Workstation meals](/guides/gilda-stars#workstation-meals), [Farmhouse](/guides/gilda-stars#farmhouse-quests) and [Manor](/guides/gilda-stars#tradesman-apothecary-and-armorer-quests) quests.
5. **Colonizing Auroria:** drop a bundle off in Diamond Shores. See [Colonizing Auroria](/guides/guild-missions#colonizing-auroria).
6. **Take today's pickups** you will finish later: the ocean quests from an Archeologist, and the [Blue Salt dailies](/guides/gilda-stars#blue-salt-dailies).

## Scheduled events

Check [timers](/timers) and plan around what starts next: [Mistmerrow](/guides/daily-world-events#mistmerrow), [Halcyona](/guides/daily-world-events#halcyona-war), [Luscas](/guides/daily-world-events#luscas), [Abyssal Attack](/guides/daily-world-events#abyssal-attack), the [Delphinad Ghost Ship](/guides/gilda-stars#delphinad-ghost-ship) and the big bosses.

## Rifts

Rifts run every 4 real hours on the in-game clock. When `x cr`, `x gr`, `x ws` or `x aegis` shows up in faction chat, drop what you're doing and join. See [Crimson Rift](/guides/daily-world-events#crimson-rift), [Grimgast Rift](/guides/daily-world-events#grimgast-rift), [Whalesong](/guides/daily-world-events#whalesong-siege) and [Aegis Island](/guides/daily-world-events#aegis-island).

## Between events

Fill the gaps with the [Library and dungeon dailies](/guides/ayanad-library-dailies), [minor world bosses](/guides/gilda-stars#minor-world-bosses), [Blue Salt](/guides/gilda-stars#blue-salt-dailies), the [Arena](/guides/daily-world-events#arena) and your weekly [Activity Token](/guides/weekly-activity-tokens) quests.
