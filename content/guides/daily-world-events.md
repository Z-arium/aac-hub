These events are your daily source of Honor, Leadership, Gilda Stars and Warrior Medals. Exact times are on the [timers](/timers) page.

**How to join a raid.** There is no group finder. When a raid forms, people type `x` and the event's short name in faction chat (for example `x cr` or `x ws`). Type the same and an addon invites you. The short names are CR (Crimson Rift), GR (Grimgast Rift), WS (Whalesong), Aegis, OC (Ocleera), Halcy (Halcyona) and MM (Mistmerrow).

**Boss kill quests start on their own.** Quests like "Defeat the Hound of Kyrios!" are *Path of Glory* quests. There is no NPC to visit: help kill the boss and the quest completes.

## Crimson Rift

**When:** every 4 real hours, at **12:00 in-game time**. To see the game clock, hover over the top right of your screen. **Level:** 30 and up.

1. Join a CR raid in faction chat (`x cr`).
2. Go to the rift zone and talk to the **Recruiter** to take **Crimson Omens 1**. Guards in the zone's Community Center hand out the rift quests too.
3. **Wave 1:** kill the Crimson Army Infantrymen and Archers. Report to the Recruiter, who gives you Crimson Omens 2.
4. **Wave 2:** kill the next wave, then report back for Crimson Omens 3.
5. **Wave 3:** kill the Crimson Army Commanders and Spearmen, then report to the Recruiter.
6. Stay for the Hounds (next section).

**Rewards:** 1,000 Honor and 10 Leadership.

## Hound of Kyrios

Two **Hounds of Kyrios** spawn at the end of every Crimson Rift.

1. Finish the three Crimson Rift waves above.
2. Stay with the raid at the end of the rift. The Hounds spawn there.
3. Hit a Hound until it dies. **Defeat the Hound of Kyrios!** completes when you have helped kill one. You don't need an NPC.

**Rewards:** **3 Gilda Stars**, 2 Clear Synthium Shards, 7 Adventurer's Growthstones and a Hound of Kyrios Soulstone. You can get this once a day.

## Anthalon

Anthalon is a world boss in **Sungold Fields** who spawns right after the Sungold Fields Crimson Rift.

1. Check [timers](/timers) or faction chat for the rift that lands in **Sungold Fields**.
2. Do that Crimson Rift and its Hounds as usual.
3. Stay put. Anthalon spawns when the rift ends.
4. Help kill him. **Defeat Anthalon!** completes on its own.

**Tips:** kill the mobs he summons quickly, because they stun. When he lifts you into the air (*Absorb Mind*), use **Shrug It Off** before you hit the ground. Stay out of 100m if you don't want to be pulled in.

**Rewards:** **3 Gilda Stars**, 2 Clear Synthium Shards and 7 Growthstones. On Sundays the same kill also counts for the weekly [Crimson Threat](/guides/weekly-activity-tokens#the-crimson-threat) token quest.

## Grimgast Rift

**When:** every 4 real hours, at **00:00 in-game time**. **Level:** 45 and up.

1. Join a GR raid in faction chat (`x gr`).
2. At the Community Center, take a materials quest from the **wooden board** and hand in 10 of one basic material: stone brick, lumber, leather, fabric or iron ingot.
3. That unlocks **Helping the War Effort** from **Quartermaster Fila**. Carry **2 packs** of Condensed Archeum from the camp to the **trebuchet**. The Archeum appears at the camp from midnight in-game time. This step gives 1 Gilda Star.
4. Once the trebuchet is built, grab a **harpoon** from the rack.
5. **Halting the Crimson Tide 1 and 2:** use the harpoon to beat two waves. Both quests complete on their own.
6. Stay for the Nightmares (next section).

**Rewards:** **8 Gilda Stars** in total, the most of any daily, plus 10 Leadership.

## Grimgast Nightmares

The **Nightmare Blade** and **Nightmare Bowstring** spawn after the second Grimgast wave, in Cinderstone Moor or Ynystere.

1. Finish the Grimgast Rift waves above and keep your **harpoon**.
2. The Nightmares carry a shield. Use the harpoon's second skill, **Time of Oblivion**, to strip it.
3. Help kill both. **Defeat the Nightmare Blade!** and **Defeat the Nightmare Bowstring!** complete on their own.

**Rewards:** **3 Gilda Stars** each, plus 2 Clear Synthium Shards and 7 Growthstones each.

## Whalesong Siege

**Where:** the Nuia Defense Base in **Whalesong Harbor**, Auroria. **Level:** 55.

1. Join a WS raid in faction chat (`x ws`).
2. Talk to **Raiko** at the base and take **Anthalon's Elite**. This also gives you Whalesong Siege 1 to 3.
3. **Waves 1 to 3:** kill Anthalon's undead troops as they attack the base. Each wave completes one Siege quest.
4. **Boss:** kill **Possessed Jakar** for **Kill Possessed Jakar**.
5. Turn in Anthalon's Elite to Raiko.

**Rewards:** **20 Leadership**. Jakar gives **4 Gilda Stars**, 10 Growthstones and 5 Cursed Armor Scraps. Anthalon's Elite gives 4 more Cursed Armor Scraps.

## Aegis Island

**Where:** **Aegis Island**, Auroria. **Level:** 55.

1. Join an Aegis raid in faction chat (`x aegis`).
2. Talk to **Scout Gaon** on the island and take **Aegis Island Guardian**. This also gives you Defend the Seal 1 to 3.
3. **Seals 1 to 3:** kill the ghouls attacking each seal, starting at the **Eastern Seal**. Each seal completes one Defend the Seal quest.
4. **Boss:** go to the **Northern Seal**, kill the **Final Sealbreaker**, then enter the **Ipnysh Gate** to finish the quest.
5. Turn in Aegis Island Guardian to Scout Gaon.

**Rewards:** **20 Leadership**. The Final Sealbreaker gives **4 Gilda Stars**, 10 Growthstones and 5 Acid Gobbets. Aegis Island Guardian gives 3 more Acid Gobbets.

## Ocleera Rift

**Where:** the **Riven Gates** on the west side of **Diamond Shores**. **Level:** 46 and up.

1. Talk to **Scout Karlsburg** and take **Sprouting Ocleera's Marks**. Kill the Sprouting Marks and their minions.
2. Take **Engorged Ocleera's Marks** from Scout Karlsburg and kill the Engorged Marks.
3. Take **Monstrous Ocleera's Mark** and kill the Monstrous Mark.
4. The **Hateful Ocleera** spawns. Help kill it for **Kill the Hateful Ocleera**. That quest starts when you are near the boss.

**Rewards:** 10 Leadership, Revenant Soulstones (5, 10 and 20), a Souleye and 3 Clear Synthium Shards. Every step also gives **Prestige** and guild XP (see [guild missions](/guides/guild-missions)).

## Comet Rift

**When:** when a comet lands. A popup in game names the zone.

1. Go to the comet and talk to **Iseren**.
2. The first time, Iseren sends you to **Elloy** on the **1st floor of the Ayanad Library**. When you come back, use the **telescope** and talk to Iseren until it is centred. Then use its **Observe** option.
3. Take the rift quests from Iseren:
   - Rift 1: kill 25 Fire Sprites.
   - Rift 2: kill 15 Flame Elementals.
   - Rift 3: kill the Flame Guardian boss.
4. Do the side quests at the same time: **Scorched Earth** (kill 10 rift elementals), **Meteor Miner** (mine a meteor) and **Crystalized Clues** (collect elemental shards from Flame Elementals).
5. Finish with **The Eternal Flame**: kill the **Undying Flame**.
6. Report back to Iseren in the Ayanad Library.

**Rewards:** **Comet Shards**. Spend them at the **Comet Workbench** in the top right of Diamond Shores on battle pets, the "Starfallen" title or the Cumulus Magitopher glider.

## Mistmerrow

Nuia, Haranya and Pirates fight over towers in each basin. On Classic the battle lasts 20 minutes. **When:** see [timers](/timers).

1. When it starts, go to the Mistmerrow **portal** in your capital: Marianople for Nuia, Austera for Haranya.
2. Take the **2 quests** from the NPC in front of the portal, then go through.
3. Talk to the NPC inside. That completes one quest and gives you a new one to destroy the towers.
4. Help your faction destroy towers until the battle ends.
5. Stay for the gates and Kadum (next section).

**Rewards:** Honor, Warrior Medals, Clear Synthium Shards and 10 Leadership.

## Mistmerrow gates and Kadum

1. After the Mistmerrow battle ends, the zone turns peaceful. Stay inside.
2. Take the **2 new quests** from the NPCs in the middle of the map.
3. Kill the 3 gates in the **SE, W and NE basins**.
4. Go back to the middle and help kill **Kadum**.
5. Turn in both quests at the NPCs in the middle.

**Rewards:** Kadum is worth **4 Gilda Stars**.

## Halcyona War

**When:** see [timers](/timers).

1. Take the first quest in your **capital**.
2. Go through the portal to **Halcyona**, or use your own portal.
3. Turn it in at your faction camp and take the second quest: **kill 10 enemies**. Kills are shared in a party or raid.
4. Help destroy the enemy base. If your side breaks the **base crystal**, loot it for a Warrior Medal Chest.
5. If you win, go back to the **Community Center** for a 2-hour buff.

**Rewards:** Honor, Clear Synthium Shards, Warrior Medals and 10 Leadership. The crystal chest gives 5 to 10 more Warrior Medals.

## Luscas

Luscas are mini-Krakens. Each day they appear at one of three spots in the **Sea of Graves**, between Auroria and Freedich Island. **When:** see [timers](/timers). **You need:** a ship, or a seat on someone else's.

1. Take **Lusca Awakening** from the **Seamist Researcher** at **Ezi's Light** in Diamond Shores. The portal in each big city also gets you close.
2. Sail out and help kill the Luscas.
3. Kill **5 Luscas** to finish the extra kill quest too.

**Rewards:** **1 Gilda Star** and 10 Shatigon's Sand from Lusca Awakening, plus Honor and 10 Leadership. The 5-kill quest gives another Gilda Star.

## Abyssal Attack

**When:** several times a week (see [timers](/timers)). **You need:** a ship, or a seat on someone else's.

1. Take **Becoming a Seaknight** and **Stopping Doomsday** from the **Abyssal Attack Recruiter**.
2. Sail to the **Sea of Graves**, between Heedmar and Freedich Island.
3. Kill all the Luscas and the **Abyssal Kraken**.
4. When a server message says Ezi's Light can be restored, go to the foot of the monument and use the **beacon** on the pedestal. It is a **3-minute channel**.
5. Protect whoever is channeling. Enemies can stop the channel by killing them, or by breaking the **lodestones** north, east and west of the monument with siege damage. If a lodestone breaks, the beacon explodes for 20,000 damage.
6. To finish Becoming a Seaknight, either restore Ezi's Light and get the Victory Flag buff, or destroy an enemy's Ocean's Guardstone.

**Rewards:** 2 Warrior Medals and 5 Shatigon's Sand from Stopping Doomsday, plus 10 Leadership.

## Arena

**Blood, Sweat, and Training** is a daily quest for one arena match. You don't have to win.

1. Talk to a **Gladiator Arena Manager** in Marianople, Austera, Seachild Wharf, Caernord or Growlgate Isle, and take **Blood, Sweat, and Training**.
2. Open the **Convenience menu** (bottom right) and queue for the **Arena**. Any mode counts.
3. Fight one opponent.
4. Go back to any Gladiator Arena Manager and turn it in.

**Rewards:** Honor.
