These events are your daily source of Honor, Leadership, Gilda Stars and Warrior Medals. Exact times are on the [timers](/timers) page.

## Crimson Rift (PvE)

Every 4 real hours, at **12:00 in-game time**. Hover over the top-right of your screen in game to see the game clock. Join a raid through faction chat. You fight three waves of monsters, then two Hound bosses.

- Quest from a guard in the Community Center: **1,000 Honor** and **10 Leadership**.
- Each Hound kill: **3 Gilda Stars**, **2 Clear Synthium Shards** and **7 Adventurer's Growthstones**.
- The daily raid quest gives 3 Gilda Stars.
- In Sungold Fields, [Anthalon](/database/world-bosses) spawns after the rift.

## Grimgast Rift (PvE)

Every 4 real hours, at **00:00 in-game time**.

1. From the Community Center board: deliver 10 materials (stone brick, lumber, leather, fabric, iron ingot).
2. Deliver 2 packs to the trebuchet.
3. Use the harpoons to beat two waves. Two Hounds then spawn. Use the harpoon's *Time of Oblivion* skill to strip their debuff.

The daily raid quest gives **8 Gilda Stars**, the most of any daily. The two Hounds (Nightmare Blade and Nightmare Bowstring) give **3 Gilda Stars** each.

## Whalesong Siege (PvE)

Raids form in faction chat as "x ws". Clear 3 waves at Whalesong Harbor, then kill **Jakar**.

- **20 Leadership** from the quests.
- Jakar is worth **3 Gilda Stars**.

## Aegis Island (PvE)

Defend the seal on Aegis Island ("x aegis" in faction chat). Clear 3 waves, then kill the **Final Sealbreaker**.

- **20 Leadership** from the quests.
- The Final Sealbreaker is worth **4 Gilda Stars**.

## Ocleera Rift (PvE)

On the west side of Diamond Shores ("x oc"). Take 3 mark quests from the quest giver, then kill the Hateful Ocleera.

- **10 Leadership** for the Hateful Ocleera.
- Also a source of **Prestige** and guild XP. See [guild missions](/guides/guild-missions).

## Comet Rift (Starfall)

A comet lands somewhere in the world and a popup tells you where. Talk to **Iseren** at the comet to start, then do the 3 rift stages and kill the **Undying Flame**. You earn **Comet Shards** to spend at the Comet Workbench in the top right of Diamond Shores (battle pets, a title and the Cumulus Magitopher glider).

## Mistmerrow (PvP)

Nuia, Haranya and Pirates fight over towers. Portals are in the capitals (Marianople and Austera). On Classic it lasts 20 minutes and has an extra kill quest.

- Entry quests: Honor, Warrior Medals, Clear Synthium Shards and Leadership.
- Afterwards, kill the 3 gates (SE, W and NE basins) and Kadum in the middle. Kadum is worth 4 Gilda Stars.

## Halcyona War (PvP)

Take the first quest in your capital, then enter Halcyona.

- The second quest asks for 10 enemy kills, which you can share in a party or raid.
- Looting an enemy base crystal gives a Warrior Medal Chest with **5 to 10 Warrior Medals**.
- After a win, return to the Community Center for a 2-hour buff.

## Luscas (PvP at sea)

A naval fight between Auroria and Freedich Island. The quest NPC is at Ezi's Light in Diamond Shores. Rewards: a good amount of Honor, 1 Gilda Star, Shatigon's Sand and Leadership. Killing five Lusca also gives 1 Gilda Star.

## Abyssal Attack (PvP at sea)

Runs several times a week (see [timers](/timers)). Luscas and the Abyssal Kraken spawn in the Sea of Graves, between Heedmar and Freedich Island. Kill them all, then channel the beacon at Ezi's Light for 3 minutes. Enemies can stop you by killing the channeler or breaking the lodestones with siege damage.

- Quests: **Becoming a Seaknight** and **Stopping Doomsday**.
- **10 Leadership**.
