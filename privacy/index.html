Privacy

Short version: we store only what you save, and you can download or delete it any time.

Without an account

Everything you set on the site (checklist progress, timer alerts, pinned tools, saved pages and builds) stays in your browser's local storage. It never leaves your device. Clearing your browser data removes it.

With an account

When you sign in, we store:

  • Your email address, or your Discord name and id if you sign in with Discord.
  • Your profile: display name, server and main character, if you fill them in.
  • The same settings listed above, so they follow you to other devices.

Accounts are run on Supabase, which hosts the database and handles sign-in. We don't sell or share your data, show ads, or use tracking cookies. The only cookies or storage the site uses are the ones that keep you signed in and remember your settings.

Your controls

On My account you can download everything we hold about you as a file, or delete your account. Deleting removes your profile and saved data straight away.

Videos

Videos are embedded from YouTube, which has its own privacy policy and may set cookies when you play one.

Contact

Questions or requests: open an issue on the site's GitHub page.
