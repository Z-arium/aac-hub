General

When was the server launched?

August 2023.


If i start playing now will i be a lot behind of others regarding gear?

For short time yes. There is catch-up box the new player receives after reaching level 50.The gearset will bring the player to around 4k gearscore.


Is there land available if i start playing?

Straight free plots no. There is demolitions happening time to time around the maps. Collecting enough gold to buy a land plot is another good way of getting one. There are also custom made new player housing zones that are worth to check. They have certain conditions a player must meet in order to own land there. Darus add new player zones time to time in the game.


Is Archeage Classic p2win?

No. In the Marketplace there are no items that can help player to reach higher gearscore. There is plenty of outfits, glider skins, pets and vehicles sold with credits(real money) but players cant buy anything to speed up their progress.
