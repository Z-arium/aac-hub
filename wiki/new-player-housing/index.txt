10:T924,<p>On ArcheAge Classic, you'll be able to find housing zones dedicated to new players. They impose the following requirements:
</p>
<ul><li>You must not own ANY other land, account-wide</li>
<li>Only 8x, 16x and 24x farms are allowed</li>
<li>You cannot prepay or put the land up for sale</li>
<li>If you obtain a plot in another area, you will no longer be able to pay taxes on your New Player plot. This means it will be forced to expire.</li></ul>
