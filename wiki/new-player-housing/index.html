New Player Housing

On ArcheAge Classic, you'll be able to find housing zones dedicated to new players. They impose the following requirements:

  • You must not own ANY other land, account-wide
  • Only 8x, 16x and 24x farms are allowed
  • You cannot prepay or put the land up for sale
  • If you obtain a plot in another area, you will no longer be able to pay taxes on your New Player plot. This means it will be forced to expire.

Swirling Meadow

Swirling Meadow is the largest New Player Housing zone added in patch 1.3.5h3. This was the first ever custom zone added to ArcheAge classic.
