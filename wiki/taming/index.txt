12:T1c747,<p>Taming is a custom proficiency that was added in <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/patch-1-3-6">Patch 1.3.6</a>. Taming allows you to tame various beasts throughout the world of Erenor and use them as mounts. Increasing your Taming proficiency increases your effectiveness at taming beasts. 
</p>

<h1 id="Getting_Started">Getting Started</h1>
<p>To begin Taming you are going to need to craft yourself the lowest tiered taming tool, the Greenhorn's Backpack.

<p>Once you have crafted your backpack it is time to start searching for a group of beasts that all have the <span><img src="/aac-hub/wiki-files/d3/d3843b7786870e0a1b6cdeca8c4bdfaf9dc0032a.png" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy"></span> Beast of the Wild buff. This buff gives them a small chance to spawn a tameable version of themselves after dying. 
</p><p>We have compiled a list of locations for all the tameables we were able to find down <a href="#Tameable_Beasts">below</a>. 
</p><p>Taming is much like sport fishing, the beast will get a buff and you have to match that buff icon with one of the skills that your backpack provides when equipped. 
</p><p><b>Taming skills:</b><br>
</p>

<h1 id="Whistle_of_the_Wild">Whistle of the Wild</h1>
<h2 id="How_to_obtain">How to obtain</h2>
<p>In order to obtain the <span><img src="/aac-hub/wiki-files/ce/ce27fdd431ccf2862f23ae4ac2175de808ab00d4.png" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy"></span> <span style="color: rgb(85, 143, 215)">Whistle of the Wild</span> you need to obtain a <span style="color: rgb(148, 146, 146)">Mundane</span>, <span style="color: rgb(119, 176, 100)">Grand</span>, <span style="color: rgb(203, 114, 216)">Arcane</span> or <span style="color: rgb(249, 82, 82)">Celestial</span> tamed beast and use a <span><img src="/aac-hub/wiki-files/d2/d25e5b1ab27010a536fbb6d5d223177573489c11.png" width="32" height="32" loading="lazy"></span> <span style="color: rgb(186, 151, 109)">Wildcaller Flute</span> to release the beast back to the wild and obtain an amount of Whistles depending on the Grade of the tamed beast. <br>
<b>Warning! This removes the tamed beast in the process.</b>

16:T12889,Taming is a custom proficiency that was added in [[Patch 1.3.6]]. Taming allows you to tame various beasts throughout the world of Erenor and use them as mounts. Increasing your Taming proficiency increases your effectiveness at taming beasts. 
= Getting Started =
To begin Taming you are going to need to craft yourself the lowest tiered taming tool, the Greenhorn's Backpack.

Once you have crafted your backpack it is time to start searching for a group of beasts that all have the [[File:Beast of the Wild.png|32px]] Beast of the Wild buff. This buff gives them a small chance to spawn a tameable version of themselves after dying. 

We have compiled a list of locations for all the tameables we were able to find down [[#Tameable_Beasts|below]]. 

Taming is much like sport fishing, the beast will get a buff and you have to match that buff icon with one of the skills that your backpack provides when equipped. 

'''Taming skills:'''<br>
* [[File:Wrestle the Wild.png|32px]] '''Wrestle the Wild''' - "Begin your attempt at taming a Wild beast."<br>
* [[File:Soothe.png|32px]] '''Soothe''' - "Calm the wild beast with words of comfort." <br>NOTE: This skill is spammable and is your main way to get taming progress similar to the potency of [[File:Big Reel In.png|32px]] Big Reel In from fishing. <br>
* [[File:Feed.png|32px]] '''Feed''' - "Temper the beast's anger by feeding it."<br>NOTE: This skill uses 1×[[File:Vita Root.png|32px]] Vita Root so be sure to bring many!<br>
* [[File:Dance!.png|32px]] '''Dance!''' - "Entertain the wild beast with a lively dance."<br>
* [[File:Show of Strength.png|32px]] '''Show of Strength''' - "Assert dominance over the beast! Dispels intimidated."<br>NOTE: The tameable beast will every now and then cast intimidation on you and you have to dispel it using this skill.<br>
* [[File:Wrangle.png|32px]] '''Wrangle''' - "Rein in the wild beast before it gets out of hand."
= Whistle of the Wild =
== How to obtain ==
In order to obtain the [[File:Whistle of the Wild.png|32px]] <span style="color: rgb(85, 143, 215);">Whistle of the Wild</span> you need to obtain a <span style="color: rgb(148, 146, 146);">Mundane</span>, <span style="color: rgb(119, 176, 100);">Grand</span>, <span style="color: rgb(203, 114, 216);">Arcane</span> or <span style="color: rgb(249, 82, 82);">Celestial</span> tamed beast and use a [[File:Wildcaller Flute.png|32px]] <span style="color: rgb(186, 151, 109);">Wildcaller Flute</span> to release the beast back to the wild and obtain an amount of Whistles depending on the Grade of the tamed beast. <br>

== Uses ==
[[File:Whistle of the Wild.png|32px]] <span style="color: rgb(85, 143, 215);">Whistle of the Wild</span> can be used for a plethora of custom Taming-related recipes. There are 2 backpack upgrades, 5 new mount consumables and 4 new mount armor sets with 3 tiers per set.
