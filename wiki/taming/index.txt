12:T2b95e,<p>Taming is a custom proficiency that was added in <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/patch-1-3-6">Patch 1.3.6</a>. Taming allows you to tame various beasts throughout the world of Erenor and use them as mounts. Increasing your Taming proficiency increases your effectiveness at taming beasts. 
</p>

<h1 id="Getting_Started">Getting Started</h1>
<p>To begin Taming you are going to need to craft yourself the lowest tiered taming tool, the Greenhorn's Backpack.

<p>Once you have crafted your backpack it is time to start searching for a group of beasts that all have the <span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/0/00/Beast_of_the_Wild.png/32px-Beast_of_the_Wild.png" width="32" height="32" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/0/00/Beast_of_the_Wild.png 1.5x" loading="lazy"></span> Beast of the Wild buff. This buff gives them a small chance to spawn a tameable version of themselves after dying. 
</p><p>We have compiled a list of locations for all the tameables we were able to find down <a href="#Tameable_Beasts">below</a>. 
</p><p>Taming is much like sport fishing, the beast will get a buff and you have to match that buff icon with one of the skills that your backpack provides when equipped.

<h1 id="Whistle_of_the_Wild">Whistle of the Wild</h1>
<h2 id="How_to_obtain">How to obtain</h2>
<p>In order to obtain the <span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/9/98/Whistle_of_the_Wild.png/32px-Whistle_of_the_Wild.png" width="32" height="32" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/9/98/Whistle_of_the_Wild.png/48px-Whistle_of_the_Wild.png 1.5x, https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/9/98/Whistle_of_the_Wild.png 2x" loading="lazy"></span> <span style="color: rgb(85, 143, 215)">Whistle of the Wild</span> you need to obtain a <span style="color: rgb(148, 146, 146)">Mundane</span>, <span style="color: rgb(119, 176, 100)">Grand</span>, <span style="color: rgb(203, 114, 216)">Arcane</span> or <span style="color: rgb(249, 82, 82)">Celestial</span> tamed beast and use a <span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/b/b0/Wildcaller_Flute.png/32px-Wildcaller_Flute.png" width="32" height="32" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/b/b0/Wildcaller_Flute.png/48px-Wildcaller_Flute.png 1.5x, https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/b/b0/Wildcaller_Flute.png 2x" loading="lazy"></span> <span style="color: rgb(186, 151, 109)">Wildcaller Flute</span> to release the beast back to the wild and obtain an amount of Whistles depending on the Grade of the tamed beast. <br>
<b>Warning! This removes the tamed beast in the process.</b>

<h2 id="Uses">Uses</h2>
