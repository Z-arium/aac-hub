</p>
<h3 id="Items_needed_for_crafting">Items needed for crafting</h3>
<p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/f/f7/Aquestria.png" width="327" height="281" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p>Arcadian Seafoam 20
</p><p>Abyssal Shard 100
</p><p>Prime Lunarite 5
</p><p>Eternal Winter Chill 20
</p><p>Swirling Whirlpool 50
</p><p>Seaweed Reins can be acquired from the questline mentioned below.
</p>
<h3 id="Quests">Quests</h3>
<p>First travel to the Freedich and meet up with the npc called Angeli. She will give a quest to kill Dahuta<b>'</b>s Defenders and Seabugs. 
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/7/7c/Angeli.png/250px-Angeli.png" width="250" height="400" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/7/7c/Angeli.png/375px-Angeli.png 1.5x, https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/7/7c/Angeli.png 2x" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p><p><b>[Trickster's Loyalty]</b>
</p><p>- Kill Dahuta<b>'</b>s Defenders 0/100
</p><p>- Kill Seabugs 0/100
</p><p>After the quest is done go back to Freedich to Angeli to receive Seaweed Reins.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/c/c5/Seawedreins.png" width="299" height="405" loading="lazy"></span>
