Aquestria questline

Aquestria is one of the best and fastest water mount in the game. It is essential to get it for the Raid v Raid content in order to follow raid lead swiftly.

Items needed for crafting

Arcadian Seafoam 20

Abyssal Shard 100

Prime Lunarite 5

Eternal Winter Chill 20

Swirling Whirlpool 50

Seaweed Reins can be acquired from the questline mentioned below.

Quests

First travel to the Freedich and meet up with the npc called Angeli. She will give a quest to kill Dahuta's Defenders and Seabugs.


[Trickster's Loyalty]

- Kill Dahuta's Defenders 0/100

- Kill Seabugs 0/100

After the quest is done go back to Freedich to Angeli to receive Seaweed Reins.


[Dahutas Steed]

Quest can be found on the entrance of Sea of Drowned Love.

- Kill Nazar

- Craft and summon the Aquestria (Nazar can be killed first before crafting the mount!)
