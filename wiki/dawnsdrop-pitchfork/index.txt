12:T4fd2,<p>The Dawnsdrop Pitchfork is an addition to the <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/dawnsdrop-tools">Dawnsdrop Tools</a> custom content added in <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Patch_1.3.4&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">Patch 1.3.4</a>. The Dawnsdrop Pitchfork is a <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Farming&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">Farming</a> and <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Gathering&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">Gathering</a> tool that allows players to harvest crops and bundles into crates, which can later be opened for their materials. Higher-grade pitchforks provide increased yields when the crates are opened.
</p>

<h1 id="Function">Function</h1>
<p>The Dawnsdrop Pitchfork harvests a fully grown seed or bundle and converts it directly into a corresponding crate. Using the pitchfork does not consume Labor.
</p><p>Labor is instead consumed when opening the resulting crate. This allows players to harvest their crops without immediately spending Labor and save the crates for later.
</p><p>Higher-grade pitchforks also increase the amount of materials obtained when the crates are opened, making pitchforking more rewarding than conventional harvesting.
</p><p>There is however one downside to "Pitchforking" as it is known by, that is that you manually have to replant your farms unlike when you use the regular automated replanting option. 
</p><p>This means you now have two distinct ways of managing Farming and Gathering:
</p>
<ul><li>Active harvesting: Use the Pitchfork to harvest crops and bundles into crates. This provides increased yields but requires manually replanting crops.</li>
<li>Passive/automated harvesting: Continue using the normal harvesting and automated replanting system. This requires less manual interaction but does not benefit from the Pitchfork's increased yields.</li></ul>

<h1 id="Starting_the_Questline">Starting the Questline</h1>
<p>Requirements:
</p>
<ul><li>230,000 Farming Proficiency</li>
<li>230,000 Gathering Proficiency</li></ul>
<p>You do not need 230,000 proficiency in both.
</p><p>Bring Dawnsdrop Wrists and Pants. 
</p>
<ol><li>Go to Sun's End (Halcyona Community Center).</li>
<li>Water crops and flowers with your Dawnsdrops equipped for the Dawnsdrop Buffs.</li>
<li>Get the quest from Neru in the Community Center.</li></ol>
<p><i>Tip: It is recommended to also start the <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/dawnsdrop-watering-can">Dawnsdrop Watering Can</a> questline and progress these simultaneously as they often share objectives.</i>

16:T3d31,The Dawnsdrop Pitchfork is an addition to the [[Dawnsdrop Tools]] custom content added in [[Patch 1.3.4]]. The Dawnsdrop Pitchfork is a [[Farming]] and [[Gathering]] tool that allows players to harvest crops and bundles into crates, which can later be opened for their materials. Higher-grade pitchforks provide increased yields when the crates are opened.
= Function =
The Dawnsdrop Pitchfork harvests a fully grown seed or bundle and converts it directly into a corresponding crate. Using the pitchfork does not consume Labor.

Labor is instead consumed when opening the resulting crate. This allows players to harvest their crops without immediately spending Labor and save the crates for later.

Higher-grade pitchforks also increase the amount of materials obtained when the crates are opened, making pitchforking more rewarding than conventional harvesting.

There is however one downside to "Pitchforking" as it is known by, that is that you manually have to replant your farms unlike when you use the regular automated replanting option. 

This means you now have two distinct ways of managing Farming and Gathering:
* Active harvesting: Use the Pitchfork to harvest crops and bundles into crates. This provides increased yields but requires manually replanting crops.
* Passive/automated harvesting: Continue using the normal harvesting and automated replanting system. This requires less manual interaction but does not benefit from the Pitchfork's increased yields.
