== Acquiring a Bloomfang Soul Fragment ==
Travel to '''Ahnimar Community Center''' to talk to Daru NPC called '''Kilun''' the Alchemy Merchant. 

[[File:Ahnimar map.jpg|690x690px]]                [[File:Kilun.png|frameless|404x404px]]


Kilun will give a quest where player has to travel to '''Villanelle, Jaun''’''s Ranch'''. At the ranch buy a cat pet. '''Cat must be in this condition when completing the quest, it musn’t be raised.''' Get also one Vita seed either from the Butcher npc next to the cat stand or any stablehand/merchant in game. After giving Kilu the cat he will ask the player to grow the Vita Seed. 
[[File:Cat_stand.png|none|Cat stand. |thumb|533x533px]][[File:Villanelle map.jpg|541x541px]]

After this travel back to '''Ahnimar Community Center''' to talk to '''Kilun'''. He will ask player to bring him some milk from '''Mahadevi Elephant'''. Travel to '''Sanddeep, Grenwolf'''. Item given by '''Kilu''' must be used on the elephant to collect the milk. '''''Don’t kill the elephants!''''' Elephant locations marked in the picture below.

[[File:Mahadevi elephant.png]]                     [[File:Sanddeep map.jpg|663x663px]]


After the milking travel back to '''Kilun''' in '''Ahnimar Community Center'''. He will ask to bring him the '''Cat Toy.''' For this players needs '''10x Old Planks''' and '''3x Goose Down'''. 

Goose Down can easily be bought from Auction house or by growing and harvesting geese. Old Planks can be acquire in two places marked in pictures below. 
[[File:Airain map.jpg|none|Old fence is located upstairs behind the elite mob. After braking and collecting Old Planks the fence will respawn after short time. |thumb|651x651px]]
[[File:Perinoor map.jpg|none|frame|After braking and collecting Old Planks the fence will respawn after short time. ]]
[[File:Fragment.png|right]]
After gathering both materials player must craft '''Cat Toy'''. This can be done examp. in both main cities '''Marianople''' and '''Austera''' at '''Pet Accessory Workbench'''. 
[[File:Pet accessory workbench.png|none|frame|Pet Accessory Workbench]]After crafting Cat Toy travel back to '''Ahnimar Community Center''' and deliver the '''Cat Toy'''. After delivering '''Kilun''' will give the '''Fragment'''. The '''Fragment''' will be permanent and the questline needs to be done only once.  

== Powerstone Effects ==
[[File:Powerstone.png]]

To make the Fragment and Powerstone into a pet that player can use to gain effect from. Powerstones are sold in Mirage Island with [[Currencies|Activity Tokens]].  

Using the powerstone on your Fragment grants you a Powerstone Pet. There is three different Powerstones and their differences are explained below.

==== Bloomfang (Kittenish) ====
Kittenish powerstone costs 60 [[Currencies|Activity Tokens]]. 

Kittenish pet will give its owner 1500 extra HP and possess the "'''Truly Cathletic'''" skill, decreasing owner's Received Damage -30% for 3 seconds.

==== Bloomfang (Spirit) ====
Spirit powerstone costs 60 [[Currencies|Activity Tokens]].

Spirit pet will give its owner 35 extra Attack Speed and possess the "'''Specter Spectacular'''" skill, increasing owner's Attack and Healing Power +10% for 9 seconds.

==== Bloomfang (Vanity) ====
Vanity powerstone costs 10 [[Currencies|Activity Tokens]].
