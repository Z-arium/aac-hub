<h2 id="Acquiring_a_Bloomfang_Soul_Fragment">Acquiring a Bloomfang Soul Fragment</h2>
<p>Travel to <b>Ahnimar Community Center</b> to talk to Daru NPC called <b>Kilun</b> the Alchemy Merchant. 
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/0/06/Ahnimar_map.jpg/690px-Ahnimar_map.jpg" width="690" height="435" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/0/06/Ahnimar_map.jpg 1.5x" loading="lazy"></span>                <span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/a/a0/Kilun.png/215px-Kilun.png" width="215" height="404" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/a/a0/Kilun.png/322px-Kilun.png 1.5x, https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/a/a0/Kilun.png 2x" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
Kilun will give a quest where player has to travel to <b>Villanelle, Jaun<i>’</i>s Ranch</b>. At the ranch buy a cat pet. <b>Cat must be in this condition when completing the quest, it musn’t be raised.</b> Get also one Vita seed either from the Butcher npc next to the cat stand or any stablehand/merchant in game. After giving Kilu the cat he will ask the player to grow the Vita Seed. 
</p>
<figure><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/9/99/Cat_stand.png/533px-Cat_stand.png" width="533" height="294" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/9/99/Cat_stand.png 1.5x" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Cat stand. </figcaption></figure><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/5/58/Villanelle_map.jpg/541px-Villanelle_map.jpg" width="541" height="344" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/5/58/Villanelle_map.jpg/812px-Villanelle_map.jpg 1.5x, https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/5/58/Villanelle_map.jpg 2x" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p>After this travel back to <b>Ahnimar Community Center</b> to talk to <b>Kilun</b>. He will ask player to bring him some milk from <b>Mahadevi Elephant</b>. Travel to <b>Sanddeep, Grenwolf</b>. Item given by <b>Kilu</b> must be used on the elephant to collect the milk. <i><b>Don’t kill the elephants!</b></i> Elephant locations marked in the picture below.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/e/e9/Mahadevi_elephant.png" width="429" height="405" loading="lazy"></span>                     <span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/5/54/Sanddeep_map.jpg/663px-Sanddeep_map.jpg" width="663" height="418" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/5/54/Sanddeep_map.jpg 1.5x" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
After the milking travel back to <b>Kilun</b> in <b>Ahnimar Community Center</b>. He will ask to bring him the <b>Cat Toy.</b> For this players needs <b>10x Old Planks</b> and <b>3x Goose Down</b>. 
</p><p>Goose Down can easily be bought from Auction house or by growing and harvesting geese. Old Planks can be acquire in two places marked in pictures below. 
</p>
<figure><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/f/f2/Airain_map.jpg/651px-Airain_map.jpg" width="651" height="413" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/f/f2/Airain_map.jpg 1.5x" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Old fence is located upstairs behind the elite mob. After braking and collecting Old Planks the fence will respawn after short time. </figcaption></figure>
<figure><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/9/92/Perinoor_map.jpg" width="883" height="553" loading="lazy"><figcaption>After braking and collecting Old Planks the fence will respawn after short time. </figcaption></figure>
<figure><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/a/af/Fragment.png" width="214" height="451" loading="lazy"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
<p>After gathering both materials player must craft <b>Cat Toy</b>. This can be done examp. in both main cities <b>Marianople</b> and <b>Austera</b> at <b>Pet Accessory Workbench</b>. 
</p>
<figure><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/5/50/Pet_accessory_workbench.png" width="298" height="312" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Pet Accessory Workbench</figcaption></figure><p>After crafting Cat Toy travel back to <b>Ahnimar Community Center</b> and deliver the <b>Cat Toy</b>. After delivering <b>Kilun</b> will give the <b>Fragment</b>. The <b>Fragment</b> will be permanent and the questline needs to be done only once.  
</p><h2 id="Powerstone_Effects">Powerstone Effects</h2>
<p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/1/14/Powerstone.png" width="38" height="37" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p>To make the Fragment and Powerstone into a pet that player can use to gain effect from. Powerstones are sold in Mirage Island with <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/currencies">Activity Tokens</a>.  
</p><p>Using the powerstone on your Fragment grants you a Powerstone Pet. There is three different Powerstones and their differences are explained below.
</p>
<h4 id="Bloomfang_(Kittenish)"><span></span>Bloomfang (Kittenish)</h4>
<p>Kittenish powerstone costs 60 <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/currencies">Activity Tokens</a>. 
</p><p>Kittenish pet will give its owner 1500 extra HP and possess the "<b>Truly Cathletic</b>" skill, decreasing owner's Received Damage -30% for 3 seconds.
</p>
<h4 id="Bloomfang_(Spirit)"><span></span>Bloomfang (Spirit)</h4>
<p>Spirit powerstone costs 60 <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/currencies">Activity Tokens</a>.
</p><p>Spirit pet will give its owner 35 extra Attack Speed and possess the "<b>Specter Spectacular</b>" skill, increasing owner's Attack and Healing Power +10% for 9 seconds.
</p>
<h4 id="Bloomfang_(Vanity)"><span></span>Bloomfang (Vanity)</h4>
<p>Vanity powerstone costs 10 <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/currencies">Activity Tokens</a>.
</p><p>Vanity powerstone doesn’t give any effects, it is just for looks.
