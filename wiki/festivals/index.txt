Festivals are events in the game running on for certain time each. Usually festivals are located in a certain map/maps. The specific map/maps are made into safe zones, the usual conflict and war cycles don't apply while festival is ongoing. Players can travel easily to the festival map by the portal appearing in the hero halls of respective nations. In Archeage Classic the [[Currencies|Daru Festival Coin]] has replaced all the other possible festival coins for the players to be able to save space in inventories and warehouses. 1 old festival coin equals to  {{Item|Daru Festival Coin|Basic|1}} . They can be changed by clicking them in the inventory.
