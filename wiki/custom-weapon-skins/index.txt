<h2 id="Summer_Contest_2025_Weapon_skins">Summer Contest 2025 Weapon skins</h2>
<p>One really cool feature about Archeage Classic are all kinds of custom events, different contest and particularly custom weapon design contest. This contest was held between <i>16.6.2025 - 13.7.2025</i>. Players were able to make their own unique designs that were voted by the community and later chosen by the Darus to become actual weapon skins in the game. 
</p><p><span><img src="/aac-hub/wiki-files/e2/e225598e84b78d038a078163e5fec5129bbb9528.png" width="40" height="43" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p>The first ever contest was themed with <b>Summer weapons</b> and they became part of the <b>Complete</b> <b>Summer Contest Crates</b> that are being sold in the Marketplace for 700 <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/currencies">credits</a>.
</p><p><b>The item IDs can be used in the Dress Up addon to see them if unsure of how they look in the game.</b> 
</p>

== Summer Contest 2025 Weapon skins ==
One really cool feature about Archeage Classic are all kinds of custom events, different contest and particularly custom weapon design contest. This contest was held between ''16.6.2025 - 13.7.2025''. Players were able to make their own unique designs that were voted by the community and later chosen by the Darus to become actual weapon skins in the game. 

[[File:Compeletesummercontestcrate.png]]

The first ever contest was themed with '''Summer weapons''' and they became part of the '''Complete''' '''Summer Contest Crates''' that are being sold in the Marketplace for 700 [[Currencies|credits]].
