Halcyona Necklace

Halcyona Necklace's name comes from the place you get the materials for it, Halcyona War. By collecting Warrior Medals and going to the

Proven Warrior Workbench in both factions bases you can create a really good necklace.

First necklace you create is called Proven Warrior Necklace but the name changes while you upgrade it. The first necklace requires 3 Warrior medals. After that the amount increases after every upgrade. Once you get to rank 6 you need to choose which of the two effects / stats you want. After rank 7 the necklace has high chance of downgrading back to rank 7. The necklace can't get destroyed while upgrading. Good way to upgrade a necklace is to have two, using one and trying to upgrade other one higher until it can replace your current best one.
