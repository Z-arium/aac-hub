1d:Ta67,== Getting there ==
[[File:AhnimarFestival.png|thumb|Ahnimar Festival Grounds]]
The Ahnimar Founder's Festival, as the name implies, takes place in '''Ahnimar'''. Specifically the '''Tadpole Tail Field''' and '''Lotus Song Garden''' regions. You can make your way here yourself, or make use of the festival world gates found in your faction capitals.

== Taking Part ==
Once you arrive at the Ahnimar community center you'll be head to the '''Lotus Song Gardens''' to speak with '''Eggwarden Dolbu''', who offers festival tokens in exchange for your help in caring for Daru eggs.

=== Raising Daru Eggs ===
After collecting your '''Daru Egg Cradle''' from '''Father Dolbu''', you'll need to incubate it within a '''Daru Egg Incubator''', which you can find nearby. Once your egg is '''moistened''' you'll need to locate one of the '''Lillypad Nursery's''', place your egg down, and follow the prompts to give it all the care a growing Daru needs. Once your egg is happy, it'll fall '''asleep''', and you'll have to come back after 10 minutes to discover what type of Daru hatchling you've been caring for. As different egg types, result in different numbers of rewards.

In total, there are 5 different types of Daru that you may have been raising, once returned to the '''Eggwarden''', you'll receive differently sized '''rewards''':

* Residency Daru Egg '''(1 Festival Coins)'''
* Salesman Daru Egg '''(2 Festival Coins)'''
* Captain Daru Egg '''(3 Festival Coins)'''
* Trade Outlet Daru Egg '''(3 Festival Coins)'''
* Astra-Blessed Daru Egg '''(5 Festival Coins)'''

=== Incapacitiating hungry Skyfins ===
'''Achoo''' needs your help. The local Skyfin population has been known to steal Daru Eggs! Take the '''Skyfin Sedative''' they supply, and help sedate the Skyfin before they can steal the eggs. Interact with the stone behind '''Achoo''' to be moved to the hill top the Skyfin's favour. You'll be rewarded with '''2 Festival Coins''' for your effort.

=== Help build Daru Egg incubators ===
'''Festival Keep Aliniri''' needs help constructing Daru Egg Incubators, deliver '''<s>50 Iron Ingots</s> 100 Lumber (2026)''' and receive '''3 Festival Coins''' as a reward.

=== Dig for hidden Treasure ===
At '''[1PM]''', '''[7PM]''', and '''[12AM]''' server-time '''(EST)''' the Ahnimar Rainbow will appear over Lotus Song Garden, head over and speak to '''Ian''' to take part in a treasure hunt.

'''Sand Mounds''' will appear, player's will have to dig through these mounds in search of lost '''Daru Treasure Chests''', once they've been found, interact with them to receive '''2 Festival Coins'''.
