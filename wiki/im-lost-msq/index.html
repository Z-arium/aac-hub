I'm Lost! (MSQ)

A guide to help you navigate through tricky or unclear parts of the Main Story Questline in ArcheAge Classic.

Introduction

In ArcheAge Classic, progressing through the Main Story Questline (MSQ) is essential for leveling. However, players often find themselves lost when the follow-up quest doesn't appear at the same NPC or area as the previous hand-in. This guide is here to help!

This page outlines commonly confusing spots in the MSQ for each race, and provides direct links to NPCs on the ArcheAge Codex database and images of their location to help you find your way.

General MSQ Tips

If the follow-up quest doesn’t appear at the NPC where you turned in your last quest:

  • Open your Options → Game Info, scroll to the bottom, and set the Quest Details Map Display slider to Unlimited. This setting allows all available quests to be shown on your world map, which is especially helpful when navigating the MSQ, as follow-up quests are sometimes given by NPCs in a different location.

Harani MSQ Navigation Issues

Chapter 2.1

  • Quest: Innocent Victims
  • NPC: Ramusi
  • Tip: This quest starts in a different region. You can find it in Tigerspine Mountains along the road connected to Arcum Iris.

Chapter 6.2

  • Quest: A Shadowhawk's Request
  • NPC: Ataka
  • Tip: This quest starts in a different region. You can find it in Silent Forest - Harani Campsite.

Firran MSQ Navigation Issues

Chapter 6.2

  • Quest: An Old Friend
  • NPC: Shavnik
  • Tip: This quest starts in a different region. You can find it in Silent Forest - Vedisa.

Nuia / Haranya (Shared) MSQ Navigation Issues

Chapter 7.1

  • Quest: Your Legend Continues
  • NPC: Gleeman Orneon
  • Tip: This chapter starts in a different region. You can find the quest in Cinderstone Moore - Burnt Castle Camp (for Nuia Continent) or Ynystere - Bulwark (for Haranya Continent) depending on which faction you are.
