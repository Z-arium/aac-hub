<h2 id="Introduction">Introduction</h2>
<p>In <b>ArcheAge Classic</b>, progressing through the <b>Main Story Questline (MSQ)</b> is essential for leveling. However, players often find themselves lost when the follow-up quest doesn't appear at the same NPC or area as the previous hand-in. This guide is here to help!
</p><p>This page outlines commonly confusing spots in the MSQ for each race, and provides direct links to NPCs on the <a rel="noopener" href="https://archeagecodex.com/us/">ArcheAge Codex</a> database and images of their location to help you find your way.
</p>
<h2 id="General_MSQ_Tips">General MSQ Tips</h2>
<p>If the follow-up quest doesn’t appear at the NPC where you turned in your last quest:
</p>
<ul><li>Open your <b>Options → Game Info</b>, scroll to the bottom, and set the <b>Quest Details Map Display</b> slider to <b>Unlimited</b>. This setting allows all available quests to be shown on your world map, which is especially helpful when navigating the MSQ, as follow-up quests are sometimes given by NPCs in a different location.</li></ul>
