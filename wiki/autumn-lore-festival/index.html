Autumn Lore Festival

Location

Festival Manager Emily will start off the Autumn Festival quest chain. The Festival is held at Lilyut Hills in Bitterwind Plains.

Quests

[Beginning of the Festival]

Emily tells u to go to talk to Seasoned Art Dealer close by.


[Autumn Lore]

Seasoned Art Dealer will give second quest to collect a story by using the Old Scroll.


This can be done by going next to the hut and clicking the scroll icon next to quest.


[A Time of Tales]

Astrologist Rave asks to bring her 30 Harvest Festival Coins. The reward is title "Storyteller".

Storyteller title


[A Gift from the Evenbards]

Nesila asks for Pagemasters Storage Chest to be crafted.


Pagemasters Storage Chest

Chest can be crafted on the event site on Festival Gift Exchanger or in Mirage Island. Crafting the chest costs 100 Daru Festival coins.



[Troubled Story Seller]

Suspicious Storyteller asks to bring him 5 Glowing Ore Pieces.


The Glowing Ores can be found behind the hut and mined normally. Each mining uses 10 labor.


After mining Suspicious Storyteller will give a quest to combine the Ores into Story Jar. Click the icon next to quest.


[A Calming Accompaniment]

Guest Author Orne asks to play a Lullaby by using the Calming Pipe 3 times.


This can be done by finding Sleepless and Noisy children who wonder around the hill. Play the flute for them by targeting them and by going close to them. Flute icon next to the quest.


[A Poultry Novel]

Nene the Hen asks to soother Blushing Chicken and collect A Base Instinct.


The little chick can be found little bit downhill in the middle of the bushes.


[Authorial Courtesy]

Guest Author Rollo appears between 8 pm and 8am near the other quest NPCs. She asks to splash water on Doxing Guests around the fireplace.

Target the npc and splash water by clicking the bucket icon next to quest information.


Festival Buff

Sharing Night Bonfire

The grilled meats around the festival area give a buff that boosts production time and Move Speed.
