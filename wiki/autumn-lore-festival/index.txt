12:T18a7,<h2 id="Location">Location</h2>
<p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/2/28/Emily.png/180px-Emily.png" width="180" height="377" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/2/28/Emily.png/271px-Emily.png 1.5x, https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/2/28/Emily.png 2x" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p>Festival Manager Emily will start off the Autumn Festival quest chain. The Festival is held at <b>Lilyut Hills in Bitterwind Plains</b>. 
</p>
<h2 id="Quests">Quests</h2>
<h3 id="[Beginning_of_the_Festival]"><span></span>[Beginning of the Festival]</h3>
<p>Emily tells u to go to talk to Seasoned Art Dealer close by.
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h3 id="[Autumn_Lore]"><span></span>[Autumn Lore]</h3>
<p>Seasoned Art Dealer will give second quest to collect a story by using the Old Scroll.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/d/dc/Artdealer.png/181px-Artdealer.png" width="181" height="340" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/d/dc/Artdealer.png 1.5x" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p><p>This can be done by going next to the hut and clicking the scroll icon next to quest.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/f/f1/Collectstory.png/437px-Collectstory.png" width="437" height="392" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/f/f1/Collectstory.png 1.5x" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h3 id="[A_Time_of_Tales]"><span></span>[A Time of Tales]</h3>
<p>Astrologist Rave asks to bring her 30 Harvest Festival Coins. The reward is title "<b>Storyteller</b>". 
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/5/5d/Astrologistraeva.png/180px-Astrologistraeva.png" width="180" height="382" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/5/5d/Astrologistraeva.png 1.5x" loading="lazy"></span>
</p>
<h4 id="Storyteller_title">Storyteller title</h4>
<p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/f/f5/Storyteller.png" width="208" height="126" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h3 id="[A_Gift_from_the_Evenbards]"><span></span>[A Gift from the Evenbards]</h3>
<p>Nesila asks for Pagemasters Storage Chest to be crafted.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/e/e5/Evenbardnesila.png/179px-Evenbardnesila.png" width="179" height="445" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/e/e5/Evenbardnesila.png 1.5x" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h4 id="Pagemasters_Storage_Chest">Pagemasters Storage Chest</h4>
<p>Chest can be crafted on the event site on Festival Gift Exchanger or in Mirage Island. Crafting the chest costs 100 <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/currencies">Daru Festival coins</a>.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/f/f4/Pagemasterschest.png" width="296" height="266" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h3 id="[Troubled_Story_Seller]"><span></span>[Troubled Story Seller]</h3>
<p>Suspicious Storyteller asks to bring him 5 Glowing Ore Pieces. 
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/0/0f/Suspstoryteller.png/180px-Suspstoryteller.png" width="180" height="373" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/0/0f/Suspstoryteller.png 1.5x" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p><p>The Glowing Ores can be found behind the hut and mined normally. Each mining uses 10 labor.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/1/1d/Glowingores.png/501px-Glowingores.png" width="501" height="315" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/1/1d/Glowingores.png 1.5x" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p><p>After mining Suspicious Storyteller will give a quest to combine the Ores into Story Jar. Click the icon next to quest.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/5/51/Mysticalstoryjar.png" width="206" height="231" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h3 id="[A_Calming_Accompaniment]"><span></span>[A Calming Accompaniment]</h3>
<p>Guest Author Orne asks to play a Lullaby by using the Calming Pipe 3 times.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/a/ab/Guestauthor.png/181px-Guestauthor.png" width="181" height="410" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/a/ab/Guestauthor.png 1.5x" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p><p>This can be done by finding Sleepless and Noisy children who wonder around the hill. Play the flute for them by targeting them and by going close to them. Flute icon next to the quest.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/e/e2/Sleeplesschild.png/482px-Sleeplesschild.png" width="482" height="371" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/e/e2/Sleeplesschild.png 1.5x" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h3 id="[A_Poultry_Novel]"><span></span>[A Poultry Novel]</h3>
<p>Nene the Hen asks to soother Blushing Chicken and collect A Base Instinct. 
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/f/fd/Nenethehen.png" width="250" height="284" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p><p>The little chick can be found little bit downhill in the middle of the bushes. 
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/4/4c/Chicky.png" width="141" height="206" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h3 id="[Authorial_Courtesy]"><span></span>[Authorial Courtesy]</h3>
<p>Guest Author Rollo appears between 8 pm and 8am near the other quest NPCs. She asks to splash water on Doxing Guests around the fireplace. 
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/1/14/Guestauthorrollo0.png/209px-Guestauthorrollo0.png" width="209" height="345" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/1/14/Guestauthorrollo0.png 1.5x" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p>Target the npc and splash water by clicking the bucket icon next to quest information.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/c/c1/Dozingguests.png" width="295" height="274" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h2 id="Festival_Buff">Festival Buff</h2>
<h3 id="Sharing_Night_Bonfire">Sharing Night Bonfire</h3>
<p>The grilled meats around the festival area give a buff that boosts production time and Move Speed.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/b/b3/Sharingn_ightbonfire.png" width="253" height="239" loading="lazy"></span>
