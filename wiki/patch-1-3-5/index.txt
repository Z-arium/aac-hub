12:T1d00,<h2 id="Starfall_is_here!"><span></span>Starfall is here!</h2>
<p>Watch out for stars falling from the sky! A warning will now appear globally about an imminent comet crashing in an area.
</p><p>Archmage Iseren will be seen watching the sky through her telescope as the comet approaches landfall. 
</p><p>She'll ask for some help from you while you're there as you venture into discovering more about these celestial objects visiting us from a distant place.
</p><p>The following daily quests have been added to guide you through your comet discovery: 
</p><p>A series of introductory quests when you meet Iseren, leading you to the Ayanad Library where you can find the Comet Workbench, [Meteor Miner], [Scorched Earth], [Crystallized Clues], [The Eternal Flame], And three other quests related to what occurs at a comet site!
</p><p>What does the arrival of these comets entail? Iseren, a mage of the Ayanad Council, has these very same questions!
</p><p><i><b>But be careful... don't stand underneath one as it lands.</b></i>
</p>
<h2 id="Patch_Notes">Patch Notes</h2>
<h3 id="Weekly_Quest_Changes">Weekly Quest Changes</h3>
<ul><li>The weekly quest locations have been moved to a new NPC called the Shadow Daru. The shadow daru will spawn at each major city on Saturday and Sunday to offer you the activity token quests you'd normally get. Shadow Daru will spawn behind the Pirate Nui on the broken bridge on Fridays.</li></ul>
<ul><li>Added new weekly quest: Have You Ever Seen A Ghost? which requires you to defeat the Delphinad Ghost Ship</li></ul>

<li>Added Scented Leaves to gathering bundles and single planting through pitchfork</li>
<li>Fixed pitchfork harvesting hay bale amounts</li>
<li>Added the following plants to be harvestable by pitchfork:
<ul><li>Jinns Tongue</li>

<h4 id="Commerce">Commerce</h4>
<ul><li>Shadow Daru are working properly now</li></ul>

<h4 id="Housing">Housing</h4>

<li>Added new craft for Pink Marlin Weapon Crate</li>
<li>Addon API updates (see below for more information)</li></ul>
<h2 id="Obsidian_Transmutators">Obsidian Transmutators</h2>
<ul><li>Transmutators will allow you to seal a Brimstone or a Tier 6 obsidian weapon or armor and then craft it into the desired version that you wish.</li>
<li>The crafting cost to craft a sealed weapon or armor depends on the tier that it was sealed in.</li>
<li>T7 has the highest costs, compared to a sealed T6.</li>
<li>The transmutation tiers are defined as such: <b>Greater Transmutation</b> - legendary and mythic only. Restricted to T7.</li>
<li><b>Normal Transmutation</b> - epic only. Restricted to T7.</li>
<li><b>Lesser transmutation</b> - divine to legendary only. Restricted to T6.</li>
<li>50 / 180 / 230k alchemy required respectively based on the tier of transmutation.</li>
<li><i><b>Transmutating an item that is unbound will make it bound.</b></i></li>
<li>The costs of T6 and T7 transmutation will require a mix of boss materials between T1 and T6, wisps, and a few materials related to both metal and wooden types.</li>
<li>Armor transmutation follows the same material costs, except it uses all three material types</li></ul>

But for those with the mettle to face it, rich treasures lie within your grasp. Prepare your ships, gather your comrades, and sail forth.
</p>
