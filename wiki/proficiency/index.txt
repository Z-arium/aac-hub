=== Proficiency category limits ===
There are 3 proficiency categories which are: '''Harvesting, Crafting and Special'''. Each proficiency can be leveled by the player but all of the proficiencies cant be maxed out so it balances out the gameplay and lets some players be professionals in some areas than others. The maximum level of Famed can be only 2 from Harvesting, 3 from Crafting and 2 from Special. Everything else can be leveled up to level 10 with Specialization snowflakes mentioned below.
