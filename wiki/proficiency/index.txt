12:Tce7,<p>Proficiencies are the key to harvesting, crafting and producing goods in Archeage. They are certain skills the character can learn and level up to be better at certain craft. The higher the level of the proficiency the less Labor cost and time spent making the items.  
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/9/97/Rankdetails.png" width="567" height="391" loading="lazy"></span>  
</p>

<h3 id="Proficiency_category_limits">Proficiency category limits</h3>
<p>There are 3 proficiency categories which are: <b>Harvesting, Crafting and Special</b>. Each proficiency can be leveled by the player but all of the proficiencies cant be maxed out so it balances out the gameplay and lets some players be professionals in some areas than others. The maximum level of Famed can be only 2 from Harvesting, 3 from Crafting and 2 from Special. Everything else can be leveled up to level 10 with Specialization snowflakes mentioned below.   
</p>
<h3 id="Harvesting">Harvesting</h3>
<p>Gathering the materials from the open world or player farms and gardens. 
</p>
<h4 id="Husbandry">Husbandry</h4>
<p>Growing livestock one by one, pens or Ranchers farmhouse. Usual things to do are milk, wool, fresh fertilizer.
</p>
<h4 id="Farming">Farming</h4>
<p>Consists of all kinds of grains and produces. 
</p>
<h4 id="Fishing">Fishing</h4>
<p>As the name implies, fish. Can be done as sportfishing or fish traps with aquafarms. 
</p>
<h4 id="Logging">Logging</h4>
<p>Cutting trees.
</p>
<h4 id="Gathering">Gathering</h4>
<p>Gathering all kinds of flowers and herbs.
</p>
<h4 id="Mining">Mining</h4>
<p>Different kind of minerals and metals that can be later turn into items. 
</p><p><br>
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h3 id="Crafting">Crafting</h3>
<p>Crafting the items such as gear pieces, weapons, vehicles, gliders, jewels and so on. 
</p>
<h4 id="Alchemy">Alchemy</h4>
<p>Potions and buffs.
</p>
<h4 id="Cooking">Cooking</h4>
<p>Food buffs.
</p>
<h4 id="Handicrafts">Handicrafts</h4>
<p>Jewels 
</p>
<h4 id="Machining">Machining</h4>
<p>Certain parts of the vehicles, gliders etc
</p>
<h4 id="Metalwork">Metalwork</h4>
<p>Plate armors
</p>
<h4 id="Printing">Printing</h4>
<p>Regrade scrolls, books, paper, ship designs
</p>
<h4 id="Masonry">Masonry</h4>
<p>Regrade charms, anchoring emblems, runes and dice
</p>
<h4 id="Tailoring">Tailoring</h4>
<p>Cloth armors
</p>
<h4 id="Leatherwork">Leatherwork</h4>
<p>Leather armors
</p>
<h4 id="Weaponry">Weaponry</h4>
<p>All kinds of weapons made of metal
</p>
<h4 id="Carpentry">Carpentry</h4>
<p>Weapons made of wood, decorations and furniture for houses
</p><p><br>
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h3 id="Special">Special</h3>
<h4 id="Construction">Construction</h4>
<p>Tax certificates, construction materials, improving farms, building management titles
</p>
<h4 id="Larceny">Larceny</h4>
<p>Opening coinpurses
</p>
<h4 id="Commerce">Commerce</h4>
<p>Crafting trade packs and selling them
</p>
<h4 id="Artistry">Artistry</h4>
<p>Making music sheets and paintings
</p>
<h4 id="Exploration">Exploration</h4>
<p>Searching on land and in the bottom of the sea for treasures and sunken ships
</p><p><br>
</p>
