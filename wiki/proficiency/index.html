Proficiency

Proficiencies are the key to harvesting, crafting and producing goods in Archeage. They are certain skills the character can learn and level up to be better at certain craft. The higher the level of the proficiency the less Labor cost and time spent making the items.

Proficiency category limits

There are 3 proficiency categories which are: Harvesting, Crafting and Special. Each proficiency can be leveled by the player but all of the proficiencies cant be maxed out so it balances out the gameplay and lets some players be professionals in some areas than others. The maximum level of Famed can be only 2 from Harvesting, 3 from Crafting and 2 from Special. Everything else can be leveled up to level 10 with Specialization snowflakes mentioned below.

Harvesting

Gathering the materials from the open world or player farms and gardens.

Husbandry

Growing livestock one by one, pens or Ranchers farmhouse. Usual things to do are milk, wool, fresh fertilizer.

Farming

Consists of all kinds of grains and produces.

Fishing

As the name implies, fish. Can be done as sportfishing or fish traps with aquafarms.

Logging

Cutting trees.

Gathering

Gathering all kinds of flowers and herbs.

Mining

Different kind of minerals and metals that can be later turn into items.



Crafting

Crafting the items such as gear pieces, weapons, vehicles, gliders, jewels and so on.

Alchemy

Potions and buffs.

Cooking

Food buffs.

Handicrafts

Jewels

Machining

Certain parts of the vehicles, gliders etc

Metalwork

Plate armors

Printing

Regrade scrolls, books, paper, ship designs

Masonry

Regrade charms, anchoring emblems, runes and dice

Tailoring

Cloth armors

Leatherwork

Leather armors

Weaponry

All kinds of weapons made of metal

Carpentry

Weapons made of wood, decorations and furniture for houses



Special

Construction

Tax certificates, construction materials, improving farms, building management titles

Larceny

Opening coinpurses

Commerce

Crafting trade packs and selling them

Artistry

Making music sheets and paintings

Exploration

Searching on land and in the bottom of the sea for treasures and sunken ships
