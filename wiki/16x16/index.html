16x16

Next in the size after 8x8 are the 16x16 land plots. They are little bit bigger areas to store packs, grow crops or livestock. They also include some houses and aquafarms.

Scarecrow Farm

After getting the 8x8 Garden from Blue Salt Brotherhood quest, the questline continues with the 16x16 Farm questline. Hamiltons guidevideo explains in a detail how to get it. It can be obtained from the quest only once by character.

Can be sold after being placed. Cannot be Full Kitted.

Improved Scarecrow Farm

The 8x8 Scarecrow Garden from the Blue Salt Brotherhood questline can be upgraded into 16x16 Farm on Carpentry workbench with come materials.

Can be sold after being placed, cannot be Full Kitted.

Aquafarm

Aquafarms are basically same as Scarecrow Farms but underwater. They can grow different kinds of corals, pearls and fish traps that are used in crafting. Aquafarm designs can be bought from the Gilda shop in Marketplace and they cost 20 Gilda Stars.

Aquafarms have a device in the middle that players can use and get more oxygen for working underwater.

Aquafarms are always Full Kits meaning they don't need to be built. They can be sold.
