12:T3092,<p>!Page still under construction! <a rel="noopener" href="https://www.ign.com/wikis/archeage/Ayanad_Earring#17._Into_the_Light">IGN Guide</a>
</p><p>The <a>Ayanad Earring</a> is a multi-stage self upgrading reward from a lengthy questline.
</p><p><b>Quest 1: Orb Of Secrets</b>
</p><p>To begin kill <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Recordkeeper_Wynn&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">Recordkeeper Wynn</a> he will drop an item for everyone in your party called <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Unknown_Orb&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">Unknown Orb</a>. Click on the orb to begin a quest, <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Orb_of_Secrets&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">Orb of Secrets</a>.
</p><p>Simply talk to <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Lemian&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">Lemian</a> on the Floor 1 Lobby of the Library,  <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Introspect_Path&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">Introspect Path</a>. He will lead you to the next quest requirements. 
</p><p><b><u>Quest 2: Special Collection</u></b> 
</p><p>Lemian will send you to Floor 1 of the Library to look for 3 items. They are located in the rooms below.
</p><p>Part 1 – A6
</p><p>Part 2 – H6
</p><p>Part 3 – F4
</p><p>You will need to go to these rooms and click on the book on the floor that will teleport you up. Look around for the item. It should be glowing or have a line indicator above it. 
</p><p><u>After collecting all 3 parts, talk to Lemian again.</u>
</p><p><br>
</p><p><b><u>Quest 3: Aranzeb’s Student</u></b>
</p><p>Lemian will then send you to meet Belstrom. <u>He is located north of the Library, on the overlooking hill above the West Purification Node.</u>
</p><p><br>
</p><p><b><u>Quest 4: Orb’s Identity</u></b>
</p><p>Belstrom will ask you to bring him 30 <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Ayanad_Wizard_Token&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">Ayanad Wizard’s Tokens</a>. Hopefully, you have enough of these on you already from doing <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Library_dailies&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">library dailies</a>.
</p><p>Simply talk to Belstrom again to get your Rare <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Lost_Ayanad_Earring&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">Lost Ayanad Earring</a>.
</p><p><br>
</p><p><b><u>Quest 5: Bound</u></b>
</p><p>After talking to Belstrom, go back to Library Floor 1, room A6. Teleport up via the book on the ground and go to the secret door in front of the glowing book.
</p><p><i><b>Remember to equip your Lost Ayanad Earring from this point on, while doing the following quests.</b></i>
</p><p><br>
</p><p><b><u>Quest 6: Stena's Spellbook</u></b>
</p><p>After talking to Belstrom, go back to Library Floor 1, room H6. Teleport up via the book on the ground and go to the secret door. In this room, there is a giant bookcase called Stena’s Bookshelf. You will need a party to defeat this boss.
</p><p>Talk to Belstrom after you obtain the item.
</p><p><br>
</p><p><b><u>Quest 7: Gildaron's Heart</u></b>
</p><p>Go back to Library Floor 1, room F4. Teleport up via the book on the ground and go to the secret door. In this room, there is a big dog called Gildaron the Disobedient. You will need a party to defeat this boss.
</p><p>Talk to Belstrom after you obtain the item.
</p><p><br>

</p><p><i>Halnaak or Alexander.</i>
</p><p>After that, talk to Belstrom again for the Arcane <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Solemn_Ayanad_Earring&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">Solemn Ayanad Earring</a>.
</p><p><br>
</p><p><b><u>Quest 9: A Speedy Recovery</u></b>
</p><p>Go to Slevin in Floor 1, A6. He will ask you to collect 3 Ayanad Senior Council Manaprints from any of the library dungeon bosses; Recordkeeper Wynn, Halnaak or Alexander.
</p><p>After obtaining the Ayanad Senior Council Manaprints , return to Slevin.

The [[Ayanad Earring (Library)|Ayanad Earring]] is a multi-stage self upgrading reward from a lengthy questline.

'''Quest 1: Orb Of Secrets'''

To begin kill [[Recordkeeper Wynn]] he will drop an item for everyone in your party called [[Unknown Orb]]. Click on the orb to begin a quest, [[Orb of Secrets]].

Simply talk to [[Lemian]] on the Floor 1 Lobby of the Library,  [[Introspect Path]]. He will lead you to the next quest requirements. 

'''<u>Quest 2: Special Collection</u>''' 

Lemian will send you to Floor 1 of the Library to look for 3 items. They are located in the rooms below.

Part 1 – A6

Part 2 – H6

Part 3 – F4

You will need to go to these rooms and click on the book on the floor that will teleport you up. Look around for the item. It should be glowing or have a line indicator above it. 

<u>After collecting all 3 parts, talk to Lemian again.</u>



'''<u>Quest 3: Aranzeb’s Student</u>'''

Lemian will then send you to meet Belstrom. <u>He is located north of the Library, on the overlooking hill above the West Purification Node.</u>



'''<u>Quest 4: Orb’s Identity</u>'''

Belstrom will ask you to bring him 30 [[Ayanad Wizard Token|Ayanad Wizard’s Tokens]]. Hopefully, you have enough of these on you already from doing [[library dailies]].

Simply talk to Belstrom again to get your Rare [[Lost Ayanad Earring]].



'''<u>Quest 5: Bound</u>'''

After talking to Belstrom, go back to Library Floor 1, room A6. Teleport up via the book on the ground and go to the secret door in front of the glowing book.

'''''Remember to equip your Lost Ayanad Earring from this point on, while doing the following quests.'''''



'''<u>Quest 6: Stena's Spellbook</u>'''

After talking to Belstrom, go back to Library Floor 1, room H6. Teleport up via the book on the ground and go to the secret door. In this room, there is a giant bookcase called Stena’s Bookshelf. You will need a party to defeat this boss.

Talk to Belstrom after you obtain the item.



'''<u>Quest 7: Gildaron's Heart</u>'''

Go back to Library Floor 1, room F4. Teleport up via the book on the ground and go to the secret door. In this room, there is a big dog called Gildaron the Disobedient. You will need a party to defeat this boss.

Talk to Belstrom after you obtain the item.



'''<u>Quest 8: Freeing Slevin</u>'''

Go to Slevin in Floor 1, A6. He will ask you to collect '''3''' [[Ayanad Senior Council Manaprint|Ayanad Senior Council Manaprints]] from any of the library dungeon bosses; ''Recordkeeper Wynn,''

''Halnaak or Alexander.''

After that, talk to Belstrom again for the Arcane [[Solemn Ayanad Earring]].



'''<u>Quest 9: A Speedy Recovery</u>'''

Go to Slevin in Floor 1, A6. He will ask you to collect 3 Ayanad Senior Council Manaprints from any of the library dungeon bosses; Recordkeeper Wynn, Halnaak or Alexander.

* 30 Ayanad Wizard’s Tokens to make an Anima Cube.

<u>After making the Anima Cube, you have to obtain 1500 Anima Fragments. Follow these steps to obtain them.</u>

* Go to Floor 3 and look for Ayanad Guard Mage or Librarian Mage. Pls refer to this document for specific locations.
* Target the mage and right click on the Anima Cube. As you are channelling, you will get the Anima Fragments.
* You get 5 Anima Fragments per channel. The mob will aggro to you after you are done channelling, but you can reset it by running out of the room.
