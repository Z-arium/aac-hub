Dawnsdrop Watering Can

The Dawnsdrop Watering Can is an addition to the Dawnsdrop Tools custom content added in Patch 1.3.2. The Dawnsdrop Pitchfork is a Farming and Gathering tool that allows players to water crops and bundles in a large area without the need of having Water in your inventory.

Starting the Questline

Requirements:

  • 230,000 Farming Proficiency
  • 230,000 Gathering Proficiency

You do not need 230,000 proficiency in both.

Bring Dawnsdrop Wrists and Pants.

  1. Go to Sun's End (Halcyona Community Center).
  2. Water crops and flowers with your Dawnsdrops equipped for the Dawnsdrop Buffs.
  3. Get the quest from Neru in the Community Center.

Tip: It is recommended to also start the Dawnsdrop Pitchfork questline and progress these simultaneously as they often share objectives.
