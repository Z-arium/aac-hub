Dawnsdrop Watering Can

The Dawnsdrop Watering Can is an addition to the Dawnsdrop Tools custom content added in Patch 1.3.2. The Dawnsdrop Pitchfork is a Farming and Gathering tool that allows players to water crops and bundles in a large area without the need of having Water in your inventory.

Starting the Questline

Requirements:

  • 230,000 Farming Proficiency
  • 230,000 Gathering Proficiency

You do not need 230,000 proficiency in both.

Bring Dawnsdrop Wrists and Pants.

  1. Go to Sun's End (Halcyona Community Center).
  2. Water crops and flowers with your Dawnsdrops equipped for the Dawnsdrop Buffs.
  3. Get the quest from Neru in the Community Center.

Tip: It is recommended to also start the Dawnsdrop Pitchfork questline and progress these simultaneously as they often share objectives.

Grand Dawnsdrop Watering Can

1. The Art of Gardening

Turn in to Velaine in Sun's End.

2. Every Drop Counts

Requires the following materials:

  • Water × 3,000

Tip: Upgraded Farmhouses offer a means for collecting water at a much faster rate. It has also been found that the cooldown of collecting Water from the Pinnacled Witch House skin for the upgraded Farmhouses is the lowest out of all Farmhouses.

Rare Dawnsdrop Watering Can

3. Tending To Your Garden

  • Use 12,500 Labor in Farming
  • Use 12,500 Labor in Gathering
  • Use your Dawnsdrop Watering Can 100 × times.

4. The Delicate Crop

Requires the following materials:

  • Corn × 10,000

5. The Delicate Flower

Requires the following materials:

  • Rose × 10,000

  • Use 20,000 Labor in Farming
  • Use 20,000 Labor in Gathering
  • Use your Dawnsdrop Watering Can 250 × times.

  • Kill Marmas the Reaper from Greater Hadir Farm and collect Massive Corn × 1.

8. The Pumpkin Season

Requires the following materials:

  • Pumpkin × 10,000

9. The Flower of Sound

  • Kill Aria from Mistsong Summit and collect Singing Flower × 1.

  • Use 25,000 Labor in Farming
  • Use 25,000 Labor in Gathering
  • Use your Dawnsdrop Watering Can 500 × times.

  • Craft the Celestial Dawnsdrop Watering Can at any Anvil.
