10:T2b40,<p>The Dawnsdrop Watering Can is an addition to the <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/dawnsdrop-tools">Dawnsdrop Tools</a> custom content added in <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/patch-1-3-2">Patch 1.3.2</a>. The Dawnsdrop Pitchfork is a <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Farming&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">Farming</a> and <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Gathering&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">Gathering</a> tool that allows players to water crops and bundles in a large area without the need of having Water in your inventory. 
</p>

<h1 id="Starting_the_Questline">Starting the Questline</h1>
<p>Requirements:
</p>
<ul><li>230,000 Farming Proficiency</li>
<li>230,000 Gathering Proficiency</li></ul>
<p>You do not need 230,000 proficiency in both.
</p><p>Bring Dawnsdrop Wrists and Pants. 
</p>
<ol><li>Go to Sun's End (Halcyona Community Center).</li>
<li>Water crops and flowers with your Dawnsdrops equipped for the Dawnsdrop Buffs.</li>
<li>Get the quest from Neru in the Community Center.</li></ol>
<p><i>Tip: It is recommended to also start the <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/dawnsdrop-pitchfork">Dawnsdrop Pitchfork</a> questline and progress these simultaneously as they often share objectives.</i>
</p>

<h2 id="Grand_Dawnsdrop_Watering_Can"><span style="color: rgb(119, 176, 100)">Grand Dawnsdrop Watering Can</span></h2>
<h3 id="1._The_Art_of_Gardening"><a rel="noopener" href="https://aa-classic.com/database/quests/9000091">1. The Art of Gardening</a></h3>
<p>Turn in to Velaine in Sun's End. 
</p>
<h3 id="2._Every_Drop_Counts"><a rel="noopener" href="https://aa-classic.com/database/quests/9000092">2. Every Drop Counts</a></h3>
<p>Requires the following materials:
</p>
<ul><li>Water × 3,000</li></ul>
<p>Tip: Upgraded Farmhouses offer a means for collecting water at a much faster rate. It has also been found that the cooldown of collecting Water from the Pinnacled Witch House skin for the upgraded Farmhouses is the lowest out of all Farmhouses. 
</p>
<h2 id="Rare_Dawnsdrop_Watering_Can"><span style="color: rgb(85, 143, 215)">Rare Dawnsdrop Watering Can</span></h2>
<h3 id="3._Tending_To_Your_Garden"><a rel="noopener" href="https://aa-classic.com/database/quests/9000093">3. Tending To Your Garden</a></h3>
<ul><li>Use 12,500 Labor in Farming</li>
<li>Use 12,500 Labor in Gathering</li>
<li>Use your Dawnsdrop Watering Can 100 × times.</li></ul>
<h3 id="4._The_Delicate_Crop"><a rel="noopener" href="https://aa-classic.com/database/quests/9000094">4. The Delicate Crop</a></h3>
<p>Requires the following materials:
</p>
<ul><li>Corn × 10,000</li></ul>

<h3 id="6._Thirsty_Corn_Seedlings"><a rel="noopener" href="https://aa-classic.com/database/quests/9000096">6. Thirsty Corn Seedlings</a></h3>
