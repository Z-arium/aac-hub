10:T21fd,<p>New map area that opened in the Patch 1.3.5u ||  Winter Patch. Located in <b>Miroir Tundra</b> is now a <b>Snowfang Village</b>. The Isle got the basic crafting stations, seed and livestock merchants as well as their own housing. In Snowfang Isle only Igloo houses survive so other types of houses can't be placed. Snowfang Isle's climate is subarctic and the zone is ongoing peace zone with no war cycle.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/2/2c/Miroir.png/750px-Miroir.png" width="750" height="449" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/2/2c/Miroir.png 1.5x" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p>

<h3 id="Getting_around">Getting around</h3>
<p>Normal vehicles won't work there so players can rent a sleigh from Sleigh Rental Slopeng. Sleigh will be usable for 15min and they cost 15 gold each. Slopeng is located in the middle of the Isle.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/4/49/Slopeng.png/506px-Slopeng.png" width="506" height="226" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/4/49/Slopeng.png/759px-Slopeng.png 1.5x, https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/4/49/Slopeng.png 2x" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/e/e9/Sleighrental.png/192px-Sleighrental.png" width="192" height="310" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/e/e9/Sleighrental.png 1.5x" loading="lazy"></span>
</p>
<h3 id="Housing">Housing</h3>
<p>Housing on Snowfang Isle consists only of Igloo houses since other houses can't take the extreme cold. There are few housing areas around the Isle and more are to be opened when they are filling. Igloo design can be bought with 150 <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/currencies">Gilda stars</a>.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/0/07/Igloodesing.png" width="210" height="278" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h3 id="Questline">Questline</h3>
<h4 id="[Miroir_Tundra]"><span></span>[Miroir Tundra]</h4>
<p>Questline starts with <b>Alfensa</b> who is located in the middle of the Isle. She wants the player to talk to Catari.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/a/ab/Alfensa.png/268px-Alfensa.png" width="268" height="287" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/a/ab/Alfensa.png 1.5x" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h4 id="[Alfensa's_Request]"><span></span>[Alfensa's Request]</h4>
<p>Catari is the Warehouse Manager Daru almost next to Alfensa. Talk to him and go back to Alfensa.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/4/4a/Catari.png/211px-Catari.png" width="211" height="352" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/4/4a/Catari.png 1.5x" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p>

<h4 id="[The_Upcoming_Winter]"><span></span>[The Upcoming Winter]</h4>
<p>King tells players to ask Prince what can be done to help the colony.
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h4 id="[Return_to_Princess]"><span></span>[Return to Princess]</h4>
<p>Prince Penguin asks the players to go send word for Princess to come back home soon.
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h4 id="[A_Royal_Request]"><span></span>[A Royal Request]</h4>
<p>Prince asks players to help 3 sickly penguins around the Isle. Use the Penguin Healing Potion on the sickly ones to cure them. Sick penguins have green smoke coming from them and buff called "Able to be penguin healed".
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/b/ba/Penguinhealed.png" width="209" height="78" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/3/34/Sicklypenguin.png" width="199" height="178" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h4 id="[Trick_or...penguin?!]"><span></span>[Trick or...penguin?!]</h4>
<p>Trick 3 penguins with manure on the Isle. Use the manure on the pinguins.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/4/40/Abletobepranked.png" width="209" height="60" loading="lazy"></span>
</p>
<h4 id="[A_Dance_for_Penguins!]"><span></span>[A Dance for Penguins!]</h4>
<p>Use the drum to dance in front of the Miroir Emperor Penguins 3 times. Use the drum on the penguins.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/c/c7/Abletoperformdance.png" width="203" height="72" loading="lazy"></span>
</p>
<h4 id="[An_Audience_with_the_King]"><span></span>[An Audience with the King]</h4>
<p>Talk to the king.
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h4 id="[A_Tasty_Fish]"><span></span>[A Tasty Fish]</h4>
<p>King needs help from the players. Get 100 shrimp and use the quest item to turn the shrimp to Minced Seafood. After acquiring the Minced Seafood talk to the King.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/3/3f/Penguinknife.png" width="148" height="64" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/9/91/Shrimp.png" width="211" height="255" loading="lazy"></span>
</p>
<h4 id="[A_Tasty_Fish_2]"><span></span>[A Tasty Fish 2]</h4>
<p>Get Glacier Clayfish and mince it too. Glacier Clayfish can be fished out of the Miroir Tundra ice fishing spots or bought from AH. Talk to the King.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/3/3f/Penguinknife.png" width="148" height="64" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/3/3a/Glacierclayfish.png" width="206" height="256" loading="lazy"></span>
</p>
<h4 id="[A_Tasty_Fish_3]"><span></span>[A Tasty Fish 3]</h4>
<p>King needs the players help to catch a Bluefin Tuna and mince it like in the previous quests. Bluefin Tuna can be found around the world and fished from Tuna schools. <b>The Blue Fin Tuna has to be Fry one!</b> 
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/3/3f/Penguinknife.png" width="148" height="64" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p><p>King Penguino will reward the player with <b>Miroir Everfrost</b> that is a material to craft <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/gliders">Snowflake Wings</a>. 
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/a/a8/Miroireverfrost.png" width="208" height="171" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p>
