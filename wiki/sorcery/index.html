List Of Skills

Active Skills

Level Icon Skill Description Combos
1 Flamebolt Can be used up to 3 times in a row without triggering a cooldown.

Attack 1: Inflicts Burning for 3.2sec. Attack 3: Converts 20% of damage dealt to Enervated enemies to mana; inflicts Conflagration on Burning enemies. All 3 attacks deal +12% additional damage to targets under freezing effects. Hold down for continuous use.

-Inflicts additional +12% damage on targets under freezing effects.

-Inflicts Conflagration on Burning targets. -Returns 20% of damage dealt to Enervated targets as Mana.

3 Freezing Arrow Fires an Ice Arrow at an enemy, dealing magic damage and decreasing Move Speed for 4 sec. -Inflicts additional +15% damage on Burning targets.

-Inflicts Deep Freeze for 20 sec on targets under freezing effects. Duration reduced -50% in PvP. -Inflicts additional +34% damage on Sleeping targets. -Eliminates the cast time of Freezing Arrow for the duration of Frigid Tracks.

10 Insulating Lens Creates a protection ward around the caster.

Increases Physical Defense +1100. Absorbs up to 2049 damage before expiring. Increases stealth detection +200%.

15 Arc Lightning Fires lightning bolts to deal Magic Damage and Shock an enemy.

Deals Magic Damage over 8 sec to Shocked enemies; also Shocks all other enemies withing 2m. When this skill levels up, it increases damage, but also cast time.

-Inflicts additional +45% damage on Impaled targets.

-Inflicts additional +43% on Electric Shock targets.

20 Freezing Earth Deals damage to all enemies within 8 m with a chance to Snare for 6.2 sec.

Snare duration reduced -50% in PvP.

-Inflicts additional +26% damage on Burning targets.

-Inflicts Ice Shard on targets under freezing effects, snaring them for 4 sec. -Freezes Tripped targets, slowing them for 4 sec.

25 Searing Rain Rains down fire on all enemies in a 10-meter radius for 7 sec, dealing Magic Damage. -Inflicts additional +44% damage on targets under freezing effects.
30 Frigid Tracks Freezes your footprints for 9 sec. The ice freezes any enemies who step on it, but melts in 4 sec.
35 Magic Circle Summons a Magic Circle around the caster for 20 sec.

Increases Magic Attack and Magic Crit Rate +9% while withing the magic circle.

40 Chain Lightning Fires lightning bolts to deal Magic Damage and Shock an enemy. May also affect other enemies withing 5 m.

Can strike up to 5 times, dealing -50% less damage with each subsequent hit.

-Inflicts additional +30% damage on Impaled targets.
45 Flame Barrier Calls forth a wall of fire that deals Fire Damage and Slows for 5 sec. -Inflicts additional +41% damage on targets under freezing effects.
50 Meteor Strike Calls down a meteor, dealing Magic Damage to all enemies in the target area.

Also inflicts Tripped on all enemies hit for 2.5 seconds.

-Inflicts additional +30% damage on targets under freezing effects.
55 Gods' Whip Calls down lightning on a target area to deal Magic Damage to enemies in a 6m radius; Inflicts Electric Shock on enemies withing a 5m radius.

Can be used consecutively up to 5 times, with each attack dealing more damage but costing more mana.

Passive Skills

Required Points Icon Skill Description
2 Mana Pool Increase Increases max mana +20%.
4 Magic Range Boost Increases the range for Sorcery skills +5m.
6 Efficient Sorcery Decreases the mana cost for Sorcery skills -20%.
7 Mana Flurry Decreases Cast Time for all skills -6%.

Grants the Mana Fountain effect each time Flamebolt's third attack, Frigid Tracks or Magic Circle is used. Mana Fountain: -Increases attack speed +2% per stack. -Decreases Cast Time -2% per stack. -Decreases Sorcery Cooldowns -2% per stack. -Decreases Physical Defense -3% per stack. -Stacks up to 8 times.

8 Recuperation Decreases the duration of Sleep and Fear -10%
