Lutesong Cherry Blossom Festival

At the height of the cherry tree's blooming, when the air of Lutesong Harbor was filled with fragrant petals.

Quests

[Love in Bloom]

Talk to Mio in Lutesong Harbor.


[Keep Lutesong Pink]

Quest given by Mio. Plant Cherry Sapling. Cherry sapling can be bought from the Vocation shop. Buy one and plant it on the green areas next to the festival area. Return to Mio afterwards.


[Sweet Celebration]

Quest given by Mio. Collect 0/3 cherries and return to Mio.


[Honeybee Hunter]

Quest given by Somi. Gigantic Honeybee will spawn at certain times and needs to be taken down.



[Supply and Demand]

Quest given by Lebeto. Bring Lebeto 50 fabric.

[Cherry Rice Wine]

Quest given by Brewmaster Haryeon.

Haryeon asks you to make Cherry Rice Wine. Table to make this is the jug next to the npc.


[Wine Tasting]

Quest given by Brewmaster Haryeon.

Drink the Wine you just made!


[Name]

Quest given by Amon. Water 3 plants.


Festival Book

Origin of the Cherry Blossom Festival book

Book to place in your house can be bought with 10 of the Daru Festival tokens.
