Cosmetic Costumes & Imaging

Costume are a great way to customize the look of your character. In Archeage Classic there are wardrobe costumes that change the whole look of the character and appearance items that change only certain part of the characters appearance. These two can't be mixed together or used at the same time. Costumes are obtainable mainly through Marketplace with credits or rewards from events or achievements. Costumes can be little bit different looking depending of the played race or sex of the character. Some costumes can be dyed to customize the color of the costume.

Costumes slot

Usually players have wardrobe costumes in their costume slot. The costume slot is the top one in character profile (default keybind "C"). From the tiny wheel players can choose Character Display settings. From there the helmet can be hidden and Back Slot Display decided what items are shown. Some wardrobe costumes have two different looks meaning players can toggle between Original and Alternate looks. Wardrobe costumes can also be hidden so the player can only see their characters gear.

Image Item

Image item is a term for costumes that can be put on top of another item through Item Fusion. Usually players fuse Stat Costumes (bought from Guild shop) with the wardrobe costumes in order to get the stat and looks at the same time. All costumes need to be made into Image items by using Fusion Alembics on them once. Once the item is an Image Item it will have the tiny blue cross on the bottom left of the item icon. Without making an item into Image item it can't be used in fusion.

In the same way, other gear pieces, weapons etc. can be turned into Image items and be put on top of the gear you wish to actually use. Be careful not to make the items you wants stats from into the appearance item!
