Zodiac Festival

"Do you believe in fate? Your path is foretold by the stars, Player."

Festival title

In order to get the title from the event player must buy a Constellation Jar from Merit Badge shop with Merit Badge × 15. The Constellation Jar will give out randomly one of the twelve titles to the player. The title will stay: it can't be changed to another or player can't acquire more titles.

Compatibility of the Constellations

Only certain constellations are compatible together and players can do the sapling quest only with matching constellation. Below is the list of the compatible constellations.

Need title on to do

[Tree of Fate: CONSTELLATION NAME] In this quest player needs to find their compatible constellation player. After that both plant their saplings and use the other players sapling to make it flourish. After that player must collect Zodiac Leafs from their own sapling.

Constellation mark

After getting the title, player can touch the pink orb in the middle of the festival area and get their constellations mark above their head.

The constellation mark stay above the head only if the player has the title equipped. Once the title is changed to something else the mark disappears. The Kismet Star Shard item that is one of the rewards in the event can be placed at players house. If touched the item gives the constellation mark for the player if they have title equipped.


Constellation extra

The Boat Horn Constellation represents confusion, exploration, and illusions.

The Bow Constellation represents concentration, searching, and vengeance.

The Blind Eye Constellation represents love, sacrifice, and the unseen.

The Brier constellation represents bad luck, curses, and despair.

The Bugle Constellation represents courage, warfare, and strategy.

The Chariot Constellation represents respect, cooperation, and balance.

The Crown Constellation represents glory, authority, and lineage.

The Hut Constellation represents humility, seclusion, and peace.

The Leopard Constellation represents obstacles, enemies, and corruption.

The Trident Constellation represents prosperity, fortune, and divine assistance.

The Unicorn Constellation represents purity, solitude, and nature.

The Wand Constellation represents wisdom, scholarship, and magic.
