Tamed Mount: Mandragora

Mount
Grand
Grand Ferocious Mandragora
A screaming root that thrives on chaos and sunlight.
The Tamed Mandragora has the following skills:
Lv10: Run!Run!Mana 20Instant30sec CooldownIncreases Pet Move Speed +50% for 10sec.
Can't be used while carrying a trade pack.
Lv20: BreakthroughBreakthroughInstant1min CooldownCharges forward for 3sec, breaking through enemy lines.
Inflicts Knockback and Trip on affected enemies.
This skill counts as a Push effect.
Can't be used while carrying a trade pack.
Lv30: ChaosChaosInstant45sec CooldownSelects a hostile target, and inflicts Fear for 2 sec if the target is not receiving any damage. or OverwhelmOverwhelmInstant10sec Cooldown[Pet Skill] Overwhelm
Lv40: SlamSlamInstant15sec CooldownStrikes an enemy with a strong blow, dealing (varies) Physical Damage and inflicting Stun for 1sec.
Right-click to activate auto-use.
Lv50: PhotosynthesisPhotosynthesisInstant1min 30sec CooldownIncreases received Healing +20% for allies within 16m for 10 sec.
Cancels if the caster moves. or Vicious BiteVicious BiteInstant18sec CooldownStrikes a distant target, dealing (varies) Physical Damage and an additional (varies) damage over 14sec.
Can't be used while charging, airborne, or carrying a trade pack.
