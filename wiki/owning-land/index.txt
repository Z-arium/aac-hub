1c:T813,<p>Land has many different uses to help a player's economy. It can be used to grow materials that can be sold or crafted. It can also be used to <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/staging-packs">stage</a> trade packs for more efficient trade running. Some players even make money from simply claiming and selling land. 
</p>
<h3 id="Taxes">Taxes</h3>
<p>Owning land requires you to pay taxes each week using <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/tax-certificate">Tax Certificates</a>, and the amount of taxes you owe scales with the amount of land you own.
</p>
<h3 id="All_the_designs">All the designs</h3>
<p>There are a variety of building designs that can be used to claim land. Generally, they fall into 3 categories: <b>Scarecrows</b>, <b>Houses</b>, and <b>Farmhouses</b>. Scarecrows serve as an empty plot that you can fill with plants, animals, or other resources. Houses serve as more of a vanity option for land owning. Farmhouses are a mixture of both, but are more costly to build and take up more space.
</p><p>Designs are categorized also by their size that they take when placing. Smallest land piece that can be owned needs a 8x8 plot and biggest being 44x44. Below a list of all available designs, what materials they need to be built and what features each one has. 
</p>

<li><a href="/aac-hub/wiki/16x16">16x16</a></li>

<li><a href="/aac-hub/wiki/44x44">44x44</a></li></ul>
<h3 id="Improving_/_Remodeling_Land"><span></span>Improving / Remodeling Land</h3>
<p>There are many designs that can be improved or remodeled fore certain purposes. Scarecrows can be upgraded into pavilions to increase the plot size to 24x24, increasing the farming space and allowing for some room for furniture. Houses can be remodeled to add functionality, such as an improved crafting station or the ability to craft fertilizer packs. Farmhouses can be remodeled to add functionality, such as seed beds or a mining drill that can be harvested.
