Tamed Mount: Ant

Mount
Grand
Grand Ferocious Ant
Small, tireless, and never alone when it matters.
The Tamed Ant has the following skills:
Lv10: Run!Run!Mana 20Instant30sec CooldownIncreases Pet Move Speed +50% for 10sec.
Can't be used while carrying a trade pack.
Lv20: CamouflageCamouflageInstant1min CooldownCan't be targeted for 3sec.
You cannot turn left or right once the charge begins.
Can't use while carrying a trade pack.

Moving or being attacked will cancel this effect.

15m.
