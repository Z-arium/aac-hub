10:T107a,<p>World bosses are big mobs that players usually gather for in order to drop good loot like gear, mounts etc. Some of them are part of questlines and need to be killed / looted for ques items. Few of the Lesser Bosses an be tamed as pets.  
</p><p>World Bosses can be put in 3 categories: Major World Bosses, World Bosses and Minor World Bosses  
</p>
<h2 id="Major_World_Bosses">Major World Bosses</h2>
<p>Major World Bosses in ArcheAge are bosses that usually fit one or more of the following: 
</p>
<ul><li>Exceptionally difficult</li>
<li>High value loot</li>
<li>Contested over in PvP on practically every occasion</li>
<li>Have static spawn times to encourage grouping up and preparing for them</li></ul>
