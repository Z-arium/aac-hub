World Bosses

World bosses are big mobs that players usually gather for in order to drop good loot like gear, mounts etc. Some of them are part of questlines and need to be killed / looted for ques items. Few of the Lesser Bosses an be tamed as pets.

World Bosses can be put in 3 categories: Major World Bosses, World Bosses and Minor World Bosses

Major World Bosses

Major World Bosses in ArcheAge are bosses that usually fit one or more of the following:

  • Exceptionally difficult
  • High value loot
  • Contested over in PvP on practically every occasion
  • Have static spawn times to encourage grouping up and preparing for them
Major World Bosses
Name Level Location Description
Kraken 50 West of Arcadian Sea A giant squid. As an ocean boss, it only receives damage from ships.
Red Dragon 50 Karkasse Ridgelands With multiple flying and ground phases, it requires a lot of preparation.
Leviathan 55 East of Arcadian Sea A boss that must be followed in the ocean and is a difficult naval battle
Hanure the Berserker 55 Hasla Classic-made Boss dropping a new set of weapons. Improved version of Hanure the Hunter
Thunderwing Titan 55 Reedwind Colossal Titan, which can be summoned in Reedwind.
Anthalon 50 Sungold Fields Spawns after Sungold CR. Mechanically simple, it's loot is very good on mage tanks.
Delphinad Ghost Ship 50 Arcadian Sea, multiple A giant Ghostship, that provides a challange every day for ships.

Spawn on a timer basis after each kill. Loot is not major, but can sometimes be fought over regardless.
