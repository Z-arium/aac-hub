Tamed Mount: Crayfish

Mount
Grand
Grand Ferocious Crayfish
Armored and angry, this crayfish pinches until nothing is left standing.
The Tamed Crayfish has the following skills:
Lv10: Run!Run!Instant30sec CooldownIncreases Move Speed +80%, then gradually decreases it over 10sec.
Can't be used while carrying a trade pack.
Lv20: Wild ChargeWild ChargeInstant30sec CooldownCharges forward for 3sec, breaking through enemy lines.
Inflicts Knockback and Trip on affected enemies.
This skill counts as a Push effect.
Can't be used while carrying a trade pack.

Lv40: Sound of HorrorSound of HorrorInstant30sec CooldownCreates a creepy sound, using its claws, threatening enemies within 4m.

Can't be used while under the effect of Run! or Wild Charge. or SlamSlamInstant45sec CooldownSlams its claws into the ground to inflict Melee Damage to nearby enemies.

Can't be used while under the effect of Run! or Wild Charge.
