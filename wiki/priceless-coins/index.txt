18:T829,<p>Priceless Coins are rare given currency the players can use to buy 30-day items, <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/priceless-coin-mounts">Mounts</a> and <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/gliders">Gliders</a>, which are possible to be upgraded into permanent one with <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/currencies">Activity Tokens</a>.
</p><p>Currently 6.2.2026 there are <b>3</b> obtainable coins in the game. 
</p><p><b>5</b> total for players who claimed the 1st and 2nd anniversary coins. 
</p>

<h2 id="1st_coin">1st coin</h2>
<p>Outside of any Mirage Portal is a Auctioneer Daru NPC. This NPC has a quest that gives the first coin.
<span><img src="/aac-hub/wiki-files/46/46083e539ed2aaa7972acf3e3d7e76f77ae7b2a9.png" width="350" height="413" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h2 id="2nd_coin">2nd coin</h2>
<p>Second coins is given by the achievement called "<b>Harvest Festival Participant</b>". Meaning player has to take part in the custom event 5 times to get the achievement. This achievement can be found under General &gt; Misc.
<span><img src="/aac-hub/wiki-files/67/67b5e61d240c36d2deec2f66387132fb5084963b.png" width="462" height="262" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h2 id="3rd_coin">3rd coin</h2>
<p>Third Priceless coin was a reward from the web page for 1st year anniversary of Archeage Classic.
</p><p><b>LIMITED TIME ONLY, NO LONGER AVAILABLE</b>
</p>
<h2 id="4th_coin">4th coin</h2>
<p>Fourth Priceless coin was a reward for the 2nd year anniversary of Archeage Classic.
</p><p>Talk to the Auctioneer Daru NPC in the main cities. He will give a quest to go to Mirage Island to talk to Daru Priest who will give the coin as reward.
</p><p><b>LIMITED TIME ONLY, NO LONGER AVAILABLE</b>
</p>
<h2 id="5th_coin">5th coin</h2>
<p>Fifth coin can be obtained from the new <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/adventurers-guide">Adventurer's Guide</a> tab. After completing Arcane grade missions the reward chest gives one Priceless Coin.
</p>

Currently 6.2.2026 there are '''3''' obtainable coins in the game. 

'''5''' total for players who claimed the 1st and 2nd anniversary coins. 

== 1st coin ==
Outside of any Mirage Portal is a Auctioneer Daru NPC. This NPC has a quest that gives the first coin.
[[File:Auctioneer.png]]


== 2nd coin ==
Second coins is given by the achievement called "'''Harvest Festival Participant'''". Meaning player has to take part in the custom event 5 times to get the achievement. This achievement can be found under General > Misc.
[[File:Harvestachievement.png]]


== 3rd coin ==
Third Priceless coin was a reward from the web page for 1st year anniversary of Archeage Classic.

'''LIMITED TIME ONLY, NO LONGER AVAILABLE'''

== 4th coin ==
Fourth Priceless coin was a reward for the 2nd year anniversary of Archeage Classic.

Talk to the Auctioneer Daru NPC in the main cities. He will give a quest to go to Mirage Island to talk to Daru Priest who will give the coin as reward.

'''LIMITED TIME ONLY, NO LONGER AVAILABLE'''

== 5th coin ==
Fifth coin can be obtained from the new [[Adventurer's Guide]] tab. After completing Arcane grade missions the reward chest gives one Priceless Coin.
