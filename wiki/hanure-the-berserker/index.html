Hanure the Berserker

Hanure the Berserker 💜

Hanure the Berserker is a relentless melee boss built around raw aggression and pressure. Found in AA Classic, this boss has a range of mechanics that can make it difficult to kill it contested. it's up for two hours ( check AAC webpage for timers )

Abilities & Keywords

Powerful Overwhelm

This ability is triggered when the tank / target of the boss is out of melee range ( it then leaps to the target)

Berserker’s Ruins

Debris falling down from the air stunning players and healing hanure ( per enemy hit )

Sharp Breath

A focused, cutting exhale of compressed force aimed in a frontal cone. only tank should be infront of this boss.

Heavenly Smash

Hanure leaps or channels his strength into a devastating overhead strike, dealing massive area damage on impact. This is his signature burst ability and often signals a high-damage phase. Players should watch for the wind-up animation and move quickly to avoid lethal hits. if you have a cleanse or purge look to help people hit.

Boss Traits

  • High physical burst damage
  • Strong anti-cluster mechanics
  • Tank pressure and sustain checks
  • Positional awareness required
  • Rage-themed melee bruiser
