Event mounts

Event mounts are those that can be obtained by doing the events to exchange them for coins or through achievements inside events.


Candy-fueled Bestcargot

Candy-fueled Bestcargot was an achievement reward for Hallowtide Festival event. Once you completed all event quests 5 times you would get achievement called "Jack of All Lanterns" and the mount.

Mount
Basic
Candy-fueled Bestcargot
Empowered by the Purple Pumpkin Candies, Bestcargot can now climb hills faster than any other snail.
Candy-Fueled Bestcargot has the following skills:
Pumpkin Candy PowerPumpkin Candy PowerMana 20Instant5sec CooldownTriggers an instant dash forward.
Buck Up, FriendsBuck Up, FriendsMana 70Instant10sec CooldownPropels all ally snails within 5m forward.
Lv20: Launch OwnerLaunch OwnerInstant20sec CooldownLaunches the rider forward. Can't be used if the rider is carrying a trade pack.

Summons a mount.
Base Move Speed 5m/s

Carrot Wings

Carrot Wings was obtainable during the Rum Runner event and costs Daru Festival Coin × 47.

Mount
Heroic
Carrot Wings
An unfortunate magical accident has gifted this Carrot Dash with a pair of stubby little wings. While the effects left the poor creature a bit… strange, its speed is without question. And the limited bursts of flight can prove quite useful to a skilled rider.
This mount has the following skills:
Lv20: Dreaming DonkeyDreaming DonkeyInstant1min CooldownIncreases the donkey's Move Speed +30% for 1min.
Can only be used while wearing a trade pack and consumes 1 Carrot.
Lv20: La Mancha's CallLa Mancha's CallInstant1min CooldownRaise your spear high and charge forward for 7sec.
You cannot turn left or right once the charge begins.
Can't be used while carrying a trade pack.
Lv20: Powerful WingbeatsPowerful WingbeatsMana 180Instant10sec CooldownEncourage Carrot Wings to fly.

Summons a mount.
Base Move Speed 8m/s

Cyanfin

Cyanfin was obtainable during the Rum Runner event and costs Daru Festival Coin × 36.

Mount
Grand
Cyanfin
It's said Dahuta, the Goddess of the Sea, created this species in a rare moment of tenderness, simply to enjoy their beauty. Others claim she utilizes their speed by harnessing them to her personal chariot.
The Cyanfin has the following skills:
Scan ShipsScan ShipsInstant1min CooldownDisplays all ships and drydocks within 100m for 10 seconds.
Dolphin DashDolphin DashInstant25sec CooldownTriggers a forward dash with powerful tailfin strokes.
Increases Move Speed +50% for 10 seconds.
Can't be used while doing tricks.
Produce Ultrasonic WavesProduce Ultrasonic WavesInstant3sec CooldownSends ultrasonic waves forward.
AcrobaticsAcrobaticsMana 180Instant10sec CooldownUtilizes the fins to launch a powerful leap. May trigger different tricks depending on the dolphin's mood.
