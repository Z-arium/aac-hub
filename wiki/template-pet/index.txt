Shows an in-game style tooltip for a pet (Pets, Powerstone Pets, Battle Pets). The skills in the description are hoverable and show their details, like the skills of mounts and gliders. Normally you use [[Template:Tooltip]], which picks the right card for any item ID.

'''Usage'''
<pre>{{Pet|56039}}</pre>

* The only required parameter is the item ID of the pet (the item, not the NPC).
* The data is read from data pages stored on the wiki (<code>Module:Data/Pets/...</code> and <code>Module:Data/Skills/...</code>, made by <code>sync_wiki_data.py</code>). A pet that is not stored yet is shown as a plain card ([[Template:Consumable]], read live) without the skill boxes until the script has been run for it.
* float (optional): <code>right</code> or <code>left</code> places the card at that side with the text flowing around it, <code>center</code> centres it.
* use (optional): replaces the "Use:" text.
* Needs <code>Module:Pet</code>, <code>Module:Consumable</code>, <code>Module:Mount</code> (for the hover box), <code>Module:DataStore</code>, <code>Template:Consumable</code>, <code>Template:Consumable/styles.css</code> and <code>Template:Mount/styles.css</code>.

'''Examples'''
<pre>{{Pet|56039}}
{{Pet|8001817|float=right}}</pre>
