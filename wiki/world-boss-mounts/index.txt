=== Earthen Roar / Crimson Lightning ===
[[File:Earthenroar.png|Earthen Roar|alt=Earthen Roar|frame]]

Earthen Roar is a drop from [[World Bosses|World Boss]] called [[Hanure the Hunter|Hanure]]. Once you obtain the mount it is ready to use.

There is a quest to upgrade Earthen Roar into Crimson Lightning. Collect 10 Crimson Lightning Essence Fragments and combined them to Crimson Lightning Essence and use the item on the mount to upgrade it.
