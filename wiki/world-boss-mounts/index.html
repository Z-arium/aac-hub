World Boss mounts

Mounts that are a drop from world bosses or dungeon bosses.


Earthen Roar / Crimson Lightning

Earthen Roar is a drop from World Boss called Hanure. Once you obtain the mount it is ready to use.

There is a quest to upgrade Earthen Roar into Crimson Lightning. Collect 10 Crimson Lightning Essence Fragments and combined them to Crimson Lightning Essence and use the item on the mount to upgrade it.

Base movement speed: 10.0m/s

Mount skills
Skill Icon Description Additional info
Run! Increases Pet Move Speed +50% for 10sec.

Can't be used while carrying trade pack.

-
Gnaw Sherpens caster's bite, dealing Physical Damage and as additional damage over time Combo: Increases severity of Bleed effects on targets.
Overrun Triggers a charge that can Stun a distant enemy for 2sec.

Can't be used while carrying trade pack.

-
Health Regen Heals pet's Health. -
Dash Triggers a forward charge for 3sec that makes you invincible.

Can't be used while carrying trade pack.

-
Item Icon Cost
Crimson Lightning Pet Helm 58 gold
Crimson Lightning Mount Saddle 200 gold
Crimson Lightning Pet Legguards 142 gold

Once Earthen Roar upgrades into Crimson Lightning the mount will become 1 rider mount and gets 1min long stealth skill. The mount also has its own unique armor set that can be bought from Stablehand NPC.




Fuschiafin

Obtainable as a rare drop from Dahuta, the boss of the Sea of Drowned Love dungeon (10-man version) accessible underwater in the Sunspeck Sea.

Base movement speed: 7.5m/s

Mount skills
Skill Icon Description Additional info
Scan Ships Displays all ships and drydocks within 100m for 10 seconds. -
Dolphin Dash Triggers a forward dash with powerful tailfin strokes. Increases Move Speed +50% for 10 seconds.

Can't be used while doing tricks.

-
Produce Ultrasonic Waves Sends ultrasonic waves forward. -
Acrobatics Utilizes the fins to launch a powerful leap. May trigger different tricks depending on the dolphin's mood.

Can't be used while carrying trade pack.
