=== Castle Sieges/Bids Returning ===
On October 18th 2023 - All six territories will have their Daru Villages demolished and guilds in the top 20 will be allowed to bid on them again.

Winners of these siege bids WILL NOT keep their peace funds. Do not spend it. Do not Lose it or you will be giving your guild in exchange for it.

So Guilds in the top 20 This week will receive mails of their eligibility to bid. 

=== Changes ===

* Siege Cycles will now be every 3 weeks instead of 2 weeks
* Defenders will now start with 80 Players
* The cost of starting a Territory Fortress Workbench will be reduced to 45 Lord Coins instead of 90 Lord Coins
* Siege bidding cycles are going to be moved up by 24 hours
** Bids will now start Tuesday instead of Wednesday and end Thursday instead of Friday
** Sieges will now take place Saturday instead of Sunday.

=== Bidding Rules ===

* Owners of a siege declaration scroll CANNOT change guilds. Will be punished if they do so anyways by any means.
* A guild (as its entire entity) can only own one castle and cannot possess a siege scroll.
* A guild cannot use a shell/alt guild for ANY PURPOSE involving sieges.
* If a siege scroll is used to declare, the declaring guild MUST be intending to siege. Allied guilds may bid on scrolls to prevent a castle from being sieged by winning a scroll and NOT declaring (this will result in no peace fund for the territory owner that cycle).
* If a sieging guild wins multiple scrolls, they can only claim one territory.
