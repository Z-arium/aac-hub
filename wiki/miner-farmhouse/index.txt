10:T8de,<p>The Miner Farmhouse is a <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Thatched_Farmhouse&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">thatched farmhouse</a> that has been upgraded through the <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Remodeling&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">remodel</a> menu. The upgrade adds 2 <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Seed_Beds&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">seed beds</a> to the farmhouse and a mining drill, in addition to changing the look of the farmhouse.
</p>
<h3 id="Mining_Drill">Mining Drill</h3>
<p>The mining drill will either be normal or lucky, and then will proceed through 3 phases. Each phase will take additional time to accumulate and will result in larger drops. To use a farmhouse's mining drill efficiently, it's recommended to harvest normal procs immediately and let the lucky procs fully accumulate before harvesting. Both procs will drop a combination of iron ore, raw stone, and an additional lucky drop when harvesting a lucky drill.
</p><p><i>The following rates are community gathered and may not be perfectly accurate</i>
