Witchcraft

List of skills

Level Icon Skill Description Combos
1 Earthen Grip Calls forth a hand from the ground to hold an enemy, inflicting Snare for 17sec. Deals Magic Damage on expiration. Being damaged by a direct attack will free the target early, but debuffs that deal damage over time will not end this effect. Snare duration reduced -50% in PvP. Inflicts magic damage on Snared targets.

Deals magic damage to Enervated targets and returns it as health to caster.

3 Enervate Deals Magic Damage and burns mana over 5sec. Decreases healing effectiveness -60% and increases duration of Stun and Trip +30%. Inflicts +55% damage on Feared targets.

Inflicts damage over time on Stunned targets.

10 Bubble Trap Traps an enemy inside a bubble for 18.8sec and slowly lifts into the air. Enemies in bubbles can't attack, but take -50% less Magic Damage. Being damaged by a direct attack will free the target early, but debuffs that deal damage over time will not end this effect. Bubble duration reduced -50% in PvP. Inflicts magic damage to Electric Shock targets.

Burning targets are thrown higher.

15 Insidious Whisper Inflicts Fear on an enemy and causes it to flee from the caster for 1.5sec. Being damaged by a direct attacks has a chance to free the target early, but debuffs that deal damage over time will not end this effect. Increases duration of Fear 1.5seconds on Burning targets.
20 Purge Allies: Removes 1 negative effect. Increases Magic Defense +1600 for 3min.

Enemies: Removes 1 buff.

Shackled targets are Silenced for 4 sec.

Silenced targets are Shackled for 4sec.

25 Play Dead Allows caster to play dead to escape danger. Cast can revive at any time. Restores 1% of the caster's health and mana every second if they are below 30%. Effect ends with movement. -
30 Courageous Action Awakens caster or an ally and grants immunity to Sleep for 1m. -
35 Lassitude Magically fatigues the target, causing them to fall asleep for 12sec after 4sec delay. Enemies will wake upon taking damage. Sleep duration reduced -50% in PvP. Increases duration of Sleep +10 seconds on targets under freezing effects.
40 Banshee Wail Causes Fear in all nearby enemies for 4.3sec, forcing them to flee and Stunning them for 2seconds. Being damaged by a direct attacks has a chance to free the target early, but debuffs that deal damage over time will not end this effect. Increases the duration of Fear +2 seconds on Burning targets.
45 Focal Concussion Deals Magic Damage and Stuns an enemy for 1.5sec. If the target has any positive buffs, Focal Concussion removes one buff and deals additional damage when Stun ends. -
50 Dahuta's Breath Sends forth a wave that deals Magic Damage and pushes enemies back. Inflicts additional +46% damage on Electric Shock targets.
55 Fiend's Knell Summons a fiend for up to 30 seconds that relentlessly attacks enemies with special skills. Summons only one demon, which can't be healed. -

4 Illusion's Favor Increases Health Regen +7 every 1 second. Decreases Cast Time for Earthen Grip and Insidious Whisper -0.5 seconds.
6 Magic Detection Increases Magic Defense +500.
7 Mitigation Decreases received Melee damage -30% for 3 seconds after receiving a Melee Critical Hit. This ability has a 20 seconds cooldown once triggered.
8 Baleful Recharge Your successful attacks have a 2% chance to trigger the Baleful Recharge buff, restoring a percentage of your maximum mana with every attacks for the following 5 seconds. This ability has a 12 sec cooldown once triggered. In addition, each enemy you Fear using Banshee's Wail or Insidious Whisper grants a stack of Exploitation.

Eploitation:

  • Decreases the Cooldown of Focal Concussion and Enervate -10% per stack.
  • Stacks up to 5 times.
  • Casting either spell consumes one stack.
  • While under the effects of Exploitation, both spells will deal 90% of their damage as mana burn instead of normal damage, restoring and equal amount of mana to you.
9 Augment Witchcraft Increases the duration of Earthen Grip, Bubble Trap, Insidious Whisper, Lassitude, and Banshee Wail +20%. Fiend's Knell now summons a more powerful fiend that last for 5 minutes, tripping enemies where it is summoned. Increases the cooldown on Fiend's Knell to 2m 30sec.
10 Enshroud Decreases Witchcraft skill cooldown -20%.
