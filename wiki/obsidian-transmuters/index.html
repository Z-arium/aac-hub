Obsidian Transmuters

Obsidian Transmuters are a new feature introduced in Patch 1.3.5, allowing players to transform one piece of Obsidian Equipment into another of the same slot. This system provides greater flexibility in gear progression and enables players to adjust their equipment to better suit their needs without starting from scratch.

Overview

The Daru, ever the pioneers of mysterious technology, have been experimenting with the nature of Obsidian Equipment. After much research (and an undisclosed number of lab explosions), they have devised a method to reshape these powerful items. However, their work conditions have led to unrest, as the Daru have begun threatening to unionize, refusing to reveal the crafting process until they receive proper compensation—specifically, Gilda-Starry-Stars.

Despite this labor dispute, transmuters have been successfully introduced, and adventurers can begin using them immediately to alter their gear.

How does transmuters work?

There are different seals that can be crafted from the Alchemy proficiency in order to seal different tier of weapons or armors.

The 'lesser' seals can be used to seal Tier 6 Obsidian weapons/armors (Divine, Epic and Legendary)

The 'brimstone' seals can be used to seal Tier 7 Obsidian weapons/armors (Epic)

The 'greater brimstone' seals can be used to seal Tier 7 Obsidian weapons/armors (Legendary and Mythic)

I sealed my equipment, then what?

Under 'Weaponry' proficiency craft recipes, you can find the 'Archeum Transmutation' section. There, you can use the sealed shard you obtained, and using some extra materials you can get your desired weapon or armor.
