=== Location ===
Event is located in White Arden, ''Ardenia'', Nuia side.

=== Quests ===

==== [The Ghost Wedding] ====
Arden Guard Finis starts the questline and he asks the player to talk to Delmin closer to the event area.

[[File:Ardeniaguard.png]]


==== [Hauntingly Beautiful] ====
Delmin needs 3 Hereaftter Roses that can be picked up close at nearby tree. Gather tree and go back to Delmin.

[[File:Delmin.png]]

[[File:Hereafterroses.png]]


==== [A Good Cause] ====
Delmin gives another quest where she wants the player to craft an Donation Envelope ('''click envelope icon''') and donating it into the box next to her. After donating go back to her. 

[[File:Delmin.png]]

[[File:Envelope.png]]

[[File:Donationbox.png]]

After donating the envelope Delmin will reward the player with the Guardian Token that can be used on the Groom and Pride to receive a Wedding Buff.

[[File:Guardianstoken.png]]

[[File:Weddingbuff.png]]

Receive buff by going close to the NPC:s and click F with the Guardian Token in the inventory. The buff lasts for 60min.

[[File:Weddingbessing.png]]


==== [The Wedding Crasher] ====
Arden Guard Musician gives a quest to drive away a singing bard and investigate Suspicious Pirate Plushie. 

The Singing Bard appears at certain hours where players need to drive him away. After he is gone there will be the Plushie spawning that need to be investigated so the quest is completed.

''For the EU region, the Singing Bard appears at 2pm, 7pm and 12am UTC.''

''For the NA region, the Singing Bard appears at 7pm, 12am and 5am UTC.''


==== [Stranded in White Arden] ====
Eldest Princess Esana gives a quest and asks for '''''3 BOUND''''' Hereafter stones so she can teleport back to Haranya. 

[[File:Eldestprincessesana.png]]

==== [Till Death Do us Part] ====
[[File:Dias.png]]

Dias needs help with crafting the wedding rings. Had to the Festival Gift Exchanger. Craft Wedding Ring with '''''3 BOUND''''' Hereafter stones. After crafting go back to him.
