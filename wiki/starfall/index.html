Starfall Questline

Start the questline by talking to the NPC Iseren at any comet event. You should see a in-game popup that the event is starting with the zone that it is landing at.

Go to the 1st floor of the library for Elloy

Go back to Iseren and interact with the telescope.

Talk to Iseren interact with the telescope again until its centered

Talk to Iseren use the observe function of the telescope

Talk to Iseren accept rift quests

List of quests:

  • elemental rift 1 fire sprite 0/25
  • rift 2 flame elemental 0/15
  • rift 3 flame guardian boss defeat the flame guardian!
  • like cr scorched earth Kill elemental rift elementals 0/10 kills
  • meteor miner mine meteor 0/1
  • crystalized clues collect elemental shards by defeating flame elementals the eternal flame
  • Defeat the Undying Flame!

continue of story:

  • the study of stars! Report back to Iseren in 1st floor of the library and you will complete it.
