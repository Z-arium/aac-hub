Starter Pets

Back to Battle Pets

Starter Pets are categorized as pets that can be bought near stablehands in various community centers around the world of ArcheAge. They are each purchased for 10 silver each and are offered in 3 different variations.

These pets and their skills are listed below.

Sabrefang

The sabrefang can be obtained from Jaun's Ranch in Villanelle. Next to the Stablehand, "Butcher Umei" it is offered in 3 variations; Tabby, Striped and Calico.

Pet Skills
Icon Skill Level Description
Smack 25 Attack dealing x Physical Damage with a chance to inflict stun for 2sec.
Health Regen 25 Restores x amount of pet's health.
Reflexes 30 Increases Critical and Evasion Rates of the pet by +30% for 10 sec.
Strong Claws 35 Increases the Attack of the pet by 40% for 10sec.
Scratch 40 Attack dealing x Physical Damage, Inflicting a 14sec bleed dealing x damage.

Combo Effect: If target is bleeding, Increases the severity of Bleed effect.

Wolfhound

The Wolfhound can be obtained from the Marianople Community Center, "Dawnsliver" and is available in 3 different variations; Black, White and Brown.

Pet Skills
Icon Skill Level Description
Scratch 25 Basic Attack dealing x additional Physical Damage.
Health Regen 25 Restores x amount of pet's health.
Berserk 30 Pet gains 1000 Attack Speed for 12sec.
Overrun 35 Pet dashes into an enemy dealing x Physical Damage, Tripping them for 2sec.
Guard Dog 40 PvE Only Pet taunts an enemy forcing it to attack the pet.

Mini-Miner

The Mini-Miner is obtainable in the Airain Rock Community Center, "Andelph" Next to Engineer Charlotte, It is available in 3 variations; Ruby,

Pet Skills
Icon Skill Level Description
Strong Blow 25 Attacks an enemy dealing x Physical Damage.

Combo Effect: Bonus damage to bleeding targets.

Self-Recovery 25 Restores x amount of pet's health over 3sec.
Engine Overload 30 Increases pet's Ranged, Magic, and Melee Power by +30 and increases Attack Speed by +516 for 10sec.
Driller 40 Fires a ranged attack dealing x Physical Damage and Slowing its target for 3sec.
That's MINE! 50 Fires a ranged attack dealing x Magical Damage for 3sec.

Triggers "Low Power" debuff preventing all other skills from being used for the duration of the debuff.

Thornboar

The Thornboar is obtainable in the Sunbite Wilds Communal Ranch, it is obtainable in 3 variations; Hedgealope, Leafcrusher and Steelspine.

Pet Skills
Icon Skill Level Description
Gnaw 25 Attack dealing x amount of Physical Damage and causing the target to bleed for x damage over 14sec.
Recover Health 25 Recovers x amount of pet's health.
Crouch 30 Buffs Pet returning 80% of received Physical/Ranged damage for 10sec.
Kick Up Dirt 35 Ranged attack dealing x Ranged Damage and blinding the target.

Blinded target has reduced Magical/Physical/Ranged Accuracy for 3sec.

Body Slam 50 PvP/PvE Dashes to target dealing x Physical Damage and taunting it.

Black Sabrefang

Different from the normal sabrefang pet, the Black Sabrefang is a pet obtainable in Mirage Isle for 5 Gilda. It is only obtainable in 1 variation.

Pet Skills
Icon Skill Level Description
Cat Steps 25 Increases movement speed of the Caster and Pet by 5% for 60sec.

If the Caster is carrying a Trade pack this bonus is increased to 30% increased movement speed.
