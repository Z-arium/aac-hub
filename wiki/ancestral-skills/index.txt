12:T5781,<p>Ancestral Skills refers to a system added in the ArcheAge Classic 1.3 Patch (also known as 3.5 on Retail)
</p><p>They consist of offering players the ability to change the 'Element' of a skill, giving it different effects. To use Ancestral Skills you must first reach Ancestral Levels
</p>

<h2 id="Ancestral_Levels">Ancestral Levels</h2>
<p>There are currently 7 Ancestral Levels. The following table details the amount of experience needed per level, as well as extra items required per level.
</p><p>Ancestral Skills are unlocked at levels 1, 4 and 7 respectively

16:T1935,Ancestral Skills refers to a system added in the ArcheAge Classic 1.3 Patch (also known as 3.5 on Retail)

They consist of offering players the ability to change the 'Element' of a skill, giving it different effects. To use Ancestral Skills you must first reach Ancestral Levels

== Ancestral Levels ==
There are currently 7 Ancestral Levels. The following table details the amount of experience needed per level, as well as extra items required per level.

Ancestral Skills are unlocked at levels 1, 4 and 7 respectively
