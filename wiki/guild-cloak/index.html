Guild Cloak

The Guild Cloak, also commonly referred to as a Prestige Cloak, is a piece of Equipment that can be purchased from the Prestige Shop using Prestige or by completing the first section (Grand) of the Adventurer's Guide.

Guild Cloaks are one of the most accessible pieces of equipment available to players. They can be obtained relatively early and upgraded over time through Synthesis.

Obtaining a Guild Cloak

Upon completing the first set of tasks in the Adventurer's Guide you will receive a box that contains a Guild Cloak, be sure to select which one you want carefully!

To obtain more Guild Cloaks, the player must first be a member of a Guild.

Players earn Prestige by completing Guild Missions and certain quests.

Once 150 Prestige has been accumulated, a Guild Cloak can be purchased from the Prestige Shop.

Choosing a Cloak

Several different Guild Cloaks are available. Each cloak provides a different effect, allowing players to choose one that complements their class and playstyle. Once upgraded to Delphinad these cloaks branch out even further giving you 2 options of stats per cloak, I'll show these in the table as "Honor ver." and "Flaming ver.".

Upgrading a Guild Cloak

Guild Cloaks are upgraded using Synthesis. Synthesis increases the cloak's experience and allows it to advance through higher Grades.

Synthesis

Open your Inventory. Open the "Regrade Item" window. Select the Synthesis option. Place the Guild Cloak into the "Base Costume" slot. Add the required Synthesis Materials. Confirm the synthesis.

Each synthesis grants experience to the cloak. Once enough experience has been accumulated, the cloak advances to the next grade.

As the cloak's grade increases, its effects become more powerful.

Synthesis Materials

Guild Cloaks require specific Synthesis Materials to be upgraded.

These materials can be obtained primarily through killing monsters in Diamond Shores after your faction has killed the Alemine Fortress which purifies the zone for a few days.

You can also obtain cloak synthesis materials from killing monsters in other purified areas in Auroria called "Auroria Purification Zones" and you can find these in Sungold Fields, Exeloch, Calmlands, Marcala, Nuimari and Heedmar. Beware though as depending on who's faction purified the zone there will be a tower at the center of the area that will kill anyone not of the same faction.

The materials required and the amount of Synthesis experience provided depend on the grade of the synthesis material.

Synthesis Material Experience

Different synthesis materials provide different amounts of Synthesis EXP when used to upgrade a cloak. The amount of EXP provided depends on the material's grade.

Auroran Synthesis Shards are available in Basic, Grand and Rare grades, while Auroran Synthesis Stones are available in Arcane, Heroic and Unique grades.

Tiering it up

In order to tier a cloak up to its next tier you need to have reached the max grade of your current tier, whether you reach 0% or 100% EXP at max grade does not have any impact on the next tier. Once that has been completed you just need some materials to tier your cloak up which will reset the stats it has, make the grade go back down to its lowest possible grade and reset the EXP. This means that until you reach Erenor cloak you don't have to be super min-maxxy with stats, just use the stats you are able to get on it.

Epherium Divine
Delphinad Epic

Rolling for different stats

Important: Each grade increase provides one opportunity to replace one existing stat. If a single synthesis provides enough EXP to increase the cloak by multiple grades, only one of those grade-up rerolls is available. To maximize the number of reroll opportunities, avoid providing enough EXP to skip a grade.

By pressing the magnifying glass you are able to see which stats are in the pool of the next grade when you are in the Synthesis tab. If you switch over to the Replace Effects tab you will see the stats that are in the pool of your current grade. DO NOT mix these two up when you are rolling for stats as that can lead to unwanted outcomes.

Cryptic Lucky Scrolls can be used to replace a single stat on Ayanad or higher cloaks with a new random one from the stat pool. You can find the entire pool of stats you can get with the Cryptic Lucky Scroll by pressing the magnifying glass in the "Replace Effects" tab of the Synthesis window.

Number of Stat Slots

As you upgrade a cloak through its grades, it gains additional stats.

  • Epherium to Ayanad Cloaks: Start with 1 stat at the lowest grades and gradually gain up to 3 stats at the highest grade.
  • Erenor Cloaks: Follow the same progression, but gain a 4th stat upon reaching Mythic grade.

In other words the cloak's grade determines how many stats it has, with higher grades providing additional stats.

Grade Epherium Delphinad Ayanad Erenor
Basic – Arcane 1 1 1 1
Heroic – Celestial 2 2 2 2
Divine – Legendary 3 3 3 3
Mythic — — — 4

Changing to a different cloak

Currently there is only one way to change your cloak into a different cloak and that is with an item called Ipnysh Cloakmuter. It can only be used on Erenor cloaks but lets you change your cloak to any of the other cloaks. This process resets the grade, stats and EXP of the cloak so you will have to get back on the Synthesis grind in order to make it Mythic again.

Why Get a Guild Cloak?

The Guild Cloak provides an accessible way for players to obtain significant amounts of useful stats through spending time grinding rather than spending a lot of gold and resources.

Because the cloak can be upgraded through synthesis and tiered up multiple times, you can continue investing into the same item as your character progresses.
