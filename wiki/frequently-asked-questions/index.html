Frequently Asked Questions

Frequently Asked Questions (AAC)

This page answers common questions for **ArcheAge Classic (AAC)** players.

Note: AAC is based on the classic **3.5-era** ArcheAge with custom changes. See: Getting Started and Custom Server Changes.

Server basics

What are the server rates?
Commonly referenced rates: **XP x2**, **Vocation x2**, **Honor (PvP) x4**, **Honor (PvE/Quests) x2**.
Do I need Patron?
No. AAC has **free Patron benefits for all players**.
How does Labor work?
Labor regenerates **online and offline**. Commonly referenced values: **40 labor / 5 minutes**, max **10,000**.

Leveling / early progression

What’s the fastest way to level?
Follow **green story quests**. Most players skip/ignore most **yellow quests** unless they specifically need them.
What is a Growthstone?
An **EXP consumable**. Use it directly from your inventory.

Catch-up gear / gearing

When do I get catch-up gear?
At **level 50**, you receive a **Catch-up Gear Box**.
What should I do right after I open the catch-up box?
  • Don’t stress perfect min/max immediately.
  • Early PvP survivability gems people commonly recommend: **Toughness (pants)** + **Resilience (chest)**.
  • Use Activity Tokens to start the upgrade quest at a **Daru Auctioneer** (Mirage portal area), then use the **Soulforged anvil (Diamond Shores)**.
Is it better to regrade myself or buy finished pieces?
Often, buying a finished piece is cheaper than eating RNG. Dungeon gear is generally an easier regrade target than some crafted/obsidian paths.

Emerald / Hasla weapons (AAC)

What are Emerald weapons?
A custom starter weapon line. Upgrading is **safe (synthesis)**, not regrade RNG.
How do I get an Emerald weapon?
  1. Bring a **normal Emerald**.
  2. Go to Hasla → **Tehmi Ruins / Abandoned Drill Camp**.
  3. Talk to **Fragment Researcher Soa** → trade Emerald for **Infused Emerald**.
  4. Use the bench behind Soa to craft the weapon.
For upgrading Emerald (Hasla) weapons, does the fodder weapon have to match weapon type?
**No.** It does not have to be the same weapon type.

Activity Tokens

What are Activity Tokens?
A server-specific progression currency used mainly on **Mirage Isle**.
Where do I get token quests?
Most are picked up from **Shadow Daru** in each main capital.
What are common token sources?
Weekly quests (Fri/Sat/Sun resets) + daily merit-style token quest. See Activity Tokens.

Trade packs / charcoal

How do I get Charcoal Stabilizer?
Deliver a **trade pack cross-continent** to a **Resource Trader** (West-made packs turned in East, or vice-versa). Common examples players mention: **Solis / Villanelle / Ynystere**.

Housing / land

Where do I get the free 8x8 + 16x16 farm?
From the **Blue Salt Brotherhood land questline**. Video guide: https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PBCb4NqijDA
I turned in the pack but can’t find the next 16x16 quest NPC.
  • **WEST:** talk to **Misty** on the Crescent Throne docks (Solzreed).
  • **EAST:** talk to **Jasmine** on the Austera docks (Solis).

Fishing

Do I need lures for ocean sportfishing?
Depends on your **rod**. When you try sportfishing it should tell you "Not enough X lure".
Where do I buy lures?
Fishing boats have a small **fish bucket** you can interact with to buy lures.
What’s the difference between Lures and Chum?
  • **Lures** are used **per cast**.
  • **Chum** is **Chopped Fish Bucket** used to **activate a fishing pool**.

Common issues / troubleshooting

My settings reset every time I close the game.
Try: go to **Character Select** before exiting. If it still happens, rename `Documents\\AAClassic` to `AAClassicOLD` to force the client to recreate settings.
Settings reset after a crash.
After changing UI/settings: press **Exit Game**, then click **Cancel** — this forces settings to save even if you later crash.
Camera bug
camera stuck and you can only move forward with mouse.
Try switching **Camera Style** between **Action / Classic**.
Is the server up / maintenance?
Check: https://status.aa-classic.com/status/main

Payments / coins

PayPal/card payment fails — another way?
Website **Donate** tab → **Alternative Payment Methods** → **PlayCardExpress** (PayPal & Gameflip).

Guilds / social

Is there a French guild?
Check **#guild-recruitment** in Discord.

Reporting bugs

If something seems broken, include: - your **level + faction + location** - what you tried - screenshot/error text

If it’s account/entitlement-related or you’re hard-stuck, open a support ticket: https://aa-classic.com/profile?tab=support
