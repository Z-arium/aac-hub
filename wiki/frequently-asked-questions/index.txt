12:T1871,<h1 id="Frequently_Asked_Questions_(AAC)"><span></span>Frequently Asked Questions (AAC)</h1>
<p>This page answers common questions for **ArcheAge Classic (AAC)** players.
</p><p><i>Note:</i> AAC is based on the classic **3.5-era** ArcheAge with custom changes. See: <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/getting-started">Getting Started</a> and <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/custom-server-changes">Custom Server Changes</a>.
</p>
<h2 id="Server_basics">Server basics</h2>
<dl><dt>What are the server rates?</dt>
<dd>Commonly referenced rates: **XP x2**, **Vocation x2**, **Honor (PvP) x4**, **Honor (PvE/Quests) x2**.</dd></dl>
<dl><dt>Do I need Patron?</dt>
<dd>No. AAC has **free Patron benefits for all players**.</dd></dl>
<dl><dt>How does Labor work?</dt>
<dd>Labor regenerates **online and offline**. Commonly referenced values: **40 labor / 5 minutes**, max **10,000**.</dd></dl>
<h2 id="Leveling_/_early_progression"><span></span>Leveling / early progression</h2>
<dl><dt>What’s the fastest way to level?</dt>
<dd>Follow **green story quests**. Most players skip/ignore most **yellow quests** unless they specifically need them.</dd></dl>
<dl><dt>What is a Growthstone?</dt>
<dd>An **EXP consumable**. Use it directly from your inventory.</dd></dl>
<h2 id="Catch-up_gear_/_gearing"><span></span>Catch-up gear / gearing</h2>
<dl><dt>When do I get catch-up gear?</dt>
<dd>At **level 50**, you receive a **Catch-up Gear Box**.</dd></dl>
<dl><dt>What should I do right after I open the catch-up box?</dt>
<dd>
<ul><li>Don’t stress perfect min/max immediately.</li>
<li>Early PvP survivability gems people commonly recommend: **Toughness (pants)** + **Resilience (chest)**.</li>
<li>Use Activity Tokens to start the upgrade quest at a **Daru Auctioneer** (Mirage portal area), then use the **Soulforged anvil (Diamond Shores)**.</li></ul></dd></dl>
<dl><dt>Is it better to regrade myself or buy finished pieces?</dt>
<dd>Often, buying a finished piece is cheaper than eating RNG. Dungeon gear is generally an easier regrade target than some crafted/obsidian paths.</dd></dl>
<h2 id="Emerald_/_Hasla_weapons_(AAC)"><span></span>Emerald / Hasla weapons (AAC)</h2>
<dl><dt>What are Emerald weapons?</dt>
<dd>A custom starter weapon line. Upgrading is **safe (synthesis)**, not regrade RNG.</dd></dl>
<dl><dt>How do I get an Emerald weapon?</dt>
<dd>
<ol><li>Bring a **normal Emerald**.</li>
<li>Go to Hasla → **Tehmi Ruins / Abandoned Drill Camp**.</li>
<li>Talk to **Fragment Researcher Soa** → trade Emerald for **Infused Emerald**.</li>
<li>Use the bench behind Soa to craft the weapon.</li></ol></dd></dl>
<dl><dt>For upgrading Emerald (Hasla) weapons, does the fodder weapon have to match weapon type?</dt>
<dd>**No.** It does not have to be the same weapon type.</dd></dl>
<h2 id="Activity_Tokens">Activity Tokens</h2>
<dl><dt>What are Activity Tokens?</dt>
<dd>A server-specific progression currency used mainly on **Mirage Isle**.</dd></dl>
<dl><dt>Where do I get token quests?</dt>
<dd>Most are picked up from **Shadow Daru** in each main capital.</dd></dl>
<dl><dt>What are common token sources?</dt>
<dd>Weekly quests (Fri/Sat/Sun resets) + daily merit-style token quest. See <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/activity-tokens">Activity Tokens</a>.</dd></dl>
<h2 id="Trade_packs_/_charcoal"><span></span>Trade packs / charcoal</h2>
<dl><dt>How do I get Charcoal Stabilizer?</dt>
<dd>Deliver a **trade pack cross-continent** to a **Resource Trader** (West-made packs turned in East, or vice-versa). Common examples players mention: **Solis / Villanelle / Ynystere**.</dd></dl>
<h2 id="Housing_/_land"><span></span>Housing / land</h2>
<dl><dt>Where do I get the free 8x8 + 16x16 farm?</dt>
<dd>From the **Blue Salt Brotherhood land questline**. Video guide: <a rel="noopener" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PBCb4NqijDA">https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=PBCb4NqijDA</a></dd></dl>
<dl><dt>I turned in the pack but can’t find the next 16x16 quest NPC.</dt>
<dd>
<ul><li>**WEST:** talk to **Misty** on the Crescent Throne docks (Solzreed).</li>
<li>**EAST:** talk to **Jasmine** on the Austera docks (Solis).</li></ul></dd></dl>
<h2 id="Fishing">Fishing</h2>
<dl><dt>Do I need lures for ocean sportfishing?</dt>
<dd>Depends on your **rod**. When you try sportfishing it should tell you "Not enough X lure".</dd></dl>
<dl><dt>Where do I buy lures?</dt>
<dd>Fishing boats have a small **fish bucket** you can interact with to buy lures.</dd></dl>
<dl><dt>What’s the difference between Lures and Chum?</dt>
<dd>
<ul><li>**Lures** are used **per cast**.</li>
<li>**Chum** is **Chopped Fish Bucket** used to **activate a fishing pool**.</li></ul></dd></dl>
<h2 id="Common_issues_/_troubleshooting"><span></span>Common issues / troubleshooting</h2>
<dl><dt>My settings reset every time I close the game.</dt>
<dd>Try: go to **Character Select** before exiting. If it still happens, rename `Documents\\AAClassic` to `AAClassicOLD` to force the client to recreate settings.</dd></dl>
<dl><dt>Settings reset after a crash.</dt>
<dd>After changing UI/settings: press **Exit Game**, then click **Cancel** — this forces settings to save even if you later crash.</dd></dl>
<dl><dt>Camera bug</dt>
<dd>camera stuck and you can only move forward with mouse.</dd>
<dd>Try switching **Camera Style** between **Action / Classic**.</dd></dl>
<dl><dt>Is the server up / maintenance?</dt>
<dd>Check: <a rel="noopener" href="https://status.aa-classic.com/status/main">https://status.aa-classic.com/status/main</a></dd></dl>
<h2 id="Payments_/_coins"><span></span>Payments / coins</h2>
<dl><dt>PayPal/card payment fails — another way?</dt>
<dd>Website **Donate** tab → **Alternative Payment Methods** → **PlayCardExpress** (PayPal &amp; Gameflip).</dd></dl>
<h2 id="Guilds_/_social"><span></span>Guilds / social</h2>
<dl><dt>Is there a French guild?</dt>
<dd>Check **#guild-recruitment** in Discord.</dd></dl>
<h2 id="Reporting_bugs">Reporting bugs</h2>
<p>If something seems broken, include:
- your **level + faction + location**
- what you tried
- screenshot/error text
</p><p>If it’s account/entitlement-related or you’re hard-stuck, open a support ticket:
<a rel="noopener" href="https://aa-classic.com/profile?tab=support">https://aa-classic.com/profile?tab=support</a>
