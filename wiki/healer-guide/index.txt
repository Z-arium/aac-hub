<h3 id="Skill_Trees">Skill Trees</h3>
<ul><li>Cleric (Vitalism, Songcraft, Auramancy)
<ul><li>Well rounded, highest healing output healer class.</li></ul></li>
<li>Heirophant (Vitalism, Witchcraft, Auramancy)
<ul><li>Sacrifices some healing output for additional tankiness and crowd control.</li></ul></li>
<li>Templar (Vitalism, Defense, Auramancy)
<ul><li>Sacrifices some healing output for significant tankiness.</li></ul></li>
<li>Soothsayer (Vitalism, Shadowplay, Auramancy)
<ul><li>Sacrifices healing output for additional mobility, generally focused on reviving allies due to stealth's ability to drop from combat.</li></ul></li></ul>
