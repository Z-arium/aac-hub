These pickaxes can be equipped and used for a short duration with a long cooldown to improve yield from regular mining. The duration and cooldown goes up and gets reduced respectively as you improve the Grade of the pickaxe.

== <span style="color: rgb(119, 176, 100);">Grand Dawnsdrop Pickaxe</span> ==
=== [https://aa-classic.com/database/quests/9000079 1. The Art of Mining] ===
Turn in at Thadius, the Blacksmith in Glitterstone. 
=== [https://aa-classic.com/database/quests/9000080 2. Digging Deeper] ===
* Collect 3000 Iron Ore.
You can purchase these straight from the Auction House. 
== <span style="color: rgb(85, 143, 215);">Rare Dawnsdrop Pickaxe</span> ==
=== [https://aa-classic.com/database/quests/9000081 3. Tool for the Job] ===
* Use 20,000 Labor in Mining.
=== [https://aa-classic.com/database/quests/9000082 4. Glint of Copper] ===
* Collect 3000 Copper Ore. 
* Collect 3000 Silver Ore.
You can purchase these straight from the Auction House. 
== <span style="color: rgb(203, 114, 216);">Arcane Dawnsdrop Pickaxe</span> ==
=== [https://aa-classic.com/database/quests/9000083 5. Elemental Heart] ===
* Kill Arkanis the Destroyer and collect the Elemental Heart in Greater Kroloal Cradle.
''Tip: Bring Enervate (Witchcraft) and other heal reduction debuffs in order to defeat the first boss in GKC.'' 
== <span style="color: rgb(215, 139, 6);">Heroic Dawnsdrop Pickaxe</span> ==
=== [https://aa-classic.com/database/quests/9000084 6. The Ember-forged Tool] ===
* Use 30,000 Labor in Mining.
=== [https://aa-classic.com/database/quests/9000085 7. Glitter and Gold] ===
* Collect 3000 Gold Ore.
You can purchase these straight from the Auction House. 
=== [https://aa-classic.com/database/quests/9000086 8. Sparks of Creation] ===
* Kill Sirothe and collect the Infernal Anima in Serpentis.
== <span style="color: rgb(225, 120, 83);">Unique Dawnsdrop Pickaxe</span> ==
=== [https://aa-classic.com/database/quests/9000087 9. Ore of Origins] ===
* Collect 2000 Archeum Ore.
You can purchase these straight from the Auction House. 
=== [https://aa-classic.com/database/quests/9000088 10. Tempered by the Flame] ===
* Use 30,000 Labor in Mining.
== <span style="color: rgb(249, 82, 82);">Celestial Dawnsdrop Pickaxe</span> ==
=== [https://aa-classic.com/database/quests/9000089 11. Essence of Purity] ===
* Collect 200 Pure Iron Ore.
You can purchase these straight from the Auction House. 
=== [https://aa-classic.com/database/quests/9000090 12. Further Refinement] ===
* Craft the <span style="color: rgb(249, 82, 82);">Celestial Dawnsdrop Pickaxe</span> at any Anvil.
