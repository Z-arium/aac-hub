Dawnsdrop Pickaxe

The Dawnsdrop Pickaxe is an addition to the Dawnsdrop Tools custom content added in Patch 1.3.2. These pickaxes can be equipped and used for a short duration with a long cooldown to improve yield from regular mining. The duration and cooldown goes up and gets reduced respectively as you improve the Grade of the pickaxe.

Starting the Questline

Requirements: 230,000 Mining Proficiency

Bring Dawnsdrop Gloves.

  1. Go to Glitterstone (Ynystere Community Center).
  2. Mine an Iron Vein with your Gloves equipped for the Dawnsdrop Buff.
  3. Get the quest from Tilu in the Community Center.

Grand Dawnsdrop Pickaxe

1. The Art of Mining

Turn in at Thadius, the Blacksmith in Glitterstone.

2. Digging Deeper

  • Collect 3000 Iron Ore.

You can purchase these straight from the Auction House.

Rare Dawnsdrop Pickaxe

3. Tool for the Job

  • Use 20,000 Labor in Mining.

4. Glint of Copper

  • Collect 3000 Copper Ore.
  • Collect 3000 Silver Ore.

You can purchase these straight from the Auction House.

Arcane Dawnsdrop Pickaxe

5. Elemental Heart

  • Kill Arkanis the Destroyer and collect the Elemental Heart in Greater Kroloal Cradle.

Tip: Bring Enervate (Witchcraft) and other heal reduction debuffs in order to defeat the first boss in GKC.

Heroic Dawnsdrop Pickaxe

6. The Ember-forged Tool

  • Use 30,000 Labor in Mining.

  • Collect 3000 Gold Ore.
