1f:T86e,Larceny in ArcheAge refers to the profession centered around farming monsters for profit. This is a great, simple profession for new players as there is no upfront investment required. The loot from monsters is a mix of coin purses, crates, and random gear that can be salvaged for archeum. 
[[File:Auroria lodestone.png|alt=Auroria Lodestone on the map|thumb|Auroria Lodestone on the map]]
Players can grind noble, jester, prince, and queen's purses and crates with scaling difficulty of the monsters.

* Noble crates and purses are found in mid tier zones main continent zones (Sanddeep, ...)
* Jesters crates and purses are found in higher tier main continent zones (Karkasse, ...)
* Princes crates and purses are found in all Auroria zones.
* Queens crates and purses are found around purified lodestones in Auroria.

Zones that are in conflict or war have higher loot drop rates.
