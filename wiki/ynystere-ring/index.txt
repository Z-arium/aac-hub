<h2 id="Getting_the_quest_starter">Getting the quest starter</h2>
<p>You can obtain the Stone Orb from the end boss of any Greater Dungeon. It drops for everyone in the party and is a 100% drop chance. Right click to start the quest.
</p><p><span><img src="/aac-hub/wiki-files/b6/b6c4066bd66a5593e245206edb855cc8d4f13fcc.webp" width="200" height="151" loading="lazy"></span>
</p>
<h2 id="The_Strange_Stone">The Strange Stone</h2>
<p>Report to Archeologist Burnar on Freedich Isle. (You will need to ride your boat here or ask someone for help. It is a contested PvP Area)
</p><p><span><img src="/aac-hub/wiki-files/54/5489ef11461fb432778b2248c0f472b81d7e3210.webp" width="658" height="396" loading="lazy"></span>
</p>
<h2 id="A_Fellow_Collector">A Fellow Collector</h2>
<p>After talking to Archeologist Burnar he will send you to Apprentice Rudal who is located in Exeloch.
</p><p><span><img src="/aac-hub/wiki-files/cb/cb8e1b3ec333e7eac76f2547d71a315eb0da30d0.png" width="795" height="446" loading="lazy"></span>
</p>
<h2 id="Rudal's_Favor"><span></span>Rudal's Favor</h2>
<p>Rudal will now send you to Diamond Shores to talk to Drillmaster Raysen.
</p><p><span><img src="/aac-hub/wiki-files/c4/c464409e65c9cdb6f02ddd301fbf4d9baa27bb7f.webp" width="760" height="415" loading="lazy"></span>
</p>
<h2 id="Consulting_an_Expert">Consulting an Expert</h2>
<p>Drillmaster Raysen will send you to Nuimari to talk to Archeologist Hermas who will give you "The Kidella Ring Manuscript".
</p><p><span><img src="/aac-hub/wiki-files/6a/6a2b6a2dd4d008a4bbaf8db60bbef4f4f979c358.webp" width="760" height="422" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
You will need to craft the Manuscript you received into a "The Kidella Ring" book and then place it in a house that you own then interact with the book to received the next quest. (You can use someone else's book if you do not have a house to put it in.)
</p><p>You will need 10 paper, 1 Memory Ink and 5 Leather to craft the book.
</p>
<h2 id="The_Stuff_of_Legends">The Stuff of Legends</h2>
<p>After Interacting with "The Kidella ring" book, You will need to head back to exeloch to talk to Apprentice Rudal again.
</p>
<h2 id="The_Ringbearer">The Ringbearer</h2>
<p>Apprentice Rudal will send you to Asaji who is at the top of Veroe in Hasla. Take the elevator to reach the top if you can not find her.
</p><p><span><img src="/aac-hub/wiki-files/3f/3f44a5c2c72c9cfba5ba9263ccf378f615271e33.png" width="702" height="434" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
Asaji will ask you to rescue Princess Surya who is in Kroloal Cradle. You will need to do the whole dungeon including the last boss to get her to spawn. <b>DO NOT LEAVE AND FORGET TO TALK TO HER.</b>
</p><p>Talk to Asaji and accept the next quest.
</p>
<h2 id="A_Dangerous_Curse">A Dangerous Curse</h2>
<p>Simply Return to Apprentice Rudar in Exeloch and you will receive the Arcane <b>Ynys Guardian Ring</b>.
</p>
<h2 id="Restoring_the_Ring">Restoring the Ring</h2>

</p>

16:Td51,The Ynystere Ring is a questline ring often used by healers.

== Getting the quest starter ==
You can obtain the Stone Orb from the end boss of any Greater Dungeon. It drops for everyone in the party and is a 100% drop chance. Right click to start the quest.

[[File:Stone orb.webp|frameless]]

== The Strange Stone ==

Report to Archeologist Burnar on Freedich Isle. (You will need to ride your boat here or ask someone for help. It is a contested PvP Area)

[[File:Quest_1.webp|658x658px]]

== A Fellow Collector ==
After talking to Archeologist Burnar he will send you to Apprentice Rudal who is located in Exeloch.

[[File:Exeloch Location.png]]

== Rudal's Favor ==
Rudal will now send you to Diamond Shores to talk to Drillmaster Raysen.

[[File:Diamond Shores.webp]]

== Consulting an Expert ==
Drillmaster Raysen will send you to Nuimari to talk to Archeologist Hermas who will give you "The Kidella Ring Manuscript".

[[File:Nuimari.webp]]


You will need to craft the Manuscript you received into a "The Kidella Ring" book and then place it in a house that you own then interact with the book to received the next quest. (You can use someone else's book if you do not have a house to put it in.)

You will need 10 paper, 1 Memory Ink and 5 Leather to craft the book.

== The Stuff of Legends ==
After Interacting with "The Kidella ring" book, You will need to head back to exeloch to talk to Apprentice Rudal again.

== The Ringbearer ==
Apprentice Rudal will send you to Asaji who is at the top of Veroe in Hasla. Take the elevator to reach the top if you can not find her.

[[File:Hasla.png]]


Asaji will ask you to rescue Princess Surya who is in Kroloal Cradle. You will need to do the whole dungeon including the last boss to get her to spawn. '''DO NOT LEAVE AND FORGET TO TALK TO HER.'''

Talk to Asaji and accept the next quest.

== A Dangerous Curse ==
Simply Return to Apprentice Rudar in Exeloch and you will receive the Arcane '''Ynys Guardian Ring'''.

== Restoring the Ring ==
Apprentice Rudar will now send you to Archaeologist Hermas in Nuimari again, You will then be sent to speak to Belstrom in Diamond Shores.

[[File:Diamond Shores2.webp]]
