<h1 id="Guilds">Guilds</h1>
<p><b>Guilds</b> are groups that allow players to team up, collect <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/prestige">Prestige</a>, and gain distinct advantages through guild buffs. Depending on the leader's vision, a guild can either focus heavily on competitive gameplay or operate strictly as a "Prestige Guild" for casual progression.
</p>
<h2 id="Levels">Levels</h2>
<p>Guilds can progress up to level 8, with each level upgrading the guild buff to provide stronger bonuses to its members and unlocking new items in the <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/prestige-shop">Prestige Shop</a>.
</p>

= Guilds =
'''Guilds''' are groups that allow players to team up, collect [[Prestige]], and gain distinct advantages through guild buffs. Depending on the leader's vision, a guild can either focus heavily on competitive gameplay or operate strictly as a "Prestige Guild" for casual progression.

== Levels ==
Guilds can progress up to level 8, with each level upgrading the guild buff to provide stronger bonuses to its members and unlocking new items in the [[Prestige Shop]].
