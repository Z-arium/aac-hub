Serpentis


Serpentis

A dungeon crucial to most characters' progress, as it lets you craft the [[1](https://darugear.com/?s=BELEzS) Dark Warrior’s Guards] for leather wearers and [[2](https://darugear.com/?s=vtlHFy) Shadow Warrior’s Shadows] for cloth wearers.

It is also useful for DPS classes or for anyone wanting to debuff their target with 1k defense penetration to both physical and magical damage (applies to targets taking damage from the wearer).

There are two very important items that drop: Faded Blooddrinker Blossom and Faded Dark Watcher’s Dagger. These items can be upgraded and give great bonuses as weapon swaps.
