=== Good news! ===
[[File:LegacyEndBox.png|frameless|right]]A new Catch-Up Event has been enabled to honor the end of the Korean servers. It will last for TWO WEEKS, until the 19th of March.
* GHA and GKC armors will drop directly at UNIQUE instead of ARCANE, making it even cheaper to work on your armor! Check out the [[Aria's Autograph]] page for more information!
* The amount of Hasla Tokens dropped has been doubled, making it easier to level up your [[Emerald Weapons|Emerald Weapon]]!
* Various dailies will reward a "Legacy's End Box". This box will give a choice between a Legacy's End Reward or Arctic Earthstones. The Earthstones can be used to craft Hasla Tokens, further increasing the speed at which you can obtain your [[Emerald Weapons|Emerald Weapon]]! But fear not, should your Emerald Weapon already be complete, the Legacy's End Reward will be an assortment of gifts to keep going in your adventure.
