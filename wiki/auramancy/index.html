Auramancy

Active Skills

Level Skill Description Combos
1 Thwart Inflicts Shaken on all enemies within a 6m radius, decreasing their Move Speed -27%, Skill Damage -8%, and Attack Speed -75.

Grants the caster 1 stack of Inspired.



Inspired:
  • Increases Attack Speed +53.
  • Vicious Implosion (Auramancy): increases damage +52%.
  • Antithesis (Vitalism): decreases cooldown 0.6 sec.
  • Mend (Vitalism): decreases cooldown 2.1
  • Fervent Healing (Vitalism): decreases cooldown 1.2 sec.
  • Stacks up to 3 times.
3 Comet's Boon Fires a flash of light that deals 1380 Magic Damage to all enemies hit.

Increases the Move Speed of all allies it passes.

N/A
10 Spell Shield Creates an energy shield for 8sec.

Decreases Received Magic Damage —32.0%, but also decreases Received Healing by an equal amount.

Does not trigger a global cooldown.

Gain Magic Shield while under Liberation.
15 Vicious Implosion Triggers a sudden, powerful implosion, pulling enemies within 10 meters towards you and dealing 982 Magic Damage. Consumes all stacks of Inspired; inflicts additional +52% damage per stack.

Increases Aggro +300% when used on Taunted targets.

20 Teleport Teleports caster 15 meters ahead.

Can't teleport through walls or other solid Objects.

Can't be used while Snared.

N/A
25 Health Lift Increases max health +2086.

Affects caster and nearby raid members for 30 minutes.

Can't be used on targets whose health has already been increased.

N/A
30 Meditate Restores +4% Of max mana per second for 9sec through meditation.

Prevents movement or other actions.

N/A
35 Shrug It Off Cancels all Stun and Impale effects, and grants immunity to these effects for 5sec.

Can only be used while under one of these effects.

Can be used during global cooldown, and does not trigger global cooldown.

N/A
40 Liberation Grants immunity to Shackle and Silence for 20 sec. N/A
45 Leech Interrupts a target's casting and inflicts Sleep for 7sec.

Steals one buff from the target. Targets will wake upon taking damage. Sleep duration reduced —50% in PvP.

Steals an additional buff from Slowed targets.

Increases Slowed targets' Sleep duration +2 seconds.

50 Protective Wings Grants immunity to Magic Damage to allies within 5m for the duration.

N/A

55 Mirror Warp Teleports caster 15m to the right or left.

Can't pass through walls or other solid objects.

Has a chance to escape enemy targetting during PvP.

Can't be used while Snared.

N/A

Passive Skills

Required Points Skill Description
2 Absorb Damage Converts 7% of received damage to health.
4 Hearten Doubles the duration of Inspire.
6 Inspiration Cloak Decreases all received damage -100 for 3 seconds after receiving critical damage.

This ability has a 20 sec cooldown once triggered.

7 Unassailable Grants a 1% chance to become immune to all damage for 2 seconds after receiving any damage.

This ability has a 20 sec cooldown once triggered.

8 Improved Targetting Increases the Accuracy of all attack types +8%.
9 Magic Condenser Increases all Critical Rates and Critical Heal Rate +6%.
10 Teleportation Discipline Teleportation has a 50% chance to eliminate its cooldown and allow consecutive teleports.

Mirror Warp has a 50% chance to sustain the buff and allow consecutive use.
