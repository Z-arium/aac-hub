Adventurer's Guide

Adventurer's guide is new customly added tab in game. The Guide has Paths that are missions that need to be done in order to proceed to the next grade of missions. The mission are to help hew players navigate the game and see what kinda things are important at the get go.

A New Adventure

Grand


REWARDS

Ancestral Warrior Medal (x5)

Activity Tokens (x50)

Epherium Cloak Kit

  • Sealed Epherium Cloak
  • Unique Auroran Synthesis Sotne (x4)

Warrior Medals (x50)


Achieve Lv50

Get to lvl 50 by doing green quests and by using Adventurers Growthstones. > Hamiltons Guide for New Players

United We Stand

Make a new guild or join an existing guild. Pressing default key SHIFT + V opens the Community tab and under it is guild tab. Pick a guild that suits your playstyle or make your on by talking to the Guild Manager.

Reach 4000 Gearscore

Get your gearscore to 4000 or more. After getting to lvl 50 the achievement rewards the player with the catch up set called Soulforged Armor and Hellkissed Weapons and equipping these should bring the player to 4k gearscore.

Vocation Knowledge

Reach 10,000 proficiency on any profession. Proficiencies are harvesting and crafting actions in the game. Can be found under Skills > usually K keybind.

Till Its Like It Is

Do enough Blue Salt Brotherhood quests to obtain Scarecrow Design. Guide for the questline here > Hamiltons Guide for Blue Salt Brotherhood Guestline (both nations in same video!)

Gotta Catch Em All

Buy a Battle Pet. Battle Pets are animals or creatures that players can use to attack enemies to help with dps or combat in general. They can wear gear to better their stats. (Pokemon fights, lol.)

Battle Pets can be obtained from different places but the easiest being pet stand that sell each pet for 10 silver. Two easy battle pets mentioned here are Cats and Wolfhounds. They both cost 10 silver per pet.

Cat pet

Wolfhound Pet


Rare

REWARDS

Ancestral Warrior Medal (x5)

Activity Tokens (x25)

Honor Points (x10 000)

Warrior Medals (x50)

Scroll: Seaskimmer Speedboat (15d)


Achieve Lv55

Keep leveling up with the quests and Adventurer Growthstones. After those are done start going to the daily events to gain experience. Guides for bosses in Guides tab of the Adventurers Guide.

United We Stand

Make a new family or join existing one. When creating a family and adding a member to the family players must buy Family Certificate from any General Merchant. Family tab is under Community (SHIFT +V) by default.

Hounded to Death

Kill Hound of Kyrios. Join a CR raid in the nation chat and complete this task by killing one of the big bosses that spawns after the mobs are dead.

Vocation Knowledge 2

Reach 40,000 proficiency on any profession.

Crop Whisperer

Build a Scarecrow Farm. Follow Hamiltons guide in order to get both designs 8x8 and 16x16. Land for new player can be found in Swirling Meadow and occasionally other places.

Into The Dungeon

Complete the "Greater Bounty: Ancient Titan Musperosa" Quest. The quests can be found on task boards outside the dungeon entrance. Port to Greater Howling Abyss and defeat the last boss.

The Ultimate Gliding Experience

Craft an Ultimate Glider. How to Make the Basic Glider explains where to get all the designs for the crafting. Crafting happens at Carpentry Workbench.

REWARDS

Abyssal Knowledge

To access Abyssal skills for each skillset players must complete a quest related to the skillset. Guide for Abyssal Skills.

Reach 4500 Gearscore

Keep watching Hamiltons video linked before. There is also written guide, Gearing Guide.

Vocation Knowledge 3

Reach 90,000 proficiency on any profession.

Emerald Weaponsmith

Obtain Hasla Emerald Weapon or Craft an Artic Earthstone.

Making a Hasla Weapon do check the written guide here or IcyZinHD guide video.

Just for the Arctic Earthstone players need to kill few mobs in Hasla or Karkasse, buy Faded Hasla Fragment from NPC in Hasla Abandoned Drill Camp and then craft the stone to complete the mission.

A Tale of Pristine Jewelry

Start Dream Ring or Ynys Ring questline.

Written guide for Dream Ring questline.

Written guide for Ynys Guardian Ring questline.

Bigger is Better

Craft a Clipper. The Clipper designs are sold in Gilda star hop for 50 gilda each. After nuying the design place the drydock in water and start building. The drydock needs variable amounts of lumber-, fabric-, and iron packs. Clipper Materials can be found in Folio (O by default) and crafted in Shipyard Workbench.

Time for Work

Obtain a Territory Work Permit. This can be bough from the guild shop with Prestige. Community > Guild > Prestige Shop


Heroic

The Dread Necromancer

Kill Anthalon. Anthalon is a world boss that spawns in Sungold Fields after Auroria Crimson Rift is done. X up in the nation for x Anth to join a raid. Check more info from the Guide for Crimson Rift (Auroria).

Epic Regrading Journey

Regrade gearpiece to Divine once.

Cloaked in Divinity

Collect Divine-grade Epherium Cloak.

Theres No Price For This

Obtain a Priceless Coin. How to get Priceless Coins?
