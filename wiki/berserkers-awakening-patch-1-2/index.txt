<h2 id="Other_Changes_and_Additions">Other Changes and Additions</h2>
<h3 id="Luxury_Liner">Luxury Liner</h3>
<p>The Luxury Liner was released ahead of time in patch 1.1.27 but is falls under the 1.2 banner
</p><p>It is a  massive party yacht, with two regradeable propellers, 10 trade pack storage slots and 2 human cannons. The design can be crafted for 2800 Gilda stars
</p>
<h3 id="Vocational_Changes">Vocational Changes</h3>
<h5 id="Family_Rework">Family Rework</h5>
<p>Families have received a small set of changes in this patch:
</p>
<ul><li>The {Family Quest:} quests now grant 20 tax certificates (these are the quests requiring at least 3 players)</li>
<li>Two new Family levels have been added: Abundant and Prosperous Family
<ul><li>Abundant Family: Increases all proficiencies +5000</li>
<li>Prosperous Family: Decreases Production time -20%</li></ul></li></ul>
<h3 id="Raised_Cottages">Raised Cottages</h3>
<p>Raised Cottage variants have been added for all 16x16 cottages. They provide more room for furniture and 2 seedbeds. They can be found under the Remodeling tab of any Cottage!
</p>
<figure><img src="/aac-hub/wiki-files/2b/2b2fcebbc52632325a6f93c71b10a7c5d13c713f.png" width="429" height="322" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Raised Cottage remodel preview</figcaption></figure>
<h3 id="Economy_Changes">Economy Changes</h3>
<ul><li>Yellow and Red Regrade Charms will now cost less charcoal to craft</li>
<li>Mining Drills will now cost 38,000 Vocation instead of 80,000</li>
<li>Serendipity Stones will now cost 100 less Earthmana Leaves</li>
<li>Delphinad Ghost Ship packs are now worth 100 gold each instead of 75 gold</li>
<li>Miner's Farmhouses have received a set of buffs
<ul><li>Labor has been reduced by 33% on the Mining Vein</li>
<li>Growth time has been reduced by 33% on all tiers of veins</li></ul></li>
<li>Trade Packs turned in for Charcoal have received a 10% increase in Charcoal Stabilizers granted</li></ul>
<h3 id="Priceless_Coin_Additions">Priceless Coin Additions</h3>
<p>With the addition of a new Priceless Coin via the Veroe Harvest Festival Achievements, we have added 2 new options for Priceless Coins:
</p>
<ul><li>30-Day Crystal Wings</li>
<li>30-Day Bouncy Cow</li></ul>
<p>These can of course be upgraded into permanent version with 120 activity tokens.
</p><p>More Priceless Coins will be made available in the coming weeks!
</p>
<h3 id="Vanity_Marketplace_Additions">Vanity Marketplace Additions</h3>
<p>On top of the marketplace release, here are some Vanity additions added with 1.2:
</p>
<ul><li>Vanity Mounts
<ul><li>Vanity Bouncy Cow: This mount looks like a Bouncy Cow, but has the skills &amp; stats of a standard Elk</li>
<li>Hellfire Steed: This incredibly good looking horse has the skills &amp; stats of a standard Snowlion</li></ul></li>
<li>Vanity Housing
<ul><li>Forest Mushroom Houses are now available as Thatched Farmhouse remodels. They do not grant the seedbed that they have on retail to avoid giving an advantage!</li>
<li>Raised Mushroom Houses are now available as Harvester's Farmhouse remodels.</li></ul></li></ul>
<h3 id="Miscellaneous_Changes">Miscellaneous Changes</h3>
<ul><li>The cooldown between each harvest of Twilight Trees &amp; Mineral Pumps has been reduced</li>
<li>Arena Managers now offer an option to restore health &amp; mana. This is targeted at people who duel in main towns</li>
<li>Image Items on Weapons and Armor can be extracted without losing the image</li>
<li>Rebirth Trauma will now let players open portals</li>
<li>Health Lift, Aranzeb's Boon and Hummingbird's Ditty will now persist on death</li></ul>
<h3 id="Bug_Fixes">Bug Fixes</h3>
<ul><li>Warm Memory Embers will now properly be created on new &amp; updated house types</li>
<li>Beanstalk now properly grants Beans</li>

=== Vanity Marketplace Additions ===
On top of the marketplace release, here are some Vanity additions added with 1.2:

* Vanity Mounts
** Vanity Bouncy Cow: This mount looks like a Bouncy Cow, but has the skills & stats of a standard Elk
** Hellfire Steed: This incredibly good looking horse has the skills & stats of a standard Snowlion
* Vanity Housing
** Forest Mushroom Houses are now available as Thatched Farmhouse remodels. They do not grant the seedbed that they have on retail to avoid giving an advantage! 
** Raised Mushroom Houses are now available as Harvester's Farmhouse remodels.

=== Miscellaneous Changes ===

* The cooldown between each harvest of Twilight Trees & Mineral Pumps has been reduced
* Arena Managers now offer an option to restore health & mana. This is targeted at people who duel in main towns
* Image Items on Weapons and Armor can be extracted without losing the image
* Rebirth Trauma will now let players open portals
* Health Lift, Aranzeb's Boon and Hummingbird's Ditty will now persist on death
