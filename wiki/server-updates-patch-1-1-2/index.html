Server Updates/Patch 1.1.2

Events

The Hallowtide Event is now Active till the first of November! Solis Headlands and Two Crowns added to Festival Zones.

Boss Changes

  • Kraken Health has been increased
  • Delphinad Ghost Ship spawns have been adjusted
    • Spawn on server restart
    • 24 hour respawn timer

Item Changes

  • Dawn of Glory are now Tradeable and Sellable
  • A number of items have seen their stack sizes increased to 1000
  • Misagon‘s Crates are no longer available from Prestige Shop
  • Regrade Charms have been removed from the General Merchant and moved into their own recipes
    • Crafting at Masonry Station
    • Their cost is various amounts of Charcoal Stabilizers, Iron Ingots and Anya Ingots
    • Higher Tier charms will be made available in later patch using similar recipes

Other

  • Daru Trinket costumes have been added. Two more costumes will release next week for the same price point, so if you don‘t like them, hold onto your Trinkets!
  • Saltwater schools of fish have seen their duration reset back to 45 minutes
  • PCE should be back later today or tomorrow at the latest so you will be able to splurge on credits
