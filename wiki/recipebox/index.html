RecipeBox

Template:RecipeBox

Shows a craft recipe, fetched from the sermeatball crafting API. Workbench, proficiency and materials come from the API.

Usage 

{{RecipeBox|9001323}}
  • The only parameter is the craft ID.
  • Needs the template RecipeMaterialLine.
View wiki source
== Template:RecipeBox ==
<includeonly>{{#get_web_data:
url=https://api.sermeatball.com/crafting/recipes/{{{1}}}?basis=latest&advantage=0&ah_tax=true
|format=json with jsonpath
|data=product=$.product_item_name,labor=$.labor,station=$.station_name,prof=$.proficiency_name,req=$.proficiency_requirement
}}<table class="wikitable">
<tr><th>Recipe</th><td>{{#external_value:product}}</td></tr>

<tr><th>Workbench</th><td>{{#external_value:station}}</td></tr>
<tr><th>Proficiency</th><td>{{#external_value:prof}}{{#ifeq:{{#external_value:req}}|0||&nbsp;{{formatnum:{{#external_value:req}}}}}}</td></tr>
<tr><th>Materials</th><td>{{#get_web_data:
url=https://api.sermeatball.com/crafting/recipes/{{{1}}}?basis=latest&advantage=0&ah_tax=true

|data=material=$.materials[*].item_name,grade=$.materials[*].item_base_grade_name,amount=$.materials[*].amount
}}{{#display_external_table:template=RecipeMaterialLine}}</td></tr>
</table></includeonly><noinclude>
