<h3 id="Magnificent">Magnificent</h3>
<p>Magnificent is the second step towards Ayanad and requires a certain piece of Illustrious gear to be crafted.
</p>
<h3 id="Epherium">Epherium</h3>
<p>Epherium is the third step towards Ayanad and requires a certain piece of Magnificent gear to be crafted.
</p>
<h3 id="Delphinad">Delphinad</h3>
<p>Delphinad is the forelast step towards Ayanad and requires a certain piece of Epherium gear to be crafted.
</p>
<h3 id="Ayanad">Ayanad</h3>
<p>Ayanad is the last step and requires a certain piece of Delphinad gear to be crafted.
</p>
