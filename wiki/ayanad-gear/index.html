Ayanad Gear

TBC!

Ayanad Gear is the second best Gear in the game and provides a strong, stat-driven alternative to Obsidian. Different to Obsidian, Ayanad is RNG based on stats, given that it is sealed and gives one of the pieces types randomly.

The different states

Illustrious

Illustrious is the first step towards Ayanad and is crafted with no previous gear piece.

Magnificent

Magnificent is the second step towards Ayanad and requires a certain piece of Illustrious gear to be crafted.

Epherium

Epherium is the third step towards Ayanad and requires a certain piece of Magnificent gear to be crafted.

Delphinad

Delphinad is the forelast step towards Ayanad and requires a certain piece of Epherium gear to be crafted.

Ayanad

Ayanad is the last step and requires a certain piece of Delphinad gear to be crafted.
