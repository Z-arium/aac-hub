Patch 1.3.1.4

Released on 20/11/2024

  • Fixed localization issues with Traderun: Dewstone to Two Crowns
  • Fixed localization for Bound Destruction Preventive Charm
  • Fixed epherium accessory salvaging wisp amount
  • Increased Whalesong and Aegis Island leadership rewards from 10 to 20.
  • Fixed localization with Daru Trinket costumes
  • Updated localization on Bound Destruction Preventive Charm to reflect that it does not prevent downgrade.
  • Ode to Recovery will now weave again
  • Freshwater Fish Schools have been reverted to their normal version. This also means Family Fishing has been corrected
  • You'll have to use Chum or Rainbow Chum to activate Freshwater pools
  • Freshwater Fish have seen their gold values increased to make the activity not a slap in the face. Fry will have a base value of 14.5, normal 18 and garg 25
  • The ratio values for Dragon Essence Stabilizers have been adjusted upwards. This brings them more in line with their 3.0 value
  • Fixed Greenman Kart localization
  • Fixed localization for Cow Kart
  • Fixed localization for Moo Bus
  • Fixed localization for Kitty Onesie
  • Fixed localization for Puppy Onesie
  • Fixed localization for Reckless Ravager's Plate
  • Fixed localization for Dairy Farmer Costume
  • Fixed localization for Milkmaid Costume
  • Fixed localization for Eclipse Knight Costume
  • Fixed localization with Daru Trinket costumes
  • Updated the icons on the Duskgleam and Veroe customization crates.
  • The Ghost Wedding Festival has started in White Arden! It will run until the 4th of December
    • The daily event will happen at 12:00, 19:00 and midnight Server Time
  • The Experimenting Again quest has been disabled properly
  • In its stead, a new category is available on the workbench in Hasla: Emerald Destruction. It'll allow destroying a weapon and getting a new emerald!
