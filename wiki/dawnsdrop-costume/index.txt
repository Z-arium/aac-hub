<h2 id="Starting_the_Questline_(Grand_Costume)"><span></span>Starting the Questline (Grand Costume)</h2>
<p><i>Requirements: 230,000 Commerce Proficiency</i> 
</p><p>Bring x3 Worms, Fishing Rod &amp; Dawnsdrop Boots. 
</p>
<ol><li>Travel to Freedich.</li>
<li>Bait fish with your boots equipped for the Dawnsdrop Buff.</li>
<li>Get the quest from Uli in the middle.</li></ol>
<p>Teleport to Rokhala and get the quest from the NPC shown on the map to receive your grand costume. 
</p>
<h2 id="Rare_Costume">Rare Costume</h2>
<p>Teleport to Halcyona and go behind the Nuia base to trade Blacksmith Buruli 1500g for the item needed. 
</p><p>Go back to Rokhala and turn in the quest to receive your Rare costume. 
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/1/14/AACBlacksmithBuruli.jpg/600px-AACBlacksmithBuruli.jpg" width="600" height="455" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/1/14/AACBlacksmithBuruli.jpg/900px-AACBlacksmithBuruli.jpg 1.5x, https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/1/14/AACBlacksmithBuruli.jpg 2x" loading="lazy"></span>
</p>
<h2 id="Heroic_Costume">Heroic Costume</h2>
<p>Pick up all the daily quests from the NPC, 25 WORLD TOUR tickets and spending 15,000 labor on commerce tasks are required to upgrade the costume to Heroic.
</p>
<ul><li>Hasla Aged Honey &gt; Ynystere - 1 Ticket (Land Route, no risk since you can go via Rokhala &amp; Hasla cycles peace)</li>
<li>Karkasse Aged Honey &gt; Sanddeep - 1 Tickets (Land Route, mild risk, donkey/car mountain or use airship)</li>
<li>Rokhala Aged Honey &gt; Cinderstone - 2 Tickets (Ocean Route, high risk, turn in can be camped, but 2 turn-in spots)</li>
<li>Airain Aged Cheese &gt; Ynystere - 2 Ticket (Ocean and very long land route, high risk, same as Cinder)</li>
<li>Golden Ruins Specialty Pack &gt; Solis/Two Crowns - 4 Tickets (Ocean Route, No Risk, just push the zone to peace and run your pack)</li></ul>
<p>Once you have collected all the required tickets, go back to Rokhala and turn in the quest to receive your Heroic costume.
</p>
