Dawnsdrop Costume

The Dawnsdrop Costume is an addition to the Dawnsdrop Tools custom content added in Patch 1.3.5g. These costumes can be worn to give bonuses to players using freighters and merchant schooners to deliver trade packs.

Starting the Questline (Grand Costume)

Requirements: 230,000 Commerce Proficiency

Bring x3 Worms, Fishing Rod & Dawnsdrop Boots.

  1. Travel to Freedich.
  2. Bait fish with your boots equipped for the Dawnsdrop Buff.
  3. Get the quest from Uli in the middle.

Teleport to Rokhala and get the quest from the NPC shown on the map to receive your grand costume.

Rare Costume

Teleport to Halcyona and go behind the Nuia base to trade Blacksmith Buruli 1500g for the item needed.

Go back to Rokhala and turn in the quest to receive your Rare costume.

Heroic Costume

Pick up all the daily quests from the NPC, 25 WORLD TOUR tickets and spending 15,000 labor on commerce tasks are required to upgrade the costume to Heroic.

  • Hasla Aged Honey > Ynystere - 1 Ticket (Land Route, no risk since you can go via Rokhala & Hasla cycles peace)
  • Karkasse Aged Honey > Sanddeep - 1 Tickets (Land Route, mild risk, donkey/car mountain or use airship)
  • Rokhala Aged Honey > Cinderstone - 2 Tickets (Ocean Route, high risk, turn in can be camped, but 2 turn-in spots)
  • Airain Aged Cheese > Ynystere - 2 Ticket (Ocean and very long land route, high risk, same as Cinder)
  • Golden Ruins Specialty Pack > Solis/Two Crowns - 4 Tickets (Ocean Route, No Risk, just push the zone to peace and run your pack)

Once you have collected all the required tickets, go back to Rokhala and turn in the quest to receive your Heroic costume.

Unique Costume

50 WORLD TOUR tickets are required to upgrade the costume to Unique

Celestial Costume

75 WORLD TOUR tickets are required to upgrade the costume to Celestial
