<h2 id="Heros">Heros</h2>
<p><i>Heavy is the burden on Erenor's Heroes.</i>
</p><p>At the end of the ranking period, the <b>top 20 characters</b> in each faction become Hero Candidates and other players given certain requirements: <i>lvl 30 character and having 500 leadership points</i> are qualified to vote on these candidates. Once the voting period is done, and the votes counted, the top 10 players will become a 'Hero of the Faction' ranked in order of their points. 
</p><p>These 10 Heroes will each have a named rank and buff they give to other players by being nearby. Heroes can also use a Hero Call to teleport their faction members back to Faction Base.
</p>
