Leadership

Leadership points can be obtained from all kinds of activities in the game. Leadership points are tallied on a leaderboard called Candidate Rankings, that consists of top 50 players with the highest Leadership points.

Heros

Heavy is the burden on Erenor's Heroes.

At the end of the ranking period, the top 20 characters in each faction become Hero Candidates and other players given certain requirements: lvl 30 character and having 500 leadership points are qualified to vote on these candidates. Once the voting period is done, and the votes counted, the top 10 players will become a 'Hero of the Faction' ranked in order of their points.

These 10 Heroes will each have a named rank and buff they give to other players by being nearby. Heroes can also use a Hero Call to teleport their faction members back to Faction Base.
