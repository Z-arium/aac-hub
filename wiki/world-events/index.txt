<h2 id="Crimson_Rift_-_CR">Crimson Rift - CR</h2><p>
Crimson Rift better known as CR is one of the most common daily activities, you can join the raids for this activity by X-ing up in the Faction/Nation chat when groups are being made. It will reward you with 1000 <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/currencies">Honor Points</a> and 10 <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/leadership">Leadership</a>.</p><figure><img alt src="/aac-hub/wiki-files/e4/e47adbb1763833ce1298a5171945757640248dd6.jpg" width="371" height="186" loading="lazy"><figcaption>CR Nuia Spawn Locations</figcaption></figure><p>

To check the time you can hover over the top right corner of your screen. </p><figure><img src="/aac-hub/wiki-files/5c/5c1c72131d23454454554cb4fa47af3f718ebb0f.png" width="368" height="198" loading="lazy"><figcaption>CR Haranya Spawn Locations</figcaption></figure><p>

<p>Grimgast Rift better known as GR is one of the most common daily activities, you can join the raids for this activity by X-ing up in the Faction/Nation chat when groups are being made.

<h2 id="Halcyona_War">Halcyona War</h2>

== Crimson Rift - CR ==

Crimson Rift better known as CR is one of the most common daily activities, you can join the raids for this activity by X-ing up in the Faction/Nation chat when groups are being made. It will reward you with 1000 [[Currencies|Honor Points]] and 10 [[Leadership]].[[File:Cr1.jpg|thumb|371x371px|CR Nuia Spawn Locations|alt=]]
This event takes place every 4 hours at 12:00 in game time. To check the time you can hover over the top right corner of your screen. [[File:Creast.png|thumb|368x368px|CR Haranya Spawn Locations]]

Take quests can be picked up from one of the guards in the Community Center and they will need to be returned once completed to acquire the rewards. <gallery widths="250" heights="250">
File:Crq1.jpg
File:Crq2.jpg
File:Crq3.jpg
</gallery>This event is mostly PvE where you will defend against 3 waves of monsters and 2 bosses (Hounds) at the end.

The Hounds have a quest of their own, killing one of them will reward you with 3 [[Currencies|Guilda Stars]] 2 [[Stat costumes and undergarments|Clear Synthium Shards]] and 7 Adventurer's Growthstone.

Bellow it will be shown the starting location of the rift and the end position where people will go to complete their quests and the most common callouts used to point the raid in the right direction.

== Grimgast Rift - GR ==
[[File:Grboard.jpg|thumb|293x293px|Community Center GR 1st quest]]
Grimgast Rift better known as GR is one of the most common daily activities, you can join the raids for this activity by X-ing up in the Faction/Nation chat when groups are being made.

This event takes place every 4 hours at 00:00 in game time. To check the time you can hover over the top right corner of your screen.

The first quest you take from the wooden board in the Community Center and it will require you to deliver 10 materials to a NPC, these materials can be: stone brick, lumber, leather, fabric, iron ingot. 

After handing in the materials it will give a 2nd quest that requires you to deliver 2 packs to the trebuchet.[[File:Gr1.jpg|thumb|417x417px|<nowiki>1 - Pick up quest from Community Center | 2 - 1st quest completion and pack spawn location</nowiki>]]<gallery widths="250" heights="250">

Once the trebuchet is built you can pick up a harpoon that will help you kill the mobs for the quest. The quests auto complete after the mobs are defeated.

== Mistmerrow - MM ==
Mistmerrow is a PvP event between Nuia, Haranya and the Pirate faction, where the three of them compete over the control of the towers in the map. The towers spawn in each basin of the map and need to be destroyed in order to be claimed.
[[File:Mmmap.jpg|thumb|474x474px|Mistmerrow Map]]At the start of the event you can get to Mistmerrow through one of the portals located in Capital Cities like Marianople (Nuia) and Austera (Haranya)
[[File:Mmportal.jpg|thumb|Mistmerrow portal]]
Before going inside, talk with the NPC in front of the portal and pick up the 2 quests. These will reward you with [[Currencies|Honor Points]], [[Currencies|Warrior Medals]], [[Stat costumes and undergarments|Clear Synthium Shards]] and [[Leadership]].

One of the quests will be completed once you talk with the NPC in Mistmerrow and you will receive an additional quest that will require you to kill the towers inside.<gallery widths="250" heights="250">

== Halcyona War ==
Halcyona War is a PvP event centered around destroying the enemy factions base.
[[File:Halcy1.jpg|thumb|500x500px|Halcyona Map]]
Pickup the first quest from the Capital City of your faction and enter the portal or teleport to with your own portal to Halcyona. There in your faction camp you will turn the first quest in and pickup a second quest that will require you to kill 10 enemies (this quest can be completed in a party or a raid).

The quests for this event reward you with [[Currencies|Honor Points]], [[Stat costumes and undergarments|Clear Synthium Shards]], [[Currencies|Warrior Medals]] and [[Leadership]].<gallery widths="250" heights="250">
File:Halcy2.jpg|alt=
File:Halcyq1.jpg|alt=
File:Halcyq2.jpg|alt=
</gallery>Once a faction destroys the enemy's base crystal you can loot the crystal for a Warrior Medal Chest that will reward between 5-10 Warrior Medals. If victorious you can return to the Community Center and pick up a buff that will last 2h.<gallery widths="250" heights="250">
