A Shroudlight is a type of ship lighting, which is an upgrade to the standard ship lighting, the basic [[Zephyr Lamp]]. 



Shroudlights are used to temporarily hide a ship from the compass/radar view of other ships. Whilst it doesn't render the ship invisible, it does remove the ship from any compass and/or radar view for a short time. Each Shroudlight use consumes 15 [[Eco-friendly Fuel]].

When a ship has 2 Shroudlights equipped, alternating use of both Shroudlights will double the time the ship is hidden from view of other ships' compasses and radar.


There are 4 tiers of Shroudlight available, and they are:

* '''Seabreeze''' Weight 550kg, Duration 60 sec, Cooldown 60 sec
* '''Mistral''' Weight 600kg, Duration 120 sec, Cooldown 120 sec
* '''Seastorm''' Weight 650kg, Duration 180 sec, Cooldown 180 sec
* '''Typhoon''' Weight 700kg, Duration 240 sec, Cooldown 240 sec
