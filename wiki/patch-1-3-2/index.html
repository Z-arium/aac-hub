Patch 1.3.2

Released on 27/11/24

  • Addon: A bug allowing some addons to corrupt settings file has been fixed
  • Addon: A bug preventing all addons to load if the settings file was corrupted has been fixed
  • Addon: The Misc. Settings tab in the Options menu has been reworded & the 'Load Addons' button changed to open the Addon List
  • Bug: A bug allowing Crystal Wings to be spammed while under rebirth has been fixed
  • Bug: The number of Building Management Titles to sell Raised Cottages has been reduced from 2 to 1 to match other buildings in the game
  • Bug: The [Trade Run: Dewstone to Two Crowns] quest has been fixed. It will no longer require the wrong pack type.
  • Bug: Arcane and Heroic treasure maps have been fixed
  • Bug: The Dimensional Storage Chest Design is back in store at your nearest Carpentry Merchant
  • Bug: Some Cargo NPCs were despawned to avoid misleading players.
  • Bug: Character Expansion Scrolls have been fixed
  • Commerce: Multi-purpose Aging Larders can now be replanted. After collecting an Aged Specialty Pack from one, there will be 5 seconds to interact with the larder to replant one.
  • Event: The Thanksgiving event is live! Drumstick will give you 100 Cornbread Stuffing a day until the 1st of December!
  • Harvesting: The Dawnsdrop Watering Can has been added to the game.
  • Housing: Three New Player Housing areas have been added. Two in Rokhala and one in Ahnimar. Unlike the previous ones, these ones will only allow FARMING land. This means Gardens, Farms and Gazebos.
  • Localization: Fixed many localized texts that went wrong with 1.3
  • Localization: Fixed a typo in the Longliner craft conversions
  • Mining: The Dawnsdrop Pickaxe has been added to the game.
  • Skill: Stone Alarm Call has been adjusted to no longer ragdoll the targets. This is to prevent targets from falling through the ground.
  • Zone: The Reedwind Conflict Cycle no longer expects mob/player kills. The zone will cycle on its own.
