Gift mounts

Gifts mounts are those that given by the Darus to the players and can't be obtained any other way (atleast currently).


Reindeer

Reindeer was given during Christmas 2023 celebrations through website as claimable item and it was Christmas gift for players from the Darus. With the reindeer came also full gearset for the mount.

Currently not obtainable anymore.

Base movement speed: 10.0m/s
