<h2 id="Acquisition">Acquisition</h2>
<figure><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/0/03/Abyssal_Crystal_Disintegration.png/500px-Abyssal_Crystal_Disintegration.png" width="500" height="261" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/0/03/Abyssal_Crystal_Disintegration.png 1.5x" loading="lazy"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
<p>Abyssal Shards can be obtained from various <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Dungeon&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">Dungeon</a>-related sources, including dungeon quests and bounties.
</p><p>They are primarily associated with the <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Greater_Dungeons&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">Greater Dungeons</a> and other dungeon content.
</p><p>You can also disintegrate <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Abyssal_Crystal&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">Abyssal Crystals</a> to obtain 15x Abyssal Shards per at an Archeum Workbench. Abyssal Crystals have been made tradeable on ArcheAge Classic so you can purchase them from other players. 
</p>
