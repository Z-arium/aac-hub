Abyssal Shard

Abyssal Shards are a dungeon material primarily used for crafting and upgrading Dungeon Equipment. They are obtained from various dungeon-related sources and are commonly required in recipes for dungeon equipment and other dungeon-related items.

Details

Property Value
Item Type Material
Rarity Grand
Binding Bind on Pickup
Max Stack 1,000
Vendor Price 18

Acquisition

Abyssal Shards can be obtained from various Dungeon-related sources, including dungeon quests and bounties.

They are primarily associated with the Greater Dungeons and other dungeon content.

You can also disintegrate Abyssal Crystals to obtain 15x Abyssal Shards per at an Archeum Workbench. Abyssal Crystals have been made tradeable on ArcheAge Classic so you can purchase them from other players.

Material Quantity Result
Abyssal Crystal 1 Abyssal Shard ×15

Uses

Abyssal Shards are used as a material in a variety of dungeon-related crafting recipes, particularly for Dungeon Equipment.

They can also be combined to create an Abyssal Crystal.
