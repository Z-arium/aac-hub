1c:T22f2,<p>AAClassic is a unique server as it runs on a much older version (3.5) than retail, on top of having its own set of custom changes.
</p><p>Below is a list of notable custom changes on AAClassic that make it more fun and fair than other servers.
</p>

<h2 id="Marketplace">Marketplace</h2>
<p>The Marketplace on AAClassic does not contain "Pay to Win" items unlike other servers. Only a few quality of life additions border the line of P2W (i.e., inventory slot increases, <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/daru-subscription-box">Daru Subscription Box</a>), otherwise everything else is cosmetic.
</p>
<h2 id="Vocation_&amp;_Economy"><span></span>Vocation &amp; Economy</h2>
<h3 id="Replanting">Replanting</h3>
<p>Replanting is a unique AAClassic feature. On some harvestables, you will find an interaction that allows you to harvest the resource and use a seed/sapling to put a new one in its place automatically. This quality of life feature is meant to make farming bundles and other resources much less manual labor and means players can spend more time in the world instead of having to spend precious time manually planting their fields.
</p>
<h3 id="Higher_Labor_&amp;_Rates"><span></span>Higher Labor &amp; Rates</h3>
<p>AAClassic runs on a higher labor rate of 40 labor every 5 minute (offline AND online). AAClassic also has boosted labor consumption rates AND yield rates on activities such as sports fishing and commerce. This allows you to burn your labor and make money faster, reducing the amount of grind required.
</p>
<h3 id="Schematic_Scraps">Schematic Scraps</h3>
<p>Ship component designs can now be evenstoned for Schematic Scraps. Schematic Scraps can be used to craft specific ship component designs that may otherwise be hard to find.
</p>
<h3 id="New_Recipes">New Recipes</h3>
<p>Special recipes have been added to a variety of items such as Regrade Charms, Lucky Points, Lunarite and more. Because these items were primarily sourced from P2W RNG boxes previously, such recipes were added to make them farmable &amp; part of the economy.
</p>
<h3 id="Custom_Buffs">Custom Buffs</h3>
<p>Custom recipes for buffs have been added to help free up buff slots and also provide an easier way to obtain loot drop buffs. You can find the recipes in the Folio menu 'o' by default.
</p>
<ul><li><b>Monarch of the Sea</b> (1hr): Kingdom's Shadow + Dahuta's Bubble effects. Select between +1800 Physical, Magical, or +810 Physical/Magical defenses.</li>
<li><b>Spellbook: Bulwark / Spellbook: Authority</b> (1hr): Unstoppable Will + Secret Gift + Honor Goblet. Select between an defensive or offensive spellbook and include the stats you need the most!</li>
<li><b>Pigglewiggle Feast / Pigglewiggle Stew</b> (1hr, 10 uses): Assorted Ribs + Jujube Juice + Jujube Sparkling Wine. Select between a delicious feast to boost your strength and agility or delight in a savory stew to increase your intellect and spirit.</li>
<li><b>Glittering Charcoal Milk</b> (1hr): Grants you 20% increased loot drop.</li>
<li><b>Mysterious Glittering Charcoal Milk</b>  (1hr): Grants you 50% increased loot drop.</li></ul>
<h2 id="Events">Events</h2>
<h3 id="Mistmerrow">Mistmerrow</h3>
<p>Mistmerrow has an added kill quest to encourage PvP at the event that provides additional rewards.
</p><p>Mistmerrow has been shortened from a duration of 30 minutes down to 20 minutes.
</p>
<h3 id="Crystalline_Bloodbath">Crystalline Bloodbath</h3>
<p>The <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/crystalline-bloodbath">Crystalline Bloodbath</a> is a completely custom event that currently takes place every Friday that calls all who are interested to PvP in Diamond Shores, a common occurrence back in the old days of ArcheAge. Completing the event will provide you with 30 Activity Tokens as a reward.
</p>
<h3 id="Preparing_for_the_Fish_Market">Preparing for the Fish Market</h3>
<p>Every Saturday you can get a <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/fishing-quest">Fishing quest</a> from any Fish Stand to fish and sell 10 fish to get 20 Activity Tokens.
</p>
<h3 id="The_Crimson_Threat">The Crimson Threat</h3>
<p>Every Sunday you can get a quest in Sungold, which requires to kill <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/anthalon">Anthalon</a> once. Completing this quest provides 15 Activity Tokens
</p>
<h3 id="Veroe_Harvest">Veroe Harvest</h3>
<p><a href="/aac-hub/wiki/veroe-harvest-festival">Harvest Festival event</a> where factions fight over rice drops from flowers to deliver packs in certain location in Hasla. Faction that takes the most packs on drop location first wins. 
</p>
<h3 id="Meteor_Landing">Meteor Landing</h3>
<p>At random timers, roughly once per day, a comet lands somewhere in the world, triggering waves of monsters and bosses. 
</p>

<h3 id="Prestige_Economy">Prestige Economy</h3>
