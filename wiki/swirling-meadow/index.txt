10:T9af,<p>Swirling Meadow is a brand new handcrafted (Crawling ♡) zone added in <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Patch_1.3.5h&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">Patch 1.3.5h</a> situated in southern Nuia (western continent). Swirling Meadow is a zone that primarily consists of <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/new-player-housing">New Player Housing</a> areas, players with no ownership of any farms or buildings are capable of placing a single farm within this zone.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/a/a0/MapSwirlingMeadow.jpg/500px-MapSwirlingMeadow.jpg" width="500" height="300" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/a/a0/MapSwirlingMeadow.jpg/750px-MapSwirlingMeadow.jpg 1.5x, https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/a/a0/MapSwirlingMeadow.jpg/1000px-MapSwirlingMeadow.jpg 2x" loading="lazy"></span>
</p>

<h1 id="Housing_Areas">Housing Areas</h1>
<figure><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/e/e7/MapHousingSwirlingMeadow.jpg/300px-MapHousingSwirlingMeadow.jpg" width="300" height="195" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/e/e7/MapHousingSwirlingMeadow.jpg/450px-MapHousingSwirlingMeadow.jpg 1.5x, https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/e/e7/MapHousingSwirlingMeadow.jpg/600px-MapHousingSwirlingMeadow.jpg 2x" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Full map can be found on <a rel="noopener" href="https://sermeatball.com/map">https://sermeatball.com/map</a></figcaption></figure>
<p>Swirling Meadow was expanded in Patch <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=February_5,_2026&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">February 5, 2026</a> which added a ton more space for farms, all of these under the <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/new-player-housing">New Player Housing</a> rules.
</p><p>In order to place a farm in this zone you have to not own any other buildings elsewhere. These farms cannot be traded through regular means or have their taxes prepaid. 
</p><p>Once you own a building elsewhere you will no longer be eligible to pay taxes on your Swirling Meadow farm, this means that you won't be receiving your weekly tax mail and your Swirling Meadow farm will be demolished within up to 2 weeks. 
</p>
<h1 id="Events">Events</h1>
<p>The zone has been used to house multiple events so far, we've seen a race track with custom F1 vehicles, daily Capybara-themed quests and more. 
</p>
