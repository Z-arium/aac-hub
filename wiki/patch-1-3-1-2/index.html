Patch 1.3.1.2

Released on 06/11/2024

  • Fixed Rampage Upgrade Ticket being bound
  • Fixed localization issue with selling specialty tooltip
  • Jack-o-snack will now give 2 to 4 hallowtide candies.
  • The Emerald Weapons have received new icons
  • Fixed localization for Greenman Van.
  • Fixed localization for Yata Vroom
  • Fixed localization for Yata Kart
  • Worm Compost has been reverted to its phase 2 11-hour decay
  • Duplicate gilda pack crafts have been corrected.
  • Reverted epherium ring and earring reforging to 18 wisps
  • Reduced the quest requirement for "A Good Reputation" from 3 Kadum's Barks to 1
  • Halcyona peace is back to lasting 2 hours
  • Fixed duplicate alchemy oil crafts
  • Fixed description on Pinnacled Witch House upgrade ticket.
  • Fixed localization error with various hauler/freighter crafts
  • Fixed localization with Crest Zephgr Tradepack
  • Removed Hangover Cure's "Hangover" buff requirement
  • Fixed pack requirement for Trade Run: Sailing to Solis Headlands
  • Added leadership to WS/AI quests
  • Fixed localization for Black Hello Kitty Roadster
  • Added a crafting recipe to the Proven Warrior Workbench to convert Honorforged medals to Ancestral Warrior Medals
  • Added an option to convert a Raised Mushroom House, Miner's Farmhouse, and Rancher's Farmhouse to Pinnacled Witch House
  • Fixed localization with Daru Thanksgiving Gift
  • A bug causing the Monday Kraken to spawn early has been fixed
  • [Perform] Ode to Recovery has been adjusted back to its 3.0 balance
  • Reedwind is back on a permanent War/Conflict cycle
  • The Divine Blessings clock is back for 2 weeks!
  • The Riven Gates now work with Bound Hereafter Stones
  • The value of the packs for the Settlers event ("Sushi") has been increased back to previous values
  • Treasure maps have seen a bug causing them to have coordinates set to 0 fixed. Existing maps with those coordinates (W 21.0.0, S 28.0.0) will be replaced manually, it may take some time.
  • The issue with vehicles unsummoning when exiting Sunbite Wilds has been fixed
  • The Hallowtide Candy cost for Stormduster was reduced to 150.
  • Added new housing areas in: Ahnimar, Mahadevi, Solis, Rokhala
  • Two new housing areas dedicated to new players have been opened. Read more about it here
  • The Diamond Shores outpost has had their profits increased by 35% for Charcoal and Gold, and 25% for Gilda Stars.
  • Localization for Reforging Recipes has been corrected
  • A new Quest has been added to Fragment Researcher Soa: It will appear to anyone owning an Emerald Weapon and will allow them to turn it in for a new Infused Emerald. All experience and gems will be lost!
  • Added Hallowtide Conversion recipes for Hallowtide Tokens to centralize into a single type
  • UCC items will now have a larger tooltip, showing off the design on them instead of being tiny on the item icon itself
  • All 1hr10mins peace timers are back to 2 hours
