Currencies

In ArcheAge Classic there are many different currencies for different uses. It can get overwhelming fast, so, here is explained some of them and their usages.

Permanent Tokens

Currency Icon Obtained from Usage

Credits

 Credits can be bought with real money through under Donate tab on the Website Profile. Marketplace

Golden Token

 Replacement to old Daru Tokens. Golden Tokens can be obtained by buying Daru Subscription Box from the Marketplace. One box will give one Golden Token. Marketplace > Award > Golden Token

Loyalty Token

 Once online the game provides 3 Loyalty Tokens every 60 minutes (up to 3 times a day). Marketplace > Award > Loyalty Token

Gilda Star

 Daily quests and other activities. Marketplace > Award > Gilda Star

or Mirage Island

Merit Badge

 Merit Quests and certain Achievements. Marketplace > Award > Merit Badge

Login Badge

 In the Login Tracker once you have completed the whole month the last reward is 1 Login Badge. Login Badge Reward NPC in the middle at Mirage Island once you enter.

Activity Token

 Sources of Activity Tokens The desk is located on the left side once you enter Mirage Island. Can be used to buy scrolls, decent starting trinkets, Synthium stones, Gilda Stars, Warrior Medals and to craft mounts.


Priceless Coin

 How to get Priceless Coins? The blue machine is located on the right side once you enter Mirage Island. With these coins you can buy a 30-day mounts or gliders that can be upgraded into permanent one with Activity Tokens.

Player Appreciation Coin

 Loyalty / Homecoming Chest that was given as a gift for old and returning players.

Warrior Medal

 Warrior Medals can be obtained from daily quests like Halcyona War, Mist Merrow etc. Warrior Medal crafting bench can be found in each factions bases. Use these medals to craft necklace and instruments and also to upgrade cloaks.

Comet Shard

 Comet Shards are obtained from mining and finishing the quests related to Starfall event.

Daru Festival Coin

 Obtained from all of the festivals held in Erenor. Rights side on the Mirage Island.

Broom Race Coin

 Given to those participated in the Hallowtide event on Mirage Island. Broom Race event during Hallowtide Festival. Mirage Island.

Blue Ribbon Coin

 Awarded to those who've performed well in the farming contest.

Basically placing Experia items on your land and gathering them gives points for you in the Blue Ribbon leaderboard. The leaderboard resets every week and the end of the week rewards will be sent. As a reward you can get gold and Blue Ribbon coins, the amount based on your performance with growing Experia items.

The Blue Ribbon NPC can be found -once entered to Mirage Island- on the right side. The NPC sells some furniture items.

Fisher King Coin

 Awarded to those who've performed well in fishing competitions; can be exchanged for various items. The Blue Ribbon NPC can be found -once entered to Mirage Island- on the right side. The NPC sells some decorative items and fishing trinkets.

Honor Points

 Honor Points can be gained by participating in conflicts/wars, arenas, Honor quests, or staying active in Auroria. Press C > and the cart image next to Honor Point icon.

Vocation Badges

 Vocation Badges allow you to purchase special vocation items. They can be earned through:
  • Family quests
  • Blue Salt Merit quests
  • Community Center Daily quests
  • Remodeled House quests
  • Farmer's Workstation Meal quests
 Press C > and the cart image next to Vocation Badge icon.

Prestige

 Can be obtained by participating in guild quests. Community > Guild > Manage > Prestige Shop
