Auroria

Introduction

Auroria is the North most continent in the world of ArcheAge and is for adventurers typically level 50 and higher.

Auroria has 10 different zone to live in and explore. Each with their own perks and downsides.

  • Diamond Shores
  • Golden Ruins
  • Sungold Fields
  • Calmlands
  • Marcala
  • Heedmar
  • Nuimari
  • Exeloch
  • Reedwind
  • Mistmerrow

Getting to Auroria

Getting to Auroria is pretty easy. There is a portal in Multiple locations all over the Eastern and Western Continent.

On the east continent a portal can be found in Austera in the Solis headlands and in Caernord in Ynystere. On the west continent a portal can be found in Marianople and in Seachild Wharf in Cinderstone Moor. You can also find a portal on the Pirate island of Growlgate in the Stormraw Sound. The portal will have 2 options. The first being to the housing area and the second being straight to the faction base.

You can also just sail there from either continent if you are up for the boat ride but it is a LONG ride.

Castles

One of the major features of Auroria (and ArcheAge in general) are the castles that you will find on Auroria. They are owned by the guild that has claimed it in the most recent siege cycle which occurs every X weeks.

Players can earn Lord Coins by working on the territory and completing various quests.

Burning Logs and Mineral Waters

Certain zones have naturally spawning resource nodes, otherwise known as "logs" and "waters", which are unique ways to take advantage of owning land in Auroria. They are generally contested by other players and you will have to fight to claim them.

Logs spawn in Calmlands and Marcala, and can be used to light Regrade Braziers for a chance to get Sunpoints, Moonpoints, and Starpoints.

Waters spawn in Heedmar and Nuimari, and can be used to water Archeum Trees for a chance to get Moonlight, Sunlight, and Starlight archeum.

Alternatively, players can use Lord Coins to craft a Blazing Tree or Mineral Water Pump that they can place on their land. After an initial timer, these will become a source of 10 of the respective resource. This is a much less risky and reliable way to take advantage of your Auroria land, but the cost of the Lord Coins comes out of your profit!
