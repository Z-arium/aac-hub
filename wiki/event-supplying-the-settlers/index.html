Event: Supplying the Settlers

The Supplying the Settlers event is also known as "Daru Chefs" or "Sushi" event.

Goals and Rewards

The event consists of three activities:

  1. Collecting Pure Ice by breaking Diamond Shores icebergs, and bringing it to Freedich outposts. The Pure Ice can then be inserted into the Ice Boxes next to the Sushi Chef. Make sure to accept the quest from the nearby NPC to recieve a reward of 30 Lord's Pence for your contribution.
    • Each outpost contains 5 Ice Boxes
    • A single Pure Ice trade pack is enough to fill an Ice Box, granting it 3 charges, which along with a Fish Fillet, allows you to produce a Fish Food Supply pack.
    • Once a Ice Box has been emptied, it'll take somewhere between 10 - 15 minutes to properly clean, before you're able to use it again!
  2. Collecting Fish Fillets by bringing Saltwater Fish to the Sushi Chef
    • Fish Fillets can be acquired by supplying the Sushi Chef with freshly caught Sports Fish
    • Fry and normal size fish will provide 2 fillets per fish.
    • Gargantuan sized fish will provide between 3 and 4 filets.
    • Fish Fillets are tradeable, and do not have an expiry, allowing you to stock up!
  3. Creating Fish Food Supply packs by interacting with a filled Ice Box while having a Fish Fillet in your inventory and bringing the created packs to one of the 6 Auroran Settlers camps. These are the areas in Sungold Fields, Calmlands, Marcala, Heedmar, Nuimari, and Exeloch, which are annoted with the "Faction Base" icons on your map.

As a reward for your efforts during this event, you will be rewarded with Lord's Pence, allowing a new way for users to generate Lord's Coins, whilst not being part of a ruling castle guild.

You will recieve -

  • 30 Lord's Pence per Ice Pack succesfully delivered to the Daru Chef's Ice Boxes.
  • 70 Lord's Pence per Fish Food Supply pack succesfully delivered to the Auroran Settlers.
