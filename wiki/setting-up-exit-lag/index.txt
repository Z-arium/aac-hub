<h2 id="How_to_Set_up_Exit_Lag:">How to Set up Exit Lag:</h2>
<p>Please refer to the image on the right if you get confused.
</p>
<ol><li>Search for Archeage</li>
<li>Click the Archeage entry</li>
<li>Set your Game Region to CA Beauharnois01</li>
<li>Press Apply Routes.</li></ol>
<p>Congratulation your game will now be routed with ExitLag! If you are still having issues make sure the switch in the top left is on like in the picture.
</p><p><a rel="noopener" href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=loLhBDwxoSs">Exitlag vs No Exitlag comparison video</a>
</p>
<h2 id="Checking_Ping_to_server:">Checking Ping to server:</h2>
<h4 id="If_you're_not_in_game"><span></span>If you're not in game</h4>
<ol><li>Click on the link OVH Speed Test</li>
<li>Click on Server selection</li>
<li>Select [CA] Beauharnois</li>
<li>Start the test</li></ol>
<h4 id="If_you're_in_game"><span></span>If you're in game</h4>
<ol><li>Press Ctrl Shift Esc to open Task Manager</li>
<li>Select the Performance Tab</li>
<li>Click the 3 dots and open Resource Monitor</li>
<li>Select the Network tab in Resource Monitor</li>
<li>Under TCP Connections locate archeage.exe</li>
