The '''[https://aa-classic.com/database/item/9002392 Fishing Stand]''' is a craftable furniture item that can only be placed on a 44x44 Mansions.

Once placed the Fishing Stand functions similarly to the Fishing Stands found in Erenor, allowing players to sell fish directly to it.

== Function ==

The Fishing Stand provides a way to sell caught fish directly at a 44x44 Mansion which opens up the possibility of new fishing hot spots to emerge.

Can only be placed on a '''44x44 Mansion'''.
Allows players to sell eligible fish.
Functions like a regular Fishing Stand for fish sales.
Can be used by players with access to the mansion.

== Crafting ==
[[File:Archeage rqR6H29h8M.png|thumb|right|The recipe can be found in the Folio (O) by searching "Fish Crane" then selecting the recipe it is used for.]]
To make a Fishing Stand you are going to need quite a long list of materials, the recipe requires a large quantity of Lumber, various Ingots, 4x Thunderstruck Trees and also the Fishing Entrepreneur Permit which you can purchase from a [[General Merchant]] for 5000g.
