Starter mounts

Starter mounts or so called "race mounts" are the mounts that each race gets on the start of the game. All starter mounts can be bought with any character. Starter mounts need to be bought from the place each race starts their journey. All these mounts need to be raised to adults before they can be used.

Lilyut Horse (Nuian)

Lilyut Horse can be obtained from an NPC in Solzreed Peninsula, Wardton with gold. There are 3 different color variations.

Mount
Basic
Gray Lilyut Horse
This horse is from the Lilyut Hills, a region known for its fine horses.
The Gray Lilyut Horse has the following skills:
Lv5: Run!Run!Mana 20Instant30sec CooldownIncreases Pet Move Speed +50% for 10sec.
Can't be used while carrying a trade pack.
Lv10: Mounted AttackMounted AttackInstant2sec CooldownDeals 350% of Melee Attack as Physical Damage with a chance of Tripping targets while mounted. Enables rider to attack an enemy behind them when in Bloodlust mode.
Lv15: Back KickBack KickInstant18sec CooldownStrengthens the mount's rearward kick, dealing (varies) Physical Damage and Tripping the enemy.
Lv20: Mounted DefenseMounted DefenseInstant1min CooldownGrants mount and rider immunity to Ranged Damage, Knockback, and Stun for 5sec.
Lv25: BreakthroughBreakthroughInstant30sec CooldownCharges forward for 3sec, breaking through enemy lines. Inflicts Knockback and Trip on affected enemies.
This skill counts as a Push effect.
Can't be used while carrying a trade pack.

Summons a mount.

Pangolin can be obtained from an NPC in Sunbite Wilds with gold. There are 3 different color variations.

Mount
Basic
Blackscale Pangolin
Pangolins are revered by Warborn as aspirational symbols: outwardly, pangolins have a tough, armored hide, but inwardly, they are incredibly gentle and loyal creatures.
This mount has the following skills:
Lv10: Run!Run!Mana 20Instant30sec CooldownIncreases Pet Move Speed +50% for 10sec.
Can't be used while carrying a trade pack.
Lv20: SlamSlamInstant18sec CooldownInstructs the pangolin to wind up, then smash the ground to create an earthquake. Damages all enemies within 3m and decreases their Move Speed -30% for 3 seconds.
Lv30: PangochargePangochargeInstant24sec CooldownTriggers a pounce that inflicts damage on a distant target and knocks it backward (Trips for 2 seconds instead if it's Slowed).
This skill counts as a Push effect.
Can't be used while carrying a trade pack.
Lv40: Stubborn DashStubborn DashInstant1min CooldownTriggers a 3 second forward roll.
Increases the pangolin and rider's Defense and Magic Defense +5000, and grants immunity to Fear and Slow.
Can't be used while carrying a trade pack.
Lv50: PatiencePatienceInstant30sec CooldownGrants defensive buffs to allies within 16m for 5 seconds. Effect ends early if you dismount.
Use:
Summons a mount.
Base Move Speed 8.5m/s
