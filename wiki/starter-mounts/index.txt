12:T11d96,<p>Starter mounts or so called "race mounts" are the mounts that each race gets on the start of the game. All starter mounts can be bought with any character. Starter mounts need to be bought from the place each race starts their journey. All these mounts need to be raised to adults before they can be used. 
</p>
