One of the quests gives a  chest as a reward that contains 3 different scroll pieces or Winter Maiden coin. After collecting all 3 of the scrolls players can craft them into a book item that can be placed into the player house.

Bring the npc 100 Iron Ingot.
[[File:Iron ingot npc.png|none|frame]]



=== [The First Step of Ice Fishing] ===
Craft a wrapped Miroir Ice Fishing Rod, bench to craft is next to this npc.

Gives the scroll chest as a reward. If all 3 of the scrolls are collected it can be crafted into a book that can be placed in a house.
