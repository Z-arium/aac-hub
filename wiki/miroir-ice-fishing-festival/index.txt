<h2 id="Through_the_Miroir_Glass_book">Through the Miroir Glass book</h2>
<p>One of the quests gives a  chest as a reward that contains 3 different scroll pieces or Winter Maiden coin. After collecting all 3 of the scrolls players can craft them into a book item that can be placed into the player house.
</p>
<figure><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/b/b6/Through_miroir_scrolls.png" width="320" height="477" loading="lazy"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
<p><br>
</p>
<h2 id="Quests">Quests</h2>
<h3 id="[Hungry_Blizzard_Cub]"><span></span>[Hungry Blizzard Cub]</h3>
<p>Feed meat to the polar bear cubs 3/3.
</p>
<figure><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/8/8b/Bear_meat.png" width="244" height="482" loading="lazy"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
<figure><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/6/6e/Polar_bear_cub.png" width="226" height="285" loading="lazy"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
<p><br>
</p>
<h3 id="[A_Legendary_Fisherman]"><span></span>[A Legendary Fisherman]</h3>
<p>Bring the npc 100 Iron Ingot.
</p>
<figure><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/4/41/Iron_ingot_npc.png" width="300" height="461" loading="lazy"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
<p><br>
</p>
<h3 id="[The_First_Step_of_Ice_Fishing]"><span></span>[The First Step of Ice Fishing]</h3>
<p>Craft a wrapped Miroir Ice Fishing Rod, bench to craft is next to this npc.
</p><p>Gives the scroll chest as a reward. If all 3 of the scrolls are collected it can be crafted into a book that can be placed in a house.
</p>
<figure><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/e/ed/Fishing_rod.png" width="243" height="419" loading="lazy"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
<figure><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/3/3f/Ice_fishing_rod.png" width="330" height="348" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Crafting requires 5 lumber. The fishing rod will expire and disappear after 9min after opening it.</figcaption></figure>
<p><br>
</p>
<h3 id="[Patience_and_a_Bit_of_Luck]"><span></span>[Patience  and a Bit of Luck]</h3>
<p><i>This quest can be repeated as many times as long as the player has the Miroir fishing rod to fish.</i>
</p><p>Fish Smelts 10/10 from the ice holes. Drops are random. <b>Right click the fishing icon to fish automatically.</b>
