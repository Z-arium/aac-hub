Abbreviations

Common abbreviations, with descriptions and link to external/internal resources if available.

Events

X something

Means that someone is making a raid for event and asks other people to join them. In Archeage Classic there is no party search, so players have to resort on addons that do the automatic invitation based on a message in the chat. If someone is making a raid for Whalesong there will be a lot of x ws messages and by typing it yourself you will get an automatic invite to this raid. Same goes to any other content with X and name of the event or boss. Usually raid leader announces in the nation/faction chat what they are about to do example: "x cr is up".

  • CR - Crimson Rift
  • GR - Grimghast Rift
  • OC - Ocleera Rift
  • WH/WS - Whalesong Harbor siege
  • Aegis - Aegis Island defending the seal
  • Halcy - Halcyona, Battle for the Golden Plains
  • Rice/Veroe - Veroe Harvest Festival
  • Fort - Fortress which appears in Diamond Shores which requires purifying
  • Puri - Purification of castle areas
  • DS - Friday Diamond shore pvp event
  • Fish - Saturday fishing event
  • Andy/Anth - Anthalon, and Sunday anthalon kill event
  • Comet - Custom nation vs nation content with bunch of quests, Starfall
  • Lusca - Luscas are so called mini-krakens that spawn in the middle of the sea. Each big city has a big portal that ports players closer from where they continue to the water bosses with a ships. A quest is given near the portal to kill 5 lusca in order to get quest reward.

  • Obs/Obi - Obsidian
  • ss - Short spear
