<h3 id="Events">Events</h3>
<p><b>X <i>something</i></b>
</p><p>Means that someone is making a raid for event and asks other people to join them. In Archeage Classic there is no party search, so players have to resort on addons that do the automatic invitation based on a message in the chat. If someone is making a raid for Whalesong there will be a lot of x ws messages and by typing it yourself you will get an automatic invite to this raid. Same goes to any other content with X and name of the event or boss. Usually raid leader announces in the nation/faction chat what they are about to do example: "x cr is up".

If someone is making a raid for Whalesong there will be a lot of x ws messages and by typing it yourself you will get an automatic invite to this raid. Same goes to any other content with X and name of the event or boss. Usually raid leader announces in the nation/faction chat what they are about to do example: "x cr is up".
