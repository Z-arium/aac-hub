<h3 id="Overview">Overview</h3>
<p>Anchoring Emblems are essential tools in upgrading equipment without the risk of breakage. They come in two varieties: <b>Celestial</b> and <b>Divine</b>, each corresponding to the enhancement of Celestial or Divine-grade Weapons, Accessories, or Armor. This page outlines the crafting process, materials needed for their creation and use.
</p>
<hr>
<h3 id="Crafting_Anchoring_Emblems">Crafting Anchoring Emblems</h3>
<h4 id="Materials_Required">Materials Required</h4>
<figure><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/5/5c/Anchor_recipe.png/300px-Anchor_recipe.png" width="300" height="253" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/5/5c/Anchor_recipe.png 1.5x" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Anchoring Emblem recipe</figcaption></figure>
<p>To craft an Anchoring Emblem, you will need to visit one of the following locations, along with the required materials of <b>100 Shards</b> and <b>2 Honorforged Medals</b> (Available from the Honor Shop):
</p>
<ul><li><b>Masonry Workbench:</b> Requires 130,000 Masonry Proficiency</li>
<li><b>Select</b> <b>Community Centers</b> with <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/grand-improved-workbench">Improved Workbenches</a> (Level 2 or higher): <b>Does not require Proficiency</b>.</li></ul>
<h4 id="Obtaining_Shards">Obtaining Shards</h4>
<p>Shards are salvaged from Epherium or Delphinad equipment. Note that only equipment of <b>Unique</b> grade or higher qualifies for salvaging. The number of shards obtained depends on the slot of Equipment:
</p><p><b>Weapons</b>
</p>
<ul><li>Any weapon: <b>55 Shards</b></li></ul>
<figure><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/e/e3/Lonestone.png" width="204" height="170" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Available "Lone Stones"</figcaption></figure>
<p><b>Armor</b>
</p>
<ul><li>Hood: <b>33 Shards</b></li>
<li>Chest: <b>55 Shards</b></li>
<li>Waist: <b>21 Shards</b></li>
<li>Sleeves: <b>21 Shards</b></li>
<li>Gloves: <b>33 Shards</b></li>
<li>Pants: <b>42 Shards</b></li>
<li>Boots: <b>33 Shards</b></li></ul>
<p><b>Accessories</b>
</p>
<ul><li>Any accessory: <b>55 Shards</b></li></ul>
<p>To salvage equipment, you must craft and use a <b>"Lone Stone"</b>, of the respective Sun, Moon or Star type, which requires <b>50,000 Alchemy Proficiency</b> as well as one of the respective <b>Archeum Essence</b> in order to craft.
</p><p><br>
</p>
<hr>
<h3 id="Crafting_Locations_Without_Proficiency">Crafting Locations Without Proficiency</h3>
<figure><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/d/de/Development_complete.png/300px-Development_complete.png" width="300" height="33" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/d/de/Development_complete.png 1.5x" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Level 3 Community Center</figcaption></figure>
<p>If you lack Masonry Proficiency, you can craft Anchoring Emblems at <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/grand-improved-workbench">Improved Workbenches</a> in <b>Level 2 or higher Community Centers</b>. These locations include:
</p>
<ul><li>Marianople</li>
<li>Hellswamp</li>
<li>Sanddeep</li>
<li>Halcyona</li>
<li>Falcorth Plains</li>
<li>Rookborne Basin</li>
<li>Windscour Savannah</li></ul>
<p>To check the level of a Community Center, view the <b>green bars</b> at the bottom right of your map (<b>Default 'M'</b>) in the corresponding zone. A fully leveled <b>Community Center</b> displays three green bars.
