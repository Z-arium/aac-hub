Ships

In ArcheAge Classic Ships can be obtained in various ways, such as buying designs with Gilda Stars, grinding specific mobs for scraps of a design, or buying ships already built with marketplace currencies.

Many ships designs are tradeable, so you can buy them from other players.

Ships

Clippers & Speedboats

Ship name Icon Obtained from Cost Features
Adventure Clipper Design can be bought from Mirage Island or Marketplace. 50 Gilda Stars It has a cannon in the right side of the ship.
Harpoon Clipper Design can be bought from Mirage Island or Marketplace. 50 Gilda Stars It has a harpoon in the front side of the ship.
Seawhip Speedboat Design can be bought from the Marketplace or can be crafted in Miarge. 1200 Gilda Stars or 6 Login Badges A small and fast boat, that can carry at least 4 people.
Seaskimmer Speedboat Speedboat Upgrade Ticket can be bought from Marketplace 400 Gilda Stars A small and fast boat, that can carry at least 4 people. Has 4 packs slots.

Merchant Schooner & Fishing Boats

Ship name Icon Obtained from Cost Features
Merchant Schooner Design can be bought from Mirage Island or Marketplace. 400 Gilda Stars It can store up to 22 trade packs. Also comes with a telescope useful for detecting ships in your map.
Fish-Find Longliner Design can be bought from Mirage Island or Marketplace. 250 Gilda Stars It can hold up to 5 small or medium fish and 2 gargantuan fish.
Predator Longliner Ship can be bought from Marketplace. 700 Merit Badges This ship doesn't needs to be build, it can be directly used after buying it from marketplace.

It can hold up to 5 small or medium fishes and 2 gargantuan fish.

Moby Drake Fish-Find Longliner and Predator Longliner can be transformed

into this ship using the Moby Drake Upgrade Ticket from Marketplace

2,000 Credits This ship upgrade is only a cosmetic change.

Doesn't have additional features than the Fish-Find Longliner nor Predator Longliner.

Small Warships

Ship name Icon Obtained from Cost Features
Lutesong Junk Design can be bought from Mirage Island or Marketplace. 250 Gilda Stars A small warship, that has 2 sails, 1 figurehead and 8 cannons (4 per side).
Eznan Cutter Design can be bought from Mirage Island or Marketplace. 250 Gilda Stars A small warship, that has 2 sails, 1 figurehead and 8 cannons (4 per side).
Growling Yawl Designs scraps can be obtained from Ghost Ships loot and mobs in the Warsmith Castle. 55 Growling Yawl Design Fragments. A small warship, that has 2 sails, 1 figurehead and 8 cannons (4 per side).

Has more HP and speed than the other small warships.Can also carry more players.

Pufferfish ship Design can be bought from Marketplace. 350 Gilda Stars. The giant cannon in the front of the pufferfish ship dealsmassive damage.

(Doesn't deal any damage to bosses).

Medium Warship & Luxury Liner

Ship name Icon Obtained from Cost Features
Enoan Galleon Designs scraps can be obtained from Luscas loot.

Whole design can be obtained from Leviathan loot.

5,000 Enoan Galleon Design Scraps. A Medium warship, that has 3 sails, 2 figurehead and 10 cannons (5 per side).

Is capable of dealing massive collision damage.

Luxury Liner Design can be crafted on a Carpentry Workbench. 2800 Gilda Stars A massive party-ship, that is slow and heavy.
