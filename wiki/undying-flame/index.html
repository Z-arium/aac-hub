Undying Flame

This article is a stub, if you have confirmed information relating to the boss fight, please contact Silverbranch on discord

The Undying Flame is the final boss of the Starfall event. Not much is currently known about this new adversary, and anyone with confirmed information should contact Silverbranch so that it can be recorded, that which we do know is recorded below.



Mechanics

Throughout the fight the Undying Flame has an alternating 30 second Flame Shield buff, disicentivizing melee and magic damage respectively whilst active.

  • Flame Shield (Anti Magic) - Causes all magic damage recieved to heal the Undying Flame as well as regenerating its man.
  • Flame Shield (Anti Melee) - Causes all melee damage to be reflected, as well as inflicting an area of effect Scorch debuff upon recieving a melee hit.

Late in the fight the Undying flame will spawn flames on the ground and appears to gain benefits from being in the flames, meaning it should be dragged out.
