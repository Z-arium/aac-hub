12:T40d0,<p>Enlightened Ayanad Accessories are custom accessories that was added in <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/patch-1-3-6">Patch 1.3.6</a>. Enlightened Ayanad Accessories allows you to improve on your Ayanad Accessories by changing their stats, adding a skill to your necklace or changing their effects into the whole new Library Accessory lineup. 
</p>

<h1 id="Enlightening_an_Ayanad_Accessory">Enlightening an Ayanad Accessory</h1>
<h2 id="Step_1_-_Ayanad_Accessory">Step 1 - Ayanad Accessory</h2>
<p>To begin the process you first need to obtain an Ayanad Accessory that you want to Enlighten. Do not worry about the main stats it gives, just consider which "Equip effect" you want. When enlightening it you'll be able to apply any stat combination onto them that you desire. 
</p><p>This is just my opinion but considering this process is quite resource intensive I recommend at bare minimum to only enlighten Divine+ Ayanad Accessories. 
</p><p>Use the in-game <a rel="noopener" href="https://aa-classic.com/database/encyclopedia/guide/9000012%7C">Equipment Encyclopedia</a> to see each Enlightened version, their equip effects are slightly improved. 
</p>

16:T2a43,Enlightened Ayanad Accessories are custom accessories that was added in [[Patch 1.3.6]]. Enlightened Ayanad Accessories allows you to improve on your Ayanad Accessories by changing their stats, adding a skill to your necklace or changing their effects into the whole new Library Accessory lineup. 
= Enlightening an Ayanad Accessory =
== Step 1 - Ayanad Accessory ==
To begin the process you first need to obtain an Ayanad Accessory that you want to Enlighten. Do not worry about the main stats it gives, just consider which "Equip effect" you want. When enlightening it you'll be able to apply any stat combination onto them that you desire. 

This is just my opinion but considering this process is quite resource intensive I recommend at bare minimum to only enlighten Divine+ Ayanad Accessories. 

Use the in-game [https://aa-classic.com/database/encyclopedia/guide/9000012| Equipment Encyclopedia] to see each Enlightened version, their equip effects are slightly improved. 

== Step 2 - Enlightened Runestone ==
To enlighten an accessory you'll need to use an [[File:Enlightened Runetone.png|32px]] <span style="color: rgb(249, 82, 82);">Enlightened Runestone</span> on the Ayanad Accessory you have prepared.

Once crafted you will need to go to the top floor in Library to use the [[File:Enlightened Runetone.png|32px]] <span style="color: rgb(249, 82, 82);">Enlightened Runestone</span> on your accessory. The easiest way to achieve this is to buy a [[File:Ayanad Scroll 3rd Floor Lobby.png|32px]] <span style="color: rgb(186, 151, 109);">Ayanad Scroll: 3rd Floor Lobby</span> from the Auction House to instantly teleport to the top of Library, it can be used from anywhere in Diamond Shores.
=== Step 2.1 - Obtaining the Dormant Runestone ===
The [[File:Dormant Runestone.png|32px]] <span style="color: rgb(186, 151, 109);">Dormant Runestone</span> can rarely be obtained from 
[[File:Scholar's Research Bundle.png|32px]] <span style="color: rgb(119, 176, 100);">Scholar's Research Bundle</span>, [[File:Scholar's Research Bundle.png|32px]] <span style="color: rgb(119, 176, 100);">Librarian's Research Bundle</span> and [[File:Scholar's Research Bundle.png|32px]] <span style="color: rgb(119, 176, 100);">Dean's Research Bundle</span> which are obtained by killing monsters in Library.
