Enlightened Ayanad Accessories

Enlightened Ayanad Accessories are custom accessories that was added in Patch 1.3.6. Enlightened Ayanad Accessories allows you to improve on your Ayanad Accessories by changing their stats, adding a skill to your necklace or changing their effects into the whole new Library Accessory lineup.

Enlightening an Ayanad Accessory

Step 1 - Ayanad Accessory

To begin the process you first need to obtain an Ayanad Accessory that you want to Enlighten. Do not worry about the main stats it gives, just consider which "Equip effect" you want. When enlightening it you'll be able to apply any stat combination onto them that you desire.

This is just my opinion but considering this process is quite resource intensive I recommend at bare minimum to only enlighten Divine+ Ayanad Accessories.

Use the in-game Equipment Encyclopedia to see each Enlightened version, their equip effects are slightly improved.

Step 2 - Enlightened Runestone

To enlighten an accessory you'll need to use an Enlightened Runestone on the Ayanad Accessory you have prepared.

Recipe Enlightened Runestone
Proficiency Handicrafts 50,000
Labor Cost × 200
Workbench Disciple's Workbench
Materials Dormant Runestone × 1
Starlight Archeum Essence × 5
Ayanad Starlight Blessing × 1
Blue Salt Hammer × 50
Use:
Spend 10 labor to unlock the secrets of Ayanad.

Once crafted you will need to go to the top floor in Library to use the Enlightened Runestone on your accessory. The easiest way to achieve this is to buy a Ayanad Scroll: 3rd Floor Lobby from the Auction House to instantly teleport to the top of Library, it can be used from anywhere in Diamond Shores.

Step 2.1 - Obtaining the Dormant Runestone

The Dormant Runestone can rarely be obtained from Scholar's Research Bundle, Librarian's Research Bundle and Dean's Research Bundle which are obtained by killing monsters in Library.

Step 2.2 - Obtaining the Ayanad Starlight Blessing

Recipe Ayanad Starlight Blessing
Proficiency N/A
Labor Cost × 500
Workbench Ipnysh Altar
Materials Acid Vessel × 2
Complete Cursed Armor × 2
Earthmana Leaf × 100
Prismatic Diamond × 25
Ayanad Accessory Scroll × 1
Black Pearl × 5

Acid Vessel × 1 requires Acid Goblet × 200 and 1,500 Labor.

  • Acid Goblet can rarely be obtained when slaying monsters in Aegis Island.

Complete Cursed Armor × 1 requires Cursed Armor Scrap × 200 and 1,500 Labor.

  • Cursed Armor Scrap can rarely be obtained when slaying monsters in Whalesong Harbor.

Step 3 - Lunasigils, Applying Stats

Once an accessory is enlightened it loses its main stats. You can apply new stats on it using a Lunasigil.

Example: You prepared an Ayanad Wave Earring for enlightenment.
