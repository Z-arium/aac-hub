12:Tb9c,<h3 id="Location">Location</h3>
<p>Event starts in Marianople and Austera depending on which nation the character belongs to. The questline continues in Miroir Tundra, Austera and Marianople.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/a/ac/Wintermaidenmessenger.png" width="373" height="405" loading="lazy"></span>
</p>
<h3 id="Quests">Quests</h3>
<h4 id="[A_Prayer_for_the_Winter_Maiden]"><span></span>[A Prayer for the Winter Maiden]</h4>
<p>Messenger asks player to touch the Christmas tree behind her. This gives <b>Kyprosas Authority</b> buff. The buff can be acquired as many times as needed so its a good buff to crab before world bosses etc.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/2/2c/Kyprosasauthority.png" width="207" height="136" loading="lazy"></span>
</p>
<h4 id="[Winter_Maiden_Festival_in_Miroir_Tundra]"><span></span>[Winter Maiden Festival in Miroir Tundra]</h4>
<p>Messenger asks player to go to Miroir Tundra and talk to King Penguini Alfredo.
</p>
<h4 id="[Icy_Intrdures]"><span></span>[Icy Intrdures]</h4>
<p>Kill 5 Cursed Snowmen and collect 5 Frozen Souls and 5 Stolen Festival Decorations. Locations shown as pictures in Marianople and Austera below.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/b/b2/Cursedsnowman.png" width="236" height="172" loading="lazy"></span>
</p>
<figure><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/a/a6/Marianoplesnowman.png/580px-Marianoplesnowman.png" width="580" height="366" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/a/a6/Marianoplesnowman.png 1.5x" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Marianople</figcaption></figure>
<figure><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/5/5d/Austerasnowman.png/586px-Austerasnowman.png" width="586" height="375" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/5/5d/Austerasnowman.png 1.5x" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Austera</figcaption></figure>
<p><br>
</p>
<h4 id="[Winter_Maiden_Festival_in_Miroir_Tundra]_2"><span></span>[Winter Maiden Festival in Miroir Tundra]</h4>
<p>Go to Miroir Tundra to visit King Penguini to wish him happy festival.
</p>
<h4 id="[A_Very_Merry_Mistmerrow]"><span></span>[A Very Merry Mistmerrow]</h4>
<p>Go to Mistmerrow, Crimson Altar and talk to Winter Maiden Messenger. Best time to go is peace time since the quest below is easier to complete without PvP.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/b/b5/Wintermaidenmessengermistmerrow.png" width="331" height="316" loading="lazy"></span>
</p>
<h4 id="[Blessing_the_Basins]"><span></span>[Blessing the Basins]</h4>
<p>Equip a Snowball Basket and help to build one of the Snowmen. Press F on the snowman to build. After that talk to the Mistmerrow Maiden Messenger.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/b/be/Snowballsnow.png" width="352" height="223" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
