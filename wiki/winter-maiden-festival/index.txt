1d:T619,=== Location ===
Event starts in Marianople and Austera depending on which nation the character belongs to. The questline continues in Miroir Tundra, Austera and Marianople.

[[File:Wintermaidenmessenger.png]]

=== Quests ===

==== [A Prayer for the Winter Maiden] ====
Messenger asks player to touch the Christmas tree behind her. This gives '''Kyprosas Authority''' buff. The buff can be acquired as many times as needed so its a good buff to crab before world bosses etc.

[[File:Kyprosasauthority.png]]

==== [Winter Maiden Festival in Miroir Tundra] ====
Messenger asks player to go to Miroir Tundra and talk to King Penguini Alfredo.

==== [Icy Intrdures] ====
Kill 5 Cursed Snowmen and collect 5 Frozen Souls and 5 Stolen Festival Decorations. Locations shown as pictures in Marianople and Austera below.

[[File:Cursedsnowman.png]]
[[File:Marianoplesnowman.png|none|thumb|580x580px|Marianople]]
[[File:Austerasnowman.png|none|thumb|586x586px|Austera]]


==== [Winter Maiden Festival in Miroir Tundra] ====
Go to Miroir Tundra to visit King Penguini to wish him happy festival.

==== [A Very Merry Mistmerrow] ====
Go to Mistmerrow, Crimson Altar and talk to Winter Maiden Messenger. Best time to go is peace time since the quest below is easier to complete without PvP.

[[File:Wintermaidenmessengermistmerrow.png]]

==== [Blessing the Basins] ====
Equip a Snowball Basket and help to build one of the Snowmen. Press F on the snowman to build. After that talk to the Mistmerrow Maiden Messenger.
