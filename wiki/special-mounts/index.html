To obtain the Siegeram Taurus you have to summon a boss at the Songspinner Field in Sanddeep. In order to summon it, a Anima Extraction Contraption is required, which can be crafted like this:

Recipe Anima Extraction Contraption × 1
Labor Cost× 20
WorkbenchCarpentry Workbench
ProficiencyMachining
Materials
Inferno Engine × 1
Armored Frame × 1
Mechanical Spring × 4
Recipe Cost2123 4 79

Once you have aquired that item, head to the Songspinner Field in Sanddeep and find a Minotaur Mage. Use the item on it and it will turn into a Siegram Taurus which will drop Anima Infused Taurus Horn × 2 upon defeat. These can be used to craft the Siegeram Taurus Bundle.

When unwrapped it will provide the following items:

Siegeram Taurus

Siegeram Taurus Faceplate

Siegeram Taurus Saddle

Siegeram Taurus Joint Plating.

It wasn't until the Demon War that the construct's military applications were fully realized; historians still credit its terrifying strength as one of the city's most valuable assets in their defense against the Warborn.

Aquestria

Aquestria can be obtained by collecting certain materials and crafting it. This grants the Grand grade version of the mount.

Recipe Aquestria × 1
Labor Cost× 800
WorkbenchPet Accessory Workbench
ProficiencyPet Gear
Materials
Arcadian Seafoam × 20
Abyssal Shard × 100
Eternal Winter Chill × 20
Seaweed Reins × 1
Prime Lunarite × 5
Swirling Whirlpool × 50

After acquiring the Grand grade Aquestria, there is a quest available at the Sea of Drowned Love entrance in the Sunspeck Sea. It involves killing the world boss Nazar nearby. This quest rewards the Rare version of the mount, which is 1.0 m/s faster.

Aquestria Questline

First travel to the Freedich and meet up with the npc called Angeli. She will give a quest to kill Dahuta's Defenders and Seabugs.

(tba 9000002 questid)

- Kill Dahuta's Defenders 0/100

- Kill Seabugs 0/100

After fullfilling the quest return to Freedich Island and hand in the quest to receive the Seaweed Reins.

Then travel to the entrance of the Sea of Drowned Love and receive the last quest for your Aquestria.

(tba 9000013 questid)

- Kill Nazar

- Craft and summon the Aquestria

After completing this quest you will recieve your Aquestria.
