1c:T7b5,= Brave Warrior's Medal =
[[File:Archeage cyZHKOXXkw.png|thumb|right]]
'''Brave Warrior's Medals''' are a special event currency introduced during the [https://aa-classic.com/news/3rd-anniversary-patch-notes 3rd Anniversary] celebration. They are primarily used to guarantee the crafting of [[Halcyona Necklace]]s up to Rank 11.

Brave Warrior's Medals are obtained by participating in several faction PvP events during the anniversary celebration, including [[Mistmerrow War]], [[Golden Plains Battle]], [[Veroe Harvest Festival]], and [[Great Heist]].

== Obtaining ==

Brave Warrior's Medals can be obtained from the following events:

== Usage ==
[[File:Archeage 2nHKDbRC8E.png|thumb|right|It is recommended to only use this currency on Rank 11 as that will on average reduce the amount of Warrior's Medals you'll need for Rank 12 and beyond the most.]]
Brave Warrior's Medals are used to guarantee the crafting of Halcyona Necklaces.

They can be used to craft Unsealed Halcyona Necklaces up to '''Rank 11 (Epic)''', allowing players to bypass the normal randomness associated with obtaining a necklace of the desired rank.
