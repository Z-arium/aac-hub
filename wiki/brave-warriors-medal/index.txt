<p><b>Brave Warrior's Medals</b> are a special event currency introduced during the <a rel="noopener" href="https://aa-classic.com/news/3rd-anniversary-patch-notes">3rd Anniversary</a> celebration. They are primarily used to guarantee the crafting of <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/halcyona-necklace">Halcyona Necklaces</a> up to Rank 11.
</p><p>Brave Warrior's Medals are obtained by participating in several faction PvP events during the anniversary celebration, including <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Mistmerrow_War&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">Mistmerrow War</a>, <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Golden_Plains_Battle&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">Golden Plains Battle</a>, <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/veroe-harvest-festival">Veroe Harvest Festival</a>, and <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Great_Heist&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">Great Heist</a>.
</p>

<h2 id="Usage">Usage</h2>
<figure><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/b/be/Archeage_2nHKDbRC8E.png/300px-Archeage_2nHKDbRC8E.png" width="300" height="255" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/b/be/Archeage_2nHKDbRC8E.png 1.5x" loading="lazy"><figcaption>It is recommended to only use this currency on Rank 11 as that will on average reduce the amount of Warrior's Medals you'll need for Rank 12 and beyond the most.</figcaption></figure>
<p>Brave Warrior's Medals are used to guarantee the crafting of Halcyona Necklaces.
</p><p>They can be used to craft Unsealed Halcyona Necklaces up to <b>Rank 11 (Epic)</b>, allowing players to bypass the normal randomness associated with obtaining a necklace of the desired rank.
