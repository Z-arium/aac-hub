Brave Warrior's Medal

Brave Warrior's Medals are a special event currency introduced during the 3rd Anniversary celebration. They are primarily used to guarantee the crafting of Halcyona Necklaces up to Rank 11.

Brave Warrior's Medals are obtained by participating in several faction PvP events during the anniversary celebration, including Mistmerrow War, Golden Plains Battle, Veroe Harvest Festival, and Great Heist.

Obtaining

Brave Warrior's Medals can be obtained from the following events:

Event Source Quest
Mistmerrow War Point Rewards The Battle of Mistmerrow Basin
Golden Plains Battle Victory Reward Obtained by interacting with the crystal
Veroe Harvest Festival Victory Reward
The Great Heist Kill Quest & Victory Rewards Capibaras and Their Tendency for Murder & Great Golden Getaway

Note: Brave Warrior's Medals are an anniversary event currency and are only available from the designated anniversary event rewards.

Usage

Brave Warrior's Medals are used to guarantee the crafting of Halcyona Necklaces.

They can be used to craft Unsealed Halcyona Necklaces up to Rank 11 (Epic), allowing players to bypass the normal randomness associated with obtaining a necklace of the desired rank.

Brave Warrior's Medal Used to guarantee crafting a Rank 8–11 Halcyona Necklace
