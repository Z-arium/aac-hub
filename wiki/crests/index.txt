10:T6d1,<h2 id="Crest_Information">Crest Information</h2>
<p>Outside of blank crest frames and sails, there's a variety of other crest formats that can be used that require custom formatting.
</p><p>In order to make a custom crest, a Crest Brainstorm is needed, which can be purchased from the Marketplace for credits.
</p><p>Crest Brainstorms are currently priced at 250 Credits each.
</p><p>Once you get a Crest Brainstorm, you will need to apply an image (detailed below) at a Crest Printer 
</p>
<h4 id="General_Notes:">General Notes:</h4>
<p>- Only one custom crest can be made at a time (If you have multiple, you will need to do it one by one)
</p><p>- All images must be <b>exactly</b> 256x256
</p><p>- Images must be added to `User\Documents\AAClassic\UCC` - with the filename ucc.png
</p>
