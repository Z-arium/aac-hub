<h2 id="Leveling_Abyssal_Skills">Leveling Abyssal Skills</h2>
<p>To obtain all the Abyssal skills you will need to level the selected skillset to 55 again, Except this time you will need to pay some items at certain points.
</p>
<ul><li><b>To reach level 25:</b> Have level 24 completed, and 3 [Abyssal Crystal].</li>
<li><b>To reach level 40:</b> Have level 39 completed, and 50 [Gilda Star]</li>
<li><b>To reach level 55:</b> Have level 54 completed, and 100 [Warrior Medal]
<ul><li>These must be done for each skillset. Stock up!</li></ul></li></ul>
