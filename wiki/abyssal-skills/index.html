Abyssal Skills

Acquiring the abyssal skills

First, level the skill tree that you want Abyssal mode on, to level 55.
Then, talk to Belstrom in Diamond Shores. He is at the northwestern area as shown on the map below. He will set you on a questline to speak to various NPCs at different locations.

Quest Line
Starting NPC Location Quest Name & Description
Belstrom Diamond Shores A Darker Power
Belstrom will send you to Wanderer Arde in Karkasse Ridgelands. If you do not have a portal to get there, there is a portal gate at the top of Veroe in Hasla.
Silent Watcher Golden Ruins A Twisting Path
The Silent Watcher will send you to Apprentice Rudal in Exeloch. If you do not have the portal, you can go to the Dungeon tab in your teleport book and port to Serpentis.
Scout Captain Camaro Golden Ruins Whispers from the Abyss
After this, you have to talk to Drillmaster Rhiona in Diamond Shores. There is portal gate from major cities like Marianople and Austera, Solis Headlands.
Maynard Ynys Island
(east of Solzreed Peninsula)		 Orchidna's Tale
Before you head to Ynys Island, it is important to have a Specialized Snowflake with you. You can buy it from a General Merchant for 200g.
Lucius Quinto Ynys Island The Demon Queen
After talking to Lucius Quinto, use the teleport scroll in your bag to get in the Queen's Chamber. Talk to Damian.
Skill Sword Queen's Chamber Select the skillset that you want to have Abyssal mode on by interacting with the respective sword. Then use the Specialization Snowflake to absorb the abyssal energy from the sword. After that, go to your skill page (K) and activate the abyssal button (looks like an eye under your skillset name

Leveling Abyssal Skills

To obtain all the Abyssal skills you will need to level the selected skillset to 55 again, Except this time you will need to pay some items at certain points.

  • To reach level 25: Have level 24 completed, and 3 [Abyssal Crystal].
  • To reach level 40: Have level 39 completed, and 50 [Gilda Star]
  • To reach level 55: Have level 54 completed, and 100 [Warrior Medal]
    • These must be done for each skillset. Stock up!

Archery

Archery
Type Icon Skill Abyssal charges needed Description
Passive Hateful Hunter - Charged Bolt, Piercing Shot, Concussive Arrow, Missile Rain, and Snipe regenerate Abyssal points.
Active Serpent Sight 2 Enhances your archery with Abyssal magic, transforming your attacks and gaining unparalleled range.

Increases the base range of bow skills to 30m, but prevents movement. Replaces Endless Arrows with the slower but more powerful Fang Bolt (312% ranged attack!) for the duration.

Lasts 5 seconds, or until the caster takes damage. Duration is reset with each successful Range Attack.

Active Rising Hatred 2 - 4 Increases maximum Abyssal Charges +2 for 12 seconds. Prevents charges from naturally depleting for the duration, and doubles charges gained through using skills. Any additional Abyssal Charges are consumed on casting, increases the duration.
Active Hunting Pack 4 - 6 Summons a pack of spectral Snowlions to charge through your enemies, dealing damage and launching them into the air.No target required, shoots straight forward from the caster and ignores terrain. Any additional charges are consumed on casting, increasing the damage and number of snowlions. (Note: additional Snowlions increase the AoE.)If cast using 6 Abyssal Charges, enemies launched by this skill are temporarily prevented from using skills.
