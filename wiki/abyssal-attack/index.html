Abyssal Attack

The Abyssal Attack occurs multiple times throughout the week. You can check the in-game calendar or the [AAC website]timers 

for specific times. This event involves several stages and mechanics, all leading to the restoration of Ezi's Light in the Sea of Graves.

Luscas and Abyssal Kraken

When the Abyssal Attack begins, Luscas and the Abyssal Kraken spawn at the center of the Sea of Graves. This location is south of Heedmar and north of Freedich Island (check your map for exact positioning).

If you are present at the start, you may notice various items and surprises floating in the water around the island. The objective is to kill all Luscas and the Kraken to progress to the next phase.

Restoring Ezi's Light

Once the Abyssal Kraken is defeated, a server-wide message will announce that the restoration of Ezi's Light is now possible.

To restore the light, head to the foot of the monument in the Sea of Graves and interact with the beacon on the pedestal. This action begins a three-minute channel that must be completed without interruption.

Enemies can stop the restoration in two ways: - Killing the player who is channeling - Destroying the lodestones located to the north, east, or west of the monument (these are marked on your map)

Lodestones must be destroyed using siege damage (such as from cannons). If they are successfully destroyed, the beacon will explode, dealing 20,000 damage and knocking back anyone nearby into the dangerous waters surrounding the monument.
