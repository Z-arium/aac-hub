10:Tc4e,<h2 id="Getting_There">Getting There</h2>
<p>Go through the portal in your factions capital, or head to Skytalon in Windscour to meet Festival Keeper Alhah, who will direct you to Festival Keeper Nairamdal in the festival grounds to the east.
</p>
<figure><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/9/9c/Alhah.png/150px-Alhah.png" width="150" height="285" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/9/9c/Alhah.png/225px-Alhah.png 1.5x, https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/9/9c/Alhah.png/300px-Alhah.png 2x" loading="lazy"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
<h2 id="A_Kindly_Caretaker.">A Kindly Caretaker.</h2>
<p>Festival Keeper Nairamdal will offer you the quest Kindly Caretaker, tasking you to look after one of the nearby animals. This animal will grow into a piece of house decor, so make sure to choose the one you want! You'll also need some additional items to look after your new friend.
</p><p>Your options are&#160;:
</p>
<ul><li>Yata Calf</li>
<li>Elk Calf</li>
<li>Calico Kitten</li>
<li>Seal-Point Kitten</li>
<li>Black Kitten</li>
<li>Gray Tabby Kitten</li>
<li>Ginger Tabby Kitten</li>
<li>Puppy</li></ul>
<p>After you've made your choice head back to Nairamdal, who will now task you with caring for your new friend, place them on the ground and proceed through a series of interactions as you nurse them back to health.
</p><p>Once complete, talk to Nairamdal to recieve your new companion in placeable decor form.
</p>
<figure><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/7/7f/Nairamdal.png/150px-Nairamdal.png" width="150" height="277" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/7/7f/Nairamdal.png/225px-Nairamdal.png 1.5x, https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/7/7f/Nairamdal.png/300px-Nairamdal.png 2x" loading="lazy"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
<h2 id="Newborn_Welsh_Corgi">Newborn Welsh Corgi</h2>
<p>You'll now be tasked with raising a newborn welsh corgi, you'll have to feed him, 5 ground grain should be sufficient, and then give him a nice bath requiring 1 water. Once that's done, hand the quest back in to Nairamdal.
</p>
<h2 id="Cows_count,_Too!"><span></span>Cows count, Too!</h2>
<p>Circling the festival grounds you can find Ranchand Jack, who wants to inform your that cows also count as pets for the pawsome festival, in return he'll reward you with 3 pawsome coins.
</p>
<h2 id="Tarkir_and_Sarir">Tarkir and Sarir</h2>
<p>At [server times] additional NPC's will visit to provide you with additional pawesome coins
</p>
<h2 id="Festival_Soup">Festival Soup</h2>
<figure><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/2/24/Thatsgoodsoup.png" width="255" height="161" loading="lazy"><figcaption></figcaption></figure>
<p>Stop by the campfire and partake in some Festival Soup and you'll recieve a 1 hour buff, granting 3% evasion, 41 attack speed, and a 4% decreased cast time.
</p>
