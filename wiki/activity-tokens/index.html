Activity Tokens

Usage of Activity Tokens

Activity Tokens can be exchanged for various rewards on mirage isle, including decent starting accessories, regrade scrolls, synthium shards, Gilda Stars, and Warrior Medals. They can also be used in combination with a Priceless Coin to acquire certain gliders and mounts. The desk is located on the left side down the stairs after entering Mirage Island.

All of the quests can be obtained from Shadow Daru in each main city capitol.

Sources of Activity Tokens

Weekly

These quests are only able to be picked up on their respective day, but can be completed throughout the week.

  • Weekly (Friday Reset) Crystalline Bloodbath quest gives 30 tokens each week once completed.
  • Weekly (Saturday Reset) Fishing quest gives 20 tokens each week once completed.
  • Weekly (Satuday Reset) Delphinad Ghost Ship to kill the ghost ship at the sea gives 33 tokens each week once completed.
  • Weekly (Satuday Reset) Ayanad Library kill bosses in the Ayanad Library gives 10 token per quest (2 quests).
  • Weekly (Sunday Reset) Anthalon kill quest gives 33 tokens each week once completed.
    • To unlock this quest, you must first complete the custom story quests (Chapter 99) where you must kill the Hounds of Kyrios and then Anthalon.

Daily

  • Daily Active Adventurers Appreciated merit quest gives 5 tokens for spending labor on professions. This can be picked up in your list of daily merit quests.

One-time

  • Complete story quest 99.1 (Hounds of Kyrios) for 1 token (use this to unlock your merit quest).
  • Complete the participate in the Veroe Harvest Festival achievement for 5 tokens.
  • Complete the "The Harvest Festival Beckons!" quest from an auctioneer for 50 tokens.
