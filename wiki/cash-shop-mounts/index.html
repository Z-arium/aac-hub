Cash shop mounts

Cash shop mounts are mounts that have been "nerfed" meaning the skills have been changed and/or the movement speed has been decreased.


Ser Meatball

Ser Meatball can be purchased from the Marketplace with 1500 credits.

Mount
Arcane
Ser Meatball
His royal furriness, anointed knight of toebeans, nemesis of rodents, breaker of tea cups and champion of the litterbox: Ser Meatball.
The Vanity Ser Meatball has skills equivalent to a Snowlion:
Lv5: Run!Run!Mana 20Instant30sec CooldownIncreases Pet Move Speed +50% for 10sec.
Can't be used while carrying a trade pack.
Lv10: Mounted ArrowshotMounted ArrowshotFires an arrow at an enemy, dealing Physical Damage equal to 80% of the rider's ranged attack.
Lv15: OverrunOverrunInstant24sec CooldownTriggers a charge that can Stun a distant enemy for 2sec.
Can't be used while carrying a trade pack.
Lv20: AnabolicaAnabolicaInstant1min CooldownIncreases the pet's and rider's Attack +30% for 30sec.
Canceled if you dismount.
Lv25: DashDashInstant30sec CooldownTriggers a forward charge for 3sec that makes you invincible. Can't be used while carrying a trade pack.
Use:
Summons a mount.
Base Move Speed 11.5m/s
