The Daru Relic event is a small event where the player receives one quest starter daily, can complete a quest chain which unlocks the ability to place a cauldron to craft special event items. This event can offer very good rewards with the [Mystic Daru's Lucky Gift]!

=== Daily Quest Chain ===

==== Mission: A Daru Relic ====
[[File:DaruRelicTicker.png|none|thumb|You can obtain the quest starter by clicking the gift here]]
After obtaining the quest starter, you'll have to use the item and then report to an Auctioneer. They can be found next to most Mirage Portals.

==== Helter Smelter ====
The second quest of the chain requires the player to obtain 50 Iron Ore, and then go to a Smelter to craft 5x [Refined Iron Ingot]
{| class="wikitable"
|+
!Quest Description[[File:Helter Smelter.png|none|thumb]]
!Refine Iron Ingot Recipe[[File:Refine Ingot Recipe.png|none|thumb]]
|}

==== Cookware Repair ====
The next step requires the player to repair the cauldron: By fusing the 5 shards obtained in the previous quest, and the 5 refined iron ingots they crafted, players will complete this quest!
{| class="wikitable"
|+
!Quest Desc[[File:Cookware Repair Quest.png|none|thumb]]
!Recipe[[File:Daru Cauldron Recipe.png|none|thumb]]
|}
With this, you'll have a Daru Cauldron! It only lasts 30 minutes so be ready to complete the rest!

==== Potions Lessons ====
With your Daru Cauldron placed down, you'll have to craft the following potions. You can do 3 of these a day thanks to the Magic Solution you got from the previous quest.
{| class="wikitable"
|+
![[File:Potions Lessons Quest.png|none|thumb]]
!Recipe (same for Vocation Elexir)[[File:Potion Recipe.png|none|thumb]]
|}

==== The Mystic Daru ====
Lastly, the player will be asked to make two Mystic Daru Summoning Potions! These can be used to spawn a Daru which gives the player a gift!
{| class="wikitable"
|+
![[File:Mystic Daru Quest.png|thumb]]
![[File:Mystic Daru Potion.png|none|thumb]]
|}

=== Other Event Information ===

* The Cauldron can be used outside of quests! You can craft more Mystic Daru Summoning Potions if you wish to try your luck and get more gifts
* By completing all quests, you'll get 3 Potent Catalyst. Those can be used to make a Radiant Daru Summoning Potion, which is the same as the Mystic one, but costs less labor and gold.
