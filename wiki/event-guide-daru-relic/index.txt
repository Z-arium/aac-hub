12:T14a6,<p><br>
The Daru Relic event is a small event where the player receives one quest starter daily, can complete a quest chain which unlocks the ability to place a cauldron to craft special event items. This event can offer very good rewards with the [Mystic Daru's Lucky Gift]!
</p>

<h3 id="Daily_Quest_Chain">Daily Quest Chain</h3>
<h4 id="Mission:_A_Daru_Relic">Mission: A Daru Relic</h4>
<figure><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/e/e2/DaruRelicTicker.png/300px-DaruRelicTicker.png" width="300" height="124" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/e/e2/DaruRelicTicker.png 1.5x" loading="lazy"><figcaption>You can obtain the quest starter by clicking the gift here</figcaption></figure>
<p>After obtaining the quest starter, you'll have to use the item and then report to an Auctioneer. They can be found next to most Mirage Portals.
</p>
<h4 id="Helter_Smelter">Helter Smelter</h4>
<p>The second quest of the chain requires the player to obtain 50 Iron Ore, and then go to a Smelter to craft 5x [Refined Iron Ingot]
</p>

<h4 id="Cookware_Repair">Cookware Repair</h4>
<p>The next step requires the player to repair the cauldron: By fusing the 5 shards obtained in the previous quest, and the 5 refined iron ingots they crafted, players will complete this quest!
</p>

</p>

<p>Lastly, the player will be asked to make two Mystic Daru Summoning Potions! These can be used to spawn a Daru which gives the player a gift!

<h3 id="Other_Event_Information">Other Event Information</h3>
<ul><li>The Cauldron can be used outside of quests! You can craft more Mystic Daru Summoning Potions if you wish to try your luck and get more gifts</li>
<li>By completing all quests, you'll get 3 Potent Catalyst. Those can be used to make a Radiant Daru Summoning Potion, which is the same as the Mystic one, but costs less labor and gold.</li></ul>
