<h3 id="Resplendent_Regrade_Scrolls">Resplendent Regrade Scrolls</h3>
<p>Resplendent Scrolls are made using a Lucky Point (Sun/Moon/Star) alongside' its corresponding Regrade Scroll
</p><p><br>
Using a Resplendent Scroll will add an additional chance upon success of upgrading an item twice, resulting in, for example, a Celestial &gt;&gt; Epic regrade in one go.
</p><p>Having a Success on any regrade using a Resplendent Scroll gives a 20% bonus chance (1 in 5) of going up an extra grade.
</p><p><br>
So for a regrade like Divine &gt; Epic at Normal Difficulty, you have a base 10% chance of regrading to Epic. Should your regrade succeed, you then have a 20% chance of going Legendary. This gives a 2% great success chance (10% * 20%)
</p>
