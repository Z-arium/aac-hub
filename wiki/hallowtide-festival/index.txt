10:T2405,<h3 id="Location">Location</h3>
<p>The festival is located in Two Crowns and Solis Headlands. 
</p>
<h4 id="Quests">Quests</h4>
<h5 id="[Pumpkins_for_the_Dead]"><span></span>[Pumpkins for the Dead]</h5>
<p>Next to the quest NPC is the workbench where he asks to craft the Festival Pumpkin Decoration. Make sure to have 5 Blue Salt Knives in the inventory to craft the item. <i>Blue Salt Knives can be bought from General merchants.</i>
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/9/93/Hallowtidequestnpc1.png" width="225" height="294" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/9/98/Hallowtideworkbench.png" width="743" height="380" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/0/03/Festivalpumpkindecoration.png" width="295" height="242" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h5 id="[Slow_Rider]"><span></span>[Slow Rider]</h5>
<p>Snail Trainer Zerk gives a quest where player picks one of the snail mounts to finish the <b>Candy Hunter</b> quest.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/0/08/Festivalkeeper.png" width="430" height="363" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/3/3e/Racingsnail.png" width="299" height="384" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p>The mount lasts for 30 minutes, enough time to finish the next quest the same NPC gives.
</p><p><br>
If the mount is missing or accidentally deleted it can be bought from the same NPC that gave the quest for 1 gold.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/8/82/Buyeventmount.png" width="434" height="354" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h5 id="[Candy_Hunter]"><span></span>[Candy Hunter]</h5>
<p>Quest asks player to summon their snail mount and ride it to the candy pouches around the beach. The snail has a skill called "<b>Sniff</b>" which needs to be used on top of the candy pouches in order to be collected. Quest cant be completed without the mount and the skill. The goal is to find a Purple Pumpkin Candy in 30 minutes. The drops are random but it shouldn't take more than few minutes to find the correct candy. 
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/f/f4/Sniffingcandy.png/462px-Sniffingcandy.png" width="462" height="584" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/f/f4/Sniffingcandy.png 1.5x" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h5 id="[Commemorative_Pumpkins]"><span></span>[Commemorative Pumpkins]</h5>
<p>Pumpkin Carver Suyeon asks the player to hop in the portal close to her to talk to Welkos.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/5/57/Pumpkin_carversuyeon.png" width="345" height="425" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/2/2d/Portaltowelkos.png" width="268" height="420" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p>Portal takes player to the Two Crowns map.
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h5 id="[Hallowtide_Origins]"><span></span>[Hallowtide Origins]</h5>
<p>Kennis asks player also talk to Welkos.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/0/0b/Festivalkmeeperkennis.png" width="231" height="399" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h5 id="[Light_Up_the_Night]"><span></span>[Light Up the Night]</h5>
<p>Welkos wants the player to lit 3 pumpkin lanterns behind him next to the fence.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/1/16/Festivla_keeperwelkos.png" width="256" height="283" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h5 id="[Troubled_Spirits]"><span></span>[Troubled Spirits]</h5>
<p>Touch the Soulstone next to the NPC.
</p><p><br>
<u>For the EU region, the Soulstone, candies, and jack-o' lanterns appear in the Two Crowns Arena at 2pm, 6pm, 10pm and 2 am UTC.</u>
</p><p><u>For the NA region, the Soulstone, candies, and jack-o' lanterns appear in the Two Crowns Arena at 7pm, 11pm, 3am and 7am UTC.</u>
</p><p><u>The first team to full their jack-o' lantern wins the larger price. If the event finishes before you can complete your quest, you must wait until the next time slot.</u>
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h5 id="[The_Haunting_of_Two_Crowns]"><span></span>[The Haunting of Two Crowns]</h5>
<p>Kill Demon War Spirits.
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h4 id="Mount">Mount</h4>
<p><a href="/aac-hub/wiki/event-mounts">Candy-fueled Bestcargot</a> can be acquired from the achievement called <b>Jack of all Lanterns</b>. It requires the player to complete all the Hallowtide quests 5 times. 
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/8/8e/Jackofalllanterns.png" width="347" height="195" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h3 id="Death">Death</h3>
<p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/8/81/Death_event.png" width="762" height="606" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><i>Have you ever wondered what death feels like, mortal?</i>
Additional questline to Hallowtide festival is the appearance of Death themself. 
</p>
<h4 id="Quests_2">Quests</h4>
<p>First time when meeting Death in Two Crowns player is given two options, <b>to live or to die</b>. First time has to be picked Decide to die so the questline begins and player becomes a Deathbringer. 
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/6/64/Liveordie.png" width="79" height="48" loading="lazy"></span>
</p>
<h5 id="[Wanted:_Mortals_of_Erenor]"><span></span>[Wanted: Mortals of Erenor]</h5>
<p>Death collects souls of those that player has killed. He asks the players to go to Halcyona and reap 3 players there and come back. Once the stacks reach 500 all the mortals around him will be rewarded.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/d/d6/Soulharvest.png" width="211" height="173" loading="lazy"></span>
</p>
<h5 id="[Wanted:_Duckling]"><span></span>[Wanted: Duckling]</h5>
<p>A quest where Death hands and chick for the player and they need to reap it. Place it down and use the skill.
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h3 id="Broom_Race">Broom Race</h3>
<h4 id="Location_2">Location</h4>
<p>Broom Race is located inside Mirage Island. The portal is located right down the stairs when entered.
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/d/d6/Broomrace.png" width="876" height="521" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p><br>
</p>
<h4 id="Quests_3">Quests</h4>
<h5 id="[Hallowtide_Challenge]"><span></span>[Hallowtide Challenge]</h5>
<p>Introduction to the place.
</p>
<h5 id="[Hallowtide_Broom_Race]"><span></span>[Hallowtide Broom Race]</h5>
<p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/1/1a/Littlewitch.png/246px-Littlewitch.png" width="246" height="438" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/1/1a/Littlewitch.png 1.5x" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p>Little Witch instructs the player how to do the race and gives temporary Broom glider.   
</p><p>Race starts when a player summons the glider normally. The broom moves up and down and turns faster than the normal gliders do. Goal is to stay on the path of the pumpkin icons to get the speed buff often as possible. Route takes player through 4 gates and those gates need to be passed in order to finish the quest. There is additional rewards for hitting certain amount of pumpkins in a row or making the race in certain timeframe.    
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/9/90/Taskboardinfo.png" width="255" height="207" loading="lazy"></span>   
</p><p>The tomb stone shaped Task Board will tell additional info on how to complete the race.                  
</p>
<h5 id="[Well_Done!]"><span></span>[Well Done!]</h5>
<p>After completing all the gates Little Witch sends the quest item into players inventory. By clicking it it starts a quest called Well Done! that can be completed at the end of the racetrack by talking to Little Witch.       
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/d/dc/Broomracetokern.png" width="207" height="257" loading="lazy"></span>            
</p>
<h4 id="Rewards">Rewards</h4>
<p>For this event the currency is different than the normal <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/currencies">Daru Festival Coin</a>. The currency to buy these items is <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/currencies">Broom Race coin</a>. 
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/e/eb/Broomracecoin.png" width="38" height="38" loading="lazy"></span>
