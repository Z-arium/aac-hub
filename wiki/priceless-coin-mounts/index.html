Wiki / Mounts & Pets

Priceless coin mounts

Mounts that can be obtained by using Priceless coins to exchange for 30-day mounts then be upgraded into permanent mounts by Activity Tokens. For these mounts the armors can only be equipped after the mount has been made permanent. Making a mount permanent costs 120 Activity Tokens.


Carrot Dash

Mount
Basic
Carrot Dash
A donkey of a different color, this brilliant citrine creature walks much faster than ordinary donkeys. It gets its unusual hue from eating far too many carrots.
The Carrot Dash has the following skills:
Lv20: Dreaming DonkeyDreaming DonkeyInstant1min CooldownIncreases the donkey's Move Speed +30% for 1min.
Can only be used while wearing a trade pack and consumes 1 Carrot.
Lv20: La Mancha's CallLa Mancha's CallInstant1min CooldownRaise your spear high and charge forward for 7sec.
You cannot turn left or right once the charge begins.
Can't be used while carrying a trade pack.
Use:
Summons a mount.
Base Move Speed 8m/s

Lv15: Back KickBack KickInstant18sec CooldownStrengthens the mount's rearward kick, dealing (varies) Physical Damage and Tripping the enemy.
Lv20: Zebra ChargeZebra ChargeInstant20sec CooldownCharges forward for 1sec, breaking through enemy lines. Inflicts Knockback and Trip on affected enemies.
This skill counts as a Push effect.
Can't be used while carrying a trade pack.

Can't be used while carrying a trade pack.

Mount
Rare
Rajani
Black war tigers are known as Rajani in honor of Empress Pavitra's legendary mount. It's said the cat devoured not only her enemies, but all of its other handlers, and would permit only the empress to touch it.
This mount has the following skills:
Lv10: GnawGnawInstant18sec CooldownSharpens mount's bite, dealing Physical Damage and additional damage. Triggers Vicious Bite if Invisible Predator is active.
Vicious Bite deals Physical Damage and reduces a target's Move Speed.
Lv20: 4-Leg Lope4-Leg LopeInstant30sec CooldownIncreases Move Speed +50% for its duration. Triggers Careen if Invisible Predator is active.
Careen drastically increases Move Speed, which then decreases gradually.
Can't be used while carrying a trade pack.
Lv30: OverwhelmOverwhelmInstant24sec CooldownCharges into a distant target and inflicts Stun for its duration. Triggers Ambush if Invisible Predator is active.
Ambush damages the charged enemy.
Can't be used while carrying a trade pack.
Lv40: DashDashInstant1min CooldownLets the mount dash invincibly forward. Triggers Rip-Roaring if Invisible Predator is active.
Rip-Roaring triggers a fast run and grants invincibility. Damages nearby enemies.
Can't be used while carrying a trade pack.
Lv50: Invisible PredatorInvisible PredatorInstant1min CooldownStealths the mount and rider for 5sec. Enhances certain other skills while active.
Can't be used while carrying a trade pack.
