10:T87df,<p>Back to <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/ships">Ships</a> or <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/ships">Galleons</a>
</p><p><br>
Sails in ArcheAge are the sole avenue for propulsion on most boats, (save fishing boats and speedboats) whilst some boats can be equipped with only one type of sail, Galleons, in particular, can be equipped with a variety of sails that have different pros and cons, we will explore these aforementioned sails in this wiki page.
</p><p><br>
</p><p>Starting with the most widely used sail on the majority of boats, The <b>Caernord Zephyr Triangular Rig.</b>
</p><p><br>
</p><p>This sail is the only equipable sail on the Adventurer's Clipper, Harpoon Clipper and Merchant Schooner. It does not have any additional tiers and can only be regraded, regrading gives the sail additional health and ship max movement speed.
</p><p>The changes to the sail are listed by tier below.

</p><p>The weight of these two sails is consistent at 6,000kg for the mainsail and 2,000kg for the foresail, the distinction between Mainsail and Foresail is trading the scaling +x% ship collision damage of the mainsail for lighter weight thus faster boat speed of the foresail.
</p><p>This goal of this sail is to have a boat that is highly maneuverable at low speeds, HP wise, this sail type comes in the middle between Growling Zephyr and Eznan Zephyr.
</p><p>This sail is not generally seen in the wild and is only 'recommended' for very specific vessel loadouts.
</p><p><br>
</p><p>Moving on to, The <b>Eznan Zephyr Square Mainsail and Foresail.</b>
</p><p>This sails primary attribute that sets it apart from other sails is its <i><b>increase in speed and decrease in siege damage upon receiving siege damage from enemy ships or world bosses</b></i>, aside from that, boasting the highest health stat of any small warship sail, coming as the tankiest sail compared to Lutesong Zephyr and Growling Zephyr sails.

</p><p>The other reason to select anything other than 2 Foresails is the functionality of the Forewind buff, If you use 2 Foresails, you will not get 2 instances of the Forewind buff, alternatively, you can equip your Mainsail in the front slot and your Foresail in the rear slot, this allows the larger hitbox of the Mainsail to block some incoming shots as well as clear a bit of headroom for any crew on the cannons.
</p><p>These are by far the most common sail seen in the wild, and with good reason, for 99% of galleon uses these sails can and should be your choice.
