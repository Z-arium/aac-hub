<h2 id="How_to_Earn_Prestige">How to Earn Prestige</h2>
<p>Prestige is primarily earned by completing Guild Missions.
</p>
<h3 id="Guild_Missions">Guild Missions</h3>
<p><b>Guild Missions</b> are one of the main sources of Prestige. Completing guild missions contributes Prestige to the character and also helps the guild progress.
</p><p>The amount of Prestige awarded depends on the specific quest or activity.

<h4 id="Colonizing_Auroria">Colonizing Auroria</h4>
<p>To complete this Guild Mission you can complete 1 of 5 daily quests found in your faction's Housing Province in Diamond Shores. 
</p><p><span><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/a/a7/HousingProvinceDiamondShores.png/600px-HousingProvinceDiamondShores.png" width="600" height="213" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/a/a7/HousingProvinceDiamondShores.png/900px-HousingProvinceDiamondShores.png 1.5x, https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/a/a7/HousingProvinceDiamondShores.png 2x" loading="lazy"></span>
</p><p>The quests require you to craft an Archeum Bundle at the nearby Auroria Archeum Bench then taking them to repair the bridge, your faction base's walls or deliver to an NPC in your faction's base.

<h4 id="Defending_Auroria">Defending Auroria</h4>
<figure><img src="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/thumb/d/d3/AACDiamondShoresSynthesisLocation.png/350px-AACDiamondShoresSynthesisLocation.png" width="350" height="279" srcset="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/images/d/d3/AACDiamondShoresSynthesisLocation.png 1.5x" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Spawn location of the Alemine Stragglers Bunkers.</figcaption></figure>
<p>The most common way to complete this quest is by destroying an Alemine Stragglers Bunker using the Harpoon Cannons found in Diamond Shores. The Harpoon Cannons can be picked up from the many Harpoon Cannon Caches laying on the ground near the Alemine Stragglers Bunkers.
</p><p>To complete this Guild Mission you can destroy any of the following:
</p>
<ul><li>Alemine Fortress</li>
<li>Alemine Bunker</li>
<li>Alemine Stragglers Bunker</li>
<li>Alemine Assault Bunker</li>
<li>Aust Mana Tower</li>
<li>Mana Tower Idol</li>
<li>Riven Gates Idol</li></ul>
