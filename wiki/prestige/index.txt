<h2 id="How_to_Earn_Prestige">How to Earn Prestige</h2>

<p>To complete this Guild Mission you can complete 1 of 5 daily quests found in your faction's Housing Province in Diamond Shores.

<h4 id="Defending_Auroria">Defending Auroria</h4>
<figure><img src="/aac-hub/wiki-files/98/98c92806a2b95c8e716d2fa32e693b1fc502d0c5.png" width="350" height="279" loading="lazy"><figcaption>Spawn location of the Alemine Stragglers Bunkers.</figcaption></figure>
<p>The most common way to complete this quest is by destroying an Alemine Stragglers Bunker using the Harpoon Cannons found in Diamond Shores. The Harpoon Cannons can be picked up from the many Harpoon Cannon Caches laying on the ground near the Alemine Stragglers Bunkers.
</p><p>To complete this Guild Mission you can destroy any of the following:
</p>
<ul><li>Alemine Fortress</li>
<li>Alemine Bunker</li>
<li>Alemine Stragglers Bunker</li>
<li>Alemine Assault Bunker</li>
<li>Aust Mana Tower</li>
<li>Mana Tower Idol</li>
<li>Riven Gates Idol</li></ul>

'''Prestige''' is a [[Currencies|Currency]] associated with [[Guilds]] in ArcheAge Classic. It is primarily earned by completing '''Guild Missions'''.

Prestige can be spent in the [[Prestige Shop]] on a variety of guild-related items, upgrade materials, gems and other useful stuff.

== How to Earn Prestige ==

Prestige is primarily earned by completing Guild Missions.

=== Guild Missions ===

'''Guild Missions''' are one of the main sources of Prestige. Completing guild missions contributes Prestige to the character and also helps the guild progress.

The amount of Prestige awarded depends on the specific quest or activity.

==== Defending Auroria ====
[[File:AACDiamondShoresSynthesisLocation.png|thumb|right|350px|Spawn location of the Alemine Stragglers Bunkers.]]
The most common way to complete this quest is by destroying an Alemine Stragglers Bunker using the Harpoon Cannons found in Diamond Shores. The Harpoon Cannons can be picked up from the many Harpoon Cannon Caches laying on the ground near the Alemine Stragglers Bunkers.

To complete this Guild Mission you can destroy any of the following:
* Alemine Fortress
* Alemine Bunker
* Alemine Stragglers Bunker
* Alemine Assault Bunker
* Aust Mana Tower
* Mana Tower Idol
* Riven Gates Idol
