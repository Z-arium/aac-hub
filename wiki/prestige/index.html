Prestige

Prestige is a Currency associated with Guilds in ArcheAge Classic. It is primarily earned by completing Guild Missions.

Prestige can be spent in the Prestige Shop on a variety of guild-related items, upgrade materials, gems and other useful stuff.

How to Earn Prestige

Prestige is primarily earned by completing Guild Missions.

Guild Missions

Guild Missions are one of the main sources of Prestige. Completing guild missions contributes Prestige to the character and also helps the guild progress.

The amount of Prestige awarded depends on the specific quest or activity.

Mission Guild XP Prestige Type
Colonizing Auroria 20 XP 10 Daily
Defending Auroria 40 XP 10 Daily
Gather Experia Patches 0 XP 10 Daily
Guild: Materials 4 XP 1 Repeatable
World Bosses 40 XP 10 Daily

Colonizing Auroria

To complete this Guild Mission you can complete 1 of 5 daily quests found in your faction's Housing Province in Diamond Shores.

The quests require you to craft an Archeum Bundle at the nearby Auroria Archeum Bench then taking them to repair the bridge, your faction base's walls or deliver to an NPC in your faction's base.

Quest Requirement Bundle Materials Guild XP Prestige
Construct the Haranyan/Nuian Alliance Bridge Faint Archeum Bundle 6× 3 XP 3
Reinforce the Ramparts Faint Archeum Bundle 6× 3 XP 3
A Grand Construction 1 Faint Archeum Bundle 6× 4 XP 3
A Grand Construction 2 Clear Archeum Bundle 6× + 2× 8 XP 5
A Grand Construction 3 Lucid Archeum Bundle 6× + 2× + 2× 12 XP 6

Defending Auroria

The most common way to complete this quest is by destroying an Alemine Stragglers Bunker using the Harpoon Cannons found in Diamond Shores. The Harpoon Cannons can be picked up from the many Harpoon Cannon Caches laying on the ground near the Alemine Stragglers Bunkers.

To complete this Guild Mission you can destroy any of the following:

  • Alemine Fortress
  • Alemine Bunker
  • Alemine Stragglers Bunker
  • Alemine Assault Bunker
  • Aust Mana Tower
  • Mana Tower Idol
  • Riven Gates Idol

Gather Experia Patches

Can be completed by doing one of the following:

  • Gather Experia Sunflower Patch ×4
  • Gather Experia Pumpkin Patch ×3
  • Gather Experia Duck Patch ×2
  • Gather Experia Pine Patch ×1
Recipe Growth Time Material 1 Material 2 Material 3

Experia Pine Patch 48h 25×Pine Sapling 45×Memory Ink 1×Heavy Hardwood

Guild: Materials

This Guild Mission randomly gives you one of the following quests:

  • Guild: Fabric
  • Guild: Leather
  • Guild: Stone Brick
  • Guild: Iron Ingot
  • Guild: Lumber

As you can tell by their names they require certain basic materials to be completed however you also need Blue Salt Hammers. This Guild Mission is repeatable so when you complete the one you are given you are randomly given a new one.

Guild Mission Material 1 Material 2
Guild: Fabric 5× Fabric 4× Blue Salt Hammer
Guild: Leather 5× Leather 4× Blue Salt Hammer
Guild: Stone Brick 5× Stone Brick 4× Blue Salt Hammer
Guild: Iron Ingot 5× Iron Ingot 4× Blue Salt Hammer
Guild: Lumber 5× Lumber 4× Blue Salt Hammer

World Bosses

This Guild Mission requires you to defeat one of the many World Bosses found in Erenor.

Other Sources

Ocleera Rift is an additional source of Prestige and Guild XP found on the western part of Diamond Shores.

Quest Guild XP Prestige Location Type
Sprouting Ocleera's Marks 2 XP 2 Quest Giver Daily
Engorged Ocleera's Marks 3 XP 2 Quest Giver Daily
Monstrous Ocleera's Mark 3 XP 2 Quest Giver Daily
Kill the Hateful Ocleera 8 XP 4 In vicinity of Ocleera Daily

Spending Prestige

Prestige can be spent through the Prestige Shop.

The Prestige Shop can be accessed through:

Community → Guild → Manage → Prestige Shop

The shop contains various items that can benefit individual players as well as their guild.
