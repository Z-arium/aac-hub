Catch-up Gear Box

The Catch-up Gear Box enables you to get started with almost any activity you want without the initial grind. It provides decent starter gear, as well as various items to get you started making money and progressing your character.

How to Claim Your Catch-up Gear Box

Reach level 50 and your catch-up gear box will automatically appear in your inventory.

For healers, you will likely want to convert the mage cloth pieces to healer cloth pieces at a Soulforged Anvil. The recipe "Conversion: Soulforged..." can be found in the folio.

What's in the box?

Item Description
Divine Hellkissed Weapon Crate (x3) Can be opened to fill out your weapon slots (main hand, offhand, bow, or a 2H weapon if desired)
Celestial Darukissed Flute A variation of the Hellkissed flute -granting around 70 stamina to help new players survive a bit better.
Celestial Soulforged Armor Boxes Provides you with a box for each set (cloth, leather, plate) of armor.
Honorable Victory Rank 5 (x6) 6000 Honor points to get you started.
1,000 Vocation Badge Boost (x5) 5000 Vocation points to get you started.
Warrior's Medal (x100) Enough for a rank 7 Halcy Necklace, or whatever else you'd like to spend it on!
Professional Tonic (x10) Boosts the experience gained via labor. Great to level other skill trees.
20% XP Increase Tonics (x30) Boosts the experience gained via all sources.
Scroll: 30 Day Fish-Find Longliner A fishing boat you can summon to start making money by fishing.
Scroll: 30 Day Merchant Schooner A merchant ship you can summon to start making money by transporting trade packs over the ocean. Can hold up to 20 packs.
Scroll: 30 Day Farm Freighter A farm freighter you can summon to start making money by transporting trade packs over the land. Can hold up to 9 packs.
30 Day Mirage Snowlion A fast mount to start you off with.
T3 Toughness Gems (x10) [Mastery] 100% slot rate gems to put in your pants to make you tankier in PvP
T3 Resilience Gems (x10) [Flexibility] 100% slot rate gems to put in your chest piece to make you tankier in PvP / PvE

Check out the Gemming Guide before putting gems in your catch-up armor.
