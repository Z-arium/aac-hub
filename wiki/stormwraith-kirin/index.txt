<h3 id="Equipment">Equipment</h3>
<hr>
<p>Obtained as a reward for the final quest.
</p>
<h3 id="How_to_Obtain">How to Obtain</h3>
<hr>
<p>The Stormwraith Kirin is acquired by completing a 20 step quest line. 
</p>
<h3 id="Questline_Guide">Questline Guide</h3>
<hr>
<p>This questline begins at Hermit Thaelin, found inside a tower at the Riven Gates in Diamond Shores.
</p><p><a rel="noopener" href="https://i.imgur.com/lUEtGkj.png">https://i.imgur.com/lUEtGkj.png</a>
</p>
<h5 id="Whispers_of_the_Past">Whispers of the Past</h5>
<p>Accept the quest from Hermit Thaelin, and you will immediately be able to talk to them again and turn it in.
</p>
<h5 id="Echoes_in_the_Library">Echoes in the Library</h5>
<p>You are required to obtain 5 Mysterious Manuscripts from Shelfwatchers in the first floor of the Ayanad Library.
</p><p>Enter the Ayanad Library's first floor, the Encyclopedia Room, and use <a rel="noopener" href="https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1uQm0m112ckntigQJ-7TOkMYpD8VsCsYmAJs6ac9lU38/edit#gid=757087794">this map</a> to find Shelfwatchers. Kill them, they will drop a manuscript off each kill. <b>Only Shelfwatchers drop them, not Birch Shelfwatchers.</b>
</p><p>Return to Hermit Thaelin at the Riven Gates tower with all 5 manuscripts.
</p>
<h5 id="A_Light_in_the_Dark">A Light in the Dark</h5>
<p>Teleport to the Serpentis dungeon entrance in Exeloch, and speak to Lerman standing outside. Turn the quest in at Lerman.
</p>
<h5 id="The_Price_of_Knowledge">The Price of Knowledge</h5>
<p>Teleport to the Hadir Farm dungeon entrance, and head east into the "overworld" version of Hadir Farm. You're looking for Executioner Nadan. Talk to them to turn in the quest.
</p><p><a rel="noopener" href="https://i.imgur.com/KbzUHvy.png">https://i.imgur.com/KbzUHvy.png</a>
</p>
<h5 id="A_Grim_Exchange">A Grim Exchange</h5>
<p>Teleport back to the Serpentis dungeon entrance in Exeloch, and speak to Lerman once again. They'll give you the next quest.
</p>
<h5 id="Shadows_Transcribed">Shadows Transcribed</h5>
<p>Head back to Hermit Thaelin at the Riven Gates tower, and turn in your quest. Grab the next one.
</p>
<h5 id="Echoes_of_the_Hunt">Echoes of the Hunt</h5>
<p>Teleport to Reedwind. Head to the mark on this screenshot, and interact with the floor that has a faint blue light glowing off it. This will be inside of an orc camp.
</p><p><a rel="noopener" href="https://i.imgur.com/Mk1VpCn.png">https://i.imgur.com/Mk1VpCn.png</a>
</p><p>Turn this quest in at Caduett in the Reedwind camp.
</p>
<h5 id="Map_to_the_Past">Map to the Past</h5>
<p>Now head to the north east of Reedwind, underneath a small broken rock pillar, there is an old hunting camp to interact with. Just like the orc camp, here's a screenshot to help.
</p><p><a rel="noopener" href="https://i.imgur.com/SGi5C7u.png">https://i.imgur.com/SGi5C7u.png</a>
</p><p>Return to Caduett in the Reedwind camp.
</p>
<h5 id="A_Relic_Unearthed">A Relic Unearthed</h5>
<p>Teleport to the Perinoor Community Center, heading towards the north side of the Skyfin Nest. Talk to Historian Parung. Here's a screenshot of the location.
</p><p><a rel="noopener" href="https://i.imgur.com/o6deHIG.png">https://i.imgur.com/o6deHIG.png</a>
</p>
<h5 id="Beyond_Memory">Beyond Memory</h5>
<p>After accepting the quest from Historian Parung, speak to Archeologist Soyu.
</p>
<h5 id="A_Catalyst_for_Connection">A Catalyst for Connection</h5>
<p>Teleport back to Reedwind, and speak with Caduett one more time. 
</p>
<h5 id="Witches_of_the_Wind">Witches of the Wind</h5>
<p>Teleport to Duskgleam in Rookborne Basin, and talk to Windwitch Antalija right beside the port in.
</p>
<h5 id="Shadows_and_Redemption">Shadows and Redemption</h5>
<p>This step requires you to kill Lerman inside of the Serpentis dungeon. Assemble a group capable of clearing Serpentis, and head in.
</p><p>Once killed, you'll complete the quest objective and be able to turn it back in at Duskgleam to Windwitch Antalija.
</p>
<h5 id="Elemental_Balance">Elemental Balance</h5>
<p>Teleport to Perinoor Community Center, and this time, head to the "City Ruins" camp, south of your teleport. Talk to Archeologist Toro.
</p>
<h5 id="The_Essence_of_Balance">The Essence of Balance</h5>
<p>Toro requires 5 Stone Spirit Anima. To acquire these, you must kill Yaska Warriors, found in the Skyfin Nest to the north east. Just like the shelfwatchers, <b>only Yaksa Warriors drop anima.</b>
</p><p>Return to Archeologist Toro once you've collected all 5.
</p>
<h5 id="Creation_of_the_Hardened_Wisp">Creation of the Hardened Wisp</h5>
<p>Teleport to Duskgleam, and speak with Windwitch Antalija again.
</p>
<h5 id="Chants_of_the_Wind">Chants of the Wind</h5>
<p>Target Windwitch Antalija, open chat and type /cheer to complete the quest objective. Talk to her once she's been cheered on.
</p><p>Everyone needs a little bit of support sometimes.
</p>
<h5 id="The_Ritual_of_Awakening">The Ritual of Awakening</h5>
<p>You must protect the nearby spirit summoning. There is a glowing circle with a star in the middle of it, head to the center and interact to drop the summoning stone.
</p>
<h5 id="The_Storm_Awakens">The Storm Awakens</h5>
<p>Take the quest item you just received, and speak to Hermit Thaelin in Diamond Shores at the Riven Gates tower. 
</p><p><i><b>Reward:</b> Level 50 Stormwraith Kirin Mount</i> 
</p>
<h5 id="The_Wraith_of_the_Storm">The Wraith of the Storm</h5>
<p>Speak to Hermit Thaelin, but with the Stormwraith Kirin summoned.
</p><p><i><b>Reward:</b> Stormwraith Kirin Mount Armor</i>
