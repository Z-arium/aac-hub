ArcheAge is a sandbox game, there are a variety of ways to make money. The main way you will make your gold is by using your labor.

Choose one that fits your playstyle and the amount of time you want to commit to making gold. As you increase your proficiency in any given profession, your [[silver per labor]] spent will increase.

If you haven't, it may also be helpful to read the [[Getting Started#Making Gold|Making Gold]] section of the [[Getting Started]] guide.

== Land ==
''See [[Owning Land]] for additional information''

Land has many different uses to help a player's economy. It can be used to grow materials that can be sold or crafted. It can also be used to [[Staging Packs|stage]] trade packs for more efficient trade running. Some players even make money from simply claiming and selling land. There are a variety of [[Building Designs|building designs]] that can be used to claim land. Generally, they fall into 3 categories: [[Scarecrows]], [[Houses]], and [[Farmhouses]].
