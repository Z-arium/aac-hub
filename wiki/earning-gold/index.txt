12:Tbb7,<p><i>This page is a WIP</i>
</p><p>ArcheAge is a sandbox game, there are a variety of ways to make money. The main way you will make your gold is by using your labor.
</p><p>Choose one that fits your playstyle and the amount of time you want to commit to making gold. As you increase your proficiency in any given profession, your <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Silver_per_labor&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">silver per labor</a> spent will increase.
</p><p>If you haven't, it may also be helpful to read the <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/getting-started#Making_Gold">Making Gold</a> section of the <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/getting-started">Getting Started</a> guide.
</p>

<h2 id="Land">Land</h2>
<p><i>See <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/owning-land">Owning Land</a> for additional information</i>
</p><p>Land has many different uses to help a player's economy. It can be used to grow materials that can be sold or crafted. It can also be used to <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/staging-packs">stage</a> trade packs for more efficient trade running. Some players even make money from simply claiming and selling land. There are a variety of <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/building-designs">building designs</a> that can be used to claim land. Generally, they fall into 3 categories: <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Scarecrows&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">Scarecrows</a>, <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Houses&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">Houses</a>, and <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/index.php?title=Farmhouses&action=edit&redlink=1" rel="noopener">Farmhouses</a>.
</p>
