Earning Gold

This page is a WIP

ArcheAge is a sandbox game, there are a variety of ways to make money. The main way you will make your gold is by using your labor.

Choose one that fits your playstyle and the amount of time you want to commit to making gold. As you increase your proficiency in any given profession, your silver per labor spent will increase.

If you haven't, it may also be helpful to read the Making Gold section of the Getting Started guide.

Land

See Owning Land for additional information

Land has many different uses to help a player's economy. It can be used to grow materials that can be sold or crafted. It can also be used to stage trade packs for more efficient trade running. Some players even make money from simply claiming and selling land. There are a variety of building designs that can be used to claim land. Generally, they fall into 3 categories: Scarecrows, Houses, and Farmhouses.

Professions

  • Larceny
  • Fishing
  • Commerce
  • Farming/Gathering
  • Mining
  • Any crafting profession

Note that players will also pay your for your labor.

Labor-free Methods

  • Working on a castle (Lord Coins)
  • Selling your Gilda Stars
  • Selling your Honor Points
  • Selling your Prestige
  • Selling your Loyalty
  • Selling your Merit Badges

Other Methods

  • Auroria Land (Logs / Waters)
  • Mysterious Chests
  • Abyssal Attack
