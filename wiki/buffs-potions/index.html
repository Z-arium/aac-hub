Buffs & Potions

If you're ever PvPing, it's often a good idea to buff up to improve your strength. Most raid leads want players to use them since they bring a lot of stats such in damage and survivability.

Buffs

Dawn of Glory

A buff obtained from Honor shop. When used the item gives option to choose from five different buffs which last for 30 minutes.

Honor Pot Options
Blue Goblet of Honor +1200 Resilience
Yellow Goblet of Honor +1400 Focus
Purple Goblet of Honor +1255 Toughness
Pink Goblet of Honor +15% Melee and Ranged Attack
Gray Goblet of Honor +15% Magic and Healing Power

Secret Gift

Increases Attack Speed +111, Skill Damage +3.0%, and Casting Time -10.0% for 1h. Also increases XP earned 20%.

Assorted Ribs

Assorted ribs also gives option to choose which stat player wants.

  • Savory Ribs Increases Strength +60 for 30min.
  • Rib Kebab Increases Agility +60 for 30min.
  • Smoked Ribs Increases Spirit +60 for 30min.
  • Roast Ribs Increases Intelligence +60 for 30min.

Assorted Pigglewiggle

Assorted Pigglewiggle also give several options to choose from after. Assorted Ribs give only one stat while Pigglewiggle is little bit advanced and gives several stats. Pigglewiggle is combination from Assorted Ribs, Jujube Sparkling Wine and Jujube Juice which by themselves would give max hp and max mp. Only one of these can be used at a time.

  • Golden Bacon increases max health +1300, max mana +1300, PvE Skill Damage +6% and Strength +60. Reduces received PvE damage -4%.
  • Royal Ham increases max health +1300, max mana +1300, PvE Skill Damage +6% and Agility +60. Reduces received PvE damage -4%.
  • Porcine Delight increases max health +1300, max mana +1300, PvE Skill Damage +6% and Spirit +60. Reduces received PvE damage -4%.
  • Imperial Sausage increases max health +1300, max mana +1300, PvE Skill Damage +6% and Intelligence +60. Reduces received PvE damage -4%.

Spellbook: Unstoppable Will

Spellbook gives also 3 options for buffs once used.

  • Spellbook: Unstoppable Force Increases Melee, Ranged, and Magic attacks +69 for 30min.

  • Spellbook: Brick Wall Decreases received melee damage -38 for 30min.

  • Spellbook: Unstoppable Will Decreases all types of received damage -21 for 30min, and increases Physical/Magic Attacks +30

Kingdom's Shadow

Kingdom's shadow gives 3 options

  • Kingdom's Heart Increases Physical Defense +1800 for 30min.

  • Ancient Library Relic Increases Magic Defense +1800 for 30min.

  • Kingdom's Shadow Increases Physical/Magical Defenses +810 for 30min.

Valau Rune and Zena Rune + Fortuna Die

Using Valau Rune and Zena Rune one after another gives a buff for 30min that converts damage into health. By using Fortuna Die after the runes the effect multiplies based on random roll.

Jujube Juice

Increases max health +1300 for 30 minutes. Decreases PvE received damage -4%.

Jujube Sparkling Wine

Increases max mana +1300 for 30 minutes. Increases PvE SKill Attacks +6%.

Potions

Most commonly used potions are mentioned here. Some if not all have higher grade ones but those are more expensive.

Desert Fire

Instant HP recovery + over time recovery effect. Restores 2082 health instantly, and then 2082 health over the next 10 seconds.

Nui's Nova

Instant HP recovery. Restores 6947 health.

Kraken's Might

Instant MP recovery. Restores 4101 mana.

Mossy Pool

Instant MP recovery + over time recovery effect. Restores 1233 mana instantly, then 1233 mana over the next 10 seconds.

Underwater potions

Underwater potions mean that they make players character able to breathe underwater and so makes it possible to stay under longer. Important for water PvP content such as Kraken. Other possible uses are for recovering the treasures underwater.

Dahuta's Bubble

Allows you to breathe underwater for 45 minutes.

Monarch of the Sea

Enables the player to select from three options.

  • Monarch of Arcadian
  • Monarch of Castaway
  • Monarch of Halcyona

Loot drop rate items

Greedy Grimoire

Increases loot drop rate +10% for 1h.

Glittery Charcoal Milk

Increases loot drop rate +30% for 1h. Only one milk can be used at a time.

Mysterious Glittery Charcoal Milk

Increases loot drop rate +50% for 1h. Only one milk can be used at a time.
