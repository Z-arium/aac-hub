Ayanad Library and dungeon dailies

The daily Library floor quests, the Ayanad Agent quests and the dungeon board dailies, step by step.

The Ayanad Library is in Diamond Shores. Its daily quests give EXP, Ayanad Wizard's Tokens, Nayah Crystal Shards and Ayanad Scroll Scraps. You need 30 Wizard's Tokens for the Ayanad Earring questline. Each floor has 3 quests in a chain: finish Part 1 to get Part 2, and Part 2 to get Part 3.

Go in a group. The Library is full of elite mobs, and kills are shared in a party.

Floor 1 dailies

Reclaim the First Floor, Parts 1 to 3. Level 51.

  1. Enter the Ayanad Library and go to the Floor 1 lobby.
  2. Click the Ayanad Library Task Board and take Reclaim the First Floor, Part 1.
  3. Kill hostile creatures on the first floor until the counter is full.
  4. Take Part 2 from the Task Board and kill more first-floor creatures.
  5. Take Part 3 and do the same.

Rewards: Part 3 gives a Nayah Crystal Shard and an Ayanad Scroll Scrap.

Floor 2 dailies

Reclaim the Second Floor, Parts 1 to 3. Level 52.

  1. Talk to Lemian on the Introspect Path (the Floor 1 lobby) and take Reclaim the Second Floor, Part 1.
  2. Go up to the second floor (Libris Garden) and kill hostile creatures until the counter is full.
  3. Take Part 2 from Lemian and repeat on the second floor.
  4. Take Part 3 and repeat.

Floor 3 dailies

Reclaim the Third Floor, Parts 1 to 3. Level 54.

  1. Go to the third floor (Verdant Skychamber) and talk to Bresgo. Take Reclaim the Third Floor, Part 1.
  2. Kill hostile creatures on the third floor until the counter is full.
  3. Take Part 2 from Bresgo and repeat.
  4. Take Part 3 and repeat.

Rewards: Part 3 gives a Nayah Crystal Shard.

Ayanad Agent quests

An Ayanad Agent, Parts 1 to 3. Level 54.

  1. Take An Ayanad Agent, Part 1 from an Ayanad Disciple, Lemian or Bresgo inside the Library.
  2. Kill hostile creatures on any floor of the Library. This fits neatly alongside the floor quests above.
  3. Take Part 2, then Part 3, from the same NPCs and keep killing.

Library bosses

The Library's dungeon bosses are Recordkeeper Wynn, Halnaak and Alexander.

  • On Saturdays, the Shadow Daru gives two Ayanad Library token quests (10 tokens each) for killing them.
  • Recordkeeper Wynn drops the Unknown Orb that starts the Ayanad Earring questline.

Dungeon board dailies

Each dungeon has a quest board outside its entrance. Its daily quests pay Mistmight Stones (standard dungeons) or Sunstrength Stones (greater dungeons), which you need for the Ynystere Ring.

  1. Pick a dungeon:
    • Standard: Palace Cellar, Sharpwind Mines, Hadir Farm, Burnt Castle, Kroloal Cradle, Howling Abyss
    • Greater: the Greater versions of the same six
  2. Before you go in, take the daily quests from the board outside the entrance.
  3. Run the dungeon with a party and kill the bosses the quests name.
  4. Turn the quests in at the board.

Rewards: 60 Mistmight Stones and 150 Sunstrength Stones count toward the Ynystere Ring.
