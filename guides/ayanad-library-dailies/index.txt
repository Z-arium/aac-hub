12:T107d,<p>The <strong>Ayanad Library</strong> is in <strong>Diamond Shores</strong>. Its daily quests give EXP, <strong>Ayanad Wizard&#39;s Tokens</strong>, Nayah Crystal Shards and Ayanad Scroll Scraps. You need 30 Wizard&#39;s Tokens for the <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/ayanad-earring-library">Ayanad Earring</a> questline. Each floor has 3 quests in a chain: finish Part 1 to get Part 2, and Part 2 to get Part 3.</p>
<p>Go in a group. The Library is full of elite mobs, and kills are shared in a party.</p>
<h2 id="floor-1-dailies">Floor 1 dailies</h2>
<p><strong>Reclaim the First Floor, Parts 1 to 3.</strong> Level 51.</p>
<ol>
<li>Enter the Ayanad Library and go to the <strong>Floor 1</strong> lobby.</li>
<li>Click the <strong>Ayanad Library Task Board</strong> and take <strong>Reclaim the First Floor, Part 1</strong>.</li>
<li>Kill hostile creatures on the first floor until the counter is full.</li>
<li>Take <strong>Part 2</strong> from the Task Board and kill more first-floor creatures.</li>
<li>Take <strong>Part 3</strong> and do the same.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Rewards:</strong> Part 3 gives a Nayah Crystal Shard and an Ayanad Scroll Scrap.</p>
<h2 id="floor-2-dailies">Floor 2 dailies</h2>
<p><strong>Reclaim the Second Floor, Parts 1 to 3.</strong> Level 52.</p>
<ol>
<li>Talk to <strong>Lemian</strong> on the <strong>Introspect Path</strong> (the Floor 1 lobby) and take <strong>Reclaim the Second Floor, Part 1</strong>.</li>
<li>Go up to the <strong>second floor</strong> (Libris Garden) and kill hostile creatures until the counter is full.</li>
<li>Take <strong>Part 2</strong> from Lemian and repeat on the second floor.</li>
<li>Take <strong>Part 3</strong> and repeat.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="floor-3-dailies">Floor 3 dailies</h2>
<p><strong>Reclaim the Third Floor, Parts 1 to 3.</strong> Level 54.</p>
<ol>
<li>Go to the <strong>third floor</strong> (Verdant Skychamber) and talk to <strong>Bresgo</strong>. Take <strong>Reclaim the Third Floor, Part 1</strong>.</li>
<li>Kill hostile creatures on the third floor until the counter is full.</li>
<li>Take <strong>Part 2</strong> from Bresgo and repeat.</li>
<li>Take <strong>Part 3</strong> and repeat.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Rewards:</strong> Part 3 gives a Nayah Crystal Shard.</p>
<h2 id="ayanad-agent-quests">Ayanad Agent quests</h2>
<p><strong>An Ayanad Agent, Parts 1 to 3.</strong> Level 54.</p>
<ol>
<li>Take <strong>An Ayanad Agent, Part 1</strong> from an <strong>Ayanad Disciple</strong>, <strong>Lemian</strong> or <strong>Bresgo</strong> inside the Library.</li>
<li>Kill hostile creatures on <strong>any floor</strong> of the Library. This fits neatly alongside the floor quests above.</li>
<li>Take <strong>Part 2</strong>, then <strong>Part 3</strong>, from the same NPCs and keep killing.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="library-bosses">Library bosses</h2>
<p>The Library&#39;s dungeon bosses are <strong>Recordkeeper Wynn</strong>, <strong>Halnaak</strong> and <strong>Alexander</strong>.</p>
<ul>
<li>On Saturdays, the Shadow Daru gives two <a href="/aac-hub/guides/weekly-activity-tokens#ayanad-library">Ayanad Library token quests</a> (10 tokens each) for killing them.</li>
<li>Recordkeeper Wynn drops the Unknown Orb that starts the <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/ayanad-earring-library">Ayanad Earring</a> questline.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="dungeon-board-dailies">Dungeon board dailies</h2>
<p>Each dungeon has a <strong>quest board outside its entrance</strong>. Its daily quests pay <strong>Mistmight Stones</strong> (standard dungeons) or <strong>Sunstrength Stones</strong> (greater dungeons), which you need for the Ynystere Ring.</p>
<ol>
<li>Pick a dungeon:<ul>
<li><strong>Standard:</strong> Palace Cellar, Sharpwind Mines, Hadir Farm, Burnt Castle, Kroloal Cradle, Howling Abyss</li>
<li><strong>Greater:</strong> the Greater versions of the same six</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Before you go in, take the daily quests from the <strong>board outside the entrance</strong>.</li>
<li>Run the dungeon with a party and kill the bosses the quests name.</li>
<li>Turn the quests in at the board.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Rewards:</strong> 60 Mistmight Stones and 150 Sunstrength Stones count toward the <a href="/aac-hub/wiki/ynystere-ring">Ynystere Ring</a>.</p>
