Gold making

Best ways to make gold right now

Every gold method ranked by gold per labor and per hour, what each one needs, how risky it is, and which life skill to level first.

The short answer

  1. Never waste labor. Labor is your real money. Spend it every day before it hits the cap.
  2. Spend it on trade packs (Commerce). It is the best all-round gold per labor on Classic, it gets better the more you do it, and Classic boosts both its labor use and its payouts.
  3. Sport fishing at sea is the other top earner if you have a boat and don't mind pirates. Saltwater fish got a 20% gold boost in patch 1.3.2.1.
  4. Farm mobs (Larceny) when you are out of labor. It needs no investment and pays for your time, not your labor.
  5. Sell what your dailies already give you. Gilda Stars, Honor, Prestige, Loyalty and Merit Badges can all be sold for free gold.

Best life skill to level right now: Commerce, because it is the steadiest money. If you want a bet on demand, level Taming on the side. It's brand new (patch 1.3.6), Whistles of the Wild go into almost every taming recipe, and tamed mounts sell on the auction house.

Why labor comes first

On Classic you regain 40 labor every 5 minutes, online or offline. That is 480 labor an hour, or about 11,500 a day. The cap is 10,000, so you go from empty to full in about 21 hours.

What this means for you:

  • Log in and spend your labor at least once a day. Every hour you sit at the cap throws away 480 labor.
  • The method that turns labor into the most gold wins over a day. Gold per hour only matters for the time you spend after your labor is gone.
  • The higher your proficiency in a skill, the more silver you make per labor. Sticking to one or two skills beats spreading out.

Use the Labor calculator to see when you'll hit the cap.

Silver per labor

Players compare methods by silver per labor (s/l): profit divided by the labor it cost.

Example from the wiki: you turn 3 logs worth 5 silver each into lumber that sells for 25 silver, for 5 labor. Profit is 25 − 15 = 10 silver, so it's 2 s/l.

Always check the auction house (P) before you craft, so you know if the raw material or the finished item is worth more.

Trade packs (best overall)

Classic boosts both the labor used and the gold paid out on commerce, so every pack is worth more than on other versions.

How to start on the East side (Haranya):

  1. Open your folio (O), go to Commerce, Haranyan Specialties, and pick a pack whose materials you can grow or buy cheaply.
  2. Craft it at the zone's Specialty Workbench (filter the map by Infrastructure, Specialty Workbench).
  3. Load packs onto a vehicle. A farm cart carries far more than your back.
  4. Before you sell, press Shift+O and check the route's turn-in percentage (70% to 130%). Sell when it's high. It drops as people sell and climbs back over time.
  5. Sell to a Gold Trader. Popular East runs end in Solis: Sunbite to Solis, Perinoor to Solis and Rokhala to Solis.

To earn more:

  • Farther routes pay more.
  • Stage packs on land just outside a zone while it's at peace, then finish the run later.
  • Larders sit on your land for 3 days and need no workbench. They can be replanted since patch 1.3.2.
  • The Freedich Gold Trader takes packs from any continent and usually pays the most per pack, but it spawns only twice a day and it's PvP. Check the timers.

Work out profit per pack with the Trade pack calculator.

Sport fishing

  • Classic doubles both the labor and the gold for sport fishing, so it burns labor quickly and pays well.
  • Saltwater fish pay 20% more since patch 1.3.2.1. Freshwater fish were also raised in 1.3.1.4, but saltwater is the better earner.
  • Part of the labor is spent at turn-in, so you lose less if pirates take your catch.
  • Pools show their timers and each has a set fish type. A Marlin pool is worth a lot.
  • Needs a fishing boat (dock deposit is 45 gold) and a good rod.

See the Fishing guide.

Mining and Anya Pebbles

  • Start at a dig site. East side: Halo Hollow in Solis Headlands, or Glitterstone Mine in Ynystere (PvP).
  • Buy Dawnsdrop Fists early. The speed bonus works at any grade.
  • Stack buffs (Luke's Helping Hand, family and statue buffs) to reach about 42,000 easily.
  • At 50,000 Mining with buffs you can mine Anya Pebbles, which sell well. Refining them into Anya Ingots sells for more but costs much more labor.
  • A guildmate's Miner Farmhouse drill gives free ore and stone without labor.

Larceny (no labor? farm mobs)

Coin purses and crates drop from mobs. Prince's Crates drop in every Auroria zone and Queen's Crates near purified lodestones. Queen's Crates can give Regrade Braziers and a small chance at a Clear Synthium Shard. This is the best use of play time once your labor is spent. Opening purses costs labor, so save some.

Free gold from things you already do

The wiki lists selling Gilda Stars, Honor Points, Prestige, Loyalty and Merit Badges as labor-free gold. On Classic most Honor and Prestige items are tradeable, so other players buy them. If you do your dailies, sell what you don't need.

  • Taming prices. The profession is new and we haven't found price data for mounts or whistles yet.
