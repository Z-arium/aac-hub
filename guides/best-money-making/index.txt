18:T298c,<h2 id="the-short-answer">The short answer</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Never waste labor.</strong> Labor is your real money. Spend it every day before it hits the cap.</li>
<li><strong>Spend it on trade packs (Commerce).</strong> It is the best all-round gold per labor on Classic, it gets better the more you do it, and Classic boosts both its labor use and its payouts.</li>
<li><strong>Sport fishing at sea</strong> is the other top earner if you have a boat and don&#39;t mind pirates. Saltwater fish got a 20% gold boost in patch 1.3.2.1.</li>
<li><strong>Farm mobs (Larceny) when you are out of labor.</strong> It needs no investment and pays for your time, not your labor.</li>
<li><strong>Sell what your dailies already give you.</strong> Gilda Stars, Honor, Prestige, Loyalty and Merit Badges can all be sold for free gold.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Best life skill to level right now:</strong> Commerce, because it is the steadiest money. If you want a bet on demand, level <strong>Taming</strong> on the side. It&#39;s brand new (patch 1.3.6), Whistles of the Wild go into almost every taming recipe, and tamed mounts sell on the auction house.</p>
<h2 id="why-labor-comes-first">Why labor comes first</h2>
<p>On Classic you regain <strong>40 labor every 5 minutes</strong>, online or offline. That is <strong>480 labor an hour</strong>, or about <strong>11,500 a day</strong>. The cap is <strong>10,000</strong>, so you go from empty to full in about <strong>21 hours</strong>.</p>
<p>What this means for you:</p>
<ul>
<li>Log in and spend your labor <strong>at least once a day</strong>. Every hour you sit at the cap throws away 480 labor.</li>
<li>The method that turns labor into the most gold wins over a day. Gold per hour only matters for the time you spend <em>after</em> your labor is gone.</li>
<li>The higher your proficiency in a skill, the more silver you make per labor. Sticking to one or two skills beats spreading out.</li>
</ul>
<p>Use the <a href="/aac-hub/tools/labor">Labor calculator</a> to see when you&#39;ll hit the cap.</p>
<h3 id="silver-per-labor">Silver per labor</h3>
<p>Players compare methods by <strong>silver per labor (s/l)</strong>: profit divided by the labor it cost.</p>
<blockquote>
<p>Example from the wiki: you turn 3 logs worth 5 silver each into lumber that sells for 25 silver, for 5 labor. Profit is 25 − 15 = 10 silver, so it&#39;s <strong>2 s/l</strong>.</p>
</blockquote>
<p>Always check the auction house (<strong>P</strong>) before you craft, so you know if the raw material or the finished item is worth more.</p>

<h2 id="trade-packs-best-overall">Trade packs (best overall)</h2>
<p>Classic boosts both the labor used and the gold paid out on commerce, so every pack is worth more than on other versions.</p>
<p><strong>How to start on the East side (Haranya):</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>Open your folio (<strong>O</strong>), go to <em>Commerce, Haranyan Specialties</em>, and pick a pack whose materials you can grow or buy cheaply.</li>
<li>Craft it at the zone&#39;s <strong>Specialty Workbench</strong> (filter the map by <em>Infrastructure, Specialty Workbench</em>).</li>
<li>Load packs onto a vehicle. A farm cart carries far more than your back.</li>
<li>Before you sell, press <strong>Shift+O</strong> and check the route&#39;s turn-in percentage (70% to 130%). Sell when it&#39;s high. It drops as people sell and climbs back over time.</li>
<li>Sell to a <strong>Gold Trader</strong>. Popular East runs end in <strong>Solis</strong>: Sunbite to Solis, Perinoor to Solis and Rokhala to Solis.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>To earn more:</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>Farther routes pay more.</li>
<li><strong>Stage</strong> packs on land just outside a zone while it&#39;s at peace, then finish the run later.</li>
<li><strong>Larders</strong> sit on your land for 3 days and need no workbench. They can be replanted since patch 1.3.2.</li>
<li>The <strong>Freedich Gold Trader</strong> takes packs from any continent and usually pays the most per pack, but it spawns only twice a day and it&#39;s PvP. Check the <a href="/aac-hub/timers">timers</a>.</li>
</ul>
<p>Work out profit per pack with the <a href="/aac-hub/tools/trade-packs">Trade pack calculator</a>.</p>
<h2 id="sport-fishing">Sport fishing</h2>
<ul>
<li>Classic <strong>doubles</strong> both the labor and the gold for sport fishing, so it burns labor quickly and pays well.</li>
<li><strong>Saltwater</strong> fish pay 20% more since patch 1.3.2.1. Freshwater fish were also raised in 1.3.1.4, but saltwater is the better earner.</li>
<li>Part of the labor is spent at turn-in, so you lose less if pirates take your catch.</li>
<li>Pools show their timers and each has a set fish type. A <strong>Marlin</strong> pool is worth a lot.</li>
<li>Needs a fishing boat (dock deposit is 45 gold) and a good rod.</li>
</ul>
<p>See the <a href="/aac-hub/guides/fishing">Fishing guide</a>.</p>
<h2 id="mining-and-anya-pebbles">Mining and Anya Pebbles</h2>
<ul>
<li>Start at a dig site. East side: <strong>Halo Hollow</strong> in Solis Headlands, or <strong>Glitterstone Mine</strong> in Ynystere (PvP).</li>
<li>Buy <strong>Dawnsdrop Fists</strong> early. The speed bonus works at any grade.</li>
<li>Stack buffs (Luke&#39;s Helping Hand, family and statue buffs) to reach about <strong>42,000</strong> easily.</li>
<li>At <strong>50,000 Mining with buffs</strong> you can mine <strong>Anya Pebbles</strong>, which sell well. Refining them into Anya Ingots sells for more but costs much more labor.</li>
<li>A guildmate&#39;s <strong>Miner Farmhouse</strong> drill gives free ore and stone without labor.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="larceny-no-labor-farm-mobs">Larceny (no labor? farm mobs)</h2>
<p>Coin purses and crates drop from mobs. Prince&#39;s Crates drop in every Auroria zone and Queen&#39;s Crates near purified lodestones. Queen&#39;s Crates can give Regrade Braziers and a small chance at a Clear Synthium Shard. This is the best use of play time once your labor is spent. Opening purses costs labor, so save some.</p>
<h2 id="free-gold-from-things-you-already-do">Free gold from things you already do</h2>
<p>The wiki lists selling Gilda Stars, Honor Points, Prestige, Loyalty and Merit Badges as labor-free gold. On Classic most Honor and Prestige items are tradeable, so other players buy them. If you do your <a href="/aac-hub/tools/checklist">dailies</a>, sell what you don&#39;t need.</p>
