Leveling from 1 to 55

The fastest path through the main story, when to use Growthstones, and what to do at 50 and 55.

Updated 2026-10-27

Levels 1 to about 37

Follow only the green main story quests for your race until you reach Heading to Halcyona. Use your Adventurer's Growthstones as you go.

  • Haranya can take the Caernord portal to Halcyona.
  • Nuia can walk south.

Levels 37 to 50

Keep following the green quests through Halcyona, Hellswamp and Sanddeep until Across the Sea. Spend the rest of your Growthstones so you are at least level 45.

Then teleport to Kroloal Cradle and continue to Arra's Ingredients. That should take you to level 50.

At level 50

Open your Catch-up Gear Box. See the Catch-up Gear Box page for what is inside.

Level 51 (optional)

Continue the story to The Riven Gates. It takes about 3 to 4 hours at a moderate pace. Stop before the Obsidian weapon quests.

Getting to 55

Reach 55 before you dive into harder content. Good XP sources:

  • Spending labor
  • Sign Quests in Golden Ruins
  • The Ayanad Library
  • Mobs in Auroria
  • World event quests
  • PvP in war or conflict zones
  • World bosses

Sources: wiki.aa-classic.com/Getting_Started
