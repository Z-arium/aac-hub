18:T5a8,<h2 id="levels-1-to-about-37">Levels 1 to about 37</h2>
<p>Follow only the <strong>green main story quests</strong> for your race until you reach <em>Heading to Halcyona</em>. Use your <strong>Adventurer&#39;s Growthstones</strong> as you go.</p>
<ul>
<li>Haranya can take the Caernord portal to Halcyona.</li>
<li>Nuia can walk south.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="levels-37-to-50">Levels 37 to 50</h2>
<p>Keep following the green quests through Halcyona, Hellswamp and Sanddeep until <em>Across the Sea</em>. Spend the rest of your Growthstones so you are at least <strong>level 45</strong>.</p>
<p>Then teleport to Kroloal Cradle and continue to <em>Arra&#39;s Ingredients</em>. That should take you to <strong>level 50</strong>.</p>
<h2 id="at-level-50">At level 50</h2>
<p>Open your <strong>Catch-up Gear Box</strong>. See the <a href="https://wiki.aa-classic.com/Catch-up_Gear_Box">Catch-up Gear Box</a> page for what is inside.</p>
<h2 id="level-51-optional">Level 51 (optional)</h2>
<p>Continue the story to <em>The Riven Gates</em>. It takes about 3 to 4 hours at a moderate pace. Stop before the Obsidian weapon quests.</p>
<h2 id="getting-to-55">Getting to 55</h2>
<p>Reach 55 before you dive into harder content. Good XP sources:</p>
<ul>
<li>Spending labor</li>
<li>Sign Quests in Golden Ruins</li>
<li>The Ayanad Library</li>
<li>Mobs in Auroria</li>
<li>World event quests</li>
<li>PvP in war or conflict zones</li>
<li>World bosses</li>
</ul>
