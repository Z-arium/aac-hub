Weekly Activity Tokens

Five weekly quests pay 136 Activity Tokens, plus 5 a day from the daily merit quest. Here is where to go.

ArcheAge Classic pays Activity Tokens for a set of weekly quests. Doing all of them gives 136 tokens a week, plus 5 a day from the daily merit quest.

The rules: each weekly quest can only be picked up on its day, and that is also when it resets (midnight UTC). Once you have it, you can finish it any time that week. Most come from the Shadow Daru, who appears in each main capital (Marianople, Austera and the other big cities) on Saturday and Sunday. On Fridays a Shadow Daru also stands behind the Pirate Nui on the broken bridge.

Day Quest Tokens
Friday The Crystalline Bloodbath 30
Saturday Preparing for the Fish Market 20
Saturday Have You Ever Seen A Ghost? (Delphinad Ghost Ship) 33
Saturday Ayanad Library (2 quests) 10 each
Sunday The Crimson Threat (Anthalon) 33

The Crystalline Bloodbath

Friday, 30 tokens.

  1. On Friday, take The Crystalline Bloodbath from the Shadow Daru.
  2. Go to the Crystalline Fields in Diamond Shores. Factions fight there over the Ascension Crystal extraction.
  3. Kill 50 enemy players there. Pirates need fewer. Group up, because the event draws a crowd.
  4. Turn it in to the Shadow Daru for 30 tokens. It also gives 20 Leadership.

Preparing for the Fish Market

Saturday, 20 tokens.

  1. On Saturday, take Preparing for the Fish Market from the Shadow Daru.
  2. Catch 10 fish anywhere. See the fishing guide.
  3. Sell them at any Fish Stand.
  4. Hand it in for 20 tokens.

Tip: do it in a party or raid. Every fish anyone sells to a vendor counts for the whole group. Fish made into decor don't count. Many players do it during the Saturday Mirage Isle Fishing Contest.

Have You Ever Seen A Ghost?

Saturday, 33 tokens.

  1. On Saturday, take it from the Shadow Daru.
  2. Help sink the Delphinad Ghost Ship. The full steps are in the Gilda Stars guide.
  3. Hand it in for 33 tokens. The same kill also counts for the daily Ghost Ships of Delphinad quest (3 Gilda Stars).

Ayanad Library

Saturday, 2 quests at 10 tokens each.

  1. On Saturday, take both Library quests from the Shadow Daru.
  2. Go to the Ayanad Library in Diamond Shores and kill the library bosses the quests ask for. Do your daily floor quests on the same run.
  3. Hand in both for 10 tokens each.

The Crimson Threat

Sunday, 33 tokens.

First you need custom story Chapter 99. In it you kill the Hounds of Kyrios (quest 99.1) and then Anthalon.

  1. On Sunday, take The Crimson Threat from the Shadow Daru.
  2. Go to Sungold Fields for the Crimson Rift. Anthalon spawns when the rift ends. Check timers and join a raid with x cr in faction chat.
  3. Help kill Anthalon.
  4. Hand it in for 33 tokens.

Active Adventurers Appreciated

Daily, 5 tokens.

  1. Unlock it by finishing story quest 99.1 (Hounds of Kyrios).
  2. Open your daily merit quests and take Active Adventurers Appreciated.
  3. Spend labor on the profession it asks for: Larceny, Commerce, Fishing or Mining.
  4. Turn it in from the merit quest list for 5 tokens.

One-time tokens

  • Story quest 99.1 (Hounds of Kyrios): 1 token.
  • Veroe Harvest Festival participation achievement: 5 tokens.
  • The Harvest Festival Beckons! from an auctioneer: 50 tokens.

Spending them

Go to Mirage Isle. The token bench is down the stairs on the left after you arrive. It sells starter accessories, regrade scrolls, Synthium Shards, Gilda Stars and Warrior Medals. With a Priceless Coin, it also sells some gliders and mounts.
