19:T1468,<p>ArcheAge Classic pays <strong>Activity Tokens</strong> for a set of weekly quests. Doing all of them gives <strong>136 tokens a week</strong>, plus 5 a day from the daily merit quest.</p>
<p><strong>The rules:</strong> each weekly quest can only be picked up on its day, and that is also when it resets (midnight UTC). Once you have it, you can finish it any time that week. Most come from the <strong>Shadow Daru</strong>, who appears in each main capital (Marianople, Austera and the other big cities) on Saturday and Sunday. On Fridays a Shadow Daru also stands behind the Pirate Nui on the broken bridge.</p>

<h2 id="the-crystalline-bloodbath">The Crystalline Bloodbath</h2>
<p><strong>Friday, 30 tokens.</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>On <strong>Friday</strong>, take <strong>The Crystalline Bloodbath</strong> from the <strong>Shadow Daru</strong>.</li>
<li>Go to the <strong>Crystalline Fields</strong> in <strong>Diamond Shores</strong>. Factions fight there over the Ascension Crystal extraction.</li>
<li>Kill <strong>50 enemy players</strong> there. Pirates need fewer. Group up, because the event draws a crowd.</li>
<li>Turn it in to the Shadow Daru for 30 tokens. It also gives 20 Leadership.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="preparing-for-the-fish-market">Preparing for the Fish Market</h2>
<p><strong>Saturday, 20 tokens.</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>On <strong>Saturday</strong>, take <strong>Preparing for the Fish Market</strong> from the <strong>Shadow Daru</strong>.</li>
<li>Catch <strong>10 fish</strong> anywhere. See the <a href="/aac-hub/guides/fishing">fishing guide</a>.</li>
<li>Sell them at any <strong>Fish Stand</strong>.</li>
<li>Hand it in for 20 tokens.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Tip:</strong> do it in a party or raid. Every fish anyone sells to a vendor counts for the whole group. Fish made into decor don&#39;t count. Many players do it during the Saturday Mirage Isle Fishing Contest.</p>
<h2 id="have-you-ever-seen-a-ghost">Have You Ever Seen A Ghost?</h2>
<p><strong>Saturday, 33 tokens.</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>On <strong>Saturday</strong>, take it from the <strong>Shadow Daru</strong>.</li>
<li>Help sink the <strong>Delphinad Ghost Ship</strong>. The full steps are in the <a href="/aac-hub/guides/gilda-stars#delphinad-ghost-ship">Gilda Stars guide</a>.</li>
<li>Hand it in for 33 tokens. The same kill also counts for the daily Ghost Ships of Delphinad quest (3 Gilda Stars).</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="ayanad-library">Ayanad Library</h2>
<p><strong>Saturday, 2 quests at 10 tokens each.</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>On <strong>Saturday</strong>, take both Library quests from the <strong>Shadow Daru</strong>.</li>
<li>Go to the <strong>Ayanad Library</strong> in Diamond Shores and kill the library bosses the quests ask for. Do your <a href="/aac-hub/guides/ayanad-library-dailies">daily floor quests</a> on the same run.</li>
<li>Hand in both for 10 tokens each.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="the-crimson-threat">The Crimson Threat</h2>
<p><strong>Sunday, 33 tokens.</strong></p>
<p><strong>First you need custom story Chapter 99.</strong> In it you kill the Hounds of Kyrios (quest 99.1) and then Anthalon.</p>
<ol>
<li>On <strong>Sunday</strong>, take <strong>The Crimson Threat</strong> from the <strong>Shadow Daru</strong>.</li>
<li>Go to <strong>Sungold Fields</strong> for the Crimson Rift. Anthalon spawns when the rift ends. Check <a href="/aac-hub/timers">timers</a> and join a raid with <code>x cr</code> in faction chat.</li>
<li>Help kill <strong>Anthalon</strong>.</li>
<li>Hand it in for 33 tokens.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="active-adventurers-appreciated">Active Adventurers Appreciated</h2>
<p><strong>Daily, 5 tokens.</strong></p>
<ol>
<li>Unlock it by finishing story quest <strong>99.1</strong> (Hounds of Kyrios).</li>
<li>Open your <strong>daily merit quests</strong> and take <strong>Active Adventurers Appreciated</strong>.</li>
<li>Spend labor on the profession it asks for: <strong>Larceny, Commerce, Fishing or Mining</strong>.</li>
<li>Turn it in from the merit quest list for 5 tokens.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="one-time-tokens">One-time tokens</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Story quest 99.1</strong> (Hounds of Kyrios): 1 token.</li>
<li><strong>Veroe Harvest Festival</strong> participation achievement: 5 tokens.</li>
<li><strong>The Harvest Festival Beckons!</strong> from an auctioneer: 50 tokens.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="spending-them">Spending them</h2>
<p>Go to <strong>Mirage Isle</strong>. The token bench is down the stairs on the left after you arrive. It sells starter accessories, regrade scrolls, Synthium Shards, Gilda Stars and Warrior Medals. With a Priceless Coin, it also sells some gliders and mounts.</p>
<p>Track your progress in the <a href="/aac-hub/tools/checklist">checklist</a>.</p>
