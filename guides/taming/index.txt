10:T10c6,<p>Taming is a <strong>custom profession added in patch 1.3.6</strong>. Tame beasts around Erenor and ride them as mounts. More Taming proficiency makes you better at it.</p>
<h2>Your first backpack</h2>
<p>Craft a <strong>Greenhorn&#39;s Backpack</strong> at a <strong>Handicraft Kiln</strong> (150 labor):</p>
<ul>
<li>5 Vita Seed</li>
<li>10 Leather</li>
<li>10 Apple</li>
</ul>
<p>Equipping it gives you the taming skills.</p>
<h2>How to tame</h2>
<ol>
<li>Find beasts with the <strong>Beast of the Wild</strong> buff. When one dies, there&#39;s a small chance a tameable copy spawns.</li>
<li>Like sport fishing, the beast shows buffs. Answer each one with the matching skill.</li>
</ol>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>
<th>Skill</th>
<th>What it does</th>
</tr>
</thead>
<tbody><tr>
<td>Wrestle the Wild</td>
<td>Starts the attempt</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Soothe</td>
<td>Your main progress skill. Spam it, like Big Reel In</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Feed</td>
<td>Uses 1 Vita Root each time. Bring plenty</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Dance!</td>
<td>Counters the buff-matching</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Show of Strength</td>
<td>Removes the Intimidated effect beasts sometimes put on you</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Wrangle</td>
<td>Reins the beast in before it gets away from you</td>
</tr>
</tbody></table>
<h2>Whistles of the Wild</h2>
<p>Release a tamed beast with a <strong>Wildcaller Flute</strong> to get Whistles of the Wild. This removes the beast for good. Whistles go into almost every taming recipe.</p>

<thead>
<tr>

</tr>

</tr>
<tr>
<td>Grand</td>
<td>about 3</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Arcane</td>
<td>about 5</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Celestial</td>
<td>about 10</td>
</tr>
</tbody></table>

<p>Higher tiers mean regrading into grades that can break, so costs climb fast. The wiki puts a full <strong>Tier 2 set at about 1,400 gold</strong> and a <strong>Tier 3 set at about 8,550 gold</strong> on average. A Tier 3 set needs about 6,900 Whistles of the Wild.</p>
