Taming guide

Classic's custom taming profession. Backpacks, the taming minigame, Whistles of the Wild and mount gear.

Updated 2026-10-28

Taming is a custom profession added in patch 1.3.6. Tame beasts around Erenor and ride them as mounts. More Taming proficiency makes you better at it.

Your first backpack

Craft a Greenhorn's Backpack at a Handicraft Kiln (150 labor):

  • 5 Vita Seed
  • 10 Leather
  • 10 Apple

Equipping it gives you the taming skills.

How to tame

  1. Find beasts with the Beast of the Wild buff. When one dies, there's a small chance a tameable copy spawns.
  2. Like sport fishing, the beast shows buffs. Answer each one with the matching skill.
Skill What it does
Wrestle the Wild Starts the attempt
Soothe Your main progress skill. Spam it, like Big Reel In
Feed Uses 1 Vita Root each time. Bring plenty
Dance! Counters the buff-matching
Show of Strength Removes the Intimidated effect beasts sometimes put on you
Wrangle Reins the beast in before it gets away from you

Whistles of the Wild

Release a tamed beast with a Wildcaller Flute to get Whistles of the Wild. This removes the beast for good. Whistles go into almost every taming recipe.

Beast grade Average whistles
Mundane 1
Grand about 3
Arcane about 5
Celestial about 10

Mount consumables

All made at the Pet Accessory Workbench.

Item Taming needed Labor Effect
Cherry Bomb 18,000 25 Fully heals your mount instantly
Green Lord's Apple 60,000 25 Heals your mount 10,000 HP over 15 s
Goldscale Dew Stew 60,000 25 Restores 5,000 mount mana over 15 s
Orange-U-Glad 130,000 30 +5.0 mount speed for 7 s (80 s cooldown)
Ba-Mana Smoothie 180,000 25 Restores all mount mana instantly

Mount armor

Each set has a 3-piece bonus that gets stronger with each tier.

Set Tier 1 Tier 2 Tier 3
Swift -10% mount, riding and pet skill cooldowns -14% -16%
Survey +16.0 mount speed while overloaded +20.0 +22.0
Stout -4.8% damage taken, +4.8% mount damage 6.1% 7.4%
Support +90 mana regen for allies, pets and summons within 25 m +120 within 25 m +130 within 40 m

Higher tiers mean regrading into grades that can break, so costs climb fast. The wiki puts a full Tier 2 set at about 1,400 gold and a Tier 3 set at about 8,550 gold on average. A Tier 3 set needs about 6,900 Whistles of the Wild.

Check your odds with the Regrade calculator (Ship/Pet table).
