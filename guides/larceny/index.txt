12:T905,<p>Larceny is farming monsters for profit. Mobs drop <strong>coin purses</strong>, <strong>crates</strong> and random gear you can salvage for archeum. It needs no upfront investment, so it&#39;s a good first profession.</p>
<h2>Where to farm</h2>
<p>There are four tiers. Monsters get harder in this order:</p>
<table>
<thead>
<tr>

<th>Where</th>
</tr>

</tr>

</tr>
<tr>
<td>Prince</td>
<td>Every Auroria zone</td>
</tr>
<tr>
<td>Queen</td>
<td>Around purified lodestones in Auroria</td>
</tr>
</tbody></table>
<p>Zones in <strong>conflict or war</strong> drop more loot.</p>
