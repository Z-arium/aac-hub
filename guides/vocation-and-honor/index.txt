1c:T1d58,<p>Classic gives <strong>double Vocation</strong>, <strong>double Honor from PvE and quests</strong>, and <strong>four times Honor from PvP</strong> (wiki FAQ). Both shops open from your character window: press <strong>C</strong>, then click the cart next to the currency.</p>
<h2 id="vocation-badges">Vocation Badges</h2>
<p>Vocation Badges buy special vocation items, like Mining Drills (38,000), Captain&#39;s Protection passes (1,000), Wild Harvest seeds and Cherry Saplings.</p>
<h3 id="where-they-come-from-best-first">Where they come from (best first)</h3>
<p>Every source is a <strong>daily quest</strong>. There&#39;s no way to grind Vocation by the hour, so <strong>a lot of Vocation means doing every Vocation daily, every day</strong>. Most of them also pay Gilda Stars, so you&#39;re already doing them for the <a href="/aac-hub/tools/checklist">checklist</a>.</p>

<h3 id="if-you-meant-proficiency">If you meant proficiency</h3>
<p>Proficiency (your level in a life skill) only goes up by <strong>spending labor on that skill</strong>. To level it faster:</p>
<ul>
<li>Stick to one or two skills. Proficiency makes every later labor point worth more.</li>
<li>Stack proficiency buffs. For Mining the wiki lists <strong>Luke&#39;s Helping Hand</strong>, <strong>Dawnsdrop gear</strong>, <strong>family buffs</strong> and <strong>statue buffs</strong>, which get most players to about 42,000 Mining.</li>
<li>Taming has its own proficiency food (Orange-U-Glad and others, see the <a href="/aac-hub/guides/taming">Taming guide</a>).</li>
<li>Achievements at 10,000, 40,000 and 90,000 proficiency in any skill give rewards (<a href="/aac-hub/wiki/adventurers-guide">Adventurer&#39;s Guide</a>).</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="honor-points">Honor Points</h2>
<p>Honor buys gems, Lunagems, Honor Badges and Honorforged Medals. On Classic most honor items are <strong>tradeable</strong>, so honor also means gold (see <a href="/aac-hub/guides/best-money-making">Best ways to make gold</a>).</p>

<h3 id="a-simple-honor-day">A simple honor day</h3>
<ol>
<li>Do every <strong>Crimson Rift</strong> and the <strong>Grimgast Rift</strong> you can make. That&#39;s about 2,400 honor each, safely.</li>
<li>Join <strong>Halcyona</strong> and <strong>Mistmerrow</strong> with your faction raid. PvP honor is ×4.</li>
<li>Do your <strong>Arena</strong> daily.</li>
<li>Say yes to jury calls.</li>
</ol>
