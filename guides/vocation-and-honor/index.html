How to earn lots of Vocation and Honor

Every source of Vocation Badges and Honor Points, ranked, plus how to level proficiency fast.

Updated 2026-10-29

Classic gives double Vocation, double Honor from PvE and quests, and four times Honor from PvP (wiki FAQ). Both shops open from your character window: press C, then click the cart next to the currency.

Vocation Badges

Vocation Badges buy special vocation items, like Mining Drills (38,000), Captain's Protection passes (1,000), Wild Harvest seeds and Cherry Saplings.

Where they come from (best first)

Every source is a daily quest. There's no way to grind Vocation by the hour, so a lot of Vocation means doing every Vocation daily, every day. Most of them also pay Gilda Stars, so you're already doing them for the checklist.

# Source What you need Notes
1 Remodeled house quests (Farmhouse and Manor) A remodeled Farmhouse or Manor, yours or a guildmate's Several buildings means several sets of quests. The biggest daily source if you have land
2 Farmer's Workstation meals A Farmer's Workstation on your land 4 cooking quests a day
3 Blue Salt merit quests Nothing 5 quests from the Blue Salt Wanderer and Animal Trainer. Best source with no land
4 Family quests A family Join an active family if you can
5 Community Center dailies Nothing The materials board at the Community Center (also part of Grimgast Rift)
One time Catch-up Gear Box New or returning character 5 × 1,000 Vocation Badge Boost = 5,000 Vocation

Per labor: these quests cost little or no labor. Cooking meals and crafting Manor orders use a bit, but it's much less than a trade run. So Vocation is close to free once your buildings are set up.

Not known yet: the wiki doesn't list how many badges each quest pays, and the Farmhouse and Manor quests are still on our list to confirm in game. Check the reward box when you take the quest in game.

If you meant proficiency

Proficiency (your level in a life skill) only goes up by spending labor on that skill. To level it faster:

  • Stick to one or two skills. Proficiency makes every later labor point worth more.
  • Stack proficiency buffs. For Mining the wiki lists Luke's Helping Hand, Dawnsdrop gear, family buffs and statue buffs, which get most players to about 42,000 Mining.
  • Taming has its own proficiency food (Orange-U-Glad and others, see the Taming guide).
  • Achievements at 10,000, 40,000 and 90,000 proficiency in any skill give rewards (Adventurer's Guide).

Honor Points

Honor buys gems, Lunagems, Honor Badges and Honorforged Medals. On Classic most honor items are tradeable, so honor also means gold (see Best ways to make gold).

Where it comes from (best first)

# Source Honor Time Notes
1 PvP: kills in war zones and PvP events (Halcyona War, Mistmerrow, Crystalline Bloodbath) Varies, but PvP honor is ×4 20 to 30 min per event The best honor per hour if your side is winning fights. Risky with weak gear
2 Grimgast Rift 1,200 base, about 2,400 after the ×2 About 30 min Also 8 Gilda Stars. Safe
3 Crimson Rift 1,200 base, about 2,400 after the ×2 (the wiki event page says 1,000) About 30 min Safe. Each rift comes every 4 hours, see timers. The wiki doesn't say if the quests pay more than once a day
4 Veroe Harvest Festival 3,000 for the kill quest, 1,500 per rice pack turned in Event length Faction PvP in Hasla, when it's running
5 Arena daily Honor (amount not listed) A few minutes Also counts toward Honor Badges
6 Luscas Honor (amount not listed) About 20 min Also 2 Gilda Stars
7 Jury duty A guild level buff adds +50 to +100 jury honor When called Accept jury calls
8 Being active in Auroria Small, steady While you play there The wiki lists it as a source
Any time Buy honor items with gold As much as you can afford Instant Honor gems are tradeable on Classic
One time Adventurer's Guide reward 10,000 — Adventurer's Guide
One time Catch-up Gear Box 6,000 (6 × Honorable Victory Rank 5) — Catch-up Gear Box guide

Leadership chests from Mobilization Orders also give Hero's Honor Elixirs (Leadership).

A simple honor day

  1. Do every Crimson Rift and the Grimgast Rift you can make. That's about 2,400 honor each, safely.
  2. Join Halcyona and Mistmerrow with your faction raid. PvP honor is ×4.
  3. Do your Arena daily.
  4. Say yes to jury calls.

Not known yet: the wiki doesn't list honor for each PvP kill, Arena, Luscas or Mistmerrow. The rift numbers come from patch 1.3 and are before the server's ×2.
