Gold making

Ways to earn gold

Labor professions, labor-free methods, and Auroria, all in one place.

On Classic you regain 40 labor every 5 minutes, online or offline, up to 10,000. Spending it well is the main way to make gold. The higher your proficiency, the more silver you make per labor.

Labor professions

  • Commerce: trade packs. Labor use and payouts are boosted on Classic.
  • Fishing: sport fishing is also boosted on Classic.
  • Larceny: farm purses and crates from mobs.
  • Farming and gathering
  • Mining
  • Any crafting profession

Other players will also pay you to spend your labor for them.

No labor needed

  • Work at a castle for Lord Coins.
  • Sell Gilda Stars, Honor Points, Prestige, Loyalty Tokens or Merit Badges.

Other ways

  • Auroria land: burning logs and mineral waters.
  • Mysterious Chests.
  • Abyssal Attack: check the timers.

Land

Land lets you grow materials to sell or craft with and stage trade packs. You can also claim land and sell it. Building designs come as scarecrows, houses and farmhouses.

Use the Labor calculator so you never waste labor at the cap.
