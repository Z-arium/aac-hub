<p>On Classic you regain <strong>40 labor every 5 minutes</strong>, online or offline, up to 10,000. Spending it well is the main way to make gold. The higher your proficiency, the more silver you make per labor.</p>
<h2 id="labor-professions">Labor professions</h2>
<ul>
<li><a href="/aac-hub/guides/trade-packs">Commerce</a>: trade packs. Labor use and payouts are boosted on Classic.</li>
<li><a href="/aac-hub/guides/fishing">Fishing</a>: sport fishing is also boosted on Classic.</li>
<li><a href="/aac-hub/guides/larceny">Larceny</a>: farm purses and crates from mobs.</li>
<li>Farming and gathering</li>
<li>Mining</li>
<li>Any crafting profession</li>
</ul>
<p>Other players will also pay you to spend your labor for them.</p>
<h2 id="no-labor-needed">No labor needed</h2>
<ul>
<li>Work at a castle for <strong>Lord Coins</strong>.</li>
<li>Sell Gilda Stars, Honor Points, Prestige, Loyalty Tokens or Merit Badges.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="other-ways">Other ways</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Auroria land:</strong> burning logs and mineral waters.</li>
<li><strong>Mysterious Chests.</strong></li>
<li><strong>Abyssal Attack:</strong> check the <a href="/aac-hub/timers">timers</a>.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="land">Land</h2>
<p>Land lets you grow materials to sell or craft with and stage trade packs. You can also claim land and sell it. Building designs come as scarecrows, houses and farmhouses.</p>
<p>Use the <a href="/aac-hub/tools/labor">Labor calculator</a> so you never waste labor at the cap.</p>
