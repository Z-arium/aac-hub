1c:T7ca,<p>The Emerald weapon is built for new players. At <strong>Divine</strong> it&#39;s roughly as good as a Celestial Ayanad weapon. Upgrading it can&#39;t fail or break, costs no gold and uses very little labor. It just takes time.</p>
<h2 id="getting-one">Getting one</h2>
<ol>
<li>Get an <strong>Emerald</strong> gem.</li>
<li>Go to the <strong>Tehmi Ruins</strong> in Hasla, or get a portal to the <strong>Abandoned Drill Camp</strong>.</li>
<li>Talk to <strong>Fragment Researcher Soa</strong>. She trades your Emerald for an <strong>Infused Emerald</strong>.</li>
<li>Craft your weapon at the special bench behind her.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="upgrading">Upgrading</h2>
<p>Emerald weapons level up by <strong>synthesis</strong>: you feed Hasla weapons into them instead of regrading. Hasla weapons drop as tokens from nearby monsters. Since the February 2, 2026 patch they also drop in Karkasse Ridgelands and Hasla.</p>

<p>You need about <strong>22,400 XP</strong> to reach Divine. Going past Divine does nothing.</p>
<p>The usual advice is six T3 weapons. The wiki editor suggests <strong>28 T1 weapons</strong> instead: you move from Unique to Celestial sooner, and the extra ~500 labor is worth the time saved. Pick what fits your drops and schedule.</p>
<h2 id="switching-weapon-type">Switching weapon type</h2>
<p>At the Abandoned Drill Camp workbench, use <strong>Hasla: Emerald Destruction</strong> to break your weapon down. You get the Infused Emerald back, but all synthesis progress is lost.</p>
<p>Since it&#39;s safe, this is a good thing to work on after your daily gold-making.</p>
