<h2 id="delphinad-ghost-ship">Delphinad Ghost Ship</h2>
<p>A giant ghost ship in the <strong>Arcadian Sea</strong>. It spawns once a day and stays up for about 10 hours. Check <a href="/aac-hub/timers">timers</a>. You fight it at sea, so you need a ship with cannons or a seat on someone else&#39;s. Raids call it <strong>DGS</strong>.</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Get the quests first.</strong> At the docks of a port town, find an <strong>Archeologist</strong> (Toro, Roycia, Burnar, Bruce, Lozinda, Karna, Carson, Hermas, Waji, Jeisson, Apitus, Lao Xiam, Bashudi and others).</li>
<li>Take <strong>Ghosts from the Depths</strong>. That unlocks <strong>Ghost Ships of Delphinad</strong> from the same Archeologist. Take it as well.</li>
<li>On <strong>Saturdays</strong>, also take the weekly <a href="/aac-hub/guides/weekly-activity-tokens#have-you-ever-seen-a-ghost">Have You Ever Seen A Ghost?</a> from the Shadow Daru. The same kill pays 33 Activity Tokens.</li>
<li><strong>Find a ship raid.</strong> Watch faction chat for <code>x dgs</code> and join. Most groups sail warships, and the ghost ship hits hard, so going alone doesn&#39;t work.</li>
<li><strong>Sail out</strong> to the Delphinad Ghost Ship in the Arcadian Sea.</li>
<li><strong>Fight:</strong> man a cannon or help repair and crew your ship. Keep firing until it sinks. If your ship goes down, swim to another raid ship.</li>
<li>When it sinks, <strong>Ghost Ships of Delphinad</strong> completes. Hand it in to an Archeologist.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Rewards:</strong> <strong>3 Gilda Stars</strong>. Its loot also drops Growling Yawl Design Fragments (55 make a small warship).</p>
<h2 id="ghosts-from-the-depths">Ghosts from the Depths</h2>
<ol>
<li>Take <strong>Ghosts from the Depths</strong> from an <strong>Archeologist</strong> at the docks of a port town.</li>
<li>Sail out on a combat ship. Join a ship group if you can, because the quest is hard alone.</li>
<li>Sink the <strong>ghost ships roaming the Arcadian Sea</strong> that the quest asks for.</li>
<li>Hand it in to an Archeologist.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Rewards:</strong> <strong>1 Gilda Star</strong>. Finishing it also opens Ghost Ships of Delphinad (above).</p>
<h2 id="enemies-of-sea-trade">Enemies of Sea Trade</h2>
<ol>
<li>Take <strong>Enemies of Sea Trade</strong> from <strong>Martus, Suiharan, Bormas, Elle or Sorgo</strong>, the naval quest NPCs at the docks.</li>
<li>Sail out and kill the <strong>Seabugs</strong> the quest marks on your map.</li>
<li>Find the targets it lists in <strong>Ezna, Austera, Freedich Island, Growlgate Isle and Diamond Shores</strong>.</li>
<li>Hand it in to any of the same NPCs.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Rewards:</strong> <strong>1 Gilda Star</strong>.</p>
<h2 id="jola-meina-and-glenn">Jola, Meina and Glenn</h2>
<p>These three world bosses are called <strong>JMG</strong> in chat. Each pays 3 Gilda Stars a day.</p>
<ol>
<li>Watch faction chat for <code>x jmg</code>, or for <code>x jola</code> / <code>x meina</code> / <code>x glenn</code>.</li>
<li><strong>Jola the Cursed:</strong> go to <strong>Exeloch</strong>, where he is performing a ritual, and help kill him.</li>
<li><strong>Meina</strong> and <strong>Glenn:</strong> go to <strong>Sungold Fields</strong> and help kill each one.</li>
<li><strong>Defeat Jola the Cursed!</strong>, <strong>Defeat Meina!</strong> and <strong>Defeat Glenn!</strong> complete on their own.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Rewards:</strong> <strong>3 Gilda Stars</strong>, 2 Clear Synthium Shards and 7 Growthstones each. Jola also drops Jola&#39;s Scaly Frill and Meina drops Meina&#39;s Ring.</p>
<h2 id="large-world-bosses">Large world bosses</h2>
<p>Each of these pays <strong>3 Gilda Stars</strong> a day through its own Path of Glory quest. The Red Dragon pays 5.</p>
<ol>
<li>Pick a boss that is up soon:<ul>
<li><strong>Captain Rangora</strong>, Freedich Island, every 8 hours</li>
<li><strong>Dahuta&#39;s Champion Nazar</strong>, Sunspeck Sea, every 4 hours</li>
<li><strong>Morpheus the Forsaken</strong>, Growlgate Isle, every 8 hours</li>
<li><strong>Hanure the Hunter</strong>, Hasla, every 14 hours</li>
<li><strong>Zaldrane the Enchantress</strong>, Sanddeep</li>
<li>Scheduled ones are on <a href="/aac-hub/timers">timers</a>: <strong>Red Dragon</strong> (Karkasse Ridgelands), <strong>Kraken</strong> and <strong>Hanure the Berserker</strong> (Hasla)</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Join the raid in faction chat (<code>x</code> plus the boss name).</li>
<li>Help with the kill. The quest completes on its own.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="minor-world-bosses">Minor world bosses</h2>
<p>These pay <strong>1 Gilda Star</strong> each, and most can be soloed at level 55. Each has its own Path of Glory quest, so do as many as you like.</p>
<ol>
<li>Pick a route. Fast respawns first:<ul>
<li><strong>Solzreed Peninsula:</strong> Akanun the Tidelord (every hour), Gilneas, Castigant Reaper</li>
<li><strong>Arcum Iris:</strong> Karon the Herdlord (every 30 minutes), Rainbow Ostrich</li>
<li><strong>Villanelle:</strong> Reedpipe Leader Palaxis (every hour), Singing Greenman Shoon</li>
<li><strong>Elsewhere (every 4 hours):</strong> Hellhook the Impaler (Lilyut Hills), Verith&#39;s Masterpiece (White Arden), Annulta the Yatamaton (Tigerspine Mountains), Werespider Arak and Jamerath the Shadowmage (Silent Forest), The General&#39;s Spirit (Perinoor Ruins)</li>
<li><strong>Level 55 Shadow Rift bosses (8 to 14 hours):</strong> Daruda the Watcher (Cinderstone), The Croakfiend (Hellswamp), Tanis the Despoiler (Karkasse), Blightshade (Rookborne Basin), Goretusk the Leveler (Windscour Savannah), Yaksa Leader (Perinoor Ruins)</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Fly or ride to the boss and kill it.</li>
<li>Each quest completes on its own.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="workstation-meals">Workstation meals</h2>
<p>You need a <strong>Farmer&#39;s Workstation</strong> on your land.</p>
<ol>
<li>Gather the base ingredients for each meal: <strong>15 Cultivated Ginseng, 50 Rice and 25 Garlic</strong>.</li>
<li>Add the meat for each meal:<ul>
<li><strong>Hearty Meal:</strong> 5 Raw Chicken Meat</li>
<li><strong>Premium Hearty Meal:</strong> 5 Raw Duck Meat</li>
<li><strong>Rare Hearty Meal:</strong> 5 Raw Goose Meat</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Go to your Workstation and take its daily cooking quests.</li>
<li>Cook each meal at the Workstation and hand it in.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Rewards:</strong> 1 Gilda Star per meal quest. The wiki counts <strong>4 Gilda</strong> a day from the Workstation.</p>
<h2 id="farmhouse-quests">Farmhouse quests</h2>
<p>Farmhouses (Thatched, Harvester&#39;s, Rancher&#39;s and Miner&#39;s Farmhouse) offer daily quests worth <strong>2 or more Gilda Stars</strong>.</p>
<ol>
<li>Go to your Farmhouse.</li>
<li>Open its daily quests from the building.</li>
<li>Do what it asks, usually farming or raising tasks on your land, and turn it in at the Farmhouse.</li>
</ol>
<p><em>These steps haven&#39;t been confirmed in game yet. If something is different, let us know.</em></p>
<h2 id="tradesman-apothecary-and-armorer-quests">Tradesman, Apothecary and Armorer quests</h2>
<p>The <strong>Tradesman&#39;s Manor</strong>, <strong>Apothecary&#39;s Manor</strong> and <strong>Armorer&#39;s Manor</strong> each offer daily quests worth <strong>2 or more Gilda Stars</strong>.</p>
<ol>
<li>Go to your Manor.</li>
<li>Open its daily quests from the building.</li>
<li>Craft or deliver what it asks for, then turn it in at the Manor.</li>
</ol>
<p><em>These steps haven&#39;t been confirmed in game yet. If something is different, let us know.</em></p>
<h2 id="proficiency-quests">Proficiency quests</h2>
<p>Once you reach <strong>50,000 proficiency</strong> in <strong>Mining, Gathering, Farming or Husbandry</strong>, a daily quest for that skill opens. Each one is worth <strong>1 Gilda Star</strong>.</p>
<ol>
<li>Raise the skill to 50,000 proficiency by spending labor in it.</li>
<li>Take the skill&#39;s daily proficiency quest.</li>
<li>Spend labor in that skill, for example mining veins or harvesting crops, until the quest is done.</li>
<li>Turn it in. With all four skills at 50,000, that&#39;s 4 Gilda Stars a day.</li>
</ol>
<p><em>These steps haven&#39;t been confirmed in game yet. If something is different, let us know.</em></p>
<h2 id="blue-salt-dailies">Blue Salt dailies</h2>
<p>Five quests, <strong>1 Gilda Star each</strong>. Take them from the <strong>Blue Salt Wanderer</strong> and the <strong>Animal Trainer</strong> on your side of the world.</p>
<p><strong>East.</strong> The NPCs are in Caernord (Ynystere), Lutesong Harbor (Villanelle) and Austera (Solis Headlands).</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Signs of the Salphira Cult:</strong> collect 12 Hellhound leashes outside the <strong>Palace Cellar</strong> in Mahadevi.</li>
<li><strong>Raising a Wild Snowlion:</strong> raise the Snowlion you are given by caring for it until it grows.</li>
<li><strong>Raising a Malfunctioning Leomorph:</strong> raise it the same way.</li>
<li><strong>A Rare Perfume:</strong> hand in 30 Chopped Produce, Dried Flowers, Ground Grain or Ground Spices.</li>
<li><strong>Guerilla Marketing:</strong> unlocks after A Rare Perfume. Place vases around the Tigerspine Mountains.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>West.</strong> The NPCs are in Seachild Wharf (Cinderstone), Ezna (Two Crowns) and Marianople.</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Roadsend in Danger:</strong> collect 8 Faulty Gunpowder around the <strong>Roadsend Guardpost</strong>.</li>
<li><strong>Raising a Wild Horse:</strong> raise the foal you are given until it grows.</li>
<li><strong>Raising a Wild Elk:</strong> raise it the same way.</li>
<li><strong>A Rare Perfume:</strong> the same as in the east.</li>
<li><strong>Guerilla Marketing:</strong> the same as in the east.</li>
</ol>
<p>Hand each one in where you took it.</p>
