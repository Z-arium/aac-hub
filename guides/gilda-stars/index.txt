12:T169b,<p>Gilda Stars buy ships, mounts and gear upgrades. If you do everything below you can earn around 70 a day. Use the <a href="/aac-hub/tools/checklist">checklist</a> to keep track.</p>
<p><strong>Boss quests need no NPC.</strong> Every &quot;Defeat ...!&quot; boss quest is a <em>Path of Glory</em> quest. Help kill the boss and it completes. Each one pays once a day.</p>

<h2>2. World bosses (3 Gilda each)</h2>
<p>Join the raid in faction chat (<code>x</code> plus the boss name) and help with the kill.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Jola the Cursed</strong> in Exeloch, during his ritual.</li>
<li><strong>Meina</strong> and <strong>Glenn</strong> in Sungold Fields. Raids call these three &quot;JMG&quot;.</li>
<li><strong>Captain Rangora</strong> on Freedich Island, every 8 hours.</li>
<li><strong>Dahuta&#39;s Champion Nazar</strong> in the Sunspeck Sea, every 4 hours.</li>
<li><strong>Morpheus the Forsaken</strong> on Growlgate Isle, every 8 hours.</li>
<li><strong>Hanure the Berserker</strong> in Hasla, Fridays. <strong>Hanure the Hunter</strong> in Hasla, every 14 hours.</li>
<li><strong>Red Dragon</strong> in Karkasse Ridgelands (5 Gilda) and the <strong>Kraken</strong> at sea. See <a href="/aac-hub/timers">timers</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Zaldrane the Enchantress</strong> in Sanddeep (3 Gilda).</li>
</ul>
<h2>3. Smaller bosses (1 Gilda each)</h2>
<p>Most respawn every 4 to 8 hours, and many can be soloed at level 55. Good ones to chain:</p>
<ul>
<li>Solzreed Peninsula: Akanun the Tidelord (every hour), Gilneas, Castigant Reaper</li>
<li>Arcum Iris: Karon the Herdlord (every 30 minutes), Rainbow Ostrich</li>
<li>Villanelle: Reedpipe Leader Palaxis (every hour), Singing Greenman Shoon</li>
<li>Others: Hellhook the Impaler (Lilyut Hills), Verith&#39;s Masterpiece (White Arden), Annulta the Yatamaton (Tigerspine Mountains), Werespider Arak and Jamerath the Shadowmage (Silent Forest), The General&#39;s Spirit (Perinoor Ruins)</li>
<li>Level 55 Shadow Rift bosses: Daruda the Watcher (Cinderstone), The Croakfiend (Hellswamp), Tanis the Despoiler (Karkasse), Blightshade (Rookborne Basin), Goretusk the Leveler (Windscour Savannah), Yaksa Leader (Perinoor Ruins)</li>
</ul>
<h2>4. Ocean quests (5 Gilda)</h2>
<ol>
<li><strong>Ghosts from the Depths</strong> (1 Gilda). Take it from any <strong>Archeologist</strong> (Toro, Roycia, Bruce, Carson, Waji, Lao Xiam and others). Sink ghost ships roaming the sea.</li>
<li><strong>Ghost Ships of Delphinad</strong> (3 Gilda). Same Archeologist, once you have the quest above. Help sink the <strong>Delphinad Ghost Ship</strong>, which spawns daily in the Arcadian Sea (see <a href="/aac-hub/timers">timers</a>).</li>
<li><strong>Enemies of Sea Trade</strong> (1 Gilda). Take it from <strong>Martus, Suiharan, Bormas, Elle or Sorgo</strong>. Kill the Seabugs, then find the targets in Ezna, Austera, Freedich Island, Growlgate Isle and Diamond Shores. Turn it in to the same NPCs.</li>
</ol>
<h2>5. Building quests (8 or more Gilda)</h2>
<p>These are done at your own house or farm.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Workstation meals:</strong> cook 4 meals at a workstation (4 Gilda).</li>
<li><strong>Farmhouse quests</strong> (2 or more Gilda).</li>
<li><strong>Tradesman, Apothecary or Armorer</strong> building quests (2 or more Gilda).</li>
</ul>
<h2>6. Blue Salt dailies (5 Gilda)</h2>
<p>Five quests at 1 Gilda each. Pick them up in the Blue Salt towns on your side of the world.</p>
<p><strong>East</strong> (Caernord, Ynystere or Lutesong Harbor, Villanelle or Austera, Solis Headlands):</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Signs of the Salphira Cult:</strong> collect leashes outside the Palace Cellar in Mahadevi.</li>
<li><strong>Raising a Wild Snowlion:</strong> raise a Wild Snowlion by caring for it.</li>
<li><strong>Raising a Malfunctioning Leomorph:</strong> raise it the same way.</li>
<li><strong>A Rare Perfume:</strong> hand in 30 Chopped Produce, Dried Flowers, Ground Grain or Ground Spices.</li>
<li><strong>Guerilla Marketing:</strong> place vases in Tigerspine or Lilyut.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>West</strong> (Marianople or Seachild Wharf, Cinderstone or Ezna, Two Crowns):</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Roadsend in Danger:</strong> collect the Faulty Gunpowder near the Roadsend Guardpost.</li>
<li><strong>Raising a Wild Horse</strong></li>
<li><strong>Raising a Wild Elk</strong></li>
<li><strong>A Rare Perfume</strong> (same as east)</li>
<li><strong>Guerilla Marketing</strong> (same as east)</li>
</ol>
<h2>7. Other quick ones</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Diamond Shores bridge and wall</strong> (2 Gilda): the Colonizing Auroria quests in your faction base. Steps are in <a href="/aac-hub/guides/guild-missions">guild missions</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Ahnimar, watering the Darus</strong> (1 Gilda).</li>
<li><strong>Proficiency tasks</strong> (1 Gilda each): mining, gathering, farming and husbandry.</li>
</ul>
