Gilda Stars every day

Every daily that pays Gilda Stars, where to pick each one up, and what to do.

Gilda Stars buy ships, mounts and gear upgrades. If you do everything below you can earn around 70 a day. Use the checklist to keep track.

Boss quests need no NPC. Every "Defeat ...!" boss quest is a Path of Glory quest. Help kill the boss and it completes. Each one pays once a day.

1. Raids (about 30 Gilda)

These are the biggest payouts. The daily world events guide has every step.

Grimgast Rift Nightmare Blade and Nightmare Bowstring 3 each

2. World bosses (3 Gilda each)

Join the raid in faction chat (x plus the boss name) and help with the kill.

  • Jola the Cursed in Exeloch, during his ritual.
  • Meina and Glenn in Sungold Fields. Raids call these three "JMG".
  • Captain Rangora on Freedich Island, every 8 hours.
  • Dahuta's Champion Nazar in the Sunspeck Sea, every 4 hours.
  • Morpheus the Forsaken on Growlgate Isle, every 8 hours.
  • Hanure the Berserker in Hasla, Fridays. Hanure the Hunter in Hasla, every 14 hours.
  • Red Dragon in Karkasse Ridgelands (5 Gilda) and the Kraken at sea. See timers.
  • Zaldrane the Enchantress in Sanddeep (3 Gilda).

3. Smaller bosses (1 Gilda each)

Most respawn every 4 to 8 hours, and many can be soloed at level 55. Good ones to chain:

  • Solzreed Peninsula: Akanun the Tidelord (every hour), Gilneas, Castigant Reaper
  • Arcum Iris: Karon the Herdlord (every 30 minutes), Rainbow Ostrich
  • Villanelle: Reedpipe Leader Palaxis (every hour), Singing Greenman Shoon
  • Others: Hellhook the Impaler (Lilyut Hills), Verith's Masterpiece (White Arden), Annulta the Yatamaton (Tigerspine Mountains), Werespider Arak and Jamerath the Shadowmage (Silent Forest), The General's Spirit (Perinoor Ruins)
  • Level 55 Shadow Rift bosses: Daruda the Watcher (Cinderstone), The Croakfiend (Hellswamp), Tanis the Despoiler (Karkasse), Blightshade (Rookborne Basin), Goretusk the Leveler (Windscour Savannah), Yaksa Leader (Perinoor Ruins)

4. Ocean quests (5 Gilda)

  1. Ghosts from the Depths (1 Gilda). Take it from any Archeologist (Toro, Roycia, Bruce, Carson, Waji, Lao Xiam and others). Sink ghost ships roaming the sea.
  2. Ghost Ships of Delphinad (3 Gilda). Same Archeologist, once you have the quest above. Help sink the Delphinad Ghost Ship, which spawns daily in the Arcadian Sea (see timers).
  3. Enemies of Sea Trade (1 Gilda). Take it from Martus, Suiharan, Bormas, Elle or Sorgo. Kill the Seabugs, then find the targets in Ezna, Austera, Freedich Island, Growlgate Isle and Diamond Shores. Turn it in to the same NPCs.

5. Building quests (8 or more Gilda)

These are done at your own house or farm.

  • Workstation meals: cook 4 meals at a workstation (4 Gilda).
  • Farmhouse quests (2 or more Gilda).
  • Tradesman, Apothecary or Armorer building quests (2 or more Gilda).

6. Blue Salt dailies (5 Gilda)

Five quests at 1 Gilda each. Pick them up in the Blue Salt towns on your side of the world.

East (Caernord, Ynystere or Lutesong Harbor, Villanelle or Austera, Solis Headlands):

  1. Signs of the Salphira Cult: collect leashes outside the Palace Cellar in Mahadevi.
  2. Raising a Wild Snowlion: raise a Wild Snowlion by caring for it.
  3. Raising a Malfunctioning Leomorph: raise it the same way.
  4. A Rare Perfume: hand in 30 Chopped Produce, Dried Flowers, Ground Grain or Ground Spices.
  5. Guerilla Marketing: place vases in Tigerspine or Lilyut.

West (Marianople or Seachild Wharf, Cinderstone or Ezna, Two Crowns):

  1. Roadsend in Danger: collect the Faulty Gunpowder near the Roadsend Guardpost.
  2. Raising a Wild Horse
  3. Raising a Wild Elk
  4. A Rare Perfume (same as east)
  5. Guerilla Marketing (same as east)

7. Other quick ones

  • Diamond Shores bridge and wall (2 Gilda): the Colonizing Auroria quests in your faction base. Steps are in guild missions.
  • Ahnimar, watering the Darus (1 Gilda).
  • Proficiency tasks (1 Gilda each): mining, gathering, farming and husbandry.
