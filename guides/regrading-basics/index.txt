<h2 id="when-gear-can-break">When gear can break</h2>
<p>Past <strong>Celestial</strong>, a failed regrade can break or downgrade your item. Use protection before you try.</p>
<h2 id="resplendent-scrolls">Resplendent scrolls</h2>
<p>A Resplendent scroll can jump <strong>two grades</strong> at once. On Normal gear, Divine to Epic is 10%, and a Resplendent success adds a 20% chance of going one grade further. That works out to a <strong>2%</strong> chance of reaching Legendary.</p>
<p>Try it in the <a href="/aac-hub/tools/regrade">Regrade calculator</a>.</p>
