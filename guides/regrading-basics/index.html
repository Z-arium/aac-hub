Regrading basics

Success rates by item type, how Resplendent scrolls work, and when your gear can break.

Updated 2026-10-27

Every item has a difficulty that sets its regrade rates:

  • Easy: gear dropped in dungeons.
  • Normal: everything else.
  • Difficult: crafted gear, Obsidian gear and world boss drops.
  • Ships and pets have their own table.

When gear can break

Past Celestial, a failed regrade can break or downgrade your item. Use protection before you try.

Resplendent scrolls

A Resplendent scroll can jump two grades at once. On Normal gear, Divine to Epic is 10%, and a Resplendent success adds a 20% chance of going one grade further. That works out to a 2% chance of reaching Legendary.

Try it in the Regrade calculator.
