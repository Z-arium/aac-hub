Progression

Your perfect day

The order to do your dailies in so nothing is missed: start-of-day pickups, scheduled events, rifts, then fillers.

The checklist follows this order. Daily quests reset at midnight UTC.

Log in and claim your login reward

  1. Log in.
  2. Open the Daily Schedule (bottom right of the screen).
  3. Go to the Login Tracker tab.
  4. Claim today's reward.

While the Daily Schedule is open, the Daily Quests and Events tabs show what's on today.

Start of day

Get everything that runs in the background going first:

  1. Take the merit quest. Start Active Adventurers Appreciated so the labor you spend today counts toward it.
  2. Start your guild missions. See guild missions.
  3. Harvest and replant your Experia patches.
  4. Do the quick building quests at home: Workstation meals, Farmhouse and Manor quests.
  5. Colonizing Auroria: drop a bundle off in Diamond Shores. See Colonizing Auroria.
  6. Take today's pickups you will finish later: the ocean quests from an Archeologist, and the Blue Salt dailies.

Scheduled events

Check timers and plan around what starts next: Mistmerrow, Halcyona, Luscas, Abyssal Attack, the Delphinad Ghost Ship and the big bosses.

Rifts

Rifts run every 4 real hours on the in-game clock. When x cr, x gr, x ws or x aegis shows up in faction chat, drop what you're doing and join. See Crimson Rift, Grimgast Rift, Whalesong and Aegis Island.

Between events

Fill the gaps with the Library and dungeon dailies, minor world bosses, Blue Salt, the Arena and your weekly Activity Token quests.
