18:Td34,<p>The <a href="/aac-hub/tools/checklist">checklist</a> follows this order. Daily quests reset at <strong>midnight UTC</strong>.</p>
<h2 id="log-in-and-claim-your-login-reward">Log in and claim your login reward</h2>
<ol>
<li>Log in.</li>
<li>Open the <strong>Daily Schedule</strong> (bottom right of the screen).</li>
<li>Go to the <strong>Login Tracker</strong> tab.</li>
<li>Claim today&#39;s reward.</li>
</ol>
<p>While the Daily Schedule is open, the <strong>Daily Quests</strong> and <strong>Events</strong> tabs show what&#39;s on today.</p>
<h2 id="start-of-day">Start of day</h2>
<p>Get everything that runs in the background going first:</p>
<ol>
<li><strong>Take the merit quest.</strong> Start <a href="/aac-hub/guides/weekly-activity-tokens#active-adventurers-appreciated">Active Adventurers Appreciated</a> so the labor you spend today counts toward it.</li>
<li><strong>Start your guild missions.</strong> See <a href="/aac-hub/guides/guild-missions#start-your-guild-missions">guild missions</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Harvest and replant</strong> your <a href="/aac-hub/guides/guild-missions#gather-experia-patches">Experia patches</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Do the quick building quests</strong> at home: <a href="/aac-hub/guides/gilda-stars#workstation-meals">Workstation meals</a>, <a href="/aac-hub/guides/gilda-stars#farmhouse-quests">Farmhouse</a> and <a href="/aac-hub/guides/gilda-stars#tradesman-apothecary-and-armorer-quests">Manor</a> quests.</li>
<li><strong>Colonizing Auroria:</strong> drop a bundle off in Diamond Shores. See <a href="/aac-hub/guides/guild-missions#colonizing-auroria">Colonizing Auroria</a>.</li>
<li><strong>Take today&#39;s pickups</strong> you will finish later: the ocean quests from an Archeologist, and the <a href="/aac-hub/guides/gilda-stars#blue-salt-dailies">Blue Salt dailies</a>.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="scheduled-events">Scheduled events</h2>
<p>Check <a href="/aac-hub/timers">timers</a> and plan around what starts next: <a href="/aac-hub/guides/daily-world-events#mistmerrow">Mistmerrow</a>, <a href="/aac-hub/guides/daily-world-events#halcyona-war">Halcyona</a>, <a href="/aac-hub/guides/daily-world-events#luscas">Luscas</a>, <a href="/aac-hub/guides/daily-world-events#abyssal-attack">Abyssal Attack</a>, the <a href="/aac-hub/guides/gilda-stars#delphinad-ghost-ship">Delphinad Ghost Ship</a> and the big bosses.</p>
<h2 id="rifts">Rifts</h2>
<p>Rifts run every 4 real hours on the in-game clock. When <code>x cr</code>, <code>x gr</code>, <code>x ws</code> or <code>x aegis</code> shows up in faction chat, drop what you&#39;re doing and join. See <a href="/aac-hub/guides/daily-world-events#crimson-rift">Crimson Rift</a>, <a href="/aac-hub/guides/daily-world-events#grimgast-rift">Grimgast Rift</a>, <a href="/aac-hub/guides/daily-world-events#whalesong-siege">Whalesong</a> and <a href="/aac-hub/guides/daily-world-events#aegis-island">Aegis Island</a>.</p>
<h2 id="between-events">Between events</h2>
<p>Fill the gaps with the <a href="/aac-hub/guides/ayanad-library-dailies">Library and dungeon dailies</a>, <a href="/aac-hub/guides/gilda-stars#minor-world-bosses">minor world bosses</a>, <a href="/aac-hub/guides/gilda-stars#blue-salt-dailies">Blue Salt</a>, the <a href="/aac-hub/guides/daily-world-events#arena">Arena</a> and your weekly <a href="/aac-hub/guides/weekly-activity-tokens">Activity Token</a> quests.</p>
