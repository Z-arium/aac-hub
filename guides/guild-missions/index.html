Guild missions and Prestige

The daily guild missions that earn Prestige, where to pick each one up, and the steps.

Updated 2026-10-28

Prestige is the guild currency. You earn it from guild missions and spend it in the Prestige Shop: open Community, then Guild, then Manage, then Prestige Shop. Each mission also gives your guild XP. You need to be in a guild.

Mission Prestige Guild XP
Colonizing Auroria 10 20
Defending Auroria 10 40
Gather Experia patches 10 0
Kill a world boss 10 40

Start your guild missions

Do this at the start of your day, so each mission counts as you play.

  1. Join a guild if you aren't in one. Ask in faction chat, or look on the AAClassic Discord.
  2. Press Shift+T to open the guild missions.
  3. Accept the daily missions: Colonizing Auroria, Defending Auroria, Gather Experia Patches and World Bosses.
  4. Work through them using the sections below. Each one pays out when its task is done.

Colonizing Auroria

Do one of the 5 building quests in your faction's base in Diamond Shores. They also count for the bridge and wall Gilda Stars.

  1. Go to your faction's housing area in Diamond Shores and take a quest:

    • Nuia: A Grand Construction from Sadris, turned in to Pella at the Nuian Base.
    • Haranya: A Grand Construction from Songa, turned in to Beathy at the Haranyan Base.
    • Pirates: Pirate Politics from Avaline, turned in to the Agent of Morpheus. Craft at the Growlgate Isle Archeum Workbench.
    • Or Construct the Alliance Bridge or Reinforce the Ramparts, which repair the bridge or your base's walls.

  2. Craft the Archeum Bundle at the nearby Auroria Archeum Workbench:

    Quest Bundle Prestige
    Alliance Bridge / Reinforce the Ramparts Faint Archeum Bundle ×6 3
    A Grand Construction 1 Faint ×6 3
    A Grand Construction 2 Clear ×6 + 2 5
    A Grand Construction 3 Lucid ×6 + 2 + 2 6

  3. Use the bundle on the bridge or wall, or hand it to the turn-in NPC.

Defending Auroria

Destroy any one of these: an Alemine Fortress, Alemine Bunker, Alemine Stragglers Bunker, Alemine Assault Bunker, Aust Mana Tower, Mana Tower Idol or Riven Gates Idol.

  1. Find an Alemine Stragglers Bunker in Diamond Shores.
  2. Pick up a Harpoon Cannon from one of the Harpoon Cannon Caches lying on the ground nearby.
  3. Shoot the bunker until it breaks.

Gather Experia patches

Plant Experia patches on your land, then harvest any one of these sets:

Patch Harvest Grows in Materials for one
Experia Sunflower Patch 4 4 hours 25 Sunflower Seeds, 4 Memory Ink, 3 Scented Leaves
Experia Pumpkin Patch 3 10 hours 25 Pumpkin Seeds, 10 Memory Ink, 5 Royal Seeds
Experia Duck Patch 2 24 hours 25 Ducklings, 20 Memory Ink, 10 Royal Seeds
Experia Pine Patch 1 48 hours 25 Pine Saplings, 45 Memory Ink, 1 Heavy Hardwood

Sunflowers are the quickest: plant 4 the day before.

Kill a world boss

Help kill any world boss. Small ones like Karon the Herdlord in Arcum Iris (every 30 minutes) are the quickest. The Gilda Stars guide lists them.

Guild: Materials (repeatable)

Worth 1 Prestige and 4 guild XP each time. You get one of Guild: Fabric, Leather, Stone Brick, Iron Ingot or Lumber. Hand in 5 of that material and 4 Blue Salt Hammers, and a new one starts.

Bonus: Ocleera Rift

The Ocleera Rift in west Diamond Shores also pays Prestige: 2 for each of the 3 mark quests from Scout Karlsburg and 4 for killing the Hateful Ocleera. Steps are in the daily world events guide.
