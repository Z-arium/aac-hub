<h2 id="start-your-guild-missions">Start your guild missions</h2>
<p>Do this at the start of your day, so each mission counts as you play.</p>
<ol>
<li>Join a guild if you aren&#39;t in one. Ask in faction chat, or look on the AAClassic Discord.</li>
<li>Press <strong>Shift+T</strong> to open the guild missions.</li>
<li>Accept the daily missions: Colonizing Auroria, Defending Auroria, Gather Experia Patches and World Bosses.</li>
<li>Work through them using the sections below. Each one pays out when its task is done.</li>
</ol>
<h2 id="colonizing-auroria">Colonizing Auroria</h2>
<p>Do <strong>one</strong> of the 5 building quests in your faction&#39;s base in <strong>Diamond Shores</strong>. They also count for the bridge and wall Gilda Stars.</p>
<ol>
<li><p>Go to your faction&#39;s housing area in Diamond Shores and take a quest:</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Nuia:</strong> A Grand Construction from <strong>Sadris</strong>, turned in to <strong>Pella</strong> at the Nuian Base.</li>
<li><strong>Haranya:</strong> A Grand Construction from <strong>Songa</strong>, turned in to <strong>Beathy</strong> at the Haranyan Base.</li>
<li><strong>Pirates:</strong> <strong>Pirate Politics</strong> from <strong>Avaline</strong>, turned in to the <strong>Agent of Morpheus</strong>. Craft at the Growlgate Isle Archeum Workbench.</li>
<li>Or <strong>Construct the Alliance Bridge</strong> or <strong>Reinforce the Ramparts</strong>, which repair the bridge or your base&#39;s walls.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li><p>Craft the <strong>Archeum Bundle</strong> at the nearby <strong>Auroria Archeum Workbench</strong>:</p>

<h2 id="defending-auroria">Defending Auroria</h2>
<p>Destroy any one of these: an Alemine Fortress, Alemine Bunker, Alemine Stragglers Bunker, Alemine Assault Bunker, Aust Mana Tower, Mana Tower Idol or Riven Gates Idol.</p>
<ol>
<li>Find an <strong>Alemine Stragglers Bunker</strong> in Diamond Shores.</li>
<li>Pick up a <strong>Harpoon Cannon</strong> from one of the <strong>Harpoon Cannon Caches</strong> lying on the ground nearby.</li>
<li>Shoot the bunker until it breaks.</li>
</ol>

<h2 id="kill-a-world-boss">Kill a world boss</h2>
<p>Help kill any world boss. Small ones like Karon the Herdlord in Arcum Iris (every 30 minutes) are the quickest. The <a href="/aac-hub/guides/gilda-stars">Gilda Stars guide</a> lists them.</p>
<h2 id="guild-materials-repeatable">Guild: Materials (repeatable)</h2>
<p>Worth 1 Prestige and 4 guild XP each time. You get one of Guild: Fabric, Leather, Stone Brick, Iron Ingot or Lumber. Hand in <strong>5 of that material and 4 Blue Salt Hammers</strong>, and a new one starts.</p>
<h2 id="bonus-ocleera-rift">Bonus: Ocleera Rift</h2>
<p>The Ocleera Rift in west Diamond Shores also pays Prestige: 2 for each of the 3 mark quests from <strong>Scout Karlsburg</strong> and 4 for killing the Hateful Ocleera. Steps are in the <a href="/aac-hub/guides/daily-world-events">daily world events guide</a>.</p>
