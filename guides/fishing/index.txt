10:T845,<h2>Kinds of fishing</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Bait fishing:</strong> a rod and bait worms in any water. Gives various fish.</li>
<li><strong>Sport fishing:</strong> freshwater (lakes and ponds) or saltwater (at sea).</li>
<li><strong>Ice fishing:</strong> in the Mirror Tundra.</li>
<li><strong>Advanced fishing:</strong> use <strong>Ground Fish Chum</strong> to bait <strong>Big Belly Fish</strong> in the Sunspeck Sea. Needs a capable rod and max Fishing proficiency.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Rods</h2>
<p>Classic brings back later rods, so you choose between <strong>cast time</strong> and <strong>damage</strong>. The <strong>Ultimate</strong> rod has both.</p>
<h2>Sport fishing</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Chum:</strong> any chopped fish bucket except squid works on a saltwater pool. Chopped squid is for freshwater.</li>
<li>When a fish bites, a minigame starts. Use the skill that matches the fish&#39;s current debuff. For example, press <em>Stand Firm Left</em> when it shows.</li>
<li><strong>Big Reel In</strong> and <strong>Reel In</strong> do most of the damage. Don&#39;t miss them.</li>
</ul>
<h2>What to do with fish</h2>
<ul>
<li>Sell them as they are.</li>
<li>Chop them into chum for fishing spots.</li>
<li>Turn them into <strong>Dawn Lake Essence</strong>. 3 Solzreed Mackerel make 1 Essence.</li>
<li>Big Belly Fish open like crates and can hold treasure maps.</li>
</ul>
<h2>Classic changes</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Old-style pools</strong> expire on a timer instead of a fish count, and each has a set fish type. A Marlin pool can be worth a lot.</li>
<li>Pool timers are always visible.</li>
<li>Every fish type takes every chum type.</li>
<li>Labor use is doubled and gold per labor is adjusted. Some of the labor now goes to the turn-in, so you lose less if pirates take your catch.</li>
<li><strong>Sunfish Schools</strong> sometimes spawn after a feeding frenzy. Chumming one alerts the whole server.</li>
</ul>
<p>On Saturdays, the Fish Market quest pays 20 <a href="/aac-hub/guides/weekly-activity-tokens">Activity Tokens</a>. Fishing boats are on the <a href="/aac-hub/database/ships">ships</a> page.</p>
