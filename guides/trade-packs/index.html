Gold making

Trade packs and commerce

Pack types, how turn-in percentages work, and where to sell for gold, Gilda or stabilizers.

Pack types

  • Specialty packs are crafted at each zone's Specialty Workbench. Some need Gilda Dust: they cost more but have their own turn-in percentage.
  • Fertilizer packs use the same recipe in every zone: 25 chopped produce, 25 ground grain, 20 trimmed meat and 50 dried flowers. Craft them at a Tradesman Manor.
  • Larders sit on your land for 3 days. Milk and lemon make cheese packs, honey and straw bundles make honey packs, and cultivated ginseng and olives make salve packs.

How payouts work

Each pack has a base value that depends on the pack, where it was made and where you sell it. Farther routes pay more.

A turn-in percentage between 70% and 130% is applied on top. Every pack sold on a route lowers its percentage, and it slowly climbs back to 130% over time. Press Shift+O in game to see current percentages.

Where to sell

Trader Pays
Gold Trader Gold
Gilda Trader Gilda
Resource Trader Charcoal Stabilizers
Freedich Resource Trader Dragon Essence Stabilizers

Gold and Gilda traders only take packs from their own continent, except on Freedich Island, which takes packs from anywhere. The Freedich Gold Trader spawns twice a day and usually pays the most, but expect PvP. Its next spawn is on the timers page.

Popular runs include Double Peace, Sunbite to Solis, Perinoor to Solis, West to East, Ynystere to Freedich and Rokhala to Solis.
