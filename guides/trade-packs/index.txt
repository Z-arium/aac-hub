18:T79a,<h2 id="pack-types">Pack types</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Specialty packs</strong> are crafted at each zone&#39;s Specialty Workbench. Some need Gilda Dust: they cost more but have their own turn-in percentage.</li>
<li><strong>Fertilizer packs</strong> use the same recipe in every zone: 25 chopped produce, 25 ground grain, 20 trimmed meat and 50 dried flowers. Craft them at a Tradesman Manor.</li>
<li><strong>Larders</strong> sit on your land for 3 days. Milk and lemon make cheese packs, honey and straw bundles make honey packs, and cultivated ginseng and olives make salve packs.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="how-payouts-work">How payouts work</h2>
<p>Each pack has a <strong>base value</strong> that depends on the pack, where it was made and where you sell it. Farther routes pay more.</p>
<p>A <strong>turn-in percentage</strong> between 70% and 130% is applied on top. Every pack sold on a route lowers its percentage, and it slowly climbs back to 130% over time. Press <strong>Shift+O</strong> in game to see current percentages.</p>

<p>Gold and Gilda traders only take packs from their own continent, except on <strong>Freedich Island</strong>, which takes packs from anywhere. The Freedich Gold Trader spawns twice a day and usually pays the most, but expect PvP. Its next spawn is on the <a href="/aac-hub/timers">timers</a> page.</p>
<p>Popular runs include Double Peace, Sunbite to Solis, Perinoor to Solis, West to East, Ynystere to Freedich and Rokhala to Solis.</p>
