Progression

Daily world events

Step by step for every daily raid and PvP event. Where to get the quests, what to do, and what you earn.

These events are your daily source of Honor, Leadership, Gilda Stars and Warrior Medals. Exact times are on the timers page.

How to join a raid: there is no group finder. When a raid forms, people type x and the event's short name in faction chat (for example x cr or x ws). Type the same and an addon invites you. Short names: CR (Crimson Rift), GR (Grimgast Rift), WS (Whalesong), Aegis, OC (Ocleera), Halcy (Halcyona), MM (Mistmerrow).

Boss kill quests start on their own. Quests like "Defeat the Hound of Kyrios!" are Path of Glory quests. There is no NPC: help kill the boss and the quest completes.

Crimson Rift (CR)

When: every 4 real hours, at 12:00 in-game time. Hover over the top right of your screen to see the game clock.

  1. Join a CR raid in faction chat.
  2. Go to the rift zone and talk to the Recruiter to take Crimson Omens 1. Guards in the zone's Community Center also hand out the rift quests.
  3. Wave 1: kill Crimson Army Infantrymen and Archers. Report to the Recruiter, who gives Crimson Omens 2.
  4. Wave 2: kill the next wave and report back for Crimson Omens 3.
  5. Wave 3: kill the Crimson Army Commanders and Spearmen, then report to the Recruiter.
  6. Bosses: two Hounds of Kyrios spawn. Help kill one to finish Defeat the Hound of Kyrios!

Rewards: 1,000 Honor and 10 Leadership from the Omens quests. The Hound gives 3 Gilda Stars, 2 Clear Synthium Shards and 7 Adventurer's Growthstones.

Anthalon: when the rift is in Sungold Fields, Anthalon spawns after it. Help kill him for Defeat Anthalon!: 3 Gilda Stars, 2 Clear Synthium Shards and 7 Growthstones. Kill the mobs he summons quickly because they stun. Use Shrug It Off when he lifts you.

Grimgast Rift (GR)

When: every 4 real hours, at 00:00 in-game time.

  1. Join a GR raid in faction chat.
  2. At the Community Center, take a materials quest from the wooden board and hand in 10 of one basic material: stone brick, lumber, leather, fabric or iron ingot.
  3. That unlocks Helping the War Effort from Quartermaster Fila. Carry 2 packs of Condensed Archeum to the trebuchet. The Archeum appears at the camp from midnight in-game time. This step gives 1 Gilda Star.
  4. Once the trebuchet is built, grab a harpoon from the rack.
  5. Halting the Crimson Tide 1 and 2: use the harpoon to clear two waves. Both quests complete on their own.
  6. Bosses: the Nightmare Blade and Nightmare Bowstring spawn. They carry a shield. Strip it with the harpoon's second skill, Time of Oblivion, then kill them.

Rewards: the GR quests give 8 Gilda Stars in total, the most of any daily, plus 10 Leadership. Each Nightmare gives another 3 Gilda Stars, 2 Clear Synthium Shards and 7 Growthstones.

Whalesong Siege (WS)

Where: the Nuia Defense Base in Whalesong Harbor, Auroria. Level 55.

  1. Join a WS raid in faction chat.
  2. Talk to Raiko at the base and take Anthalon's Elite. This also gives you Whalesong Siege 1 to 3.
  3. Waves 1 to 3: kill Anthalon's undead troops as they attack the base. Each wave completes one Siege quest.
  4. Boss: kill Possessed Jakar for Kill Possessed Jakar.
  5. Turn in Anthalon's Elite to Raiko.

Rewards: 20 Leadership. Jakar gives 4 Gilda Stars, 10 Growthstones and 5 Cursed Armor Scraps. Anthalon's Elite gives 4 more Cursed Armor Scraps.

Aegis Island

Where: Aegis Island, Auroria. Level 55.

  1. Join an Aegis raid in faction chat.
  2. Talk to Scout Gaon on the island and take Aegis Island Guardian. This also gives you Defend the Seal 1 to 3.
  3. Seals 1 to 3: kill the ghouls attacking each seal, starting at the Eastern Seal. Each seal completes one Defend the Seal quest.
  4. Boss: go to the Northern Seal and kill the Final Sealbreaker, then enter the Ipnysh Gate to finish the quest.
  5. Turn in Aegis Island Guardian to Scout Gaon.

Rewards: 20 Leadership. The Final Sealbreaker gives 4 Gilda Stars, 10 Growthstones and 5 Acid Gobbets. Aegis Island Guardian gives 3 more Acid Gobbets.

Ocleera Rift (OC)

Where: the Riven Gates on the west side of Diamond Shores. Level 46 and up.

  1. Talk to Scout Karlsburg and take Sprouting Ocleera's Marks. Kill the Sprouting Marks and their minions.
  2. Take Engorged Ocleera's Marks from Scout Karlsburg and kill the Engorged Marks.
  3. Take Monstrous Ocleera's Mark and kill the Monstrous Mark.
  4. The Hateful Ocleera spawns. Help kill it for Kill the Hateful Ocleera. That quest starts when you are near the boss.

Rewards: 10 Leadership, Revenant Soulstones (5, 10 and 20), a Souleye and 3 Clear Synthium Shards. Every step also gives Prestige and guild XP. See guild missions.

Comet Rift (Starfall)

When: when a comet lands. A popup in game names the zone.

  1. Go to the comet and talk to Iseren.
  2. The first time, Iseren sends you to Elloy on the 1st floor of the Ayanad Library. Come back, then use the telescope and talk to Iseren until it is centred. Use its Observe option.
  3. Take the rift quests from Iseren:
    • Rift 1: kill 25 Fire Sprites.
    • Rift 2: kill 15 Flame Elementals.
    • Rift 3: kill the Flame Guardian boss.
  4. Side quests run at the same time: Scorched Earth (kill 10 rift elementals), Meteor Miner (mine a meteor) and Crystalized Clues (collect elemental shards from Flame Elementals).
  5. Finish with The Eternal Flame: kill the Undying Flame.
  6. Report back to Iseren in the Ayanad Library.

Rewards: Comet Shards. Spend them at the Comet Workbench in the top right of Diamond Shores on battle pets, the "Starfallen" title or the Cumulus Magitopher glider.

Mistmerrow (MM, PvP)

Nuia, Haranya and Pirates fight over towers in each basin. On Classic it lasts 20 minutes.

  1. When it starts, go to the Mistmerrow portal in your capital: Marianople for Nuia, Austera for Haranya.
  2. Take the 2 quests from the NPC in front of the portal, then go through.
  3. Talk to the NPC inside. That completes one quest and gives you a new one to destroy the towers.
  4. Help your faction destroy towers until the battle ends.
  5. After the battle the zone turns peaceful. Take the 2 new quests from the NPCs in the middle.
  6. Kill the 3 gates in the SE, W and NE basins, then Kadum in the middle.

Rewards: Honor, Warrior Medals, Clear Synthium Shards and 10 Leadership. Kadum gives 4 Gilda Stars.

Halcyona War (Halcy, PvP)

  1. Take the first quest in your capital.
  2. Go through the portal, or use your own portal, to Halcyona.
  3. Turn it in at your faction camp and take the second quest: kill 10 enemies. Kills are shared in a party or raid.
  4. Help destroy the enemy base. If your side breaks the base crystal, loot it for a Warrior Medal Chest.
  5. If you win, go back to the Community Center for a 2-hour buff.

Rewards: Honor, Clear Synthium Shards, Warrior Medals, 10 Leadership, and 5 to 10 more Warrior Medals from the crystal chest.

Luscas (PvP at sea)

Luscas are mini-Krakens that appear each day at one of three spots in the Sea of Graves, between Auroria and Freedich Island.

  1. Take Lusca Awakening from the Seamist Researcher at Ezi's Light in Diamond Shores. Each big city's portal also gets you close.
  2. Sail out with a ship and help kill the Luscas.
  3. Kill 5 Luscas for the extra kill quest.

Rewards: 1 Gilda Star and 10 Shatigon's Sand from Lusca Awakening, plus Honor and 10 Leadership. The 5-kill quest gives another Gilda Star.

Abyssal Attack (PvP at sea)

When: several times a week. See timers.

  1. Take Becoming a Seaknight and Stopping Doomsday from the Abyssal Attack Recruiter.
  2. Sail to the Sea of Graves, between Heedmar and Freedich Island.
  3. Kill all the Luscas and the Abyssal Kraken.
  4. A server message says Ezi's Light can be restored. Go to the foot of the monument and use the beacon on the pedestal. It is a 3-minute channel.
  5. Protect the person channeling. Enemies can stop it by killing them or by breaking the lodestones north, east and west of the monument with siege damage. If a lodestone breaks, the beacon explodes for 20,000 damage.
  6. For Becoming a Seaknight, either restore Ezi's Light and get the Victory Flag buff, or destroy an enemy's Ocean's Guardstone.

Rewards: 2 Warrior Medals and 5 Shatigon's Sand from Stopping Doomsday, plus 10 Leadership.
