Daily world events

Crimson Rift, Grimgast Rift, Mistmerrow, Halcyona and Luscas. What to do and what you get.

These events are your daily source of Honor, Leadership, Gilda Stars and Warrior Medals. Exact times are on the timers page.

Crimson Rift (PvE)

Every 4 real hours, at 12:00 in-game time. Hover over the top-right of your screen in game to see the game clock. Join a raid through faction chat. You fight three waves of monsters, then two Hound bosses.

  • Quest from a guard in the Community Center: 1,000 Honor and 10 Leadership.
  • Each Hound kill: 3 Gilda Stars, 2 Clear Synthium Shards and 7 Adventurer's Growthstones.
  • The daily raid quest gives 3 Gilda Stars.
  • In Sungold Fields, Anthalon spawns after the rift.

Grimgast Rift (PvE)

Every 4 real hours, at 00:00 in-game time.

  1. From the Community Center board: deliver 10 materials (stone brick, lumber, leather, fabric, iron ingot).
  2. Deliver 2 packs to the trebuchet.
  3. Use the harpoons to beat two waves. Two Hounds then spawn. Use the harpoon's Time of Oblivion skill to strip their debuff.

The daily raid quest gives 8 Gilda Stars, the most of any daily.

Mistmerrow (PvP)

Nuia, Haranya and Pirates fight over towers. Portals are in the capitals (Marianople and Austera). On Classic it lasts 20 minutes and has an extra kill quest.

  • Entry quests: Honor, Warrior Medals, Clear Synthium Shards and Leadership.
  • Afterwards, kill the 3 gates (SE, W and NE basins) and Kadum in the middle. Kadum is worth 4 Gilda Stars.

Halcyona War (PvP)

Take the first quest in your capital, then enter Halcyona.

  • The second quest asks for 10 enemy kills, which you can share in a party or raid.
  • Looting an enemy base crystal gives a Warrior Medal Chest with 5 to 10 Warrior Medals.
  • After a win, return to the Community Center for a 2-hour buff.

Luscas (PvP at sea)

A naval fight between Auroria and Freedich Island. The quest NPC is at Ezi's Light in Diamond Shores. Rewards: a good amount of Honor, 1 Gilda Star, Shatigon's Sand and Leadership. Killing five Lusca also gives 1 Gilda Star.
