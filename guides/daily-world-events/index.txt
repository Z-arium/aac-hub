12:T367f,<p>These events are your daily source of Honor, Leadership, Gilda Stars and Warrior Medals. Exact times are on the <a href="/aac-hub/timers">timers</a> page.</p>
<p><strong>How to join a raid.</strong> There is no group finder. When a raid forms, people type <code>x</code> and the event&#39;s short name in faction chat (for example <code>x cr</code> or <code>x ws</code>). Type the same and an addon invites you. The short names are CR (Crimson Rift), GR (Grimgast Rift), WS (Whalesong), Aegis, OC (Ocleera), Halcy (Halcyona) and MM (Mistmerrow).</p>
<p><strong>Boss kill quests start on their own.</strong> Quests like &quot;Defeat the Hound of Kyrios!&quot; are <em>Path of Glory</em> quests. There is no NPC to visit: help kill the boss and the quest completes.</p>
<h2 id="crimson-rift">Crimson Rift</h2>
<p><strong>When:</strong> every 4 real hours, at <strong>12:00 in-game time</strong>. To see the game clock, hover over the top right of your screen. <strong>Level:</strong> 30 and up.</p>
<ol>
<li>Join a CR raid in faction chat (<code>x cr</code>).</li>
<li>Go to the rift zone and talk to the <strong>Recruiter</strong> to take <strong>Crimson Omens 1</strong>. Guards in the zone&#39;s Community Center hand out the rift quests too.</li>
<li><strong>Wave 1:</strong> kill the Crimson Army Infantrymen and Archers. Report to the Recruiter, who gives you Crimson Omens 2.</li>
<li><strong>Wave 2:</strong> kill the next wave, then report back for Crimson Omens 3.</li>
<li><strong>Wave 3:</strong> kill the Crimson Army Commanders and Spearmen, then report to the Recruiter.</li>
<li>Stay for the Hounds (next section).</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Rewards:</strong> 1,000 Honor and 10 Leadership.</p>
<h2 id="hound-of-kyrios">Hound of Kyrios</h2>
<p>Two <strong>Hounds of Kyrios</strong> spawn at the end of every Crimson Rift.</p>
<ol>
<li>Finish the three Crimson Rift waves above.</li>
<li>Stay with the raid at the end of the rift. The Hounds spawn there.</li>
<li>Hit a Hound until it dies. <strong>Defeat the Hound of Kyrios!</strong> completes when you have helped kill one. You don&#39;t need an NPC.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Rewards:</strong> <strong>3 Gilda Stars</strong>, 2 Clear Synthium Shards, 7 Adventurer&#39;s Growthstones and a Hound of Kyrios Soulstone. You can get this once a day.</p>
<h2 id="anthalon">Anthalon</h2>
<p>Anthalon is a world boss in <strong>Sungold Fields</strong> who spawns right after the Sungold Fields Crimson Rift.</p>
<ol>
<li>Check <a href="/aac-hub/timers">timers</a> or faction chat for the rift that lands in <strong>Sungold Fields</strong>.</li>
<li>Do that Crimson Rift and its Hounds as usual.</li>
<li>Stay put. Anthalon spawns when the rift ends.</li>
<li>Help kill him. <strong>Defeat Anthalon!</strong> completes on its own.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Tips:</strong> kill the mobs he summons quickly, because they stun. When he lifts you into the air (<em>Absorb Mind</em>), use <strong>Shrug It Off</strong> before you hit the ground. Stay out of 100m if you don&#39;t want to be pulled in.</p>
<p><strong>Rewards:</strong> <strong>3 Gilda Stars</strong>, 2 Clear Synthium Shards and 7 Growthstones. On Sundays the same kill also counts for the weekly <a href="/aac-hub/guides/weekly-activity-tokens#the-crimson-threat">Crimson Threat</a> token quest.</p>
<h2 id="grimgast-rift">Grimgast Rift</h2>
<p><strong>When:</strong> every 4 real hours, at <strong>00:00 in-game time</strong>. <strong>Level:</strong> 45 and up.</p>
<ol>
<li>Join a GR raid in faction chat (<code>x gr</code>).</li>
<li>At the Community Center, take a materials quest from the <strong>wooden board</strong> and hand in 10 of one basic material: stone brick, lumber, leather, fabric or iron ingot.</li>
<li>That unlocks <strong>Helping the War Effort</strong> from <strong>Quartermaster Fila</strong>. Carry <strong>2 packs</strong> of Condensed Archeum from the camp to the <strong>trebuchet</strong>. The Archeum appears at the camp from midnight in-game time. This step gives 1 Gilda Star.</li>
<li>Once the trebuchet is built, grab a <strong>harpoon</strong> from the rack.</li>
<li><strong>Halting the Crimson Tide 1 and 2:</strong> use the harpoon to beat two waves. Both quests complete on their own.</li>
<li>Stay for the Nightmares (next section).</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Rewards:</strong> <strong>8 Gilda Stars</strong> in total, the most of any daily, plus 10 Leadership.</p>
<h2 id="grimgast-nightmares">Grimgast Nightmares</h2>
<p>The <strong>Nightmare Blade</strong> and <strong>Nightmare Bowstring</strong> spawn after the second Grimgast wave, in Cinderstone Moor or Ynystere.</p>
<ol>
<li>Finish the Grimgast Rift waves above and keep your <strong>harpoon</strong>.</li>
<li>The Nightmares carry a shield. Use the harpoon&#39;s second skill, <strong>Time of Oblivion</strong>, to strip it.</li>
<li>Help kill both. <strong>Defeat the Nightmare Blade!</strong> and <strong>Defeat the Nightmare Bowstring!</strong> complete on their own.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Rewards:</strong> <strong>3 Gilda Stars</strong> each, plus 2 Clear Synthium Shards and 7 Growthstones each.</p>
<h2 id="whalesong-siege">Whalesong Siege</h2>
<p><strong>Where:</strong> the Nuia Defense Base in <strong>Whalesong Harbor</strong>, Auroria. <strong>Level:</strong> 55.</p>
<ol>
<li>Join a WS raid in faction chat (<code>x ws</code>).</li>
<li>Talk to <strong>Raiko</strong> at the base and take <strong>Anthalon&#39;s Elite</strong>. This also gives you Whalesong Siege 1 to 3.</li>
<li><strong>Waves 1 to 3:</strong> kill Anthalon&#39;s undead troops as they attack the base. Each wave completes one Siege quest.</li>
<li><strong>Boss:</strong> kill <strong>Possessed Jakar</strong> for <strong>Kill Possessed Jakar</strong>.</li>
<li>Turn in Anthalon&#39;s Elite to Raiko.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Rewards:</strong> <strong>20 Leadership</strong>. Jakar gives <strong>4 Gilda Stars</strong>, 10 Growthstones and 5 Cursed Armor Scraps. Anthalon&#39;s Elite gives 4 more Cursed Armor Scraps.</p>
<h2 id="aegis-island">Aegis Island</h2>
<p><strong>Where:</strong> <strong>Aegis Island</strong>, Auroria. <strong>Level:</strong> 55.</p>
<ol>
<li>Join an Aegis raid in faction chat (<code>x aegis</code>).</li>
<li>Talk to <strong>Scout Gaon</strong> on the island and take <strong>Aegis Island Guardian</strong>. This also gives you Defend the Seal 1 to 3.</li>
<li><strong>Seals 1 to 3:</strong> kill the ghouls attacking each seal, starting at the <strong>Eastern Seal</strong>. Each seal completes one Defend the Seal quest.</li>
<li><strong>Boss:</strong> go to the <strong>Northern Seal</strong>, kill the <strong>Final Sealbreaker</strong>, then enter the <strong>Ipnysh Gate</strong> to finish the quest.</li>
<li>Turn in Aegis Island Guardian to Scout Gaon.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Rewards:</strong> <strong>20 Leadership</strong>. The Final Sealbreaker gives <strong>4 Gilda Stars</strong>, 10 Growthstones and 5 Acid Gobbets. Aegis Island Guardian gives 3 more Acid Gobbets.</p>
<h2 id="ocleera-rift">Ocleera Rift</h2>
<p><strong>Where:</strong> the <strong>Riven Gates</strong> on the west side of <strong>Diamond Shores</strong>. <strong>Level:</strong> 46 and up.</p>
<ol>
<li>Talk to <strong>Scout Karlsburg</strong> and take <strong>Sprouting Ocleera&#39;s Marks</strong>. Kill the Sprouting Marks and their minions.</li>
<li>Take <strong>Engorged Ocleera&#39;s Marks</strong> from Scout Karlsburg and kill the Engorged Marks.</li>
<li>Take <strong>Monstrous Ocleera&#39;s Mark</strong> and kill the Monstrous Mark.</li>
<li>The <strong>Hateful Ocleera</strong> spawns. Help kill it for <strong>Kill the Hateful Ocleera</strong>. That quest starts when you are near the boss.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Rewards:</strong> 10 Leadership, Revenant Soulstones (5, 10 and 20), a Souleye and 3 Clear Synthium Shards. Every step also gives <strong>Prestige</strong> and guild XP (see <a href="/aac-hub/guides/guild-missions">guild missions</a>).</p>
<h2 id="comet-rift">Comet Rift</h2>
<p><strong>When:</strong> when a comet lands. A popup in game names the zone.</p>
<ol>
<li>Go to the comet and talk to <strong>Iseren</strong>.</li>
<li>The first time, Iseren sends you to <strong>Elloy</strong> on the <strong>1st floor of the Ayanad Library</strong>. When you come back, use the <strong>telescope</strong> and talk to Iseren until it is centred. Then use its <strong>Observe</strong> option.</li>
<li>Take the rift quests from Iseren:<ul>
<li>Rift 1: kill 25 Fire Sprites.</li>
<li>Rift 2: kill 15 Flame Elementals.</li>
<li>Rift 3: kill the Flame Guardian boss.</li>
</ul>
</li>
<li>Do the side quests at the same time: <strong>Scorched Earth</strong> (kill 10 rift elementals), <strong>Meteor Miner</strong> (mine a meteor) and <strong>Crystalized Clues</strong> (collect elemental shards from Flame Elementals).</li>
<li>Finish with <strong>The Eternal Flame</strong>: kill the <strong>Undying Flame</strong>.</li>
<li>Report back to Iseren in the Ayanad Library.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Rewards:</strong> <strong>Comet Shards</strong>. Spend them at the <strong>Comet Workbench</strong> in the top right of Diamond Shores on battle pets, the &quot;Starfallen&quot; title or the Cumulus Magitopher glider.</p>
<h2 id="mistmerrow">Mistmerrow</h2>
<p>Nuia, Haranya and Pirates fight over towers in each basin. On Classic the battle lasts 20 minutes. <strong>When:</strong> see <a href="/aac-hub/timers">timers</a>.</p>
<ol>
<li>When it starts, go to the Mistmerrow <strong>portal</strong> in your capital: Marianople for Nuia, Austera for Haranya.</li>
<li>Take the <strong>2 quests</strong> from the NPC in front of the portal, then go through.</li>
<li>Talk to the NPC inside. That completes one quest and gives you a new one to destroy the towers.</li>
<li>Help your faction destroy towers until the battle ends.</li>
<li>Stay for the gates and Kadum (next section).</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Rewards:</strong> Honor, Warrior Medals, Clear Synthium Shards and 10 Leadership.</p>
<h2 id="mistmerrow-gates-and-kadum">Mistmerrow gates and Kadum</h2>
<ol>
<li>After the Mistmerrow battle ends, the zone turns peaceful. Stay inside.</li>
<li>Take the <strong>2 new quests</strong> from the NPCs in the middle of the map.</li>
<li>Kill the 3 gates in the <strong>SE, W and NE basins</strong>.</li>
<li>Go back to the middle and help kill <strong>Kadum</strong>.</li>
<li>Turn in both quests at the NPCs in the middle.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Rewards:</strong> Kadum is worth <strong>4 Gilda Stars</strong>.</p>
<h2 id="halcyona-war">Halcyona War</h2>
<p><strong>When:</strong> see <a href="/aac-hub/timers">timers</a>.</p>
<ol>
<li>Take the first quest in your <strong>capital</strong>.</li>
<li>Go through the portal to <strong>Halcyona</strong>, or use your own portal.</li>
<li>Turn it in at your faction camp and take the second quest: <strong>kill 10 enemies</strong>. Kills are shared in a party or raid.</li>
<li>Help destroy the enemy base. If your side breaks the <strong>base crystal</strong>, loot it for a Warrior Medal Chest.</li>
<li>If you win, go back to the <strong>Community Center</strong> for a 2-hour buff.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Rewards:</strong> Honor, Clear Synthium Shards, Warrior Medals and 10 Leadership. The crystal chest gives 5 to 10 more Warrior Medals.</p>
<h2 id="luscas">Luscas</h2>
<p>Luscas are mini-Krakens. Each day they appear at one of three spots in the <strong>Sea of Graves</strong>, between Auroria and Freedich Island. <strong>When:</strong> see <a href="/aac-hub/timers">timers</a>. <strong>You need:</strong> a ship, or a seat on someone else&#39;s.</p>
<ol>
<li>Take <strong>Lusca Awakening</strong> from the <strong>Seamist Researcher</strong> at <strong>Ezi&#39;s Light</strong> in Diamond Shores. The portal in each big city also gets you close.</li>
<li>Sail out and help kill the Luscas.</li>
<li>Kill <strong>5 Luscas</strong> to finish the extra kill quest too.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Rewards:</strong> <strong>1 Gilda Star</strong> and 10 Shatigon&#39;s Sand from Lusca Awakening, plus Honor and 10 Leadership. The 5-kill quest gives another Gilda Star.</p>
<h2 id="abyssal-attack">Abyssal Attack</h2>
<p><strong>When:</strong> several times a week (see <a href="/aac-hub/timers">timers</a>). <strong>You need:</strong> a ship, or a seat on someone else&#39;s.</p>
<ol>
<li>Take <strong>Becoming a Seaknight</strong> and <strong>Stopping Doomsday</strong> from the <strong>Abyssal Attack Recruiter</strong>.</li>
<li>Sail to the <strong>Sea of Graves</strong>, between Heedmar and Freedich Island.</li>
<li>Kill all the Luscas and the <strong>Abyssal Kraken</strong>.</li>
<li>When a server message says Ezi&#39;s Light can be restored, go to the foot of the monument and use the <strong>beacon</strong> on the pedestal. It is a <strong>3-minute channel</strong>.</li>
<li>Protect whoever is channeling. Enemies can stop the channel by killing them, or by breaking the <strong>lodestones</strong> north, east and west of the monument with siege damage. If a lodestone breaks, the beacon explodes for 20,000 damage.</li>
<li>To finish Becoming a Seaknight, either restore Ezi&#39;s Light and get the Victory Flag buff, or destroy an enemy&#39;s Ocean&#39;s Guardstone.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Rewards:</strong> 2 Warrior Medals and 5 Shatigon&#39;s Sand from Stopping Doomsday, plus 10 Leadership.</p>
<h2 id="arena">Arena</h2>
<p><strong>Blood, Sweat, and Training</strong> is a daily quest for one arena match. You don&#39;t have to win.</p>
<ol>
<li>Talk to a <strong>Gladiator Arena Manager</strong> in Marianople, Austera, Seachild Wharf, Caernord or Growlgate Isle, and take <strong>Blood, Sweat, and Training</strong>.</li>
<li>Open the <strong>Convenience menu</strong> (bottom right) and queue for the <strong>Arena</strong>. Any mode counts.</li>
<li>Fight one opponent.</li>
<li>Go back to any Gladiator Arena Manager and turn it in.</li>
</ol>
<p><strong>Rewards:</strong> Honor.</p>
