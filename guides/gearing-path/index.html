Gearing path from 50 to endgame

Catch-up box, Emerald weapon, dungeon armor, Obsidian and what to buy instead of regrading yourself.

Watch first: the video above, Archeage Classic Gearing Guide 2025 by Thranaerttv, is the best walkthrough of the gearing path we've found. It was made for new and returning players, and the written steps below follow the same path so you can check your progress as you go.

The written guide follows the wiki's player-written gearing guide. Its main advice: buying finished gear from other players is usually cheaper than regrading it yourself.

Step 1: Catch-up gear (level 50)

Your Catch-up Gear Box appears at level 50. It has no waist or arm pieces, so fill those with cheap world gear of Rare or lower. Higher-grade pieces there would throw off your Armor Grade Bonus.

  • Lost Garden gear: search "Auroria" under Armor in the Auction House. Each piece boosts one skill per skill tree.
  • Accessories aren't in the box. Get them from mob drops, the Auction House or Activity Tokens.
  • Gems: Toughness in your pants and Resilience in your chest. The first 2 slots are 100%, so use tier 4 (pink) gems there. Use tier 3 (blue) gems in the rest, because they always succeed.
  • Upgrading catch-up gear: earn Activity Tokens, then talk to a Daru Auctioneer by a Mirage Isle portal to start the quest. The Soulforged anvil is in Diamond Shores.

Step 2: Divine dungeon armor and the Emerald weapon

  • Weapon: work your Emerald weapon up to Divine.
  • Armor: dungeon gear is "Easy" to regrade. Farm Greater Howling Abyss, regrade to Divine, and craft it into an Aria's Autograph. Open the Autograph at Mistsong Summit to pick the armor piece you want at the same grade.
  • Accessories: run the Dream Ring quest line if it suits your class. Healers may prefer the Ynystere Ring. Most classes should start the Halcyona Necklace. Fill the rest with Library, mob-drop or cheap crafted accessories, and don't overspend on them.

Step 3: Crafted and Obsidian gear

Aim for Divine or Epic, and buy it from other players when you can.

  • Obsidian tiers up with simple Folio recipes (default key O). Epic Obsidian usually goes to T6, Divine to T4 or T5.
  • Crafted gear tiers up through an RNG crafting system.
  • Gem and temper last. Crafting removes gems and tempers but keeps the grade, so wait until max tier.
  • Waist and arms: there's no Obsidian for these slots. Most classes use Serp Sleeves. Mages and healers usually use a Cruel-Glory Sash. Others use a crafted belt or an Auroria belt with a skill bonus.
  • Prestige gems (Lunascales) have a 100% slot chance on every slot and are tradeable. Crafted gems give more stats, but only the first 2 slots are 100%.
  • Then upgrade everything else: accessories, instrument, bow and off pieces. Look at stat costumes and undergarments too.

Step 4: Repeat with better gear

Next targets are an Epic Ayanad weapon, an Epic Tier 7 weapon, Epic armor or a Legendary weapon.
