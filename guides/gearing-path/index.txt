18:T1180,<p><strong>Watch first:</strong> the video above, <a href="https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=mugI8Trhrcw">Archeage Classic Gearing Guide 2025</a> by <a href="https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCzmV5UoMXUEMGzpFWAJ4y_Q">Thranaerttv</a>, is the best walkthrough of the gearing path we&#39;ve found. It was made for new and returning players, and the written steps below follow the same path so you can check your progress as you go.</p>
<p>The written guide follows the wiki&#39;s player-written gearing guide. Its main advice: <strong>buying finished gear from other players is usually cheaper than regrading it yourself.</strong></p>
<h2 id="step-1-catch-up-gear-level-50">Step 1: Catch-up gear (level 50)</h2>
<p>Your <a href="/aac-hub/guides/catch-up-gear-box">Catch-up Gear Box</a> appears at level 50. It has no waist or arm pieces, so fill those with cheap world gear of Rare or lower. Higher-grade pieces there would throw off your Armor Grade Bonus.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Lost Garden gear:</strong> search &quot;Auroria&quot; under Armor in the Auction House. Each piece boosts one skill per skill tree.</li>
<li><strong>Accessories</strong> aren&#39;t in the box. Get them from mob drops, the Auction House or Activity Tokens.</li>
<li><strong>Gems:</strong> Toughness in your pants and Resilience in your chest. The first 2 slots are 100%, so use tier 4 (pink) gems there. Use tier 3 (blue) gems in the rest, because they always succeed.</li>
<li><strong>Upgrading catch-up gear:</strong> earn Activity Tokens, then talk to a Daru Auctioneer by a Mirage Isle portal to start the quest. The Soulforged anvil is in Diamond Shores.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="step-2-divine-dungeon-armor-and-the-emerald-weapon">Step 2: Divine dungeon armor and the Emerald weapon</h2>
<ul>
<li><strong>Weapon:</strong> work your <a href="/aac-hub/guides/emerald-weapon">Emerald weapon</a> up to Divine.</li>
<li><strong>Armor:</strong> dungeon gear is &quot;Easy&quot; to regrade. Farm Greater Howling Abyss, regrade to Divine, and craft it into an <strong>Aria&#39;s Autograph</strong>. Open the Autograph at Mistsong Summit to pick the armor piece you want at the same grade.</li>
<li><strong>Accessories:</strong> run the Dream Ring quest line if it suits your class. Healers may prefer the Ynystere Ring. Most classes should start the Halcyona Necklace. Fill the rest with Library, mob-drop or cheap crafted accessories, and don&#39;t overspend on them.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="step-3-crafted-and-obsidian-gear">Step 3: Crafted and Obsidian gear</h2>
<p>Aim for Divine or Epic, and buy it from other players when you can.</p>
<ul>
<li><strong>Obsidian</strong> tiers up with simple Folio recipes (default key <strong>O</strong>). Epic Obsidian usually goes to T6, Divine to T4 or T5.</li>
<li><strong>Crafted gear</strong> tiers up through an RNG crafting system.</li>
<li><strong>Gem and temper last.</strong> Crafting removes gems and tempers but keeps the grade, so wait until max tier.</li>
<li><strong>Waist and arms:</strong> there&#39;s no Obsidian for these slots. Most classes use <strong>Serp Sleeves</strong>. Mages and healers usually use a <strong>Cruel-Glory Sash</strong>. Others use a crafted belt or an Auroria belt with a skill bonus.</li>
<li><strong>Prestige gems (Lunascales)</strong> have a 100% slot chance on every slot and are tradeable. Crafted gems give more stats, but only the first 2 slots are 100%.</li>
<li>Then upgrade everything else: accessories, instrument, bow and off pieces. Look at stat costumes and undergarments too.</li>
</ul>
<h2 id="step-4-repeat-with-better-gear">Step 4: Repeat with better gear</h2>
<p>Next targets are an Epic Ayanad weapon, an Epic Tier 7 weapon, Epic armor or a Legendary weapon.</p>
